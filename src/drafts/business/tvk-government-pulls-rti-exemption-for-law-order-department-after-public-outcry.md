On Sunday, the ruling TVK government announced the withdrawal of its order that had exempted the Public (Law and Order) department from the Right to Information (RTI) Act, responding to a wave of public outrage.

## Classification under the withdrawn order

The rescinded order had designated the department as an 'Intelligence and Security Organisation', a label that would have placed it outside the scope of the RTI Act.

## Implications for RTI coverage

By classifying the department as an intelligence body, the government intended to keep it shielded from mandatory information disclosures, effectively denying citizens the right to request records from the law‑and‑order agency.

## Public reaction and reversal

Widespread backlash against the exemption prompted the government to reverse its decision, signalling that public pressure can influence policy on transparency and accountability.
