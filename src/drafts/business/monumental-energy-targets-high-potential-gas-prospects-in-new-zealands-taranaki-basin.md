Monumental Energy Corp has completed a reinterpretation of data for the Mauku block within the Tirua Petroleum Exploration Permit (PEP 61554), resulting in the selection of a specific target area. The company is currently awaiting final permit approval from the New Zealand Petroleum and Minerals (NZPAM) governing body to proceed.

## Technical Prospects and Resource Potential

Technical analysis conducted by the bid group has identified two distinct gas condensate prospects. The Group prospect, located updip of the 2013 Mauku-1 well which recorded a 140m net reservoir, has a recoverable resource potential of 200 PJ. A second, larger target known as the Mangatoa prospect is situated at a deeper level in the mid-Cretaceous Taniwha Formation and possesses a potential exceeding 1,000 PJ.

The company's findings are supported by historical data from the 1986 Te Ranga-1 offshore well, which intersected a 100m section of the Taniwha Formation between 3,740 and 3,842m AHBKB. According to petrophysical reviews of wireline and well data, there are movable hydrocarbons within the sands of this unit.

## Operational Advantages and Logistics

The target area covers 254 sq km, spanning both onshore and offshore regions in the northern Taranaki Basin. Monumental Energy noted that the Mauku NE target is particularly compelling due to its relatively shallow depth of 2,000m and the fact that it can be drilled from an onshore location.

Infrastructure for commercialization is already in place, with gas processing facilities capable of handling and processing gas for direct market sale located as close as 30km from the target area. Additionally, recently reprocessed seismic data indicates that the southern culmination of the Mangatoa target, located below Mauku NE, is also drillable.
