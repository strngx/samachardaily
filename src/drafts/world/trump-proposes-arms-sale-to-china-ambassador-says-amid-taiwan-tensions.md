President Donald Trump told U.S. Ambassador to China David Perdue that he was prepared to sell arms to Beijing, a claim first disclosed in a Fox News interview that aired on Sunday, September 27, 2026. The ambassador relayed the offer during a conversation with host Shannon Bream, confirming that the president had raised the idea directly with Chinese officials.

## Context of the Offer

The proposal, according to Channel News Asia, was framed as a response to growing concern in Washington over recent U.S. weapons sales to Taiwan. China maintains that Taiwan is part of its territory, and any American arms transfers to the island have heightened diplomatic friction between the two powers.

## Discussion with President Xi

The Hill reported that the arms‑sale suggestion was discussed during recent meetings between President Trump and Chinese President Xi Jinping. Perdue said the two leaders exchanged views on the matter, indicating that the idea was not merely speculative but part of a broader dialogue taking place at the highest level.

## Ambassador's Remarks

During the Fox News segment, Ambassador Perdue quoted the president verbatim: “Hey, we sell arms to other people around the world.” The ambassador emphasized that the statement reflected Trump’s longstanding view that the United States should keep its defense‑export options open, even to strategic competitors.

## Implications of the Disclosure

By linking a potential China deal to the contentious issue of Taiwan arms sales, the proposal introduces a new variable into an already complex U.S.–China strategic relationship. While no formal agreement has been announced, the public disclosure suggests that Washington may be willing to negotiate defense trade concessions with Beijing, a move that could prompt scrutiny from Congress and allied nations monitoring arms‑export policies.
