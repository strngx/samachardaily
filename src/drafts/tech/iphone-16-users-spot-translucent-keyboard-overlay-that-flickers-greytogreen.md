A Reddit post on r/iphone dated September 27, 2026 reports a persistent visual glitch on the iPhone 16 that appears as a large, translucent rectangle overlaying the on‑screen keyboard.

## Description of the anomaly

The poster says the rectangle occupies roughly 80 % of the keyboard area, is not solid, and alternates between grey and green tones while remaining partially see‑through. The user notes that the shape is not a fixed image but changes color sporadically.

## User context and limitations

The Redditor admits they could not capture a clear screenshot of the issue, limiting visual verification. No additional details about app usage, iOS version, or whether the problem occurs across multiple apps were provided.

## Official response

As of the time of writing, Apple has not issued a statement or acknowledged the reported behavior. No known software update or troubleshooting guidance has been published to address the described overlay.
