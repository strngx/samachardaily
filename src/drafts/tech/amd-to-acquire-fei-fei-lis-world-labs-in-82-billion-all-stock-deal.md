AMD announced an all-stock acquisition of artificial intelligence startup World Labs in a transaction valued at approximately $8.2 billion. As part of the agreement, World Labs co-founder Dr. Fei-Fei Li will join AMD as executive vice president and chief scientist, reporting directly to Chief Executive Officer Lisa Su. The transaction is projected to close by the end of 2026.

## Strategic AI Integration and Product Portfolio

AMD stated that the acquisition is designed to enhance its capacity to build integrated AI hardware, software, and systems customized around the requirements of emerging models and applications. World Labs launched in 2024 and achieved a $1 billion valuation within months of its founding. In 2025, the startup released its initial commercial offering, a world-generation model named Marble that enables users to generate interactive 3D environments from text prompts.

## Executive Leadership and Research Focus

Following the completion of the acquisition, the World Labs team will maintain its primary operational focus on advancing AI model research. In a statement published to her Substack, Dr. Li explained that joining AMD represents the appropriate path to advance foundation models beyond traditional large language models. She stated that the combined entities remain committed to advancing the frontier of artificial intelligence to address practical problems while collaborating openly with the broader ecosystem.

AMD Chief Executive Officer Lisa Su emphasized that constructing compute platforms for upcoming AI architectures requires a comprehensive understanding of model evolution. Su noted that Dr. Li and the World Labs research team bring model expertise and leadership that will directly inform the development of next-generation hardware, software, and systems designed to strengthen an open AI ecosystem.

## Industry Context and AI Acquisitions

The acquisition marks another multi-billion dollar transaction within the artificial intelligence sector in late 2026. Earlier in the month, rival chip manufacturer Nvidia announced an agreement to acquire AI platform Hugging Face for nearly $13 billion.
