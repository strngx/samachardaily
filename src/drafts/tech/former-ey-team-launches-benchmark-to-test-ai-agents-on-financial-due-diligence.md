A team of former EY employees has created the Finance Agents Benchmark, known as FAB, to evaluate how artificial intelligence agents perform in financial due diligence tasks.

## Evaluating AI Performance in Due Diligence

According to the creators of the benchmark, FAB specifically tests the ability of AI agents to navigate the processes required for financial due diligence. The framework evaluates whether automated systems can identify relevant facts within financial records.

## Current Technical Shortfalls

The developers reported that while current AI agents can successfully locate relevant facts, they still struggle to carry those facts through an entire, complete due diligence process. The creators noted that complex financial work serves as a foundational element of the global economy, making rigorous evaluation of automated tools essential.
