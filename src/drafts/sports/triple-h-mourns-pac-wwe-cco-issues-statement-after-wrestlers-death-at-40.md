Professional wrestler Benjamin Satterley, who performed under the ring name PAC, died on September 28, 2026 at the age of 40.

## Statement from Triple H

WWE Chief Creative Officer Triple H issued a public statement expressing condolences to PAC’s family, friends, and fans, and acknowledging the wrestler’s contributions to the sport.

The announcement has prompted an outpouring of tributes across the wrestling community, with peers and promoters noting PAC’s impact on both WWE and the broader independent circuit.

No further details about memorial arrangements or organizational actions have been disclosed.
