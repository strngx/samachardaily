Brandon Ingram will not be available when the Los Angeles Clippers begin the 2026‑27 NBA season, as he continues recovery from a recent operation.

## Injury and surgery

The procedure addressed two distinct problems: a right heel injury and a partially torn Achilles tendon. Both conditions required surgical intervention, prompting the team to place Ingram on the inactive list until he is cleared for play.

## Role in the Clippers' rebuild

Ingram is identified as a key component of the Clippers' roster overhaul that followed the trade of Kawhi Leonard. His absence at the season's start removes a primary scoring and defensive option that the franchise counted on to solidify its post‑Leonard identity.
