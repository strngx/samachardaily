Political leaders from the Bharatiya Janata Party and the Indian National Congress paid tribute to revolutionary freedom fighter Bhagat Singh on Monday, marking his birth anniversary on September 28, 2026. The commemorations cut across party lines, with chief ministers, union ministers, and the Leader of the Opposition in the Lok Sabha issuing statements that emphasized his courage, patriotism, and sacrifice during India's struggle for independence.

## BJP and Union Government Tributes

Uttar Pradesh Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath posted a tribute on social media platform X, describing Bhagat Singh as an "immortal revolutionary" who dedicated his life to the country's freedom. Adityanath wrote that Singh's "courage, sacrifice, and martyrdom for safeguarding the nation's pride and honour remain an eternal source of inspiration for all of us." Maharashtra Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis issued a statement honouring the "great revolutionary Shaheed Bhagat Singh, whose courage, patriotism, and supreme sacrifice became a powerful symbol of India's struggle for freedom."

Union Education Minister Pralhad Joshi remembered Singh on his birth anniversary, stating that his "indomitable courage, unwavering patriotism and supreme sacrifice for the nation remain an eternal source of inspiration for every Indian." Joshi added that Singh's "spirit of courage and sacrifice continues to ignite the flame of patriotism in generations to come." Union Minister G. Kishan Reddy paid "humble tribute" to "Shaheed-e-Azam Sardar Bhagat Singh," noting that his "extraordinary courage, supreme sacrifice, and unwavering commitment to the cause of freedom continue to inspire generations." Reddy highlighted Singh's "revolutionary ideas, intellectual conviction, and spirit of resistance" as a "lasting source of inspiration for the youth of India."

## Congress Leadership Tributes

Leader of the Opposition in the Lok Sabha, Rahul Gandhi, issued a statement paying "humble respects" to "Shaheed-e-Azam Bhagat Singh Ji." Gandhi said Singh's "struggle for India's independence, indomitable courage, and supreme sacrifice will forever remain a source of inspiration for all of us." Congress President Mallikarjun Kharge paid tribute by sharing Hindi verses: "Likh raha hun mai anjaam jiska...kal aagaaj aayega... Mere lahu ka har ek katra...inkalab layega." Kharge added, "On the birth anniversary of the great revolutionary who sacrificed everything for the freedom of the nation, Shaheed-e-Azam Bhagat Singh ji, I pay my respectful homage."

## Bhagat Singh's Enduring Legacy

The tributes collectively underscored Bhagat Singh's historical role as a revolutionary who fought against the British colonial regime and raised his voice against social inequality and divisive forces. Each statement framed his legacy as a continuing inspiration for contemporary India, with particular emphasis on his intellectual conviction and revolutionary ideas that resonate with younger generations. The cross-party participation in the commemorations reflects a shared political consensus on Singh's place in the national pantheon of freedom fighters.
