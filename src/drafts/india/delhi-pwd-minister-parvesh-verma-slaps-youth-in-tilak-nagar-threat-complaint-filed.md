A video released on social media captured Delhi Public Works Department (PWD) Minister Parvesh Verma striking a local youth with an open hand during a routine road‑inspection in the Tilak Nagar neighbourhood of Delhi. The footage, which quickly spread online, shows the minister in broad daylight confronting the young man, who appears visibly shocked after the slap. The incident was recorded by by‑standers and uploaded to multiple platforms, where it amassed thousands of views within hours of posting.

## Incident Details

The Times of India reported that the altercation occurred while the minister was overseeing repair work on a municipal road. The youth, identified in the video as “Saheb,” approached the minister to discuss a lingering pothole issue that had been raised repeatedly by residents. According to the video, Verma responded by raising his hand and delivering a single slap before walking away. No other physical contact was shown, and the surrounding crowd appeared to react with surprise rather than intervene.

## Legal Complaint

Following the viral spread of the video, the youth filed a formal complaint with Delhi police alleging that Minister Verma not only slapped him but also threatened to kill him. The complaint, filed on the same day the video surfaced, cites verbal intimidation in addition to the physical act. Police officials have logged the complaint under sections relating to assault and criminal intimidation, though no arrest or further legal action has been disclosed as of the report date.

## Political Reaction

The Cockroach Janata Party (CJP), a minor political outfit that positions itself as a voice for younger voters, issued a statement condemning the minister’s conduct. The party’s spokesperson quoted, “Gen Z wants answers, not intimidation,” directly referencing the viral video. The CJP’s criticism underscores a broader demand for accountability from elected officials, especially when interactions with the public turn violent.

## Public and Media Response

Social media users across Delhi expressed outrage, with many demanding a transparent inquiry into the minister’s behavior. Commentators highlighted the incident as part of a pattern of alleged misuse of power by senior officials. The Times of India noted that the video’s rapid circulation amplified public scrutiny, prompting calls for the minister to address the allegations in a public forum.

## Legal and Administrative Outlook

Delhi’s PWD department has not issued an official comment on the incident, and the minister’s office declined to respond to media inquiries at the time of reporting. Legal experts cited by the Times of India indicated that the complaint could lead to a formal investigation by the police, and, depending on findings, may result in disciplinary action within the department or even a criminal case. The outcome could influence the minister’s standing within the ruling party and affect upcoming municipal projects overseen by the PWD.
