The director of state‑run Oil and Natural Gas Corporation (ONGC) is publicly advocating for the introduction of E20 petrol across India, positioning the blend as a strategic priority for the sector.

According to the director, shifting to E20 petrol could materially lower the volume of petroleum that India imports, offering a tangible avenue to reduce the country’s trade deficit in energy.

The push for E20 is also framed as a step toward greater energy self‑reliance, aligning with broader national objectives to lessen dependence on foreign fuel supplies.
