Funds totaling Rs 201 crore have been disbursed by the Punjab state administration to upgrade infrastructure across Scheduled Caste habitations, according to details released by Minister Dr Baljit Kaur.

## Rural Infrastructure Allocation

Under the state welfare scheme, 3,763 SC-majority villages have been selected for comprehensive development into model villages. The Rs 201 crore allocation is designated to fund essential public infrastructure, civic upgrades, and community facilities in the identified habitations.
