Prakash Reddy, a senior leader of the Bharatiya Janata Party, declared that throwing mud on the Election Commission equates to an attack on democracy, a remark made during the ongoing SIR controversy.

## Immediate Statement

He emphasized the metaphor, stating that the physical act of hurling mud on the commission's symbols directly undermines the institution's credibility and, by extension, the democratic process itself.

## Context of the SIR Controversy

The SIR controversy, involving legal challenges and political disagreements about the Election Commission's conduct, provided the immediate backdrop for Reddy's criticism.

## Party Position

Reddy's comment reflects the party's broader stance that any physical desecration of electoral bodies is unacceptable, reinforcing its criticism of opposition tactics that target the Election Commission.
