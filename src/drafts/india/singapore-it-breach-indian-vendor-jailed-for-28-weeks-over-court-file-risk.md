A court in Singapore has sentenced an Indian IT vendor to 28 weeks in prison following operational failures that exposed 18,016 files to potential risk. The judicial ruling targets third-party technical handling surrounding records linked to the Singapore State Courts.

## Security Risks Surrounding Judicial Files

The legal action against the contractor emphasizes strict accountability standards applied to public sector data management in Singapore. Official evidence established that the Indian IT vendor was handed the 28-week custodial sentence after placing a total of 18,016 court records at risk.

## Vendor Accountability and Legal Outcome

The 28-week sentence reflects a firm stance by Singaporean authorities regarding cybersecurity risks introduced by external service providers. The legal proceedings concluded with the vendor's imprisonment to address the security exposure involving state court documentation.
