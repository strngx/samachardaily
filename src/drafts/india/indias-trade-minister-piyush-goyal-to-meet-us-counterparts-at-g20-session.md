New Delhi – Commerce and Industry Minister Piyush Goyal is set to travel to the United States for the upcoming gathering of G20 trade ministers, where he will engage directly with his American counterparts.

## Key Operational Details

The minister’s itinerary places the United States as the host venue for the G20 trade ministers' meeting, and his schedule includes a series of bilateral discussions with U.S. trade officials. These meetings are scheduled as an integral component of the broader G20 ministerial session.

Goyal, who heads India’s commerce and industry portfolio, will use the visit to represent Indian trade interests within the G20 framework. His presence underscores India’s participation in the multilateral dialogue that brings together the world’s major economies.

## Stakeholder Impact & Next Steps

The bilateral talks are expected to provide a platform for both sides to exchange views on trade cooperation, although specific outcomes have not been disclosed. No confirmed next steps have been reported beyond the scheduled meetings.
