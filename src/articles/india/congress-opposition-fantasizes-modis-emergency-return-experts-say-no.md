An OrangeNews9 commentary says sections of the Congress‑led opposition are entertaining a "political fantasy" that intense provocation of Prime Minister Narendra Modi could force him to impose an Emergency like the one declared in June 1975.

The piece argues that even if Modi faces relentless street agitation, personal attacks or being cornered, such pressure will not compel him to revert to authoritarian rule, rejecting the opposition’s speculation.

By dismissing the scenario, the article underscores that the feared return of an Emergency remains unrealistic.
