Special Judge Dig Vinay Singh of the Delhi High Court ordered a stay on the dismissal of Congress MP Akoijam, who faces allegations in the JNU harassment case.

The judge also directed the Delhi Police to submit its response to Akoijam’s bail plea, emphasizing that no coercive action will be taken against the MP until the court’s next scheduled hearing.

The police’s reply will be examined at that hearing, after which the court will decide whether any further measures are warranted.
