A church inauguration in West Bengal’s South 24 Parganas district was disrupted by a mob of 30‑35 people who stormed the venue, vandalised the property and assaulted clergy and attendees.

Witnesses said the attackers damaged the premises, destroyed mobile phones and physically harmed several women among the devotees, while threatening to halt religious activities. The violence left multiple victims injured and prompted an immediate police response.

Christian groups swiftly condemned the assault, calling it a targeted intimidation of minority communities. The incident has heightened concerns about the safety of religious gatherings in the region and sparked calls for stronger protection measures.
