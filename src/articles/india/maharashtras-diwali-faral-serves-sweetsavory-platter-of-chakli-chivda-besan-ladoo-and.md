Maharashtra’s Diwali Faral is a festive platter that brings together the state’s favourite sweet and savoury treats during the Diwali holiday.

The spread typically includes crunchy chakli, spiced chivda, soft besan ladoo and crisp karanji, offering a balanced mix of flavors that typify regional celebrations.

By assembling these items on a single plate, the faral highlights the region’s culinary heritage and serves as a shared indulgence for families gathering for Diwali, underscoring the diversity of Maharashtrian festive cuisine.
