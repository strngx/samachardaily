The Delhi Metro Rail Corporation will begin services at 4:30 a.m. on November 16 instead of the usual 6 a.m., facilitating devotees traveling for Usha Arghya during Chhath Puja. Trains will operate on a 15-minute frequency across all lines until regular revenue services commence.

Chief Minister Rekha Gupta announced the extended timings on X, inviting devotees to join her at Elephant Ghat near ITO to offer arghya to the rising sun. She said the arrangement ensures mothers, sisters and elders reach ghats without difficulty.

Gupta added that the BJP government has made elaborate Chhath arrangements across Delhi, calling on families to worship Chhathi Maiya with devotion. She said no devotee's worship should be disrupted.
