Delhi and the National Capital Region are entering the fortnight with the highest air quality index levels of the year, driven by stubble burning, increased festival traffic, and firecracker emissions.

Diwali falls on November 8 this year, likely pushing pollution levels further. Historical data show severe pollution days typically occur in the first half of November, while low temperatures and atmospheric conditions compound the problem.

The Times of India reported the seasonal trend based on historical air quality data from the region.
