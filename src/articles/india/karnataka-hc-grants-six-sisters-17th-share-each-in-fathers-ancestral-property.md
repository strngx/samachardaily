The Karnataka High Court has awarded six sisters a one-seventh share each in their father’s ancestral property, rejecting an objection from their brothers’ wives who cited an earlier partition. The court ruled the sisters are entitled to the shares under the Hindu Succession Act.

The brothers’ spouses had claimed an earlier partition of the property, but the court found no evidence to substantiate that claim.

The judgment also includes the late mother’s rightful portion in the sisters’ shares.
