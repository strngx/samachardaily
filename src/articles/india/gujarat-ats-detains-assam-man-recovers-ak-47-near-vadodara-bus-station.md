Md Saharul Islam, a native of Assam, was detained by the Gujarat Anti-Terrorism Squad during a special operation in Vadodara district. The action followed a specific intelligence input.

The ATS recovered an AK-47 rifle and 23 live cartridges from a suspicious vehicle near Jarod bus station. Preliminary investigations have allegedly revealed links between him and the Pakistan-based terrorist organisation Jaish-e-Mohammed.

The ATS is now questioning the accused to establish the source of the recovered weapons, identify his alleged network in Gujarat and determine who was supposed to receive the consignment.
