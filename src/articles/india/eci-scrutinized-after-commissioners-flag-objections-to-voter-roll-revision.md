Reports indicate that two Election Commissioners raised objections on several occasions concerning the Special Intensive Revision (SIR) of electoral rolls, specifically questioning voter registration, deletions and access to the electoral‑roll database.

The Election Commission of India rejected claims of a rift, stating that individual observations and differing views are part of its normal decision‑making process and that all SIR decisions were taken unanimously. The episode has sparked questions about the procedures used during the roll revision and calls for greater clarity on decision‑making safeguards.
