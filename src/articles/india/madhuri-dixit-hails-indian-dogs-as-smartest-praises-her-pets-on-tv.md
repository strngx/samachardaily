Bollywood icon Madhuri Dixit told a contestant on the game show Kon Honar Crorepati that she is a pet parent to two dogs – Indie, an Indian breed, and Koko, a small Poodle.

She described Indian dogs as ‘very smart, very intelligent’, noting that her pets follow the household routine and become visibly excited when a staff member announces a visitor’s arrival over the phone.

Dixit, who regularly posts pictures of her pets, also asked the audience to clap for the ‘Indie babies’ and mentioned speaking to her dog in baby language.
