Bengaluru: Karnataka Home Minister Priyank Kharge on Saturday said the use of camera-equipped Meta smart glasses during meetings with senior ministers or the Chief Minister could raise doubts, stressing that visitors should follow protocol and respect the dignity of the host.

The warning came amid a broader debate over whether such devices should be permitted in government settings, with Kharge suggesting that their use could cast a shadow of suspicion over interactions between top leaders and officials.

Kharge's remarks reflect growing concern among state-level officials about the implications of wearable technology in formal political and administrative contexts.
