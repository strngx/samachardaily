According to The Economic Times report, health insurance claims related to pollution have increased markedly over the last three years, rising 4.3 times, while cardiovascular and respiratory conditions constitute the majority of these claims, the report said.

The claims significantly affect working professionals and senior citizens, while Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities account for a substantial portion of these claims across India.

A seasonal pattern in the data indicates these claims peak during October to March, coinciding with worsening air quality, with that October-to-March period accounting for the highest claim concentration.
