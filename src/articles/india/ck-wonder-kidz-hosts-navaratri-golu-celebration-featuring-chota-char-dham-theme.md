CK Wonder Kidz organized a nine-day Navaratri Golu celebration in Chennai, showcasing India's spiritual heritage through a Chota Char Dham theme. The event aimed to connect young learners with devotional traditions and cultural values.

Children and parents participated in various cultural activities, devotional traditions, and festive experiences throughout the celebration. The program emphasized the values of Win @ Life alongside traditional Golu displays.

The Golu celebration concluded on October 16, 2026, marking the end of the nine-day festivities that brought together families for spiritual and cultural engagement in Chennai.
