A senior bank manager in Kolkata has been accused of leaking account data that facilitated a Rs 2.7 crore fraud scheme. Prosecutors say the manager used cloned cheques to siphon money from a Bihar corporation, resulting in 56 unauthorized withdrawals that are now under investigation.

The case highlights vulnerabilities in cheque authentication and data protection within the banking sector. Authorities are probing how the manager accessed and shared the account information, while the corporation seeks recovery of the misappropriated funds.

The alleged breach has prompted a review of internal controls at the bank, with regulators examining how sensitive account information could be accessed. The corporation has filed a police complaint, and the bank is cooperating with the ongoing probe.
