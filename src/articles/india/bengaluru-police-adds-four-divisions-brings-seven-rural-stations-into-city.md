Bengaluru City Police has added four new divisions and incorporated seven rural police stations within city limits, restructuring its divisional framework and expanding urban staffing. This move aims to improve coverage and response in the growing metropolis.

The expansion reflects the police's effort to align jurisdictional boundaries with urban development, bringing previously rural stations under city policing. Details on implementation timeline or staffing numbers were not provided.

The restructuring is expected to enhance service delivery and operational efficiency across Bengaluru, though no further rollout details have been disclosed.
