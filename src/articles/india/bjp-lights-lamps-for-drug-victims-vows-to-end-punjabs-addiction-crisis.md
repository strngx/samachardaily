On Sunday, November 8, Punjab’s BJP gathered at its Chandigarh state office to observe Diwali and Bandi Chhor Divas by lighting special lamps honoring young people who died from drug abuse. Punjab BJP President Kewal Singh Dhillon led the ceremony, lighting a lamp dedicated to the youths lost to addiction and offering prayers for families shattered by the drug menace.

The party linked Bandi Chhor Divas, commemorating the sixth Sikh Guru’s release from oppression, to its resolve to eradicate the drug crisis affecting Punjab’s youth and families. Dhillon prayed for the state to be freed from the scourge of drugs, urging peace, prosperity and happiness to return to every household, and reaffirmed the BJP’s commitment to stand with affected families until the menace is eliminated.
