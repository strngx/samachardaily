The Indian rupee slipped below the 94 per dollar level, ending its recent appreciation streak as state‑owned banks intensified dollar buying ahead of the October inflation data release. The depreciation was also reflected in regional currency markets.

Rising crude oil prices, a reversal in domestic equity markets, and hawkish signals from the US Federal Reserve added further pressure on the currency, weighing it down against the strengthening dollar, which together intensified the downward pressure.

Analysts noted the combination of heightened dollar demand, elevated oil costs and equity market volatility highlighted the rupee's vulnerability.
