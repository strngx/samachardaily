New Zealand have announced their squad for the upcoming two-match Test series against India, with Tom Latham confirmed as captain. The side will begin the series on home soil later this month.

The opening Test is scheduled at the Basin Reserve in Wellington, with play set to begin on November 19. New Zealand will host the first of the two fixtures there.

The series then shifts to Hagley Oval in Christchurch, where the second Test starts on November 27, eight days after the opening match.
