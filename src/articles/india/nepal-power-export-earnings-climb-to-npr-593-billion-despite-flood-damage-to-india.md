Nepal exported 633.99 million units of electricity to India during Aswin, the third month of fiscal year 2026-27, the Nepal Electricity Authority said Oct 25.

Export revenue increased by NPR 962 million compared with the same month in fiscal year 2025-26, when earnings were NPR 4.96 billion, according to the NEA.

Floods damaged 12 hydropower projects including seven operational and three approved for export, suspending Bangladesh electricity supplies; the Trishuli and Chilime plants in Rasuwa were hit on Aug 26, and Nepal resumed Bangladesh exports on Friday after India's Central Electricity Authority authorised power from alternative facilities.
