Royal Enfield is set to launch the Himalayan 750 in India by early 2027. The motorcycle will be powered by a 750cc twin-cylinder engine and will serve as the company's flagship adventure-touring model. It will be the latest addition to the Himalayan line-up.

The Himalayan name has built a reputation for long-distance travel, rugged terrain and a straightforward riding experience. The new model aims to carry that reputation forward, and its arrival could mark a defining moment for Royal Enfield's adventure-motorcycling ambitions. It is intended for long-distance travel and rugged terrain.
