Fresh snowfall has blanketed Shri Gangotri Dham in Uttarakhand, turning the sacred pilgrimage site into a picturesque winter destination. The snow-covered landscape has drawn tourists and pilgrims alike, eager to experience the chilly charm of the Himalayan region.

The fresh snowfall adds to the spiritual and natural allure of Gangotri Dham, one of the Char Dham pilgrimage sites. Visitors are advised to prepare for cold weather conditions and check local travel advisories before planning their visit.

With winter setting in across the Himalayas, several high-altitude destinations in India are witnessing renewed interest from travelers looking to escape the plains' heat and enjoy snowy vistas.
