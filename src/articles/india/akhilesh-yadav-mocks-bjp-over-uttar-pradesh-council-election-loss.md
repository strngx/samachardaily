The Samajwadi Party chief said the BJP's loss in the Uttar Pradesh Legislative Council elections would set the stage for his party's assembly election campaign. He credited teachers and young voters and alleged that the BJP manipulated the poll.

Yadav also stressed unity among marginalised communities and thanked supporters for their vigilance during the counting process. The Samajwadi Party leader made the remarks after the recently declared council poll results.

The BJP faced criticism from the Samajwadi Party after its defeat. Yadav's comments focused on the election outcome and the factors he said led to it.
