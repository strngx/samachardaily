The Tamil Nadu Vanniyar Katchi (TVK) revealed its slate of candidates for the two critical Assembly bypolls in Madurantakam and Dharapuram, both scheduled to be held in 2026.

The Election Commission has set the polling date for Madurantakam on March 12, 2026, while Dharapuram will vote on March 19, 2026, with results expected within 48 hours of each count.

TVK's nominee for Madurantakam is former municipal councilor R. Selvaraj, a veteran of local governance who previously contested the seat in the 2021 general election, while the party has fielded former district officer S. Murugan for Dharapuram, marking his first foray into electoral politics.

The All India Anna Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam (AIADMK) has announced a coordinated campaign strategy, deploying senior leader O. Panneerselvam to both constituencies and emphasizing welfare schemes such as free rice distribution and rural electrification to sway undecided voters.

Political analysts note that the bypolls could serve as a barometer for the ruling DMK's popularity ahead of the next state assembly election, with TVK positioning itself as a kingmaker by leveraging its caste-based support base.

Historically, bypolls in Tamil Nadu have often foreshadowed broader shifts in state politics; the 2015 bypolls in the same districts saw a swing that presaged the DMK's resurgence in the 2016 general election.

The upcoming contests also arrive amid a national trend of regional parties asserting greater influence in coalition dynamics, a factor that could reshape alliances at the centre if TVK secures victories in these seats.
