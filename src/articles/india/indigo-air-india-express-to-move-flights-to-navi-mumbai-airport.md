IndiGo has announced that it will begin international operations from Navi Mumbai airport in February 2027. The carrier plans to gradually shift twelve daily international departures from Mumbai airport to the new facility.

Air India Express will start moving its domestic operations to Navi Mumbai from December 2026. After completing the domestic transfer, the airline will also relocate selected international services.

The reallocation of flights is occurring ahead of the redevelopment of Mumbai's Terminal 1.
