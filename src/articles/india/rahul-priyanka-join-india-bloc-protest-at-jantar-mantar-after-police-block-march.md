Rahul Gandhi and Priyanka Gandhi joined the INDIA bloc’s protest after Delhi Police blocked their march toward Jantar Mantar, where the coalition had gathered to oppose Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar.

The demonstration alleges bias in the election commission’s oversight and calls for Kumar’s accountability, marking an escalation in the INDIA bloc’s ongoing dispute with the election body.

Clashes broke out as officers detained several protesters, and the confrontation with Delhi Police has intensified, according to the Breaking Ground episode hosted by Swati Joshi.
