The Delhi High Court on Wednesday (5 November 2026) issued a notice on a Public Interest Litigation seeking a time‑bound safety review of the acidity drug Ranitidine. The PIL, filed in New Delhi, calls for judicial oversight of the drug’s safety assessment, citing concerns over its long‑term health impacts.

The court’s notice marks the first judicial response to the petition, which demands that the drug’s regulator complete a comprehensive safety evaluation within a specified period. Stakeholders are awaiting the court’s next directions to determine the timeline for the review and any interim measures.
