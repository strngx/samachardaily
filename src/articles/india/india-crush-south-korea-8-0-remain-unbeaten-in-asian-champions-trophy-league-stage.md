India erupted for an 8‑0 demolition of South Korea in Jalandhar on Monday, sealing a massive win in the Asian Champions Trophy league phase and showcasing their offensive dominance.

The result maintained India's unbeaten record across the league stage, securing top‑group placement and momentum heading into the knockout rounds, while also highlighting their depth in attack.

South Korea suffered its heaviest defeat in the tournament, a 8‑goal margin that underscored India's attacking depth and set a strong benchmark for the rest of the competition, leaving the visitors to reflect on a challenging day in Jalandhar.
