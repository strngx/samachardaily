Delhi is currently grappling with significant air pollution, as the air quality index surged to 379 at 10 am on Wednesday, placing the city in the very poor category. The reading was recorded at 10 am. The high index reflects worsening conditions.

The Central Pollution Control Board classifies this reading as very poor, indicating hazardous air quality that may affect residents' health and daily activities. The surge reflects the ongoing pollution crisis.

The spike comes just a week before Diwali, a major festival, raising concerns about health risks during the celebrations. The situation remains critical.
