Sonia Gandhi has recalled the emotional turmoil she faced in 2004 after the Congress-led UPA's electoral victory when asked to accept the prime ministerial post.

She said both Rahul and Priyanka opposed the idea, citing the trauma of their grandmother's assassination and father's murder, and that she gave a noncommittal answer publicly. During alliance meetings she responded Theek hai which leaders immediately interpreted as acceptance prompting congratulations before she could complete her thought.

After weighing consequences she nominated Dr Manmohan Singh as Congress Parliamentary Party leader and the room erupted in applause with Rahul and Priyanka standing and applauding.
