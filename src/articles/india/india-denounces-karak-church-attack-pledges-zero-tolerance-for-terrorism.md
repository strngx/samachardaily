India has condemned a deadly assault on a Greek Orthodox church in Jordan’s Karak region, which left seven dead and 34 injured during a funeral service. The Ministry of External Affairs and Minister S. Jaishankar offered condolences and reiterated New Delhi’s zero‑tolerance policy toward terrorism.

The attack also drew condemnation from Jordanian King Abdullah II, Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney and United Nations Secretary‑General António Guterres, who all denounced the violence. The coordinated international response underscores the global consensus against such atrocities.

India’s statement reinforces its long‑standing diplomatic stance against terrorism, signaling continued cooperation with allies in security matters. The incident highlights the vulnerability of religious sites and prompts renewed calls for enhanced protection worldwide.
