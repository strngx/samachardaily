Police in Delhi reported that a man was stabbed to death after his own cousin tipped off the authorities about a planned robbery. The incident occurred in the capital, prompting a swift investigation.

Investigators have detained the cousin, two other accused adults and a juvenile in connection with the robbery‑murder. Three additional suspects continue to evade capture.

The arrests highlight police efforts to curb violent crime, though officials did not disclose further details about the victim or motive.
