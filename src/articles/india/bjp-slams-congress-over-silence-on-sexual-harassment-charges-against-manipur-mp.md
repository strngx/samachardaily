Odisha BJP Mahila Morcha President Dr. Aishwarya Biswal launched a sharp attack on the Congress leadership, questioning why Rahul Gandhi and senior party figures remained silent over sexual harassment allegations against Manipur MP and Jawaharlal Nehru University professor Angomcha Bimol Akoijam.

Speaking at a press conference at the state BJP headquarters in Bhubaneswar, Biswal urged the Congress to take a clear stand on the charges, accusing the party of double standards in addressing misconduct allegations.

The BJP's criticism comes as political tensions intensify, with the party leveraging the controversy to press the opposition on its stance regarding accountability and women's issues.
