A Level‑I fire broke out at a timber warehouse on Anand Road in Mumbai’s Malad West on Monday night, prompting an immediate response from fire brigade personnel and civic authorities. The blaze was contained quickly and no injuries were reported, though the incident required the mobilisation of emergency services.

The episode highlights the need for robust fire safety measures in commercial timber storage facilities across the city. Authorities are reviewing existing protocols to prevent similar incidents, while local businesses are being reminded of essential safety practices. An investigation into the cause is underway to determine if any safety lapses contributed.
