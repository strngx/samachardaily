Passengers across India are still struggling to travel as the fallout from a nationwide halt to air travel continues to disrupt flights. Many remain stranded, with normal services yet to resume.

The ground stop was enforced on Friday evening, and its effects have rippled through the weekend. Delays spread across the aviation network as the disruption persisted.

As of reports filed from New Delhi on November 15, thousands of passengers remained affected by the delays, which continued to disrupt air travel nationwide.
