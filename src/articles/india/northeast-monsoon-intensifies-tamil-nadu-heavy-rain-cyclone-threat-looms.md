The northeast monsoon has accelerated across Tamil Nadu, delivering a 4% surplus of rainfall since October. The Regional Meteorological Centre forecasts heavy rain with thunderstorms and strong winds in Ramanathapuram and Thoothukudi on Tuesday, while moderate showers are expected across a broad swath of districts including Thanjavur, Tiruvarur, Nagapattinam, Madurai and Kanniyakumari.

A low‑pressure area is poised to develop over the southeastern Bay of Bengal around November 12, potentially intensifying into a cyclonic storm that could approach Sri Lanka by the 15th. This system may bring another spell of substantial rain between November 15‑20, with a possible landfall along Tamil Nadu’s coastline near November 19, though exact intensity remains uncertain.
