The Enforcement Directorate has issued a summons to nominated Rajya Sabha MP Satnam Singh Sandhu, directing him to appear at its Jalandhar office for questioning in a funds‑transfer case.

Sandhu, who also serves as Chancellor of Chandigarh University, is alleged by ED sources to have received money from companies linked to the Vuenow Group.

The Vuenow Group is currently under investigation for reportedly operating a Ponzi scheme, prompting the agency to probe the flow of its funds to the parliamentarian.
