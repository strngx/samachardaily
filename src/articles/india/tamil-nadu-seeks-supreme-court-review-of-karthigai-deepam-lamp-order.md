Tamil Nadu has approached the Supreme Court with a petition seeking review of its recent order that permits the traditional Karthigai Deepam lamp to be illuminated, an arrangement issued earlier this month for a special cultural observance.

The lighting is scheduled for November 24 and would occur at Deepathoon, a stone pillar atop Thiruparankundram hill in Madurai, under a one-time permission granted by the court. Authorities say the temporary measure aims to accommodate pilgrim traffic without permanent alterations.

The petition underscores concerns about potential environmental impact while preserving the ritual's significance for devotees. The filing shows the state's intent to reassess the temporary lamp‑lighting permission, highlighting a contestation of the court's order within the legal process.
