Three individuals were taken into custody after a youth died when a white car dragged him about 100 metres in Bengaluru.

Police say the car approached the group at high speed; when a man identified as Mukhia signaled the driver to slow, the driver instead made a U‑turn and allegedly struck him, continuing the drag that caused the victim’s death.

The three suspects remain in police custody pending further investigation.
