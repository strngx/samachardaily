The Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation has ordered the demolition of the Musafirkhana building, a property associated with fugitive Dawood Ibrahim. The property is located in Mumbai.

Congress MLA Amin Patel has argued that existing court orders impose a stay on the demolition, warning that proceeding could trigger unrest. He pointed to the court orders as a barrier to the demolition.

As the demolition date approaches, tensions are rising within the local Muslim community, which is determined to protect the site, highlighting the complex issues of property ownership and legal authority in Mumbai. The community's opposition is growing as the date nears.
