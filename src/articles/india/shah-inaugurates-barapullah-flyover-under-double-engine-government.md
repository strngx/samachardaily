The Chief Minister inaugurated the Barapullah flyover yesterday, officially opening this major infrastructure project to the public. CM Rekha has emphasized that the success stems from a 'double-engine government'—a strategic partnership between the Centre and the Delhi state government—to overcome bureaucratic delays.

Under this joint governance model, the Centre and Delhi authorities are working together to unblock stalled projects. The combined leadership approach is intended to fast-track the completion of critical transportation works that had been pending for some time.
