Union Home Minister Amit Shah led the Ekta Run in New Delhi on October 31, marking Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel's 151st birth anniversary and National Unity Day. He called Patel's life an inspiration of sacrifice and patriotism for every youth and credited him with forging an undivided India in 1,218 days.

Shah appealed for a completely drug-free India by December 31, 2029, describing a Nasha Mukt Bharat as the first step toward a Viksit Bharat. He cited the abrogation of Article 370 and strides against Naxalism as landmark achievements under Prime Minister Narendra Modi. Earlier, President Droupadi Murmu, Vice-President C.P. Radhakrishnan, Delhi Lieutenant Governor Taranjit Singh Sandhu and Chief Minister Rekha Gupta paid floral tributes to Patel.
