The INDIA bloc scheduled an anti‑CEC rally at Delhi’s Ramlila Maidan, targeting a 10 lakh (one‑million) turnout. Police have mobilised about 4,000 officers and installed roughly 200 CCTV units to monitor the gathering.

Opposition leader Rahul Gandhi urged participants to patronise small traders during the event, framing the rally as part of a broader push against the Election Commission’s voter‑roll revision exercise.

Delhi police confirmed the rally is permitted but said no permission has been granted for a separate CP march.
