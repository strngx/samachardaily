The National Company Law Appellate Tribunal (NCLAT) Chennai Bench has taken up a petition filed by MD Varadarajan, the suspended director of Kniss Laboratories Pvt. Ltd., against Pharmarule and another respondent.

Varadarajan alleges that the actions of Pharmarule contributed to his suspension and is seeking judicial relief, while the respondents contest the claims before the appellate court.

The dispute highlights ongoing corporate litigation involving the pharmaceutical firm, with the tribunal’s ruling poised to influence the company’s leadership and operational decisions.
