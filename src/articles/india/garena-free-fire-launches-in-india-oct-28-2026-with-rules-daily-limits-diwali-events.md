Garena Free Fire launches in India on October 28, 2026, bringing new player rules that include daily playtime caps, mandatory parental consent for minors, and spending limits on in‑game purchases, with the game available on the official website and mobile apps. The launch aligns with the Diwali festival.

The update also adds India‑based servers for lower latency, introduces Diwali‑themed events, and provides a schedule for players to check launch times, with in‑app alerts notifying daily gaming limits.

These measures aim to curb excessive gaming, comply with Indian regulations, and enhance user experience during the Diwali festive period.
