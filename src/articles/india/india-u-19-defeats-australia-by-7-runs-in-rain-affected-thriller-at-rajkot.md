The Indian Under-19 cricket team held off Australia Under-19 to secure a narrow triumph in a weather-affected match hosted in Rajkot. Among the players taking the field were the sons of former Indian international stalwarts Rahul Dravid and Virender Sehwag, drawing significant attention to the junior international clash.

All-rounder Yashbardhan Chauhan emerged as the star performer of the encounter, delivering a decisive display that pushed the Indian squad past their opponents by seven runs in the tightly contested match.
