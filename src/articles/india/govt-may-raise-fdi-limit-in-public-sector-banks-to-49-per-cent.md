A government proposal to raise the foreign direct investment limit in public sector banks from 20 per cent to 49 per cent was reported from New Delhi on October 21 by IANS.

The change would increase the permitted foreign ownership ceiling in public sector banks. The existing cap on foreign investment in these banks stands at 20 per cent.

Because the plan remains under consideration, no formal decision or implementation date has been announced. The report was issued by IANS from New Delhi.
