BJP staged a protest march in West Delhi’s Hari Nagar, urging Congress to apologize for the 1984 anti‑Sikh violence. Union minister Hardeep Singh Puri labeled the killings a well‑planned genocide rather than a riot, while party leaders highlighted the tragedy’s ongoing impact. The march aimed to pressure the party into a formal apology and compensation for victims.

Several prominent leaders participated, criticizing Congress for its perceived failure to acknowledge the tragedy. Organizers said the timing was deliberate to draw attention to both historical injustice and current governance concerns, as the opposition’s INDIA bloc rally focused on alleged electoral irregularities, underscoring heightened political friction on the same day.
