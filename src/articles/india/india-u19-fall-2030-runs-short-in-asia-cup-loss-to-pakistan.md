India’s Under‑19 cricket side lost to Pakistan in the Men’s Asia Cup match played in Muscat on Nov 8. The game was part of the tournament’s group stage.

Captain Lakshya Raichandani said the team fell short of a par total by 20‑30 runs, admitting they did not put enough runs on the board after Pakistan’s Hamza performed well.

The result puts pressure on the squad ahead of its next group encounter.
