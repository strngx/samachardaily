Union Coal and Mines Minister G Kishan Reddy told graduates at the 17th convocation of the National Institute of Technology Raipur on Tuesday that India's greatest opportunities lie in solving key challenges in energy, mining and healthcare.

He argued that indigenous innovations are essential for the country's energy security and could also serve global needs, urging the nation to prioritize homegrown technology across these sectors.

Reddy emphasized that fostering domestic research and production would not only secure India's energy supply but also position the country as a technology exporter, reinforcing its role on the world stage.
