Two Class 10 classmates returning from coaching classes were cornered, subjected to moral policing and assaulted by a group of men along a roadside in Jamui district, Bihar, at around 7 PM in September 2026. Police identified the victims using viral social‑media footage and vehicle registration numbers, then launched suo motu action.

The incident spurred the formation of a Special Investigation Team that included female officers to interview the traumatized minors, highlighting a shift toward gender‑sensitive handling. The case also underscored the broader vulnerability of thousands of students who commute daily between homes, schools and coaching centres across India.

Experts cited in the source recommend three structural measures: deploying proactive, gender‑sensitive patrols; establishing dedicated student helplines and rapid digital response systems; and creating “Safe Transit Corridors” equipped with continuous street lighting, CCTV surveillance, verified transport operators and visible police presence.
