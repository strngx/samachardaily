Uttar Pradesh's 2027 census opened with nearly 70,000 people filing online self-reports on the first day. The development covered the state's digital census effort. The figure marked the opening phase.

The self-enumeration window in the state ran from November 16 to November 30. The following house-to-house enumeration phase was scheduled from December 1 until January 4, 2027.

The online submissions preceded the planned in-person household visits. The first day's total was reported by the end of the initial digital stage, covering Uttar Pradesh's self-enumeration effort. The physical survey had not yet begun.
