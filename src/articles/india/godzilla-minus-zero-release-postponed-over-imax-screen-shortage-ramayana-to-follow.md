The release of 'Godzilla Minus Zero' has been postponed due to scheduling conflicts and a shortage of IMAX screens, curbing the anticipated box office showdown with 'Ramayana'.

The upcoming 'Ramayana: Rise of a Legend' is also set for an IMAX launch, and distributors of 'Godzilla Minus Zero' are reviewing alternative distribution options, though no official announcement has been made.

The IMAX screen crunch highlights the difficulty of fitting multiple high‑budget releases into limited premium screens, affecting both films' rollout. Industry observers note that the delay may push the film's release to a later date, pending further scheduling adjustments.
