According to The Print, the Indian Air Force is likely to receive its first TEJAS MK-1A in early 2027, potentially as soon as January, just ahead of the Aero India 2027 exhibition in Bangalore, marking a key step for the force.

The induction of the 4.5-generation TEJAS MK-1A is critical for the IAF, which currently operates at its lowest fighter squadron strength in 60 years, fielding 29 active squadrons against a sanctioned strength of 42.

The TEJAS MK-1A, a 4.5-generation fighter, is critical for the IAF's modernisation efforts given the force's current squadron shortfall.
