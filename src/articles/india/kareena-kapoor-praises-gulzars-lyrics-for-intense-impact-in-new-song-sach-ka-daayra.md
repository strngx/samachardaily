Actress Kareena Kapoor spoke to the press in Mumbai on September 10, describing the newly released track "Sach Ka Daayra" from Meghna Gulzar's upcoming film Daayra as a song whose lyrical intensity "stays with you."

Kapoor highlighted that the words, penned by veteran lyricist Gulzar, give the composition a depth that resonates beyond the immediate listening experience, emphasizing the emotional weight of the piece.

The song forms part of the soundtrack for Daayra, a film that has generated anticipation in the Indian entertainment industry, though specific plot details remain undisclosed in the available statements.

Kapoor's comments were reported by MENAFN through its IANS feed, noting her appreciation for the collaboration between Gulzar and Meghna Gulzar, the director of the film.

While the track has just been released, early reactions suggest that its lyrical content may contribute significantly to the film's overall narrative tone, according to Kapoor's observations.

The actress did not provide further details about the film's release schedule or additional promotional activities during the interview.
