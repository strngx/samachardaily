Maruti Suzuki has launched the next-generation Baleno facelift in India, making the updated model available to customers nationwide. The company announced that the new Baleno will be sold at a starting price of Rs 6.10 lakh, ex-showroom. This price is for the base variant and represents the entry point for the facelifted hatchback. The launch was first reported by KalingaTV, which provided the initial details of the update. The introduction of the facelift marks a significant step in the model's lifecycle.

The facelift includes a revised style that updates the exterior of the Baleno. This revised style is one of the key elements of the next-generation update. In addition, the model receives an updated feature list that adds new features to the hatchback. The updated feature list is designed to improve the overall ownership experience.

The main change the Baleno received is the new petrol engine. This engine is a 1.2-litre Z Series three-cylinder petrol engine. The engine is part of the Z Series and has a displacement of 1.2 litres. It is a three-cylinder configuration.

Maruti Suzuki has made the updated Baleno available for purchase immediately after the launch. The starting price of Rs 6.10 lakh, ex-showroom, is the entry price for the new model.
