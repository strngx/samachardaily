Delhi recorded an overall Air Quality Index of 451 at 8 am on Tuesday, placing the capital in the hazardous category just days before Diwali, according to ANI. Multiple monitoring stations across the city showed severe or very poor readings, with Rohini Sector 30 among the worst-affected areas.

AQI.in logged a higher reading of 484 at 8:41 am, with PM2.5 at 359 micrograms per cubic metre and PM10 at 482. Low wind speeds below 6 kmph and a persistent smoky haze limited pollutant dispersion, pushing New Delhi to 18th globally for pollution that morning. Police have intensified vehicle checks in response.
