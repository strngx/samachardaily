Punjab Chief Minister Bhagwant Mann has launched the second phase of the 2027 Census in Sangrur. The effort starts with an online self-enumeration drive from November 16 to 30, 2026, before shifting to door-to-door visits beginning December 1, 2026 and concluding on January 4, 2027.

The rollout covers Sangrur district first and plans to extend to nearby areas as officials coordinate. High digital uptake is expected during the online window, while field agents will conduct door-to-door interviews throughout the subsequent months.

Residents can register through the new online portal, and local offices will assist with paperwork during the field phase, aiming for full census coverage by early 2027.
