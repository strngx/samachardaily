The Supreme Court on Wednesday reserved its verdict in the Beant Singh assassination case, focusing on Balwant Singh Rajoana’s petition to commute his death sentence to life imprisonment.

A bench led by Justice Vikram Nath observed that the President has not decided his mercy petition since 2012, creating an inordinate delay that Rajoana argues should warrant a reduction from death to life imprisonment.

The court’s decision, expected later, highlights the procedural importance of timely presidential clemency reviews in capital cases and follows years of legal scrutiny over the 1991 assassination.
