Priyanka Chopra and Katrina Kaif were among the celebrities who posted Diwali greetings on social media. Amitabh Bachchan, Anushka Sharma, Ram Charan and several other stars also extended wishes.

The posts included family pictures and heartfelt messages, moving between festive family moments and messages for fans. The greetings formed around the festival of lights.

Bollywood and South Indian stars marked the festival through the social media posts, sharing wishes with audiences.
