Union Defence Minister Rajnath Singh and Uttar Pradesh Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath on Sunday jointly laid the foundation stone for twelve major development projects in Lucknow. The cumulative financial outlay for the newly unveiled infrastructure initiatives stands at approximately ₹101 crore, marking a significant civic investment aimed at bolstering public works and municipal facilities across the state capital.

The high-profile ceremony highlighted direct administrative coordination between Union and state leadership. Defence Minister Rajnath Singh, who represents the Lucknow parliamentary constituency, joined Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath to formally inaugurate the development push. The joint appearance signals a concerted effort by both leaders to accelerate urban infrastructure upgrades and public amenities in Lucknow.

Under the newly announced program, twelve distinct civic and municipal projects will be undertaken across key zones in the city. Valued collectively at ₹101 crore, the package is structured to address pressing infrastructure needs, enhance local public services, and streamline urban connectivity. The formal foundation laying marks the official shift of these projects into active development.

The official rollout on Sunday reflects broader administrative priorities by the Uttar Pradesh government to modernize regional urban hubs. By launching a consolidated tranche of twelve projects simultaneously, the state administration and local municipal authorities aim to optimize oversight, streamline resource allocation, and ensure time-bound progress across all work sites.

The ceremonial foundation laying concluded with official presentations detailing the administrative framework governing the ₹101 crore outlay. Local urban bodies and public works departments in Lucknow will oversee project implementation to ensure construction proceeds according to designated civic timelines.
