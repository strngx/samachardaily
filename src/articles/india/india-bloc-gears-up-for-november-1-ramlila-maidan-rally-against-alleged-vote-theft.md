Preparations are under way at Delhi’s Ramlila Maidan for the INDIA bloc’s rally scheduled on November 1, aimed at protesting alleged vote theft involving the State Election Commission and Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar.

Congress figures Priyanka Gandhi Vadra and KC Venugopal inspected the site, reviewing logistics and security arrangements. Deputy Commissioner of Police (Central) Rohit Rajbir also toured the ground, taking stock of the setup.

The rally is expected to draw large crowds as the bloc seeks to highlight concerns over the electoral process and to mobilize supporters across the capital.
