Gujarat inaugurates its 25th Sanskrutik Van in Porbandar on October 1, a 14‑hectare, Rs 20‑crore forest near Odadar village. Chief Minister Bhupendra Patel will lead the ceremony, expanding a 2004 initiative from Modi’s tenure. The forest blends Gandhi’s Sarvodaya philosophy with environmental conservation, local culture, recreation and public awareness.

Zones such as Mahatma Van, Satya Van, Ahinsa Van, Shanti Van, Sarvodaya Van, Gram Swaraj Eco‑Village and Ayush Van host thousands of saplings, including 1.25 lakh saplings across Sarvodaya Van. Artisans from Chingriya will showcase handwoven shawls, a cafeteria serves food, and a natural play area welcomes tourists, students and Divyang Jan. The state allocated 89 vehicles worth Rs 11.20 crore to the Forest Department, which Patel will flag off during the programme.
