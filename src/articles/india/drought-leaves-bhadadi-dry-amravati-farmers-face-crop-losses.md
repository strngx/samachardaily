Drought conditions in Wardha have left the Bhadadi riverbed dry, and the water shortage has affected agriculture, causing significant losses to farmers. The dry riverbed has added to the water shortage in the district and hit farming.

Farmers in Amravati are facing financial difficulties as drought conditions damage crops. The crop damage has created financial strain for farmers in the region.

After the soybean crop, the cotton crop has also dried up, causing major losses to farmers. The drought has affected crop production and led to major losses. The water shortage has hit farmers hard.
