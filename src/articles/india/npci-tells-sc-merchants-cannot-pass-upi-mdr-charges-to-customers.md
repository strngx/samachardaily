The Supreme Court on September 28 sought responses from the Centre and other parties, including the National Payments Corporation of India, on a petition challenging MDR on specified UPI person-to-merchant transactions above Rs 2,000.

In its reply, NPCI has informed the court that merchants cannot pass on Merchant Discount Rate charges to customers under the revised UPI pricing framework.

The submission clarifies that the cost burden cannot be shifted to end users for these transactions.
