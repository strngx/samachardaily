On Tuesday, the Election Commission said the Model Code of Conduct (MCC) rules that bind serving government employees will also be enforced on retired officials and other persons engaged by state and Union territory administrations.

The commission communicated the extension through a letter addressed to the chief secretaries and chief electoral officers of Goa, Manipur, Punjab and Uttar Pradesh, signalling that the same election‑neutrality standards now cover these additional categories.
