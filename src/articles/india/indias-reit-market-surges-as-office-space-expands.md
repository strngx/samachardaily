India's Real Estate Investment Trusts (REITs) are capturing investor interest as the market experiences significant expansion. Investors can now acquire shares in publicly traded REITs or boost their portfolios through mutual funds and exchange‑traded funds, providing a straightforward path to diversification without direct property ownership.

The growth trajectory is being driven by a rise in available office space and robust demand from multinational corporations seeking footholds in the region. This increasing supply of commercial real estate, coupled with strong corporate interest, is creating a fertile environment for REIT performance.

As a result, the REIT landscape is emerging as a compelling investment option for both individual and institutional participants. The combination of diversified product structures, expanding office assets, and sustained corporate demand positions REITs as a key vehicle for capital allocation in India's evolving real estate sector.
