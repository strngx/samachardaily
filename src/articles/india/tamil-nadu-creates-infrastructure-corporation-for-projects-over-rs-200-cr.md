The Tamil Nadu government announced on November 10 in Chennai the establishment of a new public sector corporation. The corporation was created by the state government and will oversee large‑scale infrastructure projects.

The corporation will manage projects valued above Rs 200 crore. This is part of a major initiative aimed at accelerating infrastructure development across the state.

The corporation will operate as a state‑owned entity under the state government. It will function as a dedicated body for the execution of these projects. The corporation will be accountable to the state government.
