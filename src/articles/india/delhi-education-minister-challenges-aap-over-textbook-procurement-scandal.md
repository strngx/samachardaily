Delhi Education Minister Sood has publicly challenged the Aam Aadmi Party (AAP) over allegations of irregular procurement in Delhi's textbook supply chain. The minister is using price comparisons and quality complaints as evidence to counter the opposition's claims of a potential scam.

The dispute centers on procurement practices that the AAP has repeatedly accused the government of mishandling. Sood's response focuses on verifying costs and ensuring textbook quality to restore public confidence in the state's education supply system.
