On 25 September 2026, New Delhi hosted a cultural event dubbed “bhajan clubbing”. The format turned traditional Hindu devotional songs into a concert‑like experience, with young participants—predominantly members of Generation Z—singing, clapping and dancing to fast‑paced modern music.

Organisers staged the gathering to commemorate the 110th birth anniversary of Sangh, blending reverence with contemporary entertainment. The event illustrates a growing trend of youth reinterpreting religious expression through popular music styles and large‑scale, club‑type settings.
