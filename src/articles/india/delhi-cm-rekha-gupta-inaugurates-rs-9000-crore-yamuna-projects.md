Delhi Chief Minister Rekha Gupta on Thursday called the day joyful for the city's residents while addressing the media after inaugurating a series of projects.

The projects are valued at approximately Rs 9,000 crore. They aim to accelerate the rejuvenation of the Yamuna river. Additionally, the work will improve water infrastructure across the capital.

Gupta emphasized that her government has made consistent progress in river restoration over the past one and a half years, reinforcing its commitment to environmental improvement. She noted that the administration has moved forward strongly with the river rejuvenation work since taking office.
