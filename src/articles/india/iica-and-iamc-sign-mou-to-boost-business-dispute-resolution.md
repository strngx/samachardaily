IICA and Hyderabad’s IAMC signed an MoU to boost alternative dispute resolution for businesses. The agreement, signed by IICA Director‑General Gyaneshwar Kumar Singh and IAMC CEO A.J. Jawad, was executed by Col. Amandeep Singh Puri and Jawad. The partnership will create a specialized pool of arbitrators, mediators and dispute‑resolution professionals through short‑ and long‑term training programmes.

Both institutions will emphasize professional excellence, best practices and innovation in arbitration, mediation and institutional development, with a focus on the MSME sector’s contribution to growth, GDP and exports. They also aim to support Haryana’s trillion‑dollar economy vision by 2047 and to develop joint initiatives and policy‑oriented research. The MoU was organized by IICA’s Centre of Excellence in ADR under Dr. Naveen Sirohi.
