External Affairs Minister S Jaishankar met Canadian Foreign Minister Anita Anand in New Delhi to review the state of bilateral cooperation between India and Canada.

The two leaders discussed collaboration across key sectors including trade, investment, education, energy and defence, according to a statement from the Ministry of External Affairs.

Regional and global developments were also on the agenda during the bilateral meeting, which took place during Anand's visit to the Indian capital.
