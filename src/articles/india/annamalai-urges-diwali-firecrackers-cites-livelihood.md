President K. Annamalai has urged people across Tamil Nadu to celebrate Diwali by purchasing and bursting firecrackers in large numbers, emphasizing the cultural significance of the festival and its traditions.

He cited the livelihood of eight lakh workers in the firecracker sector as a critical factor driving his call for widespread participation in the festivities, highlighting the economic importance of the industry.

The appeal comes as Diwali approaches, with the President seeking to ensure that the celebration is both culturally rich and economically supportive of the workers who produce the fireworks.
