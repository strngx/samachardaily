CJP leader Abhijeet Dipke said a nationwide protest will take place on October 10, asserting that the threat of arrest of CJP leaders before the Delhi rally will not stop the movement. He told reporters, “You cannot stop us,” and emphasized that the UAPA threat will not scare participants.

Authorities have warned that CJP leaders may be detained ahead of the October 10 Delhi protest, but Dipke insists the demonstration will proceed across the country. The group also raised the question of whether Amit Shah will be arrested, while pledging to mobilize citizens nationwide on the scheduled date.
