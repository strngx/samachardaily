The Congress party has declared its nominees for the upcoming Legislative Council elections across two Graduate constituencies in Telangana. Sama Ram Mohan Reddy has been selected to contest from the Mahbubnagar-Ranga Reddy-Hyderabad Graduates' constituency, while Ramreddy Sarvotham Reddy will represent the party in the Warangal-Khammam-Nalgonda Graduates' seat.

Both constituencies encompass several key districts in the state and contain a large electorate of eligible graduate voters. These political preparations come ahead of the end of the incumbent Legislative Council members' terms, which are set to expire in March 2027.

With candidate selections finalized, campaign outreach is expected to concentrate heavily on issues concerning educated youth, employment opportunities, and educational reforms across the region.
