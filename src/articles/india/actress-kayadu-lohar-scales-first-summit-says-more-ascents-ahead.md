The actress Kayadu Lohar said she had climbed her first mountain. She posted pictures from the top of a minor peak, describing it as the start of a longer journey in the mountains.

The actress said she had always been a mountain girl and would keep pursuing mountains. She made the announcement, adding that she would continue.

The report also noted her film career, including a pivotal role in director Mari Selvaraj's upcoming film 'Manjanathi', while the film's ongoing schedule wrapped last month. The film also features actor Kathir in the lead.
