Producers of the Hindi film "Hanuman Ansh" have issued a formal notice stating that no individual may claim independent authority over the movie's rights, amid a growing dispute.

The warning comes after the film posted reported box‑office earnings of ₹342 crore, with some outlets citing a ₹450 crore total, positioning it among India's top‑grossing releases. Director Vishal Chaturvedi is highlighted as the only debut director to breach the ₹300 crore threshold.

The rights clash also touches on publicity and distribution plans for the announced sequel, "Hanuman Ansh 2".
