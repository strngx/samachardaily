Leaders of the opposition INDIA bloc gathered in Bihar to call for public unity to defend democracy and protect the nation's Constitution. The coalition organized the demonstration to raise public awareness regarding democratic safeguards and electoral integrity.

During the rally, alliance members voiced sharp protests against alleged vote-tampering, framing such practices as an attack on constitutional principles. The leaders also formally urged structural reforms concerning the Chief Election Commissioner to ensure fair electoral processes.
