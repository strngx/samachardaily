India’s effort to produce an indigenous fighter aircraft engine has stretched close to four decades, yet every fighter jet in the Indian Air Force’s fleet still uses powerplants sourced from abroad.

Analyst Palak Gupta notes in a CNBCTV18 analysis that this reliance highlights one of the toughest shortfalls in the nation’s defence‑industrial ambitions, despite significant investments over the years.
