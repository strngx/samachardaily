The dispute at the centre of the FSSAI notice is whether Red Bull's beverage genuinely qualifies as an energy drink, with the product's sugar content raised as the key concern rather than any energy-boosting effect. The notice challenges the reasoning behind the energy-drink label.

The Food Safety and Standards Authority of India has asked the company to defend that claim, bringing the drink's marketing under regulatory review. The action examines how the beverage's advertised benefits are presented to consumers.
