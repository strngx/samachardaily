Mokama MLA Anant Singh’s daughter’s wedding was billed as one of Bihar’s grandest celebrations, with arrangements to feed four lakh people.

Footage, however, revealed plates flying, guests snatching food from the ground and meals served on cardboard, while sabzi was looted and lathis brandished to control the crowd. The chaotic scenes highlighted severe logistical failures despite the event’s advertised scale.

The stark visual contrast underscores the disparity between the advertised magnitude of the celebration and the on‑ground reality of resource management and crowd control. It also raises questions about event planning for large public gatherings.
