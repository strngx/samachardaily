Opposition leaders staged a 'Save democracy' march in New Delhi, demanding the resignation of Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar over alleged electoral malpractice, as protesters gathered outside the Election Commission office. Several opposition MPs participated in the march, calling for the removal of the top election official. The protest highlighted concerns over the integrity of the electoral process.

Rahul Gandhi and Priyanka Gandhi Vadra breached police barricades, with Rahul climbing atop the barriers, as several Opposition MPs were detained by authorities during the high-drama confrontation. The incident occurred at the Election Commission office in New Delhi.
