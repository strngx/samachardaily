Pune Police announced today that new no‑parking zones will be enforced in seven administrative divisions of the city. The move comes as part of a broader effort to streamline traffic flow and reduce illegal parking that has long plagued busy corridors. Officials emphasized that motorists should take note of the revised restrictions and parking arrangements while travelling through the affected areas, and they urged the public to consult official notices for precise details.

The city of Pune has experienced rapid urban growth over the past decade, leading to heightened congestion on its arterial roads and narrow lanes. Traffic bottlenecks, especially during peak hours, have often been exacerbated by vehicles parked in unauthorized spots, obstructing both private and public transport. By designating specific zones where parking is prohibited, the police aim to create clearer pathways for commuters, emergency services, and public transport vehicles, thereby improving overall mobility.

According to the police briefing, each of the seven divisions will feature clearly marked signage indicating the start and end points of the no‑parking zones. Enforcement teams will be deployed to monitor compliance, and violators are expected to face standard penalties as outlined in the municipal traffic regulations. While the exact fine amounts were not disclosed, officials reiterated that the enforcement will be consistent and that repeat offenders could face escalated consequences.

Motorists are advised to check the Pune Police website, local news bulletins, and on‑ground signage for the exact boundaries of the new zones before setting out. The police also suggested using navigation apps that have been updated with the latest parking restrictions to avoid inadvertent violations. By planning routes in advance, commuters can minimize delays and avoid potential fines, contributing to smoother traffic conditions across the city.

Police spokesperson Rajesh Kulkarni said, "These restrictions are a necessary step to address chronic parking violations that disrupt traffic and compromise safety. We request full cooperation from the public and will continue to monitor the impact of these zones to ensure they serve the intended purpose." The statement underscored the department's commitment to maintaining order on Pune's roads while balancing the needs of residents and businesses.
