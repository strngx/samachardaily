The Diva-Panvel section is set to see overnight power and traffic work from November 22 to November 29. The activity relates to the Panvel-Kalamboli Coaching Complex project.

Rail services between Mumbai, the Konkan region and south India may face delays, diversions and rescheduling due to the overnight work. The disruption is expected during the week-long period on the Diva-Panvel stretch.

The scheduled restrictions are connected to the coaching complex project. Trains operating across the affected route may see changes. The work is expected to affect trains using the route.
