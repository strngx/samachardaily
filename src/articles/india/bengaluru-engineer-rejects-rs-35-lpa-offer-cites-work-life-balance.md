Bengaluru engineering graduate Rupesh posted on X that he rejected a job offer from a Bengaluru firm promising a Rs 35 LPA salary, describing the package as highly attractive yet ultimately unappealing.

Rupesh explained his decision stemmed from a lower fixed-pay component, leading him to prioritize work‑life balance and financial stability over an impressive total compensation, concluding the opportunity did not align with his career priorities.

His stance reflects a broader trend where professionals weigh stability and personal time against high salary offers in the competitive tech job market, underscoring shifting priorities.
