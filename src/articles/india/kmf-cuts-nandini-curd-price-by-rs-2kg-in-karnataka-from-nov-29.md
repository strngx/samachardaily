KMF has reduced the price of Nandini curd by Rs 2 per kg in Karnataka, with the new rates taking effect from November 29. The revision follows an instruction from the state government.

Under the revised pricing, a 500g pack of Nandini curd will cost Rs 33, while the 1kg pack will be available at Rs 66.

The reduction means consumers across Karnataka will pay less for Nandini curd, with the 500g pack priced at exactly half the 1kg rate.
