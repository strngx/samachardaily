Prime Minister Narendra Modi approved the Northern Bullet Train Corridor, Ashwini Vaishnaw announced on Saturday. The route will start in Delhi, passing through Agra, Kanpur, Lucknow, Ayodhya, Varanasi, Prayagraj, Patna and Siliguri, extending to Guwahati, with travel times such as Delhi‑Agra in 59 minutes.

Vaishnaw said the fare will be economical and construction will be fully Make‑in‑India, built by Indian youth and welders from pillar to girder, benefiting youth, middle‑income groups and small‑scale artisans. The first Traction Substation for the Mumbai‑Ahmedabad bullet train is nearing completion at Bilimora in Gujarat’s Navsari district, with work on the 14 planned substations for the 508‑km corridor.
