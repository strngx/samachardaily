On Wednesday, the Supreme Court raised alarm about political executives influencing criminal prosecutions, noting that prosecutorial tactics often shift when a new government takes power.

The observation arose during hearings on the cash‑for‑jobs case involving former Tamil Nadu minister V Senthil Balaji, where the bench stressed the need for fairness and warned against any prejudice to the accused.

The court directed the trial court to adjourn the proceedings, aiming to ensure future actions are not colored by political considerations.
