Policing officials in Delhi said a family of six people from Bangladesh was traced. The group included two minors, and the individuals reportedly lacked valid documents. They had been staying in the national capital, according to reports. The police confirmed the tracing.

The police said the movement of the six was restricted after the tracing. The case was recorded in New Delhi on November 27. The North District unit was involved, and the development concerned the family's residence in the capital. The report tied the group to the family.
