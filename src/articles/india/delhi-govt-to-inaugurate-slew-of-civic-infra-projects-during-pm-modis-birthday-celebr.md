The Delhi government announced that a series of civic and infrastructure projects will be inaugurated on the prime minister’s birthday, framing the day as a "Sewa Pakhwada" – a week dedicated to public service. The timing aligns with the political calendar, allowing the administration to showcase delivery ahead of upcoming municipal elections.

Key projects include the completion of Barapullah Phase‑III, extensive water and sewerage network upgrades, the opening of new government schools, and the commissioning of solar‑powered street lighting in underserved colonies. Collectively, the initiatives are projected to benefit over two million residents and involve an estimated investment of several hundred crore rupees.

Opposition parties and civil‑society groups have called for transparent monitoring, warning that rushed inaugurations could mask implementation gaps. Urban planning experts note that while the projects address chronic deficits, sustained maintenance and community engagement will be critical to translate the launches into lasting service improvements.
