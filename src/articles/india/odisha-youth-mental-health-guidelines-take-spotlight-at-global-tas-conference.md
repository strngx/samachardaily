The Government of Odisha's Standard Operating Guidelines on Mental Health and Wellbeing of Children, Adolescents and Young Adults were presented at the 11th Global Conference on Together Against Stigma (TAS), held at AIIMS in New Delhi with support from UNICEF India.

The guidelines were launched by the Chief Minister of Odisha, and the conference appearance carried the state framework onto an international stage. The TAS conference is a global forum, and the presentation placed the Odisha document before that wider audience.

The guidelines specifically address the mental health and wellbeing of three age groups — children, adolescents and young adults — setting out the state's standard operating approach for each.
