All 11 people who went missing after their boats capsized in the flooded Bethupalli lake in Telangana have been rescued, officials confirmed on November 5. The group included personnel from the Telangana State Disaster Response Force, the Fire Services Department, and a local resident.

The vessels overturned in turbulent waters at the lake, prompting an immediate search and rescue operation. Emergency teams located and brought all 11 individuals to safety.
