Delhi's chief minister flagged off a Delhi-Bihar interstate bus service under a reciprocal transport agreement between the governments of Delhi and Bihar.

The agreement covers 18 routes and provides for regular bus services throughout the year. Nearly 20 lakh workers and labourers from Bihar and Purvanchal who live and work in Delhi are expected to benefit from the improved connectivity.

The launch marks the operational rollout of the reciprocal arrangement, which is aimed at meeting the year-round travel needs of the capital's migrant labour population from Bihar and Purvanchal through regular interstate bus services.
