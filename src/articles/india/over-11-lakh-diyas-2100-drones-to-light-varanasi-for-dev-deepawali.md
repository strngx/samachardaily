The Uttar Pradesh government is finalising extensive preparations in Varanasi for a festival event. The government is preparing to host an estimated 10 to 15 lakh people, officials said.

A large lighting plan has been prepared, with officials citing more than eleven lakh diyas and 2,100 drones for the event. The officials said the display will be used during the celebration.

The final arrangements were being finalised. The event is scheduled for Tuesday in Varanasi. Officials said the celebration will use the lights. The preparations remain underway. The government has made these plans.
