BARC India is facing mounting financial pressure, with its monthly operating expenditure touching nearly Rs 25 crore. The Broadcast Audience Research Council warns that rising costs threaten its ability to run programs and publish data, and jeopardizes its funding streams.

The board's dwindling revenue and soaring bills raise questions about the council's long-term viability. Analysts say the strain could force cuts to fieldwork or delay releases, undermining public confidence in media research.

The issue was first reported by The Sunday Guardian, highlighting the council's struggle amid a dry market for advertising.
