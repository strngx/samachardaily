Delhi's public transport fleet is set to cross the 7,000-mark with the induction of 100 electric buses on Friday. The addition will take the city's total bus strength to approximately 7,088, while the dedicated electric bus fleet will reach 5,338. Delhi is modernizing its public transit network with cleaner, energy-efficient vehicles that reduce carbon emissions.

Chief Minister Rekha Gupta will flag off the new buses from the Kashmere Gate Inter-State Bus Terminus (ISBT). The induction increases Delhi's cleaner public transit options as the city continues to expand its electric vehicle infrastructure.
