U.S. Ambassador to India Sergio Gor spent two days in Bengaluru meeting senior Indian officials, senior executives of local industry bodies and representatives of American companies. The talks, held at the city's International Trade Centre, were framed as a concerted effort to broaden the scope of US‑India commercial interaction and to identify concrete avenues where U.S. firms can expand their footprint in the Indian market.

During the sessions, Gor highlighted the growing demand for technology, renewable energy, healthcare and advanced manufacturing solutions in India, positioning American firms as ready partners to meet that demand. He underscored Bengaluru’s reputation as the nation’s technology hub, noting that the city’s ecosystem of startups, research institutions and multinational corporations offers a fertile ground for joint ventures, research collaborations and investment inflows.

Gor reminded participants that bilateral trade between the United States and India has risen steadily over recent years, creating a foundation for deeper engagement. He pointed to existing frameworks such as the U.S.-India Trade Policy Forum as mechanisms that can be leveraged to fast‑track sector‑specific agreements, reduce regulatory bottlenecks and protect intellectual property for innovative products entering the Indian market.

Indian officials responded positively, acknowledging the strategic importance of diversifying the country’s supplier base and attracting high‑value foreign investment. They expressed willingness to facilitate smoother entry for U.S. businesses, including streamlined customs procedures and clearer guidelines for joint research projects. Both sides agreed that a sustained dialogue, rather than isolated meetings, would be essential to translate interest into measurable trade growth.

The delegation concluded with a pledge to set up a joint working group that will meet quarterly to monitor progress, share market intelligence and resolve any operational challenges that arise. The group is expected to produce a roadmap outlining priority sectors, timelines for pilot projects and a schedule of follow‑up meetings in major Indian economic centres.
