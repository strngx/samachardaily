Latur police on Sunday registered a case against two men accused of desecrating a poster of 18th‑century ruler Tipu Sultan during a Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) rally. The alleged act was reported by a local lawyer who filed a complaint after the protest.

Authorities have charged the duo under relevant provisions of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, the newly enacted criminal code. The filing demonstrates law‑enforcement’s response to claims of disrespect toward historical symbols amid political gatherings.
