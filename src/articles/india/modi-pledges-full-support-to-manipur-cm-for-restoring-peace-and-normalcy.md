In the meeting, Prime Minister Narendra Modi pledged comprehensive assistance to Chief Minister Yumnam Khemchand Singh for restoring peace and normalcy across Manipur.

Singh outlined initiatives to stabilize the state, including resuming bus services and ensuring the flow of goods. He also requested key support for highway upgrades and sought updates on the AIIMS status for the Regional Institute of Medical Sciences, citing ongoing development needs for the region.

The Prime Minister acknowledged the improvements noted in the region and committed to supporting reasonable issues raised by the state government during the talks.
