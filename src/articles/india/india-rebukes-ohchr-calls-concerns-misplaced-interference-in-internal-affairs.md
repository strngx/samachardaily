India on Saturday rejected the Office of the United Nations High Commissioner for Human Rights (OHCHR) statement, calling its concerns misplaced and an unwarranted interference in the country's internal affairs. The response came from Geneva, where the Indian delegation dismissed the remarks as inappropriate and contrary to New Delhi's sovereign decision‑making process.

The statement underscores New Delhi's firm stance against external commentary on matters it considers sovereign. No further details were provided, but the rebuke signals India's continued resistance to international scrutiny of its domestic policies.

The rebuke reflects India's broader diplomatic posture of defending its internal affairs from perceived external meddling, a position it has consistently upheld in international forums.
