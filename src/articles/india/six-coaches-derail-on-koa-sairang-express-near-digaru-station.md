On the morning of the incident, six coaches of the Koa-Sairang Express derailed near Digaru Railway Station in Assam at 6:54 am, causing a significant disruption to the train service.

Separately, political figures in Maharashtra were honoring the late Ajit Pawar on Bhai Dooj. Supriya Sule shared a video from the previous year's celebration on social media, describing it as the first Diwali and Bhai Dooj without him. The Thackeray family, including Manas Thackeray, Jayjaywanti, Raj Thackeray, and Aaditya Thackeray, gathered at Jayjaywanti's residence to celebrate the occasion.
