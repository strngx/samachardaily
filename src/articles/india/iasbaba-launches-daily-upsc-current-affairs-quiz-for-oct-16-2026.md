IASbaba posted its Daily Current Affairs Quiz for 16 October 2026, aimed at UPSC Prelims candidates. The test draws questions from leading outlets such as The Hindu, Indian Express and PIB.

The quiz emphasizes both conceptual understanding and factual recall, offering a distinct preparation angle compared with other study material. Access is restricted to paid subscribers, with a link to the archive of earlier quizzes.

Aspiring civil servants can use the daily quiz to gauge readiness and stay updated on current events, a key component of the UPSC syllabus.
