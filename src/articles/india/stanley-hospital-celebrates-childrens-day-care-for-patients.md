Make-A-Wish India joined a government medical college hospital's early intervention unit for a holiday event. Young patients receiving care were the focus. The hospital described the occasion as a Children's Day celebration.

The outreach gave supportive items to over seventy children at the hospital during treatment. It mixed festive observance with support. The supplies were for children undergoing medical care.

The District Early Intervention Centre was the site of the Children's Day activity. The event included gifting and celebration. The medical college hospital's DEIC hosted the observance.
