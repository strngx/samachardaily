An unusual contradiction in Delhi's excise system is at the centre of a new liquor policy the city is preparing. The framework aims to discourage residents from leaving the Capital to buy alcohol.

Many consumers currently cross Delhi's borders to purchase liquor that is either cheaper or better stocked than what is available within the city. Each such purchase outside the Capital represents excise revenue the government does not collect on sales that could have taken place inside Delhi.

The details were shared with The Tribune by officials described as being aware of the matter, who said the policy was still being prepared.
