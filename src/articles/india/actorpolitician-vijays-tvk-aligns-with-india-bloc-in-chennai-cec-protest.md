Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam (TVK), a newly formed party headed by actor‑politician C Joseph Vijay, announced its participation in the opposition’s protest in Chennai targeting Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar and the Special Intensive Revision (SIR) of electoral rolls.

VCK leader Thol Thirumavalavan confirmed TVK’s entry into the demonstration, noting the party’s alignment with the state‑level INDIA bloc that is organising the rally.

The addition of Vijay’s high‑profile party widens the protest’s political spectrum, intensifying scrutiny of the CEC’s roll‑revision drive.
