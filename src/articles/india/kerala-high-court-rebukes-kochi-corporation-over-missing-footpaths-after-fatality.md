Kerala High Court has rebuked Kochi Corporation for alleged failure to restore footpaths following a pedestrian’s death linked to the Kakkanad bus tragedy. The court cited violations of pedestrian rights and urged the civic body to take strict corrective action.

The bench emphasized that missing footpaths endanger public safety and demanded immediate remedial measures. It directed the corporation to take corrective action and ensure compliance with safety standards across the city.

The judgment reflects the court’s resolve to hold municipal bodies accountable for infrastructure lapses that jeopardize pedestrian safety and to prevent future tragedies.
