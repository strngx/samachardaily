Stand‑up comedian and YouTube personality Samay Raina sparked a social‑media frenzy on Tuesday when his roast segment targeting actor Varun Dhawan aired on the comedy series ‘India’s Got Latent 2’. The episode, streamed live on NDTV’s digital platform, featured Raina delivering a rapid‑fire series of jokes that quickly amassed millions of views and dominated trending lists across Twitter and Instagram.

Raina’s jokes zeroed in on Dhawan’s recent releases, specifically the action‑drama ‘Border 2’ and the romantic‑action film ‘Dilwale’. He ridiculed the over‑the‑top battle sequences in ‘Border 2’, quipping that the film’s explosions looked more like a school science project than a war epic. The comedian also poked fun at ‘Dilwale’s’ melodramatic love story, suggesting that even the lead characters needed a GPS to find their own emotions.

Varun Dhawan, a leading figure in contemporary Bollywood, rose to fame with the 2012 hit ‘Student of the Year’ and has since headlined a string of commercial successes. His recent foray into action with ‘Border 2’ and a return to romance in ‘Dilwale’ marked a strategic shift from the youthful comedies that defined his early career. The roast, therefore, struck a chord by juxtaposing his evolving filmography with the comedian’s irreverent commentary.

The clip’s virality was amplified by a cascade of user‑generated content: fans recreated Raina’s punchlines, memes proliferated, and the hashtag #SamayRoastsVarun trended for several hours. Within 24 hours, the video logged over 12 million views on YouTube, while NDTV reported a 45 percent spike in traffic to its entertainment section. Critics noted the roast’s timing, arriving just weeks after the theatrical releases of the two films, thereby extending their publicity cycle.

Raina’s roast is part of a broader trend of digital comedians leveraging live‑stream platforms to critique mainstream cinema. Similar formats have previously featured roasts of other Bollywood stars, blurring the line between promotional publicity and satirical commentary. Industry observers suggest that such segments can influence audience perception, especially among younger viewers who consume entertainment primarily online.
