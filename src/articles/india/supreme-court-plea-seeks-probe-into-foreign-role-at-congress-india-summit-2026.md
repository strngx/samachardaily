A petition filed in the Supreme Court has alleged that foreign political representatives interfered in India's domestic politics during the Congress-organised India Summit 2026, held in Hyderabad.

The petitioner, Zahid Ali, has asked the court to order a probe into the event and to examine the alleged interference in the country's internal political affairs.

Beyond the investigation, the plea seeks restrictions on foreign delegates' political participation in India's internal affairs, along with a public apology from the Telangana Government.
