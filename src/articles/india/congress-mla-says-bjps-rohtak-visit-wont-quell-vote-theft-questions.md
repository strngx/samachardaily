Congress MLA Bharat Bhushan Batra said on Friday that the BJP could bring its national president, Chief Minister and entire Cabinet to Rohtak, but such a high‑profile visit would not silence questions over alleged vote theft and electoral transparency. He stressed that the presence of top BJP leaders would not erase the concerns being raised.

Batra urged the BJP to shift focus from demonstrations against the Opposition to addressing the specific allegations of vote theft. He argued that the party should answer the questions rather than staging protests, highlighting the need for electoral accountability.

The remarks come amid ongoing political tension in the region, where electoral integrity remains a contentious issue.
