Korea Aerospace Industries (KAI) is assessing an Indian Air Force requirement for roughly 150 advanced jet trainers, according to Korean media reports. The IAF issued a Request for Information in August as it seeks a successor to its ageing Hawk MK-132 fleet.

KAI confirmed it received the RFI and is studying the requirement. The South Korean manufacturer's T-50 Golden Eagle family is expected to compete for the contract, which would mark a significant defence procurement for the IAF.
