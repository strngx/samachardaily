Karnataka BJP leader R Ashoka said his party would oppose any move by the state's Congress government to change or scrap laws dealing with cattle slaughter and religious conversion. The warning was issued against possible amendments or repeal.

The Leader of the Opposition in the Karnataka Assembly made the remarks in Mangaluru on November 19. The BJP said it would resist any attempt to amend or repeal those state laws.

The warning focused on possible legislative action by the Congress government. It did not mention a specific bill or amendment.
