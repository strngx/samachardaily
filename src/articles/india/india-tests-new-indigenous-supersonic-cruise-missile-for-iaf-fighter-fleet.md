India is set to begin testing a new indigenous ramjet-powered supersonic cruise missile developed by the Defence Research and Development Laboratory (DRDL). This missile represents a significant advancement in India's long-range strike capabilities, distinct from both the BrahMos and ASTRA MK-3 systems, and marks a key step in the nation's defense modernization drive.

The upcoming testing is designed to evaluate the missile's performance and integration potential for the IAF's fighter jet fleet. As part of this effort, DRDL continues its work on indigenous aerospace technologies to reduce reliance on foreign systems and bolster strategic autonomy.
