A farmer was mauled to death by a tiger on Saturday morning around 7:30 am while gathering fodder for his cattle in the drought‑hit Lokhandwadi‑Sonepathar area of Beed district, Maharashtra.

The victim, identified as Devidas (Mahadev) Takle, a resident of Deolali village in Ashti tehsil, was killed at the scene after the tiger attacked while he was collecting fodder for his cattle in the early hours of Saturday.

The CSR Journal reported the tragedy, highlighting the danger wildlife presents in drought‑hit Maharashtra as farmers venture to forest edges for fodder amid severe water scarcity.
