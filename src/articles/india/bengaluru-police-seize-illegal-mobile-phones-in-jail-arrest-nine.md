Police in Bengaluru have arrested nine individuals following the discovery of three separate caches of unauthorized mobile phones inside a local correctional facility during a targeted security operation.

Under Indian prison regulations and facility security codes, the possession and use of mobile phones by inmates is strictly banned. Law enforcement authorities conduct periodic surprise inspections to prevent communication contraband, noting that illicit devices can be used to bypass security protocols or coordinate unlawful activities from within prison grounds.

The nine individuals apprehended in connection with the seizures face charges for contravening prison management statutes and related legal provisions governing correctional facilities. Bengaluru police officials stated that administrative oversight and search procedures will remain active to detect contraband smuggling networks and maintain security across detention centers.
