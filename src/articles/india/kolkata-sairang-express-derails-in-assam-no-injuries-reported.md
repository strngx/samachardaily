Train No 13125 was the service involved in the incident. Its locomotive and five coaches also derailed at Digaru Railway Station in Kamrup Metro district.

The Northeast Frontier Railway said no injuries or casualties were reported. The railway identified the train as the Kolkata-Sairang Express and said the derailment occurred at Digaru Railway Station in Kamrup Metro district, Assam, on Wednesday morning.

The incident occurred in Assam on Wednesday. The locomotive and five coaches of Train No 13125 were involved, according to the Northeast Frontier Railway, which provided the information.
