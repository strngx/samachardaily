Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles will raise prices by an average of 0.5 per cent from December 1, the earliest of the increases announced so far.

Audi India will lift prices by up to 2 per cent from January 1, taking the round of hikes into the luxury segment a month after Tata's revision takes effect.

Both announcements follow a price increase by Maruti Suzuki, with the three revisions spread across different segments and effective dates.
