Sukhu inaugurated a new 100-bed building at the Shahpur hospital, adding to the facility's inpatient capacity. He used the occasion to comment on the condition of the public health sector.

During his address, Sukhu said only 35% of the staff strength was available at healthcare institutions under the previous BJP government. He contrasted that with the efforts now under way.

The government, he said, is taking steps to raise the availability of doctors, nurses, paramedical and technical staff to 70%, addressing a shortfall he attributed to the earlier administration.
