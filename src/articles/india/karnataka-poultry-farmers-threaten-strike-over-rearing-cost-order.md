Karnataka’s poultry sector stayed central to a welfare debate as farmers sought formal recognition for their work. The state had been examining how the industry fits into its broader agricultural policy.

Protesters assembled at Freedom Park and gave notice of a possible statewide work stoppage starting November 25. Their action was tied to the government’s failure to enforce the rearing cost order, according to the protest report. The farmers’ status demand and the stalled cost-order enforcement now form the core of the Karnataka poultry issue.
