The literary portal Scroll.in released a preview of ‘The Perception Game: A Glitzy Adventure’, a collaborative novel by Shailendra Singh and Mahendra Jakhar. The excerpt transports readers to 1970s Bombay, a city then pulsing with neon lights, crowded bazaars, and a thriving underworld. In the narrative, a young protagonist, Sikankar, unexpectedly crosses paths with a notorious don while navigating the chaotic streets of the city. The encounter, described in vivid, cinematic language, becomes a turning point that propels Sikandar into a world of intrigue, ambition, and moral ambiguity. The authors blend historical details—such as the era's iconic taxi routes and the looming presence of organized crime—with a fictional storyline that explores how a single moment can reshape a person's trajectory.

The piece, positioned as a teaser for the forthcoming full-length novel, showcases the authors' intent to merge a gritty crime saga with a broader commentary on aspiration and identity in post‑independence India. By anchoring the plot in a recognizable period of Bombay’s history, Singh and Jakhar aim to evoke nostalgia while interrogating the city’s mythic underbelly. The excerpt’s release has generated discussion among literary circles about the resurgence of crime‑driven narratives set against Indian backdrops, a genre that has seen renewed interest in recent years.

Scroll.in’s publication of the excerpt also includes a brief author note, indicating that the novel will delve deeper into the complexities of power, loyalty, and the cost of ambition. Readers are invited to anticipate a full manuscript that promises to blend suspense with a richly textured portrait of a city in transition.
