India's weather office warned that Cyclonic Storm Akvan is strengthening. The India Meteorological Department issued an Orange Message and said it would grow into a stronger cyclone by November 19. The system was a cyclonic storm.

The storm is likely to make landfall near Nellore and Tuni at around midday on November 20.

The IMD forecast very heavy rainfall across Tamil Nadu, Puducherry, Karaikal, coastal Andhra Pradesh and Odisha from November 17 to 21, with winds reaching up to 145 kmph at landfall. The weather office issued the warning.
