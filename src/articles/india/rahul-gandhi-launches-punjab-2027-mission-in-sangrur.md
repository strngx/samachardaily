Leader of Opposition in Lok Sabha Rahul Gandhi inaugurated Congress’s 2027 Punjab election mission in Sangrur, positioning the party ahead of the upcoming Vidhan Sabha polls. The choice of Sangrur sends a clear signal to political rivals that Gandhi intends to contest the electoral battle seriously.

By targeting AAP’s power centre in Punjab’s heartland, the Congress aims to erode the governing party’s dominance in the region. The launch underscores Gandhi’s focus on consolidating opposition strength for the 2027 assembly elections.

Political observers view the timing as a strategic effort to consolidate opposition votes before the 2027 polls, reflecting Gandhi’s commitment to contesting the election seriously.
