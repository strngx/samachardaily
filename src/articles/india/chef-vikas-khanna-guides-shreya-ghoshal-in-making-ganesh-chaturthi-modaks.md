Chef Vikas Khanna shared an Instagram video of singer Shreya Ghoshal joining him in festive preparations for Ganesh Chaturthi, including decorating a Lord Ganesha idol and learning to shape traditional sweet dumplings called modaks.

## What happened

On September 24, Khanna posted the video on his verified Instagram account (@vikaskhannagroup), which accumulated significant engagement from fans of both personalities. The celebration took place in North America, where Ghoshal had arrived as part of *The Unstoppable Tour 2026* — described as her most extensive North American concert tour to date.

## What Vikas Khanna said

In the post's caption, Khanna described Ghoshal's voice as "sacred" and said it is capable of "healing wounds." He recalled a friend's remark that "Shreya's voice is home," and described her presence as feeling like "a daughter returning home." He mentioned shared moments, including a Ganga Aarti, before the modak-making session. NDTV Food's coverage of the video further documented Khanna's praise of Ghoshal's musical legacy.

## Background

Modaks are a traditional steamed sweet dumpling, typically made from rice flour with a jaggery and coconut filling, considered the favourite offering (*prasad*) of Lord Ganesha during the Ganesh Chaturthi festival. Khanna, a Michelin-starred chef and author, has frequently used his public platform to celebrate Indian culinary traditions abroad.

## Why it matters

The collaboration brought public attention to Ganesh Chaturthi rituals from the Indian diaspora in North America, blending culinary heritage with celebrity reach in a format well-suited to social media engagement. Both Khanna and Ghoshal command large followings, and the video offered a moment of cultural connection for Indian communities celebrating the festival outside India.
