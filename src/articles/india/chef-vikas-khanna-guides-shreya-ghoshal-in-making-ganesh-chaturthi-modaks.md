On September 24, chef Vikas Khanna shared an Instagram video of singer Shreya Ghoshal joining him in festive preparations for Ganesh Chaturthi, including decorating a Lord Ganesha idol and learning to shape traditional sweet dumplings called modaks.

In the post’s caption, Khanna described Ghoshal’s voice as “sacred” and capable of healing wounds, recalling a friend’s remark that “Shreya’s voice is home.” He added that her presence felt like a daughter returning home, mentioning moments with Myshu and a shared Ganga Aarti before the cooking lesson.

The video follows Ghoshal’s recent arrival in Canada as part of ‘The Unstoppable Tour 2026,’ the latest leg of her most extensive North American tour, underscoring the cross‑continental reach of the celebration.
