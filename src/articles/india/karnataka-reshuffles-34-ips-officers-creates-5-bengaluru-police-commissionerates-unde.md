The Karnataka government transferred 34 senior IPS officers Wednesday, including Bengaluru City Police Commissioner Seemanth Kumar Singh, with immediate effect.

Bengaluru has been reorganised into five new police commissionerates—Central, East, West, North and South—each headed by a separate commissioner. R. Hitendra, a 1996-batch IPS officer, has been appointed city police commissioner and head of the restructured system.

Senior IPS officer Hemant M. Nimbalkar has been appointed Bengaluru City Traffic Police Commissioner with additional charge of the upgraded Anti-Rowdy Squad. Outgoing commissioner Seemanth Kumar Singh said he spent one-and-a-half years trying to make the city drug-free and expressed pride in being the last police commissioner of undivided Bengaluru.
