New Delhi, Oct 31 – Two advocates have moved the Supreme Court seeking contempt proceedings against the Congress’ Kerala unit over a controversial social media post that targeted Chief Justice of India Surya Kant.

The legal challenge marks the first instance of a state Congress unit being taken to court over remarks directed at a sitting CJI, highlighting heightened scrutiny of political social media activity. The petition alleges that the post, which has since been removed, violated the judiciary’s dignity and could influence public perception of the apex court and may undermine confidence in judicial independence.
