Three Aam Aadmi Party MLAs in Punjab formally joined the Bharatiya Janata Party on Saturday, marking a significant political shift ahead of the 2027 assembly elections. The legislators said they were leaving because the AAP government had failed to deliver on key promises made to voters.

AAP leadership sharply criticised the BJP for recruiting defectors instead of building its own base in the state. The party accused its rival of using inducements to engineer defections rather than contesting elections on merit.
