On Monday, Manoj Jarange, a prominent Maratha leader, commenced his 11th day of an indefinite hunger strike outside the Maharashtra Legislative Assembly, demanding that the state honour the 16% reservation promised to the Maratha community under the 2018 amendment.

The Maharashtra government, citing a recent verification exercise, has questioned the authenticity of Jarange’s Kunbi caste certificate, alleging procedural lapses. Officials say the certificate does not meet the criteria set by the state’s reservation board, a claim Jarange has flatly rejected as politically motivated.

State Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis urged the protestors to end the fast, warning of health risks, while opposition parties have rallied behind Jarange, accusing the ruling BJP-Shiv Sena alliance of reneging on its electoral promise. Legal experts note that the Supreme Court’s 2022 verdict on caste‑based reservations remains a pivotal reference point for any further judicial scrutiny.
