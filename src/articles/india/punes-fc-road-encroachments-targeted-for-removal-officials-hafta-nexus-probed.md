Pune Municipal Corporation corporator Nivedita Ekbote announced that encroachments on the city's iconic FC Road will be cleared, stating the issue has been on the civic agenda since the current corporators took office but has worsened sharply in recent years.

Ekbote also said an alleged hafta — protection-money — nexus involving officials is under scrutiny. She noted FC Road's popularity with students and youngsters has fuelled a surge in eateries and commercial outlets, intensifying the encroachment problem.
