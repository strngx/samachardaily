Chief Justice of India Surya Kant spoke at the first national conference organised by the SC, ST & OBC Advocates Association in New Delhi on Friday.

He described the Constitution as a "continuing responsibility" and said the work of giving life to its ideals passes from one generation to the next.

The CJI's address characterised the Constitution as an obligation handed down through successive generations rather than a completed task.
