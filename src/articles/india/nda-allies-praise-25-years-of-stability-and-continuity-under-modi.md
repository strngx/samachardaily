NDA constituent parties have publicly hailed Narendra Modi's 25-year political career, describing it as a period defined by stability and continuity for the country.

According to the report, allies contend that Modi's legacy is shaped not only by electoral success but also by his demonstrated ability to sustain alliances. They emphasized his capacity to accommodate parties with different regional and political interests within the broader alliance.

The assessment, attributed to NDA constituents in the report, suggests that managing ideological diversity remains a core component of the alliance's endurance under his leadership.
