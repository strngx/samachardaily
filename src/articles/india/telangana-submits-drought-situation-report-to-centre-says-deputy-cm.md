Telangana Deputy Chief Minister Mallu Bhatti Vikramarka said on Wednesday that a report on the drought situation in the state had already been submitted to the Centre by the state government.

The Deputy Chief Minister made the statement in Hyderabad on October 28, according to the IANS dispatch carried by Bhaskarlive.

The report relates to drought conditions in Telangana, though the dispatch did not specify when it was sent, what assistance the state seeks, or whether the Centre has responded to the submission.
