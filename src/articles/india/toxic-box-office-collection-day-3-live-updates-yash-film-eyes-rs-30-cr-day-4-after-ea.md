Day 3 saw Yash’s latest release, ‘Toxic’, add Rs 26.60 cr to its Friday takings, lifting the domestic gross to Rs 197.70 cr and the worldwide haul to Rs 221.45 cr. The numbers, while robust, hinted at a slowdown compared with the opening surge, prompting the “crash” comment in trade circles.

Industry watchers now focus on Day 4, with distributors eyeing a Rs 30 cr collection to sustain momentum. The target comes amid a crowded release calendar, and analysts note that any dip could affect screen allocations for the film’s second weekend.

Trade bodies such as the Film Federation of India have confirmed that official figures will be released after the close of business on Day 4. Meanwhile, the film’s producers remain optimistic, citing strong word‑of‑mouth and a growing fan base for Yash across regional markets.
