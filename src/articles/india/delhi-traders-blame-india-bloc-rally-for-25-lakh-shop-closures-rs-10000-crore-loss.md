Approximately 2.5 lakh shops across Delhi were shut, marking a massive disruption to the city's retail landscape. The closures involved a large number of market outlets spanning the capital. The shutdown affected daily commerce and customer access across the city.

The traders quantified the economic impact at Rs 10,000 crore, highlighting the severity of the loss. This figure reflects the combined revenue decline reported by the affected traders.

Delhi traders blamed the INDIA bloc rally for the business disruption, linking the political event to the shop closures. They said the rally's organization coincided with the widespread shutdown.
