The Sunday Guardian published a digital feature titled “Children’s Day 2026: 100+ Heartwarming Wishes, Inspirational Quotes, Beautiful Messages, Greetings, Images, Captions & WhatsApp Status,” offering a curated set of wishes, quotes, messages, images, captions and WhatsApp status ideas.

The release aligns with India’s annual Children’s Day on November 14, a day that commemorates the birth anniversary of former Prime Minister Pandit Jawaharlal Nehru.

The collection is aimed at readers who wish to share ready‑made greetings across social media and personalize their celebrations for the occasion.
