On Monday, Nov 23, at about 2:30 pm, a man identified as Nabeel brandished a knife inside Delhi’s Jama Masjid police station, stabbing officers and wounding five personnel, including injuries to their faces and necks.

He had been summoned after a complaint alleging harassment by his mother and sister. When he attempted to escape, police shot him, arrested Nabeel, and three of the injured officers were listed as critical.

The episode highlights vulnerabilities in police stations when handling volatile domestic disputes, underscoring safety concerns for law‑enforcement personnel.
