Delhi has deployed over 20,000 police and paramilitary personnel across the city ahead of the Cockroach Janata Party's October 10 demonstration against CEC Gyanesh Kumar. The substantial security buildup precedes the scheduled rally, which organizers intend to hold despite existing restrictions on public assemblies.

Authorities confirmed that no permission has been granted for a gathering at Jantar Mantar, where prohibitory orders currently remain in force.

Police have urged protesters to maintain peace and avoid spreading misinformation during the event, emphasizing that unauthorized assemblies are not permitted at the site.
