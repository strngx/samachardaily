Mumbai Indians announced their 2027 retention and release list, moving captain Hardik Pandya to Punjab Kings in exchange for left‑arm pacer Arshdeep Singh.

The trade frees salary‑cap space as the franchise releases New Zealand fast bowler Trent Boult and Indian swing bowler Deepak Chahar, freeing funds for new signings. All‑rounder Shardul Thakur is retained for the upcoming season.

These adjustments reshape MI's pace attack and give Punjab Kings a new bowling option ahead of the next IPL campaign.
