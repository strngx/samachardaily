The Pink Line, part of Namma Metro's Phase 2 network, was flagged off by Prime Minister Narendra Modi and Karnataka Chief Minister D.K. Shivakumar on November 13. The Hindu reported the launch as part of its "Decode Karnataka" series, questioning whether the new corridor will be a traffic gamechanger.

The addition represents a significant expansion of Bengaluru's metro network. The launch marks a key development in Karnataka's urban transit infrastructure, with commuters watching to see if the Pink Line delivers meaningful traffic relief.
