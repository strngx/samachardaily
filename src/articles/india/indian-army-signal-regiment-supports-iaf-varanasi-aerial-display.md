The Indian Air Force's Air Force Day Aerial Display 2026 will be held at Namo Ghat in Varanasi on October 22, 24 and 25.

The Indian Army's 4 Air Formation Signal Regiment will provide communications support for the event, establishing reliable links between ground control and the aircraft. The support will be maintained throughout the three-day display, ensuring effective and continuous communication during the aerial performances.

This collaboration underscores the joint operational capabilities of the Indian armed forces. Such coordination is vital for the safe execution of the event. The joint effort highlights the importance of inter-service cooperation.
