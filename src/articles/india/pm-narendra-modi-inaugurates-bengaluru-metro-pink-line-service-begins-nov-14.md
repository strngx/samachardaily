PM Narendra Modi held the sovereign ceremony for Bengaluru Metro's Pink Line, unveiling the new metro branch. The line will open to commuters on November 14, providing a direct link from Banerghatta Road in south Bengaluru to HSR Layout, Jayanagar and Whitefield in the eastern and central districts, thereby easing travel for millions and cutting travel time. The route includes several planned stations before its first interchange point.

Details of construction costs and timelines are limited to paid plan subscribers. The project seeks to reduce congestion on major routes and boost connectivity across the cities' south-east corridor.
