New Delhi, September 1, 2026 – The National Institute of Fashion Technology (NIFT) staged a combined convocation ceremony today, bringing together graduates from its network of campuses across the country. The event was held at the institute’s flagship campus in New Delhi and marked the culmination of a year-long academic cycle for students in fashion design, technology, management and related disciplines.

During the ceremony, the Director General of NIFT presided over the proceedings, administering the traditional pledge that underscores the institute’s commitment to creativity, sustainability and ethical practice in the fashion sector. Following the pledge, the Director General symbolically conferred degrees upon each graduate, a gesture that formally recognized their academic achievements and qualified them to enter the professional arena.

NIFT, established in 1986, has grown into India’s premier fashion education institution, operating 18 campuses and offering undergraduate, postgraduate and doctoral programmes. The institute’s curriculum blends design fundamentals with industry‑relevant skills, and its alumni include notable designers, entrepreneurs and policy makers who have shaped both domestic and global fashion landscapes. The combined convocation reflects NIFT’s effort to foster a unified community among its dispersed student body.

The graduating cohort comprises students from diverse specialisations, including apparel design, textile technology, fashion communication and fashion management. While exact numbers were not disclosed, the ceremony highlighted the institute’s emphasis on interdisciplinary collaboration, with many graduates having completed joint projects that address sustainability, digital innovation and inclusive design. Faculty members and industry partners were also present to applaud the students’ accomplishments and to signal ongoing mentorship and placement opportunities.

In his address, the Director General emphasized the role of NIFT graduates in driving India’s emerging fashion ecosystem, especially as the sector navigates challenges such as supply‑chain disruptions and evolving consumer preferences. He urged the new alumni to leverage their training to champion responsible production, embrace technology, and contribute to the nation’s creative economy.
