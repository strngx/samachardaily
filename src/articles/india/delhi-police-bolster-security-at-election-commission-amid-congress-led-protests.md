Delhi Police have set up a cordon around Jantar Mantar and increased the number of officers stationed outside the Election Commission of India’s Delhi office in anticipation of protests.

The demonstrations are organized by the Delhi Congress, which opposes recent electoral roll revisions and demands the resignation of Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar. Parallel protests are reported in Madhya Pradesh and Punjab, also targeting the chief election commissioner, while opposition parties are preparing impeachment proceedings against Kumar in Parliament over alleged irregularities.
