A legal notice has been sent to Sonia Gandhi by Satyaki, the grandnephew of Savarkar, over references contained in a memoir. The communication was issued from Pune, The Free Press Journal reported.

The notice calls for an unconditional written apology and a public clarification. It further seeks corrections to two disputed passages in the book.

Gandhi has been given 15 days from the receipt of the notice to meet these demands. Should she fail to comply within that window, Satyaki has warned that he will initiate legal proceedings before the Pune District Court.
