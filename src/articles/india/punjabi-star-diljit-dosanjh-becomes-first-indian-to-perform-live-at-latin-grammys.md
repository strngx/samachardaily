Punjabi superstar Diljit Dosanjh became the first Indian artist to deliver a live performance at the 27th Latin Grammy Awards in Las Vegas, sharing the stage with Puerto Rican singer Daddy Yankee as part of the ceremony.

He posted a glimpse of the moment on Instagram, captioning it PANJABI AA GAYE @latingrammys OYE. Dunia Di Har … to highlight his participation.

The ceremony, held in Las Vegas, underscored his historic role as the inaugural Indian live performer at this major Latin music event.
