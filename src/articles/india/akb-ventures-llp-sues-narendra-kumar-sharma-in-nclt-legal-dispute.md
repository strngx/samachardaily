A consortium of AKB Ventures LLP has formally sued Narendra Kumar Sharma, representing Spaze Towers Pvt. Ltd., before the National Corporate Tribunal (NCLT) in New Delhi. The legal action addresses a dispute concerning corporate governance and operational oversight within the entity.

This case exemplifies the growing use of specialized corporate tribunals in India to resolve complex business conflicts. The NCLT bench will evaluate evidence and determine the outcome based on the documented allegations and claims presented by both sides.
