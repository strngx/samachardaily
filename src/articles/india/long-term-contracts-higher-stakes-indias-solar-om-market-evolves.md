India’s solar operations and maintenance (O&M) sector is shifting from basic equipment servicing to a broader focus on long‑term generation performance, equipment availability and operational reliability. Developers and asset owners are increasingly signing contracts that run for 10 to 12 years, reflecting higher expectations for sustained output.

The rise of hybrid solar‑plus‑storage projects is driving O&M firms to offer predictive‑maintenance tools, specialised engineering support and lifecycle‑cost management services. These capabilities are becoming essential to meet the evolving contractual demands. Providers that can integrate data analytics into maintenance schedules are positioned to capture the growing market share.
