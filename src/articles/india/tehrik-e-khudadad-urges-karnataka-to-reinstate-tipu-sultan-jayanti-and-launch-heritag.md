Tehrik-e-Khudadad delivered a memorandum to Karnataka Chief Minister D.K. Shivakumar on Tuesday, urging reinstatement of the official Tipu Sultan Jayanti on November 10. The petition, presented by Hyderabad Karnataka region president Mohammed Rafeeque Mulla via the Kalaburagi Deputy Commissioner, calls for restoring the celebration.

The group also seeks textbook revisions to highlight Tipu Sultan and Hyder Ali’s achievements in rocket technology, administration, sericulture and manufacturing. It proposes eight initiatives, including a heritage museum, applied‑science centre, silk projects, a crafts hub, an enterprise network, an innovation challenge, a university and scholarship programmes. The organisation demands a decision before month‑end and asks departments to prepare feasibility and financial plans.
