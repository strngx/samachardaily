Bhopal Police have announced a ₹30,000 reward for information leading to caretaker Varun Kumar, who is wanted for the alleged murder of retired IAS officer Rakesh Agarwal on September 30. Kumar fled the scene with his phone, prompting a massive manhunt.

More than 150 personnel are operating across 12 teams, searching for Kumar in Madhya Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh, Bihar and Maharashtra. The police appeal aims to locate the suspect quickly and bring closure to the high‑profile killing.
