The India Meteorological Department has issued a yellow alert for Bengaluru as heavy rain and thunderstorms are forecast through Thursday evening. The warning spans several districts, marking a significant weather change for the capital region.

Authorities caution that this rapid weather shift could lead to traffic congestion and power outages. Additionally, the northeast monsoon is expected to begin over the southern peninsula, compounding the impact on local infrastructure and daily routines.
