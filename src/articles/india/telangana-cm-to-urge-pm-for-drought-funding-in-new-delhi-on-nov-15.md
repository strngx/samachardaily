Chief Minister A Revanth Reddy and Irrigation Minister N Uttam Kumar Reddy will meet Prime Minister Narendra Modi in New Delhi on November 15, urging the Centre to extend financial assistance to help Telangana confront the drought situation exacerbated by El Niño.

The leaders aim to secure funds for immediate relief measures and long‑term water management strategies as the state faces acute water shortages.

Telangana has been experiencing below‑normal rainfall, and the chief minister hopes the central support will enable rapid deployment of water tankers and agricultural aid immediately effectively.
