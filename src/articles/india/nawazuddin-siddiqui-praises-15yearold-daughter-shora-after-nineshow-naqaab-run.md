Acclaimed film actor Nawazuddin Siddiqui has expressed pride in his 15-year-old daughter Shora Siddiqui following her stage performance in the dramatic production *Naqaab*, which concluded a multi-city run of nine live shows across the United States.

### Live Theatre Debut in 'Naqaab'
According to reporting from *The Hawk*, the 90-minute stage drama premiered in Chicago before touring other American venues. Siddiqui, who also acted alongside his daughter in the production, revealed that he intentionally assigned Shora the production's most demanding role. 

The character required extensive stage presence from the opening sequence to the final act, demanding substantial dialogue memorization and sustained emotional delivery. Siddiqui explained that he wanted her to experience the rigorous discipline of live stagecraft—where actors operate without safety nets or second takes—rather than entering performing arts through protected or gradual compromises.

### Next Steps and Professional Training
Despite initial parental anxieties regarding line delivery under stage pressure, Siddiqui praised Shora's poise, timing, and composure across all nine performances. He emphasized that formal education remains her immediate priority, confirming that once she completes her academic schooling, he will fully support whatever professional theatre or acting conservatory training she decides to pursue. Siddiqui noted that this demanding initial run provided invaluable practical grounding for her creative growth.
