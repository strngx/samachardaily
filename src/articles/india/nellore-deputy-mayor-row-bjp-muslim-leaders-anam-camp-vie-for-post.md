The BJP has staked a claim to the deputy mayor's post in the Nellore Municipal Corporation after winning five NMC divisions for the first time.

Muslim leaders have pressed a rival demand for the office, citing the election of 10 Muslim corporators to the corporation as the ground for their claim.

Minister Anam has pitched his nephew, Anam Ranga Mayur Reddy, to MLA Kotamreddy for the post. With multiple claimants in the field, the coalition's choice for the deputy mayor's office remains unsettled.
