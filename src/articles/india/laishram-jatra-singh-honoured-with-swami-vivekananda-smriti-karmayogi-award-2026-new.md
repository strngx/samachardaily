Laishram Jatra Singh received the Swami Vivekananda Smriti Karmayogi Award 2026 at a ceremony in New Delhi on Saturday. The honour recognised 55 years of his social service and work for the national interest in Northeast India.

VHP International President Advocate Alok Kumar presented the award, emphasizing that serving society with dedication defines Karmayoga. He stated that every citizen bears responsibility toward the nation, society and culture.

Geetarshi Gyananand Ji, chief guest at the event, urged young people to adopt Swami Vivekananda's teachings as a path to societal contribution. After receiving the award, Laishram Jatra Singh thanked organizers and pledged to continue his service to society and the nation.
