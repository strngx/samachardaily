A massive search and rescue operation has been launched after an Indian Army boat met with an accident in the Yamuna River, leaving a soldier missing in the water. Emergency response teams were immediately deployed to the area following the incident to locate the missing personnel.

The search effort involves coordinated teams from the Indian Army and the State Disaster Response Force (SDRF). Operations are currently concentrated along the river stretches surrounding Yamunanagar in Haryana, with personnel from neighboring Uttar Pradesh also joining the joint effort.

Rescue divers and disaster response personnel are actively scanning the water flow and riverbanks downriver from the accident site. The involvement of teams from adjoining states underscores the wide geographical scope of the search, given the river's swift currents and broad basin.

Military vessels frequently navigate inland water channels like the Yamuna for routine transit, training exercises, and operational duties. Joint disaster management protocols have been activated to ensure seamless coordination between civil administration and military authorities during the rescue mission.

Search operations remain ongoing as personnel continue to comb the river and its banks. Official updates regarding the exact circumstances that led to the boat accident and the identity of the missing soldier are expected as the rescue mission progresses.
