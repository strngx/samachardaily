BJP MLA Ranbir Singh Pathania on Wednesday hailed the Modi government's 'zero tolerance towards terrorism' while addressing a gathering at Ayesha International School in Udhampur, following a Rangoli competition. He claimed that under Prime Minister Narendra Modi's leadership, a new sense of security and confidence has been created in Jammu and Kashmir, citing measures against terrorism, its financing, and support networks.

Pathania further emphasized that the demolition and attachment of properties linked to terrorists and their supporters sent a strong signal against the terror ecosystem. He also highlighted PM Modi's 25-year public service journey, mentioning crises like the COVID-19 pandemic, demonetisation, and geopolitical challenges, asserting that India emerged stronger globally. He urged citizens to embrace the 'Viksit Bharat' vision as a people's movement.

On local development, Pathania announced road construction initiatives in Udhampur East, including works from Ambi Nallah towards Spa village and repairs on damaged stretches. He stressed that better connectivity would boost education, healthcare, tourism, and rural economy, reaffirming his commitment to quality infrastructure.
