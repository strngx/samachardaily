Belagavi artist Shilpa Khadakbavi presents contemporary artworks at a charity show held in Kerala, showcasing her modern pieces as part of a fundraising effort. The event highlights the role of art in supporting the society's fundraising.

The exhibition is organized to support the Pain and Palliative Care Society, aiming to raise funds for its palliative care services, generate financial support for its programs, and aid pain relief and end-of-life care initiatives.

Organizers highlight that the event brings together artists and donors to aid the society's programs, emphasizing artistic contributions to the cause.
