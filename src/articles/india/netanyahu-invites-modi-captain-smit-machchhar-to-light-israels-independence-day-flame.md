Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu has invited Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi and Flydubai pilot Captain Smit Machchhar to ignite the celebratory flame at Israel's upcoming Independence Day ceremony.

The invitation extends an honor typically reserved for Israeli citizens, acknowledging Machchhar's courageous actions during a mid‑air attack and signalling deeper bilateral cooperation. The gesture reflects Israel's practice of honoring distinguished individuals, reinforcing diplomatic ties with India.

The lighting ceremony, a traditional element of Israel's Independence Day celebrations, underscores the mutual appreciation of bravery and serves as a symbolic bridge between the two nations' peoples.
