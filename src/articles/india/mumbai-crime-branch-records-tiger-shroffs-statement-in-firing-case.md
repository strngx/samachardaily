Mumbai Crime Branch has recorded a detailed statement from actor Tiger Shroff in connection with a firing incident that took place in the Juhu area of Mumbai on October 18. Investigators questioned the actor about the incident and whether he had received any threats prior to the attack, seeking clarification on the circumstances surrounding the event. The statement was recorded on October 19 as the investigation into the matter continues.

This recording represents a key step in the ongoing inquiry, providing official documentation of the actor's account while authorities work to determine the facts. The incident occurred in Juhu, and the timeline of events is being carefully documented by the crime branch.
