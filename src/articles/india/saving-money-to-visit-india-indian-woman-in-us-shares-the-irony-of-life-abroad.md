The 32‑year‑old software engineer moved to the United States three years ago for a graduate program and later secured a tech job in Seattle. While the career boost and higher earnings have been rewarding, daily life has been marked by a quiet yearning for the flavors and social fabric of India.

She recounts evenings spent scrolling through recipes for dal, roti and street‑food snacks, only to realize that the taste of authentic Indian spices is hard to replicate abroad. The irony, she says, lies in the fact that the higher disposable income she earns is now being earmarked for a trip back home, a journey she hopes will satisfy both her culinary cravings and the pull of family gatherings.

Her experience mirrors that of many Indian expatriates who balance professional aspirations with cultural ties. Community groups, weekend potlucks and virtual festivals have helped bridge the gap, yet the pull of home remains strong, prompting many to allocate a portion of their earnings toward periodic visits.
