The Bharatiya Janata Party's Uttar Pradesh unit has finalised an extensive organisational blueprint targeting the 2027 Assembly elections, centred on reinforcing booth-level infrastructure, widening social outreach, and mapping influential figures in every constituency.

As part of the preparation, the party will convene two-session meetings with former office-bearers. The first session will collect worker feedback on the political climate and organisational hurdles, while the second will focus on formulating winning strategies for the state polls.
