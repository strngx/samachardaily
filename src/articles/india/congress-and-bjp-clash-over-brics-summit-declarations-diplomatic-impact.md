The declaration issued at the recent BRICS summit has sparked a fresh round of political debate in India, prompting the ruling BJP and the government to label it a diplomatic success.

Opposition parties, led by the Congress, have voiced doubts about whether the declaration delivers any substantive advantage for India, challenging the government's assessment.
