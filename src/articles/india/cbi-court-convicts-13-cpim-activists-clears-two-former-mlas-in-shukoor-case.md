Former MLAs P. Jayarajan and T.V. Rajesh were acquitted by a CBI special court in Ernakulam on Thursday in the 2012 murder of Ariyil Shukoor, a 23-year-old Muslim Students Federation activist. The court found 13 CPI(M) activists guilty, while 18 accused in all were cleared, including Shyamjith, son of CPI(M) state secretary M.V. Govindan.

Shukoor was hacked to death at Keezhara in Kannur on February 20, 2012. The CBI alleged the conspiracy was hatched at Thaliparamba Co-operative Hospital, where Jayarajan and Rajesh were admitted after an attack on them that day. Both leaders had denied the allegations.

Industries Minister P.K. Kunhalikutty said the IUML would decide its future course after examining the complete judgment.
