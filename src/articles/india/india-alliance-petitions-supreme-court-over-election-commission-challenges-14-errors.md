The INDIA alliance has moved a petition to the Supreme Court challenging the Election Commission. Fourteen errors were identified in the filing, and the court will only hear the case once these issues are resolved.

Rahul Gandhi plans a tea meeting for opposition MPs to discuss next steps. Meanwhile, the Congress party has directed its workers to remain in Delhi. A separate controversy arose when a Kerala Congress post regarding the Chief Justice of India was removed following the dispute. The removal followed intense debate over the post's content. This legal battle highlights ongoing tensions between political parties and judicial oversight in Maharashtra's political landscape.
