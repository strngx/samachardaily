Toronto, Los Angeles, and Mumbai-focused media enterprise QYOU Media Inc. has published its financial results for the second quarter of fiscal year 2026, delivering a notable 27 percent year-over-year surge in total revenue. The financial performance highlights a strong growth trajectory for the company as it continues to capitalize on its core operational focus across digital-first content creation, production, and marketing services in major international markets.

According to official disclosures released on August 31, 2026, the company attributed the sustained revenue acceleration directly to its ongoing strategic commitment to the global creator economy business. By integrating social-first content creators with conventional distribution networks and brand partnerships across North America and India, QYOU Media has sought to capture expanding commercial interest from advertisers targeting digital-native demographics.

Operative across primary hubs in Toronto, Los Angeles, and Mumbai, QYOU Media Inc. maintains dual public listings on the TSX Venture Exchange under the ticker symbol TSXV: QYOU and on the OTCQB Venture Market under the ticker OTCQB: QYOUF. The enterprise's operational presence in India places it at the center of one of the world's fastest-expanding digital media markets, complementing its established North American entertainment footprint.

Following the release of the quarterly figures, company leadership is scheduled to conduct a live shareholder conference call to review the Q2 FY 2026 financial metrics and provide additional operational context. The live event will take place on Tuesday, September 1, 2026, at 1:30 PM Eastern Standard Time (EST), offering institutional and retail investors an opportunity to evaluate management's strategic priorities for the business.
