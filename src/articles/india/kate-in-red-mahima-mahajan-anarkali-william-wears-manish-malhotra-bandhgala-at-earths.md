Kate Middleton and Prince William attended the Earthshot Prize Awards in Mumbai wearing creations by Indian designers. Kate wore a vibrant red anarkali designed by Mahima Mahajan, while Prince William chose a black bandhgala from Manish Malhotra.

The ceremony honored innovators tackling environmental issues and saw a host of celebrities gather in a celebration of local artistry and global initiatives.

Their outfits by Mahima Mahajan and Manish Malhotra were highlighted at the Mumbai ceremony, which paired Indian designer wear with the Earthshot Prize's global environmental mission.
