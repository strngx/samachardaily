Patna, Nov 18: The Bihar government, led by Chief Minister Samrat Choudhary, approved 14 proposals on Wednesday, including Rs 66.58 crore for a detailed project report of a proposed six-lane greenfield expressway linking Buxar and Bhagalpur.

The proposed expressway would start at Buxar and pass through Arwal, Jehanabad, Rajgir, Sikandra and Munger before reaching Bhagalpur, with a future link to the proposed Raxaul-Haldia highway.

The cabinet also approved a cancer hospital at Sadar Hospital in Gangwara, Darbhanga, an IIT research park in Patna and a Rs 11,200 crore state guarantee for foodgrain procurement.
