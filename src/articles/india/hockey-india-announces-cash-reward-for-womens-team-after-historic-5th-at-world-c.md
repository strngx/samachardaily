Hockey India confirmed on Monday that each member of the Indian women’s hockey team will receive a cash reward of Rs 50 lakh (approximately $60,000) in recognition of the side’s historic fifth‑place finish at the 2026 Hockey World Cup. The decision was announced shortly after the team secured the fifth spot by defeating Germany in the placement match, a result that marked the best ever performance by an Indian women’s side at a World Cup tournament. The reward will be disbursed to the 18‑member squad, including coaching and support staff, as part of the federation’s existing incentive scheme for international achievements.

The victory over Germany, a traditional powerhouse, came after a tightly contested match that ended 2‑1 in India’s favour. Goals from forward Rani Rampal and midfielder Deepika Thakur turned the tide in the second half, while the Indian defence held firm against a late German surge. The win not only secured the fifth‑place slot but also earned the team a place in the top‑tier of the global rankings for the first time. Players and officials celebrated the achievement on the field, highlighting the progress made since the team’s early World Cup outings, where they often struggled to advance beyond the group stage.
