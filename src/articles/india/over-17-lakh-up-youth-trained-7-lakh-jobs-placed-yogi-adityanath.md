Lucknow: Uttar Pradesh Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath on Saturday stated that over 17 lakh young people in the state have been trained.

He said more than 7 lakh of those trained were placed in jobs, and confirmed the figures in a statement from Lucknow.

The chief minister’s office in Lucknow issued the update, as the state continues to report progress on its skill development and job placement efforts. The announcement marks a new official employment-related figure from the chief minister’s office. The figures were announced on Saturday.
