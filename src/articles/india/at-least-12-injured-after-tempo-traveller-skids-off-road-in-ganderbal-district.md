At least 12 persons were injured Tuesday evening after a Tempo Traveller skidded off the road near Sumbal Bala on SSG Road in central Kashmir’s Ganderbal district, officials said. The vehicle was carrying tourists from Gujarat, West Bengal and Punjab.

Injured were rushed to Primary Health Centre Gund, where emergency treatment was provided, before several were referred to SKIMS Soura in Srinagar for specialized care. Identified occupants include Sharda Bi and Ramesh Bhai of Ahmedabad, Gujarat, Daysha of Surat, Gujarat, and Swarnadyyti Ghosh of West Bengal, among others.

Police have taken cognisance of the incident and initiated further investigation into the cause of the skid.
