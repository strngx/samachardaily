Prime Minister Narendra Modi conducted a series of bilateral meetings with the leaders of Singapore, Thailand, and the Philippines in Manila on Tuesday. The talks took place on the sidelines of the ASEAN-related summits and the Quad leaders' meeting underway in the Philippine capital.

Discussions centered on deepening strategic partnerships across trade, security, and regional cooperation frameworks. The engagements reflect India's sustained diplomatic outreach to Southeast Asian nations amid evolving Indo-Pacific dynamics.
