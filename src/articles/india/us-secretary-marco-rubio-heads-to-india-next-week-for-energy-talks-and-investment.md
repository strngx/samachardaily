US State Secretary Marco Rubio will arrive in India next week, marking a high‑level diplomatic push to deepen the bilateral strategic partnership. His itinerary includes meetings with senior Indian officials and business leaders in Mumbai and a visit to the energy hub of Jamnagar, underscoring a focus on energy cooperation and investment.

The discussions aim to accelerate joint energy projects, expand trade opportunities and foster greater economic collaboration between the United States and India, reinforcing recent high‑level engagements. Rubio's visit signals continued commitment to collaborative development in key sectors such as renewable energy and infrastructure.
