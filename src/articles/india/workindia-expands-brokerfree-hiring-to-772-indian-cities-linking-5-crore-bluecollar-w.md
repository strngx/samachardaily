WorkIndia, a Bengaluru‑based startup, has extended its broker‑free hiring platform beyond metropolitan areas, now covering 772 cities across India.

The platform directly matches more than 5 crore blue‑collar workers with small and medium enterprises, removing traditional broker fees and thereby lowering recruitment costs for employers. Employers can post openings and receive applications through the app without paying intermediaries.

By streamlining the connection between labour supply and demand, the service seeks to broaden job access for workers while helping businesses fill vacancies more economically.
