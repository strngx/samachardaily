Former Indian Administrative Service officer Divya Mittal has announced her decision to launch a political career in Uttar Pradesh. She plans to contest upcoming elections as an independent candidate, transitioning from administrative service to frontline electoral politics.

Mittal defined her core motivation as advocating for citizens whose daily complaints and grievances fail to secure attention from government officials. Her independent run aims to provide direct representation for these unaddressed public concerns across the state.
