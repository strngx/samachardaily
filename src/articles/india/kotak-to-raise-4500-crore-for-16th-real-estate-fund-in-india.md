Kotak Investment Advisors announced it will raise ₹4,500 crore via its 16th real estate fund, marking a significant step to deepen its exposure to India's property market.

The vehicle will allocate capital to both residential and commercial assets across major metros and select tier‑II locations, using a mix of debt and equity to enhance liquidity for developers and support ongoing project financing and provide greater financing flexibility.

Targeting institutional investors, the fund seeks to conduct multiple transactions that will bolster financing availability and accelerate growth in the real estate sector.
