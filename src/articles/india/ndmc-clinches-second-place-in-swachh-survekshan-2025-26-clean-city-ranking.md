The New Delhi Municipal Council (NDMC) secured the second position in the Swachh Survekshan 2025-26 'Clean City' ranking, placing it among urban local bodies with populations between 50,000 and 300,000.

President Droupadi Murmu presented the award at a ceremony in Vigyan Bhawan on Wednesday, where NDMC Chairman Manoj Kumar Dwivedi and Vice‑Chairperson Kuljeet accepted the honour on behalf of the council.

The result underscores the council’s continued efforts to maintain high sanitation standards and improve urban cleanliness across the capital region. This achievement reflects the council’s systematic waste management and public health initiatives.
