World leaders marked Diwali on Sunday in New Delhi, extending greetings that highlighted the festival’s shared message.

Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney, French President Emmanuel Macron, Sri Lankan President Anura Kumara Dissanayake and UAE President Sheikh Mohamed bin Zayed Al Nahyan each sent Diwali greetings, highlighting the shared message of the festival.

Their coordinated messages underscored Diwali’s global profile and reinforced the festival’s role as a unifying cultural event beyond South Asia, illustrating worldwide participation. The weekend saw numerous world leaders sharing Diwali wishes, highlighting the festival’s significance on the global stage.
