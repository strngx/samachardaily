The 12th batch of the Familiarisation (FAM) Programme run by Dubai-based India Jewellery Exposition Centre (IJEX), a GJEPC initiative, has concluded, bringing together aspiring jewellery exporters from Mumbai, Udaipur and Coimbatore.

The programme is designed to prepare Indian jewellers for global markets, supporting manufacturers and exporters in exploring international markets and building business. The latest batch follows eleven earlier rounds.

IJEX has so far incubated more than 150 exporters from over 15 Indian cities.
