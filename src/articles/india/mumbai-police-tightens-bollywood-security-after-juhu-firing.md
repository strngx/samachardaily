After a firing in Mumbai's Juhu area struck actor Tiger Shroff's car, Bollywood security concerns intensified across the film industry.

Police said two or more Bollywood A-listers now had Y-plus cover, with one actor's protection raised from X to Y-plus and another shifted from Y to Y-plus. The identities of the two were withheld for security reasons, while protection for several other film personalities was also reviewed.

The wider review reportedly includes additional police deployment, enhanced protection at residences and public appearances, and security arrangements for some family members and associates.
