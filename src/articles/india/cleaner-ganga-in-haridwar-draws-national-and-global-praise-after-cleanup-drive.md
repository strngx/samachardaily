Haridwar has tapped all 22 drains that once discharged into the Ganga, diverting them to treatment plants, the Uttarakhand Peyjal Nigam said. Five STPs with a combined capacity of 145 million litres per day are operational.

The river’s deep blue appearance has drawn positive discussion in India and abroad, contrasting with earlier pollution concerns. Officials attribute the change to Namami Gange work and cleanliness campaigns under Chief Minister Pushkar Singh Dhami’s government.

During the Ganga Bandi period, when flow was diverted for maintenance, the Haridwar administration led a cleanup involving government departments, religious institutions, voluntary organisations and trade associations. More than 100 tonnes of waste were collected, including plastic, ritual remnants and discarded clothing.
