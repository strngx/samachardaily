The National Students' Union of India organised a mask distribution drive in Delhi on Tuesday, targeting pedestrians and commuters on Janpath and Barakhamba Roads as Delhi's air quality deteriorated.

Volunteers distributed protective masks to street vendors, commuters and other pedestrians routinely exposed to Delhi's polluted air along the busy thoroughfares as part of a student-led initiative.

The campaign sought to inform the public about health risks linked to prolonged exposure to poor air quality, especially for vulnerable groups moving through Delhi's congested streets daily, aligning with the drive's awareness objective.
