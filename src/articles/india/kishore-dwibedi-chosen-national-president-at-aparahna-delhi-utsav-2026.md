Literary personalities, poets and researchers gathered at the Jagannath Temple in Hauz Khas, New Delhi, on Sunday for the Aparahna Delhi Utsav–2026. The event drew lovers of Odia literature from different parts of India to the temple premises.

Kishore Dwibedi was chosen national president during the gathering. The temple premises resonated with Odia language, literature and culture throughout the day.

The grand literary festival was jointly organised and celebrated Odia language, literature and culture.
