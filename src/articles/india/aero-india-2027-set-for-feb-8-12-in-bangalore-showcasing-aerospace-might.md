The 16th edition of Aero India, billed as Asia’s largest air show, is scheduled for 8–12 February 2027 at Air Force Station Yelahanka in Bangalore. The event was announced by the Press Information Bureau and is positioned as a showcase of India’s growing aerospace capabilities and its expanding network of global defence partners.

Online registration for exhibitors and the booking of exhibition space will commence on 3 September 2026 via the official portal www.aeroindia.gov.in. Organisers have highlighted that the show will foreground India’s aerospace strength, defence innovation and the depth of its international collaborations, inviting both domestic manufacturers and foreign firms to present their latest technologies.

Since its launch, Aero India has evolved into a marquee platform for the Indian defence sector, drawing attention from senior officials, industry leaders and foreign delegations. The exhibition traditionally features a mix of aircraft displays, technology demonstrations and business-to-business meetings, providing a conduit for the Indian Ministry of Defence and private enterprises to forge new contracts and joint ventures.

The upcoming edition is expected to continue this trajectory, with a focus on emerging domains such as unmanned aerial systems, advanced avionics and indigenous engine development. By congregating key stakeholders under one roof, the show aims to accelerate technology transfer, stimulate domestic production and reinforce India’s strategic autonomy in the aerospace arena.

Organised under the aegis of the Indian government, the Aero India 2027 edition underscores the nation’s ambition to cement its status as a regional aerospace hub. The event will also serve as a barometer for the health of the defence export market, as participants gauge interest from overseas buyers and assess the competitiveness of Indian platforms.
