US Secretary of State Marco Rubio touched down in Mumbai on Tuesday. At a reception he called the strategic alignment between India and the United States 'extraordinary,' setting a diplomatic tone for the visit.

On Wednesday, Rubio is slated to hold closed‑door talks with Indian billionaire Gautam Adani and Tata Group chairman N Chandrasekaran. The meetings precede his scheduled trip to Jamnagar, where he will visit Reliance Industries as part of ongoing India‑US economic discussions.
