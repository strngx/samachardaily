Rajasthan Congress president Govind Singh Dotasra launched a sharp attack against Chief Minister Bhajanlal Sharma and the ruling Bharatiya Janata Party, alleging the use of undemocratic methods following the conclusion of urban local body polling. The allegations center on Sikar, where Dotasra accused the BJP leadership of actively fencing independent candidates to secure political leverage after the civic elections.

Countering the claims made by the opposition leadership, BJP leader Saraf rejected the allegations, refuting Dotasra's charges regarding the post-poll containment of independent candidates. The political confrontation underlines escalating friction between the state's ruling party and opposition regarding municipal governance outcomes.
