The Tamil Nadu Hindu Religious and Charitable Endowments Department has opened three 24‑hour information centres to assist Ayyappa devotees planning the Sabarimala pilgrimage. The facilities are operational during the annual Mandala‑Makaravilakku season, providing round‑the‑clock support for pilgrims from the state.

The centres aim to streamline travel guidance, accommodation details, and safety advisories for devotees undertaking the trek. By offering immediate access to essential information, the department seeks to improve the pilgrimage experience and reduce logistical challenges for Tamil Nadu’s large contingent of worshippers.

The initiative reflects the state’s commitment to smooth religious travel and growing demand for real‑time assistance among Ayyappa followers. It also aligns with broader safety and convenience efforts for large‑scale pilgrimages across South India.
