Patna, Nov 19: Bihar held a state-level workshop on free trade outreach, organised by the Union Ministry of Commerce and the Bihar Department of Industries. Chief Minister Samrat Chaudhary and Industries Minister Shreyasi Singh inaugurated the programme.

Chaudhary said the state must expand business activity and strengthen direct import-export arrangements. He cited road, rail, air and waterway upgrades, a Central subsidy for container transport, and new silt and airport policies.

He directed district officials to address business concerns, run monthly industry meetings and promote local products under the One District, One Product initiative.
