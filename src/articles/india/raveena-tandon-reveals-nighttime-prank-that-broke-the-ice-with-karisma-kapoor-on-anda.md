Appearing as a special guest on Sony Entertainment Television’s ‘India’s Best Dancer’ season 5, Raveena Tandon told host Harssh Limbachiyaa that the cast of the 1994 comedy Andaz Apna Apna was constantly laughing, even recalling a scene where Aamir Khan receives a kidnapper’s ransom call while she giggled off‑camera.

She said the real ice‑breaker with co‑star Karisma Kapoor happened during a night shoot when both actresses were tied back‑to‑back to a pillar from 9 PM to 5 AM; after the male crew left for a “dinner break” prank, Raveena asked Karisma if she wanted juice, prompting the first conversation between the two.

Karisma Kapoor later reflected on the film’s first group song ‘Ye Raat Aur Ye Doori’, shot at Mehboob Studio, noting that all four leads were newcomers, nervous yet energized by the comedy timing, and that the experience served as a valuable learning curve for the cast.
