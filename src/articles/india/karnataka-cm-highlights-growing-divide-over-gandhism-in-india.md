Karnataka Chief Minister Shivakumar said India is witnessing a clash between supporters and critics of Gandhism, highlighting a deepening national divide on the philosophy. The remarks came as the state marked Gandhi Jayanti with official ceremonies.

During the day, Shivakumar paid tribute to Mahatma Gandhi, honoured pourakarmikas at Vidhana Soudha, and attended functions at the KPCC headquarters and Gandhi Bhavan, underscoring his commitment to both the leader and civic workers.

Shivakumar's tribute to pourakarmikas highlighted the government's recognition of sanitation workers, linking Gandhian values of service to civic duty. The multi‑venue schedule underscored the CM's effort to connect Gandhi's legacy with contemporary governance.
