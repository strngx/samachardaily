ACT FOR A BETTER TOMORROW, one of Kashmir's oldest and most respected theatre organisations, celebrated its 50-year Golden Jubilee in Srinagar on Nov. 12.

The group said it completed five decades of uninterrupted service to theatre, art, culture and society, as chairman Mushtaaque Ali Ahmad Khan pledged service for another 50 years. Its productions have won state and national awards.

The Golden Jubilee also featured distinguished guests, veteran artists, cultural performances, a souvenir and documentary release, and announcements for ACT's next decade, including an academy, youth leadership centre and archive.
