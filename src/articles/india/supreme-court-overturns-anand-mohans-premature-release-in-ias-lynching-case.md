The Supreme Court on Thursday cancelled the Bihar government’s decision to grant premature release to former MP Anand Mohan, convicted in the 1994 lynching of IAS officer G Krishnaiah.

A bench of Justices Dipankar Datta and Sheel directed Mohan to surrender within one month and return to prison, reversing the premature release order.

The TOI report covered the Supreme Court’s directive for Mohan’s surrender within one month and return to prison.
