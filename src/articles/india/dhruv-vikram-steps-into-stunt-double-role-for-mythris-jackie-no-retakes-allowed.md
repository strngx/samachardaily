Dhruv Vikram, son of veteran star Vikram, is cast as a stuntman in the upcoming Tamil action drama ‘Jackie – No Retakes Allowed’. The film is directed by Karan Arvind Kumar and marks the fourth Tamil venture from the production house Mythri Movie Makers.

A promo titled “Jackie – First Move” reveals that Dhruv’s character, Jackie, serves as a body double when the hero, Random Star Rahul, refuses a simple stunt of running toward fifty on‑screen villains. The sequence shows Jackie’s girlfriend warning him not to repeat a previous injury, followed by a fight that turns out to involve real villains rather than stunt extras.

The project is produced by Naveen Yerneni and Y Ravi Shankar under the Mythri banner. It features National Award‑winning composer Santhosh Narayanan, cinematographer Viki, editor Jayasuriya, production designer GM Sekhar, and action choreography by veteran stunt‑master Vikram Mor.
