Smita Jadhav and Kaustubh Gawade will submit their nomination papers for the Pune Graduate and Teachers’ constituencies today, in the presence of Ravindra Chavan.

Internal dissent has surfaced within the Mahayuti alliance regarding the Pune Teachers’ Constituency, prompting Sachin Sanap and Mangesh Chivte to also file their nomination papers.

Separately, the accused co-pilot of a flydubai aircraft made a major confession, alleging he planned to crash the plane at Israel’s Ben Gurion Airport before targeting a US military base in Jordan after that plan failed. The bravery of an Indian pilot reportedly saved hundreds of lives.
