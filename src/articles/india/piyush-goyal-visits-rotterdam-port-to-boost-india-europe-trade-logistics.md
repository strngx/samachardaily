Union Commerce and Industry Minister Piyush Goyal visited the Port of Rotterdam in the Netherlands on October 25, 2026, to focus on logistics modernisation and enhance maritime logistics, supply chain connectivity, and trade between India and Europe.

Rotterdam Port CEO Boudewijn Siemons accompanied the minister.

The visit is part of the Indian government's ongoing process to intensify economic relations with the Netherlands and help Indian exporters prepare for opportunities in the European market.
