The BJP on Thursday felicitated former Jammu and Kashmir General Secretary (Organisation) Ashok Koul at a ceremony in Srinagar's Countryside Hotel, Nowgam, recognising his role in expanding the party's organisational network across the Union Territory. J&K BJP President and Rajya Sabha MP Sat Sharma said the party values sincere contributions regardless of position, lauding Koul's disciplined working style and grassroots expansion efforts.

Leader of the Opposition Sunil Sharma said the event reflected the BJP's tradition of acknowledging dedication and selfless service, noting that Koul had worked under challenging circumstances while guiding party workers. Koul thanked the party leadership and workers for their support, urging colleagues to maintain unity, discipline and dedication in service of society and the organisation.

The ceremony was attended by BJP General Secretaries Mohammad Anwar Khan and Gopal Mahajan, J&K Waqf Board Chairperson Dr Darakhshan Andrabi, MLA RS Pathania and other senior party leaders.
