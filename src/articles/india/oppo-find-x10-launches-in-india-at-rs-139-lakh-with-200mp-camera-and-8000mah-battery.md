Oppo has introduced the Find X10 in India, pricing the base variant at Rs 1,39,999. The launch adds another flagship to the company's Indian lineup, targeting premium buyers seeking top‑tier hardware.

The device offers a 6.59‑inch 1.5K LTPO OLED display with a 144 Hz refresh rate, driven by a MediaTek Dimensity 9600M chipset. It packs an 8,000 mAh battery with 80 W wired and 50 W wireless charging, and a camera suite led by a 200 MP primary sensor plus a 50 MP ultra‑wide lens.
