Singer Adnan Sami publicly criticised the Pranaam Services operating at Mumbai Airport’s Terminal 2, alleging unprofessional conduct during his recent travel.

He said airport staff mishandled his assistance request and delayed the provision of flight information, and he also took issue with the chatbot’s response to his complaint.

His remarks have drawn attention to service standards at the busy hub, prompting scrutiny of both human assistance and automated support channels.
