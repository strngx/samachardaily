Northeast Frontier Railway has reconstructed the 146-year-old Sukna railway station, a historic stop on the Darjeeling Himalayan Railway, to preserve the iconic Toy Train heritage.

The rebuild is paired with a range of tourist‑centric initiatives introduced as passenger footfall reaches record levels, aiming to draw more visitors to the line.

Officials say the project demonstrates a commitment to protecting the railway’s legacy while capitalising on its popularity for regional tourism.
