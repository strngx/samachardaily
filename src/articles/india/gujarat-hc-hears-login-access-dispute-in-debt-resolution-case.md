The Gujarat High Court is hearing a case concerning login access defaults in a debt resolution matter, with Anand Chandubhai Thakkar and others as petitioners against the Chief State Tax Commissioner and other respondents.

The dispute centers on access credentials and authentication protocols, including email OTP, WhatsApp OTP, and Google username verification, which are critical for accessing debt resolution law portals.

The case highlights procedural challenges in digital access mechanisms within Gujarat's tax and debt recovery framework, though specific outcomes remain pending.
