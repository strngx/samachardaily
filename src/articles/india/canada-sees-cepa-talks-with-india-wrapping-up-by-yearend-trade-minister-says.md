Canada’s minister for international trade announced that the nation is optimistic negotiations on a comprehensive economic partnership agreement (CEPA) with India will be concluded by the end of the year.

The statement, made on Saturday, signals the government’s confidence that the bilateral deal can be sealed within the stated timeframe.
