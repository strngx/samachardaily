The Indian Air Force (IAF) outlined its drone requirements to industry during an outreach programme at Vigyan Bhawan in New Delhi on 21 October. Around 100 drone manufacturers, MSMEs and start‑ups attended the session, where the service presented its Unmanned and Autonomous Systems Roadmap and explained its long‑term vision for unmanned platforms.

This direct engagement marks a decisive step in shaping India’s future unmanned warfare capabilities, aligning industry output with the IAF’s long‑term strategic needs. The IAF emphasized that the roadmap guides industry development to meet future operational requirements across multiple domains.
