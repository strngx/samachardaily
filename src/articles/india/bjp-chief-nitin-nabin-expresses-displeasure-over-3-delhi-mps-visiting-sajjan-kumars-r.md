On Saturday, BJP president Nitin Nabin called three Delhi MPs—Ashwini Kumar Choubey, Mahendra Nath Pandey and Sanjay Singh—to a party office, chastising them for traveling to the residence of Sajjan Kumar after the former minister’s death on August 20.

Kumar, who was serving a life sentence for his role in the 1984 anti‑Sikh riots, died at Safdarjung Hospital. The MPs had gone to offer condolences, a move Nabin said contravened the party’s stance on distancing itself from individuals linked to the riots.

Nabin warned that any further association with convicted rioters would attract internal disciplinary action, emphasizing the BJP’s commitment to upholding the party’s image and the sensitivities surrounding the 1984 violence.
