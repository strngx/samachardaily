Karnataka Chief Minister D.K. Shivakumar on Friday accused several BJP leaders of falsely claiming credit for Prime Minister Narendra Modi’s November 13 visit to Bengaluru, which was made on the state government’s official invitation.

Shivakumar said the visit, aimed at inaugurating the metro Pink Line, was organized by the state, not by the central leadership, and urged the BJP to stop misrepresenting the event. The criticism came during a press briefing where the CM highlighted the state’s role in arranging the ceremony. The remarks were made after the CM reviewed preparations for the upcoming state elections, underscoring the political stakes.
