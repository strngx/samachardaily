Meerut police have arrested 22-year-old Uzaid for plotting suicide attacks on the Army camp. He was detained after being questioned about his plan to harm fellow service members. Authorities say the scheme called for an assault inside the base and threatens the community.

During interrogation Uzaid admitted to viewing anti-India content on Instagram and YouTube, claiming personal exposure drove his radicalisation. Police are reviewing the videos for extremist links.

The arrest signals a growing threat from online propaganda and reinforces the authorities push to stop such plots before they reach the military installation.
