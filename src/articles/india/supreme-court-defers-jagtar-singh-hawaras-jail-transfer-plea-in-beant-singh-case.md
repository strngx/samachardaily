The Supreme Court on Tuesday deferred hearing a plea filed by Babbar Khalsa operative Jagtar Singh Hawara, who is serving life imprisonment for the 1995 assassination of former Punjab Chief Minister Beant Singh, seeking transfer from Delhi's Tihar Jail to a prison in Punjab.

A bench of Justices M.M. Sundresh and P.B. Varale posted the petition, but the court deferred ruling on the transfer request.

The court's deferral keeps the transfer request pending, maintaining Hawara's confinement at Tihar while the matter awaits further proceedings before the Supreme Court.
