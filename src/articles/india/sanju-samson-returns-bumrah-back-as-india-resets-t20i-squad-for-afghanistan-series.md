India has recalled Sanju Samson and Jasprit Bumrah to its T20I squad for the upcoming series against Afghanistan, opting for a full-strength lineup as the side sharpens its preparation for the next phase of the international calendar. The Board of Control for Cricket in India (BCCI) announced the revised squad, marking the return of two of Indian cricket's most sought-after limited-overs specialists after their absence from the previous T20I selection.

Headlining the changes is the comeback of Sanju Samson, the wicketkeeper-batter who had fallen out of favour in recent T20I squads. His reinstatement signals a clear vote of confidence from the selectors in his finishing abilities at the top of the order and behind the stumps. Joining him is pace spearhead Jasprit Bumrah, whose return is being treated as a significant boost given his status as India's premier fast-bowling option across formats and his effectiveness during the death overs.

The reshuffle has come at the cost of eight players who were part of India's previous T20I squad. Their exclusion underlines the selectors' intent to finalise a core group ahead of a busy international window, with performance and workload management reportedly factoring into the decisions. The BCCI has typically used bilateral series against Associate-level opposition as opportunities to rotate personnel while testing combinations.

The Afghanistan series itself carries added intrigue, given the rapid rise of Afghanistan as a competitive T20I force, with match-winners across batting, spin and pace departments. For India, the fixtures offer a controlled environment to integrate returning senior players, evaluate bench strength, and build cohesion before more demanding assignments. The series is also expected to give fringe players a chance to stake their claim under match conditions.

The squad announcement comes at a time when India is balancing international commitments with franchise cricket, and the selectors have been mindful of giving frontline players adequate rest while ensuring competitive combinations. With Samson and Bumrah back in the fold, and a refreshed supporting cast around them, India will be expected to field an XI closer to its strongest available lineup when the teams take the field.
