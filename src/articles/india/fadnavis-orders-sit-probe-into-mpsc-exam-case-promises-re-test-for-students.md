Maharashtra Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis held a formal meeting with a delegation of student representatives on Thursday to address growing grievances regarding the conduct of the Maharashtra Public Service Commission (MPSC) State Services Main Examination 2025. Responding directly to the concerns raised by the aspirants during the interaction, the Chief Minister pledged that the state government would establish a Special Investigation Team (SIT) to conduct a comprehensive probe into the matter.

In a decision aimed at resolving the uncertainty surrounding the exam, Chief Minister Fadnavis assured the student delegation that the government would allow a re-examination for the MPSC State Services Main Examination 2025. The commitment to grant a re-test provides substantial relief to affected candidates who had demanded official intervention into reported irregularities and procedural issues associated with the competitive test.

The MPSC State Services Main Examination serves as the key recruitment pathway for critical administrative and civil service positions across Maharashtra's state government departments. Because thousands of candidates prepare for extended periods to clear these competitive assessments, allegations of administrative lapses or exam compromises carry significant weight. By ordering an SIT investigation, the state government aims to ensure full accountability and scrutinize procedural deficiencies that led to candidate dissatisfaction.

During the Thursday delegation meeting, Chief Minister Fadnavis emphasized the government's commitment to protecting the interests of students and preserving the integrity of state competitive exams. The decision to initiate an SIT probe alongside a re-examination highlights executive recognition of the grievances brought forward by student groups, who have consistently sought institutional transparency and corrective action from top state officials.

Following the Chief Minister's assurances, candidate delegates expressed confidence that the dual actions of an independent SIT investigation and a revised exam schedule would deliver fair outcomes for all impacted test-takers. State authorities and the examination commission are now expected to coordinate administrative procedures to formalize the SIT's mandate and establish clear guidelines for conducting the MPSC State Services Main Examination 2025 re-test.
