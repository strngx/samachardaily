India is in the final stages of negotiations with the Philippines to expand a BrahMos supersonic cruise missile sale, sources familiar with the talks said, Reuters reported.

The draft agreement envisions about five BrahMos launch systems and nearly 60 missiles, with a price tag estimated between $700 million and $800 million, a scale far beyond earlier transactions.

If concluded, the pact would deepen defence cooperation between the two countries and represent one of India's largest regional arms exports, though implementation timelines were not disclosed.
