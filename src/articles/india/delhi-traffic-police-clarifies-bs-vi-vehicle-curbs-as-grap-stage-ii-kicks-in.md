Delhi Traffic Police has issued an advisory under GRAP Stage-II. Delhi-registered petrol and diesel cars meeting BS-VI or higher standards with valid PUC certificates are allowed, along with electric and CNG vehicles. Vehicles barred include Delhi-registered petrol and diesel cars at BS-III or older, and outside-Delhi petrol and diesel cars at BS-IV or older. BS-IV and older commercial vehicles, older interstate diesel buses, and heavy trucks without Delhi destinations face restrictions. Vehicles without valid PUC certificates are prohibited.

Police turned back 609 vehicles through November 2 and issued 433 no-entry challans. Border checks covered 14,930 vehicles, with 1,539 turned back and 62 impounded. Traffic Police issued 493 PUC challans worth Rs 49.3 lakh. Jahangirpuri recorded an AQI of 451.
