The Ernakulam court in Kerala has rejected the prosecution’s plea to cancel the anticipatory bail granted to a judge’s minor son, who is accused in a fatal hit‑and‑run case that killed a 55‑year‑old man. The bail had been granted earlier. The court’s decision came despite strong objections from the victim’s family and the public prosecutor.

In a related development, the juvenile’s father secured anticipatory bail from a separate court in Kochi, marking another setback for the family of the 55‑year‑old man killed in the road accident. The victim’s family had expressed objections.
