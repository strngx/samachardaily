The Sunday Guardian’s piece, “Charting a circular path for wind energy,” shifts focus from batteries and solar panels to wind turbines, urging a circular approach for the sector. It notes India’s clean‑energy circularity debate has centered on imported lithium, cobalt and other battery materials, highlighting supply‑chain risks.

The article outlines how wind‑energy components could be designed for reuse, refurbishment and recycling, reducing dependence on foreign inputs and aligning with broader sustainability goals. It stresses the need for domestic material loops to complement existing solar and storage initiatives.

By framing wind power within the circular‑economy narrative, the piece suggests a pathway for scaling clean energy while safeguarding resource security. Analysis positions wind turbines as a next frontier for circular design in India’s energy transition.
