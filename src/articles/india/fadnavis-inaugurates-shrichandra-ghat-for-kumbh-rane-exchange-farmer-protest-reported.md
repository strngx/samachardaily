Chief Minister Fadnavis inaugurated Shrichandra Ghat for Simhastha Kumbh Mela. The ghat features a flag displaying representations of the 14 jewels that emerged from the Samudra Manthan, and has been recognised as a counterpart to Kushavart.

Navnath Ban responded to criticism from Nilesh Rane by posting a photograph with Narayan Rane on social media, captioning it 'Junior Navnath Ban with Senior Rane Saheb.' The post circulated widely on social platforms.

Farmers from Vaijapur and Gangapur intensified their agitation demanding water from Nandur Madhameshwar, announcing plans to block the Samruddhi Expressway. Police denied permission for the protest and deployed a large security contingent on the highway.
