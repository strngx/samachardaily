IDBI Bank's digital banking infrastructure suffered a major disruption on Sunday, September 6, 2026, leaving customers across India unable to access core services through the bank's mobile application and online platforms. According to outage-tracker Downdetector, complaint volumes spiked sharply during the day, with users flagging failures in fund transfers, mobile banking login and general app functionality. The pattern of grievances indicated that the disruption was not limited to a single service but cut across the bank's primary customer-facing digital touchpoints.

IDBI Bank, classified as a public sector bank since the government's majority stake acquisition in 2019, has historically struggled with the pace of its digital transformation relative to larger peers such as State Bank of India, Punjab National Bank and Bank of Baroda. While the lender has rolled out upgrades to its mobile platform and core banking systems in recent years, periodic outages have continued to surface, often coinciding with month-end transaction surges or backend maintenance cycles. Sunday's disruption comes at a particularly sensitive time, as customers routinely use weekend hours for utility bill payments, peer-to-peer transfers and UPI-linked transactions routed through the bank's systems.

Customer complaints on Downdetector broke down broadly across three categories: mobile banking app crashes, failed or pending fund transfers, and inability to authenticate login credentials. Several users reported seeing error messages or hanging screens when attempting to initiate NEFT, IMPS or UPI transactions through IDBI-issued accounts. A smaller share of complaints related to internet banking desktop access, suggesting the fault may lie in the bank's unified authentication or API gateway layer rather than a single channel.

The bank had not issued a public statement on the root cause or estimated restoration time at the time of reporting. Downdetector's status page, which aggregates user-submitted outage reports, remained the most reliable real-time indicator for affected customers. IDBI Bank customers were advised to retry transactions after a brief interval and to verify settlement status through SMS alerts or passbook updates before initiating duplicate transfers, to avoid double debits during the service window.
