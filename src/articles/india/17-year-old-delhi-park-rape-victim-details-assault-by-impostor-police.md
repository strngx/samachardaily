The 17‑year‑old victim of the recent Delhi park gang‑rape gave a chilling account of the assault that occurred at Aastha Kunj Park near Kalkaji Mandir in South Delhi, saying the attackers introduced themselves as police officers and used the phrase “Asif bhai, gun nikal” before subjecting her to brutal violence.

Media reports note that the testimony has revived public anxiety about women’s safety in the national capital and prompted renewed scrutiny of incidents involving police impersonation.
