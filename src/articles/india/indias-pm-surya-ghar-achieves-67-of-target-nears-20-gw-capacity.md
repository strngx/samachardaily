The PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana has achieved 67% of its target, bringing India closer to a 20‑gigawatt residential solar capacity. This initiative is recognized as the largest driver of rooftop solar installations across the nation.

According to the latest data, installations under the scheme have reached 19,639.6 megawatts, spread across 5.66 million rooftop systems. These figures reflect the rapid expansion of residential solar capacity in the country.

The program continues to grow, reinforcing its role in India's push for renewable energy and providing households with sustainable power solutions.
