Power requirements in India are seen climbing four to five times by 2047, with overall primary energy consumption projected to grow two to 2.5 times during the same period.

Rajnath Ram, Adviser (Energy) at NITI Aayog, raised the energy security concern at the India Future Fuels event, connecting the sharp increase to the country's fast-growing economy and expanding energy needs.

The outlook covers the next two decades and describes an unprecedented jump in consumption as India's economic activity expands.
