China has strongly opposed India's decision to standardize names for 28 strategic locations in Ladakh, labeling the action illegal and invalid. The Foreign Ministry emphasized that these territories remain rightfully Chinese.

The naming initiative specifically targets major sites including Gogra and Hot Spring to improve official cartography and public awareness in the sensitive border region. This move occurs amid heightened tensions following the 2020 standoff between the two nations.

Both countries continue navigating their complex relationship as diplomatic channels remain strained. The dispute highlights ongoing competition over sovereignty and territorial recognition in the Himalayan frontier.
