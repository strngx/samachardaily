Delhi authorities imposed Rs 68.4 lakh in fines and environmental compensation on the inaugural day of the Winter Action Plan, a scheme launched to curb winter pollution. By 5 pm on Sunday, officials inspected nearly 15,000 vehicles across the city, denying entry to 1,539 vehicles at border points and issuing 566 challans for pollution‑related violations.

The fines demonstrate a decisive enforcement push to curb winter pollution, aiming to deter non‑compliant vehicle movements and improve air quality. The checks were carried out on Sunday, indicating ongoing enforcement efforts. The enforcement actions reflect the city’s commitment to meet winter air quality standards.
