Opposition leaders, led by Rahul Gandhi, are set to gather at Congress President Mallikarjun Kharge’s residence to present a public impeachment‑style proposal against the Chief Election Commissioner. They will later march from Connaught Place to Ramlila Maidan for a large rally.

The BJP has announced a counter‑march titled “Save Democracy, Remove Anarchy” in Delhi, with several Union ministers and senior leaders expected to attend. Amit Shah has attacked Rahul Gandhi, likening his response to blaming an umpire after a loss.

Police will deploy across key sites to maintain order as the rival demonstrations bring heightened political activity to Delhi.
