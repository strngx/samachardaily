Navi Mumbai police have arrested Shailendra Dwivedi, accused of posing as a journalist, in connection with a ₹19 lakh housing fraud in the city's Ulwe area.

Dwivedi is named in multiple first information reports registered across Maharashtra, with several criminal cases on record against him, police said. The Navi Mumbai case centres on the ₹19 lakh housing scam in Ulwe.

The arrest places the accused in custody in the Navi Mumbai case even as complaints against him are registered in other jurisdictions in the state.
