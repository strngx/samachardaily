A glacial lake outburst at Sintaphu Lake in Bhutan's Gasa district Thursday evening sent floodwaters down the Mo Chhu and Punatsangchhu rivers, prompting the evacuation of over 10,000 people in West Bengal's Alipurduar district overnight. Authorities accommodated 4,000–5,000 residents in school shelters and shifted 6,000 more to safer ground as the Sankosh River rose.

Bhutan reported damage to the Laya access road and Gasa Tshachu hot spring facilities, with no deaths. The Punatsangchhu-II hydropower project opened dam gates. India issued a flood advisory for Alipurduar and Assam's Dhubri district, deploying SDRF and NDRF teams while the Central Water Commission monitored river levels.

By Friday morning, Alipurduar officials noted reduced water intensity, but warnings remained active along the Sankosh. No major flooding or casualties were confirmed in Assam. Sintaphu, among Bhutan's 17 hazardous glacial lakes assessed in 2023, feeds the Mo Chhu basin that becomes the Sankosh in India.
