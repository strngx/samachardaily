On Sunday, Delhi’s first day under the new winter pollution curbs turned entry points into severe bottlenecks as vehicle checks generated long queues across the city, disrupting commuter flow.

Air quality slipped further into the poor category, and the most visible disruption emerged at the Delhi‑Gurugram border where NH‑48 traffic backed up for several kilometres. The slowdown forced drivers to wait extended periods, raising concerns about fuel consumption and emissions.

The curbs, introduced to curb winter smog, immediately highlighted significant congestion challenges at key transit gates despite their health rationale.
