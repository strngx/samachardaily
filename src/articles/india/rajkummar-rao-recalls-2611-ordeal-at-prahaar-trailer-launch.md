At the Prahaar trailer launch held at Mumbai's CSMT Station, Rajkummar Rao opened up about his personal experience during the 26/11 attacks.

Rao, who had moved to Mumbai just months before the terror strikes, recalled spending three terrifying days glued to the television with friends, praying for the safety of the police force.

He expressed immense relief when Ajmal Kasab was finally captured, bringing an end to the three-day siege that shook the nation.
