Neha Bora has refused to sign a peace bond for the fourth time and has been summoned again on November 20 in connection with the INDIA bloc and CJP leaders case.

The peace bond, a legal mechanism dating back to 1361 under King Edward III, is designed to prevent breaches of peace. Bora's repeated refusal has raised questions about whether she has outsmarted CJP leaders by rejecting the bond while aligning with the INDIA bloc.

The case continues to unfold as Bora faces another summons, with the centuries-old peace bond law testing modern political strategies.
