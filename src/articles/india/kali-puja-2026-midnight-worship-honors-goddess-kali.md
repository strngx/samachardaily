Kali Puja 2026 brings midnight worship to West Bengal as devotees honor Goddess Kali during Kartik Amavasya. The festival, celebrated on the new moon night, centers on rituals at Nishita Kaal to seek protection from evil and overcome fear. Temples and pandals are illuminated with diyas while priests perform ceremonies, offering red hibiscus flowers and traditional bhog offerings.

The midnight worship stems from ancient beliefs that Goddess Kali appeared at Amavasya to destroy demons and protect the universe. Mythological accounts describe how she drank Raktabija's blood to prevent his offspring from multiplying, symbolizing her role as destroyer of evil. The darkness of the new moon night represents infinite cosmic energy and the victory of light over ignorance, making this night a powerful time for spiritual transformation.
