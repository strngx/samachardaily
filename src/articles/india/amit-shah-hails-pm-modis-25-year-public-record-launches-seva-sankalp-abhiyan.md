Speaking at a public ceremony in Vadnagar, Gujarat, Union Home Minister Amit Shah commemorated Prime Minister Narendra Modi’s completion of 25 years in continuous public governance, describing the period since October 2001 as a defining era of structural economic reforms and grassroots social empowerment.

To mark the milestone, Shah officially launched the ‘Seva Sankalp Abhiyan,’ a nationwide public service initiative organizing community-focused welfare activities across the country. The campaign encompasses a comprehensive fortnight of civic drives, featuring voluntary blood donation camps, free preventive health screenings in rural primary health centres, specialized nutrition awareness programs, and extensive tree-planting drives coordinated across district administrative units.

As part of the inaugural events, Shah flagged off a youth motorcycle rally embarking on a 1,400-kilometer journey connecting Vadnagar in Gujarat’s Mehsana district—the Prime Minister’s birthplace—with his parliamentary constituency of Varanasi in eastern Uttar Pradesh. Organizers noted that the route was designed to symbolize cultural and developmental linkages across northern and western India, with scheduled stops at key civic institutions along the corridor.

In his address, the Home Minister outlined key governance benchmarks established over the 25-year timeline spanning Modi’s tenures as Gujarat Chief Minister and Prime Minister. Shah highlighted the rollout of digital public infrastructure, targeted welfare distribution through direct benefit transfers (DBT), financial inclusion initiatives, and major manufacturing policies as foundational elements supporting India's long-term economic trajectory.
