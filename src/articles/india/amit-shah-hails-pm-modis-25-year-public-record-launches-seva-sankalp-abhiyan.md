Speaking on Thursday in Vadnagar, Union Home Minister Amit Shah characterized Prime Minister Narendra Modi's quarter-century in public office as a transformative period centered on empowering underprivileged communities and establishing structural foundations for national prosperity.

To mark the milestone, Shah officially launched a broad public outreach drive titled 'Seva Sankalp Abhiyan' and flagged off a motorcycle rally departing from Vadnagar with its final destination set for Varanasi.
