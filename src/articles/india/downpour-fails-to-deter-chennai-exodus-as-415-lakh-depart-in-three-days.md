Chennai recorded an outflow of 4.15 lakh passengers over the three-day period ending October 17 midnight, with heavy downpour failing to deter large-scale departures from the city.

Transport services logged 1.5 lakh passengers leaving on a single day during the October 15-17 window, reflecting sustained travel demand despite persistent rainfall across the region.

Departure services operated continuously through the period, enabling residents and visitors to maintain planned movements despite the adverse monsoon conditions.
