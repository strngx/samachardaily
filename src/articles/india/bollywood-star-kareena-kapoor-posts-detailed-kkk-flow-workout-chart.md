The actress uploaded a photo of a whiteboard titled “KKK Flow” to her social media, captioning the post with a simple “Loving it”. The chart lists a blend of strength, conditioning and cardio moves, marking a fresh glimpse into her current training regimen.

The routine is split into two five‑round sections. Part A calls for 10 step‑back kettlebell chops, 10 wide‑stance kettlebell lift‑to‑row repetitions and two laps of sled pushes. Part B, labeled “Zone 2”, comprises a 200‑metre sprint on the ski‑erg, a 200‑metre row and a one‑minute bear‑hold hold.

Kareena has repeatedly stressed a balanced approach to fitness, rejecting restrictive diets even during her widely discussed 2008 “size‑zero” transformation for Tashan. She continues to enjoy simple Indian foods such as parathas, dal, rice and khichdi with ghee. Her latest screen appearance was in Meghna Gulzar’s crime thriller Daayra, released on September 18, where she co‑stars with Malayalam actor Prithviraj Sukumaran.
