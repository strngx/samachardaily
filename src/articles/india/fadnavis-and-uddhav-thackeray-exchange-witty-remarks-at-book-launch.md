Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis and Uddhav Thackeray exchanged witty remarks during the launch of the book 'Maharashtrachya Hasyarekha'. The two leaders made lighthearted references to the fact that their phone conversations had stopped after 2019, a similarity in their comments that has since triggered discussion within political circles.

In a separate event, Raj Thackeray and Uddhav Thackeray attended the launch of Sanjay Raut's book 'Bodh Kirtan'. During the ceremony, Raj Thackeray recalled Raut's courage as a journalist and shared a specific anecdote regarding an interview Raut conducted with Balasaheb Thackeray.
