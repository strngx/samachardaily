Uttar Pradesh Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath carried his mother’s bier in Haridwar, joining her final journey despite a long-standing separation from his family after embracing monastic life at a young age.

Savitri Devi’s mortal remains were brought from the ancestral village of Panchur earlier in the morning. Deputy Chief Minister Brajesh Pathak and other senior ministers from Uttar Pradesh and Uttarakhand attended the final rites, along with religious leaders and representatives of various organisations. Security and crowd-control arrangements were made for the VIP presence and visitors.

Adityanath travelled to Uttarakhand after completing official engagements and postponing some scheduled programmes. He is expected to return to Lucknow to resume duties, with Prime Minister Narendra Modi scheduled to visit the state capital later in the day.
