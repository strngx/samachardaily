Rahul Gandhi told supporters that the fight is not over following a meeting of I.N.D.I.A Bloc leaders at his residence, where they held an informal 'chai pe charcha' discussion.

Union Home Minister Amit Shah responded by challenging Rahul Gandhi and his associates in a public statement, saying they should brace themselves for a crushing defeat in the 2029 general election.
