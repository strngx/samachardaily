At the Rashtriya Ekta Diwas gathering in Gujarat, Prime Minister Narendra Modi labelled the Opposition an 'Avrodh Paksh' or obstructive force, warning that attempts to divide the nation would be resisted.

Modi emphasised the need for national unity while citing internal security challenges, including Naxalism and border security concerns, and referenced the government's push for a Uniform Civil Code.

He urged citizens to stand against divisive forces, framing the event as a stand for national cohesion.
