The Odisha BJP has said demonetisation helped reduce terrorism and end Naxalism. The state party made the claim as it pointed to the 2016 demonetisation exercise and its economic effects.

State BJP spokesperson Satyabrata Panda made the assertion in Bhubaneswar. He said the 2016 demonetisation exercise also boosted the economy and reduced terrorism, according to the party.

The Deccan Herald reported the Naxalism reduction statement. The Odisha BJP linked the 2016 demonetisation exercise to its security and economic assessments. The remarks centred on the party's assessment of the policy's impact.
