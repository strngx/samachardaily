A Pakistani Netflix series headlined by Fawad Khan and Mahira Khan has become the centre of a major controversy in India, with the title drawing widespread attention before its release on the platform.

Jo Bachay Hain Sang Samait Lo is an adaptation of an Urdu novel, and the question of which book the show is drawn from has emerged as a focal point of the discussion around it.

The series is scheduled to premiere on Netflix on December 4, with Khan and Mahira Khan in the lead roles.
