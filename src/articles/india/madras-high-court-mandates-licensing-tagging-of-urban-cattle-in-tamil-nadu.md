The Madras High Court has mandated that all cattle residing in urban zones of Tamil Nadu must obtain mandatory licensing, be tagged, and face strict penalties to curb the increasing number of accidents involving livestock on city roads.

This order targets the growing road menace caused by unregistered cattle, aiming to lower motor accident rates, protect public welfare, and improve traffic safety throughout the state.

Violators will incur penalties as the court enforces compliance, with the measure expected to bring measurable reductions in cattle‑related traffic incidents and prevent fatalities significantly.
