India’s 2026 southwest monsoon concluded with rainfall 13 percent below normal, a shortfall linked to the intensified El Nino that has persisted over the Indian Ocean this year.

The deficit has lowered reservoir levels and reduced water for irrigation, prompting a decline in crop yields across several states, according to agricultural monitors.

Meteorologists warn the same El Nino pattern could modify the country’s winter weather, though precise effects remain uncertain.
