Three individuals, two of them women, were arrested on suspicion of involvement in the gang‑rape of a 22‑year‑old woman in Delhi’s Preet Vihar area.

Police say eight people, including the victim, met at a Preet Vihar café before the assault; after the gathering, two male suspects allegedly attacked the woman outside, and she had known the accused, which prompted her to join the group.

Police continue their search, with investigations ongoing to apprehend the remaining accused.
