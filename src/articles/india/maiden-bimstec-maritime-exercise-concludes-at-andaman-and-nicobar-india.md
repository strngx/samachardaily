The maiden BIMSTEC Maritime Security Exercise concluded on November 3 at Sri Vijaya Puram in the Andaman and Nicobar Islands, the Ministry of Defence said. Harbour and sea-phase operations ended after five days of coordinated activities under the Andaman and Nicobar Command.

Participating naval vessels included India’s INS Taragiri, INS Karmuk and INS Saryu, Myanmar’s UMS King Aung Zeya and Sri Lanka’s SLNS Gajabahu. Senior representatives such as BIMSTEC Secretary General Indra Mani Pandey attended the harbour phase engagements.

The exercise underscored a shared commitment to maritime security and stability across the Bay of Bengal. Officials said it marks an important step toward institutionalising greater maritime engagement among BIMSTEC member states.
