President Droupadi Murmu hoisted the flag at Nashik's Ramkund while Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis led the parallel ceremony at Trimbakeshwar's Kushavarta Tirtha, formally inaugurating the 2027 Simhastha Kumbh Mela on Friday.

Separately, the INDIA opposition alliance announced a November 1 rally at Delhi's Ramlila Maidan to challenge the Election Commission over alleged vote theft, while the Bombay High Court admitted a petition on the Gokul Milk Union chairperson election, issuing notices to 12 Mahayuti-affiliated directors for a Monday hearing.
