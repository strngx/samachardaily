Nike has unveiled its Full Stride Ahead strategy, a comprehensive plan that will overhaul its supply chain, regional operating structure and workforce, signaling a major shift in how the sportswear giant operates worldwide.

The overhaul will modify how Nike sources materials, manages regional operations and structures its employee base to enhance agility, aiming to streamline procurement and improve responsiveness.

The new India capability centre is integral to this transformation, serving as a hub for growth initiatives and operational innovation across the company, positioning India as a key node in Nike’s global expansion.
