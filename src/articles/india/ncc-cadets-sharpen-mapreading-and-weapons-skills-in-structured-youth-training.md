NCC cadets are sharpening map‑reading and weapons‑handling skills, part of the corps’ structured training program that runs alongside academic studies. The exercises involve practical navigation drills and safe firearms handling under supervision, reinforcing the organisation’s emphasis on discipline and selfless service.

These activities reflect NCC’s broader aim to develop character, leadership and patriotism in young people, complementing its community‑service projects and adventure‑sport programmes. By integrating military‑oriented training with academic commitments, the corps seeks to produce well‑rounded citizens ready to contribute to the nation.
