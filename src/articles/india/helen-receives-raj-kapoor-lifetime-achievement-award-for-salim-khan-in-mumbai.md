Mumbai, September 10, 2026 – Veteran actress Helen stood on stage at the Raj Kapoor Lifetime Achievement Award ceremony and accepted the honour on behalf of her husband, celebrated screenwriter Salim Khan. In a brief media interaction after the event, Helen expressed gratitude to the film fraternity, noting that the award recognised Khan’s decades‑long contribution to Indian cinema.

Salim Khan, best known for co‑founding the prolific Salim‑Javed writing duo that scripted blockbusters such as "Sholay" and "Deewaar," has been a pivotal figure in shaping Bollywood narratives since the 1970s. The Raj Kapoor Lifetime Achievement Award, instituted by the Raj Kapoor Memorial Trust, is presented annually to individuals whose body of work reflects artistic excellence and enduring influence, mirroring the legacy of the legendary actor‑director Raj Kapoor.

The ceremony, organised by the Indian Film Association and attended by senior industry personalities, highlighted Khan’s recent projects, including his mentorship of emerging writers and his involvement in the screenplay of the 2025 hit "Mere Desh Ka Veer". While Khan was unable to attend due to health reasons, Helen’s presence underscored the personal dimension of the tribute, as she recounted shared memories of their early days in the industry.

Industry insiders noted that the award comes at a time when veteran creators are receiving renewed recognition amid a wave of nostalgia‑driven productions. The event also featured tributes to other stalwarts, reinforcing the sector’s emphasis on preserving cinematic heritage while fostering new talent.

Helen concluded her remarks by urging the younger generation to study classic scripts and uphold the storytelling standards set by pioneers like Raj Kapoor and Salim Khan. She thanked the organisers, fellow artists, and the audience for celebrating a career that has spanned over five decades.
