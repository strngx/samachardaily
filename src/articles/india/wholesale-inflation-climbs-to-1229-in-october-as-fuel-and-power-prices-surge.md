The Wholesale Price Index, with 2022-23 as the base year, rose to 113.3 in October from 112.5 in September, official data from the Ministry of Commerce & Industry showed. Fuel and power inflation jumped to 32.84% from 25.85%, making the category the largest contributor to the increase.

Primary articles inflation edged up to 9.24% from 8.67%, while manufactured products eased to 9.54% from 9.76%. The WPI Food Index cooled marginally to 8.58% from 8.66%, even as food price pressures stayed elevated.

The government retained August’s final WPI inflation reading at 9.92%, with the final index at 110.8. The October provisional WPI was compiled with a weighted response rate of 81.1%.
