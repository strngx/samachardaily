The National Testing Agency (NTA) has launched a permanent pool of independent observers and initiated a comprehensive overhaul of its examination system. The move follows recent security breaches and allegations of irregularities reported by the Press Trust of India.

NTA says the new observer pool, drawn from shortlisted centres, will enhance the integrity and reliability of its tests. The agency aims to strengthen oversight and restore confidence in the fairness of its assessments.

This reform is presented as a direct response to past lapses and is intended to safeguard the credibility of India's major testing framework. No further implementation details or timelines have been disclosed yet.
