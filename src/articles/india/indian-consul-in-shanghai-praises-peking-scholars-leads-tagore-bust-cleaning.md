The Consulate General of India in Shanghai held a special academic engagement felicitating faculty members and researchers from Peking University’s Department of South Asian Studies, commending the institution’s sustained contributions to Indology and cross-cultural scholarship.

During the diplomatic interaction, the Indian Consul General praised Chinese scholars for their dedicated work in translating classical Sanskrit treatises, Buddhist philosophical canons, and modern Indian literature into Mandarin. The envoy noted that academic collaboration, scholarly research exchanges, and linguistic translation projects provide essential intellectual foundations for fostering deeper mutual understanding and cultural dialogue between the peoples of India and China.

In conjunction with the academic gathering, the Consulate led a community volunteer maintenance drive at the prominent bronze bust of Rabindranath Tagore located in Shanghai. The commemorative preservation effort honored the legacy of the Nobel laureate's landmark 1924 cultural lecture tour across Chinese cities—including Shanghai, Hangzhou, and Beijing—which established enduring literary connections between the intellectual communities of both nations.

Additionally, consular officials and staff conducted an on-site inspection and facility improvement drive across the Indian Visa Application Centres in Shanghai. The initiative focused on optimizing visitor processing areas, enhancing public assistance infrastructure, and ensuring seamless service delivery for business travelers, tourists, and academic delegates applying for Indian visas.
