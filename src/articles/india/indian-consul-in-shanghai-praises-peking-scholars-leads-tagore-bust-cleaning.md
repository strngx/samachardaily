India's Consul General in Shanghai praised scholars from Peking University for their contributions to India studies, noting the value of academic collaboration between the two countries.

The consul also organized a cleaning drive that targeted a Tagore bust and the city's visa centres, aiming to maintain the appearance and functionality of these cultural and consular sites.
