More than 1.25 crore rural women now lead enterprises across Uttar Pradesh, an official progress review released on November 22, 2026, by the Uttar Pradesh State Rural Livelihoods Mission (UPSRLM) said. The mission has organized 11.97 lakh Self-Help Groups, linking 1.39 crore families to sustainable livelihoods.

Institutional credit underpins the shift: ₹13,346 crore in bank loans to over 11.22 lakh SHGs, plus ₹1,393 crore in Revolving Funds and ₹8,659 crore in Community Investment Funds over nine years. Separately, 40,048 active Banking Correspondent Sakhis facilitated digital transactions exceeding ₹48,000 crore.

Women also lead dairy procurement across 31 districts, collecting over 13 lakh liters of milk daily and helping 76,000-plus become Lakhpati Didis. District SHG brands include Vindhya Prerna, Son Prerna, and Kashi Prerana.
