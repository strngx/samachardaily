The Enforcement Directorate (ED) recovered ₹32 lakh in unaccounted cash, around 200.5 grams of gold and 12 luxury watches during searches linked to alleged irregularities in the 2016 Uttarakhand Subordinate Service Selection Commission (UKSSSC) Gram Panchayat Vikas Adhikari examination. The valuables were recovered from the Dehradun residence of Chandan Singh Jeena, who is currently associated with former Uttarakhand Chief Minister and Congress leader Harish Rawat.

The searches, conducted on September 10, 2026, were part of a money-laundering investigation arising from allegations of manipulation in the recruitment examination. A day after the searches, Rawat announced that he would step down from two organisational positions in the Congress, saying he did not want the controversy to weaken the party's political campaign against corruption. The case remains an investigation, and the ED's allegations have not been established as findings of guilt.

## What Happened in the ED Searches

The ED said its September 10 searches covered multiple locations in Dehradun connected with the 2016 UKSSSC Gram Panchayat Vikas Adhikari examination. The premises searched included those associated with former UKSSSC officials, a private computer agency involved in processing examination material and people whom investigators suspect were linked to the alleged irregularities.

One of the searches took place at the residence of Chandan Singh Jeena. According to the ED, investigators recovered ₹32 lakh in unaccounted Indian currency, gold coins and chains weighing approximately 200.5 grams, and 12 luxury wristwatches.

The watches included brands such as Rolex, Rado, Piaget, Movado, Tissot and Casio Edifice, according to reports citing the agency's statement. The ED also said bank accounts belonging to Jeena and members of his family, containing an aggregate balance of about ₹52.16 lakh, were frozen under the Prevention of Money Laundering Act.

The agency estimated the total value of assets seized or frozen at approximately ₹1.28 crore, excluding the valuation of the luxury watches. Jeena's mobile phone and other digital devices were also seized for forensic examination.

## Harish Rawat Steps Down From Congress Posts

The ED action quickly developed into a political issue because of Jeena's long association with Harish Rawat.

Jeena currently serves as Rawat's personal assistant and had earlier worked as an Officer on Special Duty (OSD) when Rawat was Uttarakhand chief minister between 2014 and 2017. Rawat subsequently announced his decision to step down from the executive of the Uttarakhand Pradesh Congress Committee and from his position as a permanent invitee to the Congress Working Committee.

Rawat said he did not want anything connected with him to weaken the Congress's campaign against corruption. His decision was therefore presented as a political and organisational step rather than a resignation from the Congress party itself. 

There was some initial confusion over the wording of Rawat's announcement. Reports on September 12 differed on whether he had immediately resigned from the two positions or had offered to do so. Rawat later clarified that he was not leaving the Congress and that his decision concerned organisational posts, not membership of the party. 

## What the ED Investigation Is About

The underlying case relates to alleged irregularities in the 2016 Gram Panchayat Vikas Adhikari examination conducted by the UKSSSC.

According to the ED, its investigation is based on four FIRs registered by the Uttarakhand Police and Vigilance Bureau. The agency alleges that OMR answer sheets were diverted from secure custody and taken to the residence of the commission's then secretary.

The ED further alleges that information from under-filled OMR sheets was passed to personnel connected with a private computer agency. Investigators allege that answer bubbles were subsequently filled or altered before the sheets were resealed, with illegal gratification allegedly routed through middlemen.

These are allegations forming part of an ongoing investigation. The recovery of cash, gold and watches from a person connected to the case does not by itself establish that the valuables were proceeds of the alleged examination irregularities.

The distinction is important because the investigation involves multiple people, locations and alleged activities. The ED's search and seizure actions are investigative measures under the Prevention of Money Laundering Act and are not equivalent to a final judicial determination of guilt.

## Who Is Chandan Singh Jeena?

Chandan Singh Jeena is significant to the case because of his professional association with Harish Rawat.

Jeena served as an OSD to Rawat during his tenure as Uttarakhand chief minister from 2014 to 2017. He later continued to be associated with Rawat as his personal assistant.

The ED's September searches were not limited to Jeena's residence. The agency also searched premises linked to former UKSSSC officials, a private computer agency owner and other people allegedly connected with the examination case.

The investigation is therefore broader than the relationship between Rawat and Jeena. The central question for investigators is how the alleged examination manipulation was carried out, who may have participated and whether money or assets were generated through the alleged activity. 
## What Was Recovered From Jeena's Residence?

The ED's reported recoveries provide some of the clearest confirmed details from the search.

- ₹32 lakh in unaccounted cash
- Approximately 200.5 grams of gold in coins and chains
- 12 luxury wristwatches
- Bank balances of about ₹52.16 lakh in accounts belonging to Jeena and family members
- Mobile and other digital devices for forensic examination

Reports citing the ED said the gold included 13 coins and seven chains. The watches included luxury brands such as Rolex, Rado, Piaget, Movado and Tissot. The ED valued the seized and frozen assets at approximately ₹1.28 crore, excluding the luxury watches.

The value of the watches was not included in the ₹1.28 crore figure cited by the agency. Their precise valuation therefore requires separate assessment.

## Rawat's Position on the Allegations

Rawat has defended Jeena while simultaneously saying he does not want the controversy to affect the Congress's political campaign.

Reports on his response said Rawat expressed disbelief in the allegations that his aide had manipulated OMR answer sheets. At the same time, he said that if wrongdoing were ultimately established, appropriate action should follow.

Rawat's response also connected the episode with the wider political debate over corruption and recruitment examinations. His decision to give up the two organisational posts was framed around preventing the controversy from weakening the Congress's position on those issues. 

The political context is significant because Uttarakhand is due to hold its next Assembly election in 2027. The ED action therefore came at a time when parties are preparing for the next electoral contest, increasing the political attention around a case involving a former chief minister and a close associate.

## Why the 2016 Recruitment Examination Matters

Recruitment examinations are politically sensitive in Uttarakhand because they determine access to government employment and affect large numbers of young candidates.

The 2016 UKSSSC examination at the centre of the investigation was for the post of Gram Panchayat Vikas Adhikari. The ED's case focuses on allegations that examination material was improperly accessed and manipulated.

For investigators, the alleged movement and alteration of OMR sheets is important because these sheets are used to record candidates' answers and calculate examination results. Any deliberate alteration after an examination could potentially affect the fairness of the recruitment process.

The ED's investigation is intended to establish the financial trail associated with the alleged irregularities. That includes identifying transactions, people involved, assets and any possible links between alleged unlawful activity and money or property.

## Political Impact in Uttarakhand

Harish Rawat remains a prominent Congress figure in Uttarakhand, making his decision to leave the two organisational positions politically significant even though he did not leave the party.

The episode places attention on the Congress's handling of allegations involving people who have worked closely with senior leaders. Rawat has argued that his withdrawal from the posts is intended to ensure that the party's campaign is not weakened by the controversy.

At the same time, the matter has given political opponents an opportunity to raise questions about the relationship between political figures and people associated with them. Those questions remain part of the political debate and should be distinguished from facts established through investigation or court proceedings.

The issue also comes as Uttarakhand approaches the 2027 Assembly election cycle. The case could therefore remain politically relevant as the investigation develops and parties debate corruption, recruitment examinations and accountability.

## What the Investigation Could Establish

The next stage of the investigation will focus on evidence collected during the searches and the financial and digital information obtained from the premises.

The seized mobile phone and other digital devices could be examined for information relevant to the investigation. Bank accounts frozen by the agency may also help investigators examine financial transactions and establish whether there are links between individuals, payments and assets.

The broader examination case will require investigators to determine whether the alleged OMR manipulation occurred, who was involved and whether illegal payments were made. Any eventual prosecution would have to be supported by evidence and would remain subject to judicial proceedings.

The recovery of assets alone does not establish their source. Their connection, if any, to the alleged recruitment irregularities is a matter for investigators and, ultimately where applicable, the courts to determine.

## What We Do Not Yet Know

Several questions remain open.

It is not yet established through a final court finding whether the cash, gold or watches recovered from Jeena's premises were proceeds of the alleged examination irregularities. The ED has described the cash as unaccounted and has linked the searches to its money-laundering investigation, but the precise origin and ownership of individual assets will need to be established through evidence.

It is also not established that Harish Rawat was involved in the alleged manipulation of examination material. The ED's search at the premises of his aide and Rawat's subsequent political decision are separate from a finding of personal criminal liability.

Further investigation, forensic examination of seized devices, financial analysis and any subsequent legal proceedings will determine how the case develops.

For now, the confirmed political development is that Rawat has sought to step away from his two Congress organisational positions while remaining associated with the party. The investigation into the alleged 2016 recruitment examination irregularities continues.
