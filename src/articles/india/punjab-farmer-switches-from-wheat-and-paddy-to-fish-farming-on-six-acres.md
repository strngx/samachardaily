Navjotpal Singh, a farmer from Ferozepur in Punjab, transitioned from wheat and paddy to fish farming six years ago on six acres of land.

He now manages six acres of fish ponds, produces around 15 tonnes of fish annually, and reports annual returns of up to Rs 2.25 lakh per acre.

Singh adopted scientific practices with support from Guru Angad Dev Veterinary and Animal Sciences University, choosing fish varieties based on profitability and local conditions as interest in aquaculture increases.
