Former Minister and MLA Surjit Singh Slathia and BJP J&K Vice President Adv. Rekha Mahajan criticized the opposition for what they termed a misinformation campaign surrounding the Special Intensive Revision (SIR) of electoral rolls. The remarks were made during an intellectual meeting on SIR in Samba.

Slathia emphasized that electoral roll revision was a routine and necessary process to ensure the accuracy of voter lists, countering opposition claims that the exercise aimed to disenfranchise voters. He urged the public to rely on official communications rather than speculative narratives.

Mahajan supported Slathia's stance, accusing opposition parties of deliberately distorting facts to create unrest ahead of the revision process. She reiterated the government's commitment to conducting free and fair elections through transparent electoral mechanisms.
