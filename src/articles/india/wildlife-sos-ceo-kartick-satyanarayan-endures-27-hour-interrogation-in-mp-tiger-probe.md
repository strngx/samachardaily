Wildlife SOS co‑founder and CEO Kartick Satyanarayan completed 27 hours of interrogation over three consecutive days in Shivpuri, Madhya Pradesh, following a Supreme Court order dated September 21, 2026, with sessions held from September 28 to September 30, each lasting more than nine hours.

The Supreme Court directive required the STSF to conduct a thorough inquiry into alleged irregularities concerning tiger conservation and related financial matters, prompting the extended questioning.

Satyanarayan’s three‑day testimony, spanning over 27 hours, underscores the seriousness of the investigation and the agency’s commitment to address the reported concerns.
