The makers of ace director Mani Ratnam’s upcoming film ‘Inji Vellam’ announced a January 29 release date for next year. The announcement was made on Wednesday by the production team in Chennai, according to IANS.

The entertainer stars Vijay Sethupathi and Sai Pallavi in leading roles, fueling high audience anticipation. ‘Inji Vellam’ is billed as one of the most eagerly awaited projects of the year, highlighting the star power behind the production.

The film marks another collaboration between Ratnam and the lead actors, reinforcing his reputation for delivering major cinematic events. It joins his roster of high‑profile releases that consistently draw attention across the industry.
