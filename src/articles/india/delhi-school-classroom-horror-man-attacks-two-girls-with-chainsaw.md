A man entered a classroom at a local school on the outskirts of Delhi during lessons and attacked two girls with a chainsaw, according to reports. The violent episode unfolded in the school setting, raising alarm among those present.

Witnesses said the assailant stormed into the classroom, triggering chaos and panic among students and teachers. The sudden intrusion and use of a chainsaw created an emergency situation that required immediate response.

Authorities have launched an investigation into the incident, though no further details on injuries, arrests, or motive have been released. The school remains sealed as officials gather evidence.
