Mumbai opened Tuesday under bright, cool skies and light winds, with daytime temperatures around 26 degrees Celsius. Within hours, thick smog drifted eastward, coating the main freeway, Dadar and Worli neighborhoods. Residents soon spotted haze lingering over streets and highways.

Citywide monitoring recorded an AQI of 158, which falls into the Poor rating. Smoke visibility was reported across Wadala, Dadar and Worli, leading officials to issue warnings about respiratory exposure.

Authorities expect the haze to linger through the week, urging people to stay indoors and wear masks during peak pollution periods.
