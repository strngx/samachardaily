India’s Advanced Medium Combat Aircraft (AMCA) programme, aimed at fielding a fifth‑generation stealth fighter, has moved into the prototype stage after three Indian contenders formally lodged bids for development work.

A high‑level review committee created by the Ministry of Defence has begun assessing the submissions, marking the first formal evaluation of the design and technology proposals.

The assessment represents a milestone in India’s drive for an indigenous advanced combat aircraft.
