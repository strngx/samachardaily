The Municipal Corporation of Delhi (MCD) issued 10,367 challans and imposed fines exceeding Rs 1.11 crore between November 2 and 9 for violations of pollution‑control norms. The enforcement came as the capital’s air quality deteriorated under the Graded Response Action Plan (GRAP), prompting stricter monitoring of emissions from vehicles, industries and construction sites.

MCD officials said the penalties target illegal diesel buses, smoky factories and other sources that breach clean‑air rules. The action underscores the civic body’s effort to curb smog‑forming pollutants amid rising public health concerns. No further details on specific violators were released.
