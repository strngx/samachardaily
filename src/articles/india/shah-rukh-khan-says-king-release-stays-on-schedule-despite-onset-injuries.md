Shah Rukh Khan publicly confirmed that his forthcoming film *King* will proceed on its original release schedule, addressing rumours of a possible postponement following reports of on-set injuries sustained during filming.

## What Shah Rukh Khan said

According to trade reporting by Bollywood Hungama and corroborating coverage in the Times of India, Khan dismissed postponement rumours in a public statement, confirming that the injuries he sustained during the *King* shoot were minor and would not alter the film's planned release date.

*King* is directed by **Sujoy Ghosh**, known for his work on *Kahaani* and *Badla*, and is described in trade coverage as an action thriller.

## Background

On-set injury reports in Bollywood frequently generate speculation about production delays, particularly for high-budget productions with complex action sequences. *King* reportedly involves extensive stunt work, which may have contributed to the minor injuries Khan disclosed.

## Why it matters

Shah Rukh Khan's direct public confirmation serves to stabilise anticipation for one of the most commercially anticipated Hindi films in the pipeline. For box-office analysts and film exhibitors who plan screen allocation months in advance, clarity on the release timeline is commercially significant.
