Aam Aadmi Party leaders organized a protest march in Delhi to denounce alleged widespread vote theft and irregularities in the Election Commission’s Special Intensive Revision (SIR) process. Police intervened and blocked the demonstration, preventing the activists from proceeding with their rally. The protest highlighted concerns over systematic disenfranchisement that the party claims is occurring during the ongoing electoral revisions.

Senior leader Saurabh Bhardwaj accused authorities of orchestrating voter deletions on a large scale, describing the practice as outright fraud. He labeled the extent of the alleged disenfranchisement as a coordinated effort to manipulate the electoral outcome. The AAP’s actions underscore growing tensions between the party and election officials over transparency in the SIR process.
