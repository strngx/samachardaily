Belarusian Foreign Minister Maxim Ryzhenkov touched down in New Delhi on Sunday, Nov 15, on an official visit. He will co‑chair the 12th India‑Belarus Intergovernmental Commission alongside Commerce Minister Jitin Prasada, focusing on trade, science and cultural cooperation.

The commission meeting is set for Monday at Vanijya Bhavan, after which Ryzhenkov will hold a round‑table with Confederation of Indian Industry leaders. He will also meet Minister of State for External Affairs Kirti Vardhan Singh on Tuesday before departing on Wednesday, Nov 18.

The trip follows a September meeting with External Affairs Minister S. Jaishankar in New York and New Delhi, highlighting the warm and expanding bilateral relationship.
