The brutal killing of a young woman in West Delhi has shocked the city, prompting a detailed police investigation that has uncovered a disturbing sequence of events leading up to her death. According to Delhi police, the victim, identified as Muskan, was found dead in her apartment in early 2024. The case quickly escalated into a high‑profile homicide inquiry after her family reported that she had been missing for several days before her body was discovered. Investigators have pieced together a timeline that points to a pattern of personal turmoil, substance abuse and alleged domestic violence that preceded the fatal incident.

Muskan was in the process of building a career as a social media content creator while simultaneously pursuing a Bachelor of Arts degree through Delhi University's School of Open Learning. An officer familiar with the case said she met a man named Imran in 2024, and the two began a relationship that soon turned abusive. Police records indicate that Muskan attempted suicide in the months before her murder, an act that was reportedly linked to escalating emotional distress and the influence of illicit drugs. Friends and acquaintances noted a sudden change in her demeanor, describing her as increasingly withdrawn and dependent on substances, which they feared were being supplied by Imran.

The investigation has revealed that Imran, who is now a fugitive, is alleged to have been the primary catalyst for the abuse, drug supply and the circumstances that culminated in Muskan's death. Police have issued a lookout notice for Imran and are urging the public to provide any information that could lead to his arrest. The case remains under active investigation, with forensic teams examining the crime scene, digital footprints and mobile records to establish a comprehensive picture of the events that led to the tragedy.

## Why It Matters

The case underscores the intersecting challenges of gender‑based violence, mental‑health crises, and substance‑abuse among young Indian women, especially those navigating the pressures of digital fame and academic commitments. Muskan's story highlights how abusive relationships can exacerbate vulnerability, often pushing victims toward self‑harm and making them targets of further violence. It also raises questions about the adequacy of support systems for students and content creators who may lack traditional safety nets.

Law enforcement agencies are under pressure to not only bring the alleged perpetrator to justice but also to address the broader systemic gaps that allow such patterns of abuse to go unchecked. The ongoing investigation may prompt calls for stricter monitoring of online harassment, improved counseling services within educational institutions, and more robust mechanisms for reporting and responding to domestic abuse. As the search for Imran continues, the case serves as a stark reminder of the urgent need for coordinated societal and governmental action to protect vulnerable individuals.
