Zuniga, an outsider in the field, won the General Rajendrasinhji Million at Pune Racecourse on Thursday, prevailing by a short head over Rampage. The horse took the lead in the final 50 metres, surging ahead to secure the victory in the closing stages of the race.

A bumping incident between the leading horses prompted scrutiny from officials, though no objection was raised, while Noraiah's poor start eliminated any chance of a competitive finish. Stewards reviewed the incident but did not file a protest. The result was allowed to stand after the review.
