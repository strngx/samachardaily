Mumbai police have arrested Yash Singh, a 20‑year‑old from Airoli in Navi Mumbai, after the Navi Mumbai Anti‑Terror Squad tied him to reconnaissance of sensitive locations in the city. The ATS conducted the investigation that resulted in his apprehension.

The arrest comes after bomb‑threat emails were sent to the Mumbai airport and the mayor’s office, leading security agencies to tighten surveillance across the city to deter any further terrorist activity.

Officials said Singh was acting on directions from external criminals, and the heightened security is meant to safeguard public venues in the metropolitan area.
