Nearly 19% of Mumbai's population remains without sewerage access, even as the city's existing network covers 85% of its geographical area. The Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation (BMC) is moving to bridge this critical infrastructure deficit by planning a massive network expansion.

The civic body plans to appoint consultants to prepare a Detailed Project Report (DPR) targeting gaps in slums and institutional colonies. To execute this, twelve consulting agencies have already been shortlisted to design the expansion and strengthening of the city's sewerage system.
