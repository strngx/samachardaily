Prime Minister Narendra Modi chaired the 55th PRAGATI meeting at Seva Teerth in New Delhi on November 26, reviewing five road, power, coal and railway projects worth over Rs 78,000 crore. He said delays postpone benefits for citizens, businesses and the economy beyond cost escalation.

He urged ministries and state governments to resolve pending issues with clear responsibility and realistic timelines, while maintaining high construction quality. Modi stressed outcomes over outlays for the road project and timely, dignified rehabilitation and resettlement for the coal project.

He reviewed ITI ecosystem and PM-SETU, asking PSUs to assess future manpower needs and strengthen ITI linkages. Modi suggested decentralised solar and wind projects near consumption centres to improve affordable power and ease transmission pressure.
