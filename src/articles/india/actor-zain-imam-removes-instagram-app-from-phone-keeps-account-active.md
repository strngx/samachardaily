Mumbai, Sep 11 – Television actor Zain Imam announced that he has removed the Instagram application from his mobile device.

He clarified that the decision does not involve deactivating his Instagram profile; he intends to continue maintaining the account despite the app’s absence on his phone.

Imam added, “There’s a rather beautiful life happening beyond screens,” suggesting a personal shift toward offline experiences.
