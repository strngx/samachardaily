Karnataka open their Syed Mushtaq Ali Trophy campaign against Bengal in an Elite Group A match at Wankhede Stadium in Mumbai. The fixture is the tournament opener for the state side in Group A.

The new-look Karnataka side is seeking redemption after missing the knockouts for three consecutive years. A revamped squad takes the field at Wankhede Stadium hoping to change that record.

Bengal stands as the first opponent in Karnataka's attempt to return to the knockout stage of the competition.
