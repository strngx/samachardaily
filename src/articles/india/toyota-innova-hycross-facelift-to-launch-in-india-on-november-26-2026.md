Toyota has confirmed that the Innova Hycross facelift will be launched in India on November 26, 2026. The launch will introduce the updated multi-purpose vehicle to the market. The launch date was announced by Toyota.

Prototypes of the facelifted model have been observed on multiple occasions, indicating that the vehicle is undergoing final testing and validation before its official debut. These sightings indicate that the model is nearing production.

The new version is expected to come equipped with a flex-fuel strong-hybrid powertrain. The vehicle will be offered with this new powertrain.
