The Railway Protection Force stepped up Deepavali vigilance across Chennai, apprehending five individuals carrying firecrackers at Chennai Central and Egmore stations. The arrests were part of an intensified safety drive aimed at preventing fire-related incidents on the railway network during the festive season.

In parallel, the RPF fined nine people for trespassing on tracks during the same awareness campaign. The actions underscore the force’s focus on maintaining order and safety for passengers and railway operations amid heightened festival activity.

The awareness drive also highlighted the importance of compliance with railway rules during the festival, reinforcing safety protocols for passengers and rail operations.
