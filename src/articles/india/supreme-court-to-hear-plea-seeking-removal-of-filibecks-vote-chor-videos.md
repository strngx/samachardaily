The Supreme Court is set to take up a plea over Giacomo Filibeck's 'Vote Chor' remark. At the heart of the petition is a demand to remove videos that carry the comments attributed to Filibeck.

The plea presses on two fronts. Alongside the video takedown, it seeks a Special Investigation Team (SIT) probe into the participation of foreign delegates at a Hyderabad event.

The petitioner alleges that the delegates' involvement amounted to interference in India's domestic political affairs, the concern that anchors the request for a formal investigation.
