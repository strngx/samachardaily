Venture Intelligence data shared with The Economic Times shows that deeptech startups in India raised $574 million from 61 transactions during calendar year 2026.

That amount represents almost 84% of the $679 million raised across all 94 venture‑capital deals in the country for the same period, highlighting deeptech’s dominance in the funding landscape.

Among the notable rounds, satellite‑imaging firm Pixxel attracted $100 million, while aerospace startup Skyroot secured $60 million, the latter propelling it to become India’s first spacetech unicorn.
