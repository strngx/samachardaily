Indian deeptech startups secured $574 million across 61 transactions during the 2026 calendar year, according to venture funding data compiled by Venture Intelligence and reported by The Economic Times.

Seed and Series A funding rounds accounted for 84% of total capital deployed into the sector, demonstrating institutional investor focus on early-stage intellectual property development. Investment activity concentrated heavily in artificial intelligence infrastructure, space technology, semiconductor fabrication design, quantum computing components, and advanced robotics.

To evaluate complex technical architectures and longer development horizons, mainstream venture capital firms have increasingly formed strategic partnerships with specialized technical advisory networks and global domain experts. This collaborative evaluation model allows generalist funds to assess scientific viability and patent portfolios before leading multi-million-dollar funding rounds.

Industry analysts observe that while consumer software platforms face moderating private market valuations, deeptech ventures are securing premium valuations based on defensible proprietary research, government manufacturing incentives, and cross-border commercial applications across aerospace, defense, and industrial automation.
