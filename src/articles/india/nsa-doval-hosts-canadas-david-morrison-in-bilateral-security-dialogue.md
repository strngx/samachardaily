During a bilateral security dialogue, India's National Security Advisor Ajit Doval hosted Canadian Foreign Minister David Morrison. The two leaders reviewed progress in law enforcement cooperation and jointly identified priority areas for enhanced collaboration between New Delhi and Ottawa.

The session focused on operationalizing shared security objectives. The discussions aimed at strengthening the India-Canada partnership through coordinated security measures. Canadian Foreign Minister Anita Anand is scheduled to visit India later this month to continue the collaborative effort and address outstanding matters from the meeting. This encounter underscores the growing strategic alignment between the two nations on defense and intelligence matters.
