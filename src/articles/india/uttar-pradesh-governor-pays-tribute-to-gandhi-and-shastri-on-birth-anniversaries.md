On Friday, Uttar Pradesh Governor Anandiben Patel paid tribute to Mahatma Gandhi and former Prime Minister Lal Bahadur Shastri on their birth anniversaries in Lucknow, and she said their lives inspire truth, simplicity and national service.

The tribute, which was offered on the occasion of their birth anniversaries, highlighted the enduring values of these leaders and their continued relevance for the nation, emphasizing the importance of following their example.

She also noted that the ideals of Gandhi and Shastri continue to guide many, and the tribute served as a reminder of their lasting contributions to the country.
