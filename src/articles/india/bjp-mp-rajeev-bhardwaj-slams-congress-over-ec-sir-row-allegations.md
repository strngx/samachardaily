Speaking at a press conference in Dharamshala, Bharatiya Janata Party Member of Parliament Rajeev Bhardwaj addressed ongoing allegations against the Election Commission concerning the SIR process. Bhardwaj emphasized the necessity of presenting verified facts directly to the public during the controversy.

Bhardwaj criticized the Congress party, claiming that its political footprint is shrinking. He alleged that the opposition party is resorting to targeted attacks against Prime Minister Narendra Modi and key constitutional bodies amid the dispute.
