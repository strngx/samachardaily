The Kerala High Court has raised concern over santhies allegedly violating pooja timings at the Vaikom Mahadeva temple in Kottayam district, directing the Travancore Devaswom Board (TDB) to take note. Justices Devan Ramachandran and Johnson John noted the breach despite an earlier undertaking by the Board.

The remarks followed an advocate commissioner's report saying poojas were conducted 'according to their whims and fancies', triggering several complaints. 'The report certainly requires to be noticed by the Devaswom Board immediately,' the bench said.

The report also flagged the accountant's post lying vacant since November 2022, with Rs 34 lakh misappropriation allegations, and the Kazhakam post empty for over eight years. It said unauthorised santhies were performing rituals. The Board assured the court it would examine the issues.
