Samajwadi Party chief Akhilesh Yadav said on Wednesday in Lucknow that Uttar Pradesh Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath’s chair is not safe, labeling the BJP as the real crisis.

He added that ordinary citizens, farmers and the younger generation will eventually take away the chief minister’s seat, signalling a direct challenge to the ruling party in the state.

Yadav made the remarks during a public statement in Lucknow, emphasizing that widespread discontent among agricultural communities and youth could translate into political action against the incumbent government.
