The Centre announced it will convene a special session of the Lok Sabha to push through the women’s quota constitutional amendment, aiming for a narrow 15‑18 vote lead.

Passage requires a two‑thirds majority, i.e., at least 361 votes out of the house’s 543‑seat strength. With three vacancies, the current membership stands at 540, but the government says it can secure the needed tally.

Officials said the expected margin reflects confidence in party discipline and coalition support, positioning the bill for approval in the upcoming session.
