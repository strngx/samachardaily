India has secured the top rank in the latest global business climate survey conducted jointly by Business Sweden and the Embassy of Sweden, outperforming more than 40 international markets evaluated in the study.

The survey findings reveal that 86% of Swedish companies operating in India view the local investment environment positively. Respondents cited key structural drivers, including sustained public infrastructure development, access to a deep pool of skilled engineering and technical talent, and expanding domestic consumer demand across urban and semi-urban centres.

A significant majority of surveyed enterprises confirmed plans to increase capital expenditure and scale their operational footprints in India over the upcoming financial year. These expansion initiatives span key industrial sectors such as automotive engineering, telecommunications, healthcare, and green transition technologies.

Business Sweden highlighted that Swedish multinational corporations are increasingly integrating their Indian subsidiaries into global supply chains and research networks, moving beyond commercial sales to establish localized manufacturing hubs and digital engineering centres.
