India and Ecuador concluded the sixth round of Foreign Office Consultations in New Delhi on Tuesday, with Secretary (East) Rudrendra Tandon and Ecuador's Deputy Foreign Minister Alejandro Davalos leading delegations that reviewed bilateral progress and exchanged views on regional and global developments.

During his visit, Davalos held separate meetings with the Commerce, Health, and Pharmaceuticals secretaries as well as Indian business forums, building on External Affairs Minister S. Jaishankar's September discussion with his Ecuadorian counterpart in New York on expanding economic cooperation.

Technology and pharmaceuticals anchor the partnership; India has trained 223 Ecuadorian professionals in five years while a Centre of Excellence in IT has skilled 813 more. Trade flows reflect complementarity — Ecuador supplies gold, oil and cocoa while importing Indian automobiles, two-wheelers and pharmaceuticals.
