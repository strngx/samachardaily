A Karnataka court has ordered the state government to draw up strict eligibility rules for chairpersons of additional committees that review applications to regularise unauthorised land occupation. The directive invokes Rule 108-EE(1)(a) of the Karnataka Land Revenue Rules.

The order follows a judicial direction not to appoint election losers to public posts. The new guidelines will set the criteria for who may head these land regularisation panels.

The state government must now prepare the screening framework, ensuring election losers are excluded from appointment to these public posts.
