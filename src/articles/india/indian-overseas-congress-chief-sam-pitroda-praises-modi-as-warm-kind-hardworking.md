Indian Overseas Congress chief Sam Pitroda praised Prime Minister Narendra Modi as a warm, kind, loving and caring person, calling him a good orator and a hardworking leader. Pitroda said he respects Modi as a person, considers him a Gujarati friend he has met with his wife, and emphasized that he has no personal issues with the prime minister, adding that he has nothing against him as a Gujarati leader, friend and well-wisher.

He added that any disagreements are ideological rather than personal, noting that he can disagree with Modi and still share a meal. Pitroda also recalled that in 2018 Sonia Gandhi described Modi as a big orator but criticized his speeches.
