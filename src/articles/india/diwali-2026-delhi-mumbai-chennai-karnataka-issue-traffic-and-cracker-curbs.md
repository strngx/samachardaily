As Diwali 2026 approaches, several Indian cities have issued traffic and safety advisories. The guidelines are intended to manage the expected increase in travel and to ensure public safety during the festival.

Delhi will restrict roads around Anand Vihar ISBT until 15 November. Mumbai has banned noisy crackers and laser lights, aiming to reduce noise and visual disturbance.

Karnataka has allowed only green crackers on specific days. Chennai has implemented traffic diversions, special buses and extra police to handle the anticipated congestion. These measures are intended to reduce congestion and improve safety on the roads.
