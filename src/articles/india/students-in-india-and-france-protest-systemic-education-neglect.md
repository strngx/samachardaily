Students in India and France are expressing anger over systemic failures and government neglect within their respective education sectors. While the protests occur in different regions, they share a common focus on the deterioration of educational infrastructure and policy.

Comparisons have been drawn between the CJP’s school campaign in India and student-led demonstrations in Europe. Although no direct link exists between these movements, both are driven by a shared frustration regarding the lack of state attention to academic issues.
