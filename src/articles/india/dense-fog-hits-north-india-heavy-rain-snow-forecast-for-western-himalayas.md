Dense fog blanketed northern India, sharply cutting visibility across major highways and airports. The western disturbance has also brought heavy rain and thunderstorms to Tamil Nadu, Puducherry and the Andaman Islands, while the Himalayan region braces for rain and snowfall.

Meteorologists warn the unsettled pattern will persist through the week, affecting travel and agriculture in the north and south. The fresh western disturbance is expected to intensify, delivering snow at higher elevations in the Himalayas and further rain in the southern peninsular coast.

The weather disruptions could delay flights and road traffic, while farmers in the south anticipate beneficial moisture after a dry spell. Authorities have issued advisories for commuters to exercise caution on fog‑affected routes.
