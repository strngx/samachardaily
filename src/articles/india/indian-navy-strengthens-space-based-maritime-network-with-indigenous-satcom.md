The Indian Navy is accelerating the development of an indigenous satellite communications system as part of a push to strengthen its space-based maritime network. The system is intended to support operational planning and coordination across maritime forces.

The move reflects a growing recognition that satellites are central to navigation, communications, intelligence collection, operational planning, and military coordination in an increasingly contested domain. This recognition underscores the strategic importance of space-based assets in modern naval warfare.

By focusing on indigenous technology, the navy seeks to enhance the resilience and security of its communications architecture while reducing reliance on external providers.
