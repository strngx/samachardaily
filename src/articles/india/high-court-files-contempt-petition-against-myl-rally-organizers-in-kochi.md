In Kochi, Kerala, a MYL rally ignited traffic chaos and drew a high-court legal push. Organisers now face a fresh contempt claim after authorities allege road blockage during the demonstration, citing recent police reports.

The Kerala High Court has filed a formal contempt petition targeting rally organisers and officials for disrupting traffic flow at the event, seeking injunctive relief and damages.

This suit could set a precedent on public order for large gatherings and forces authorities to address crowd management concerns. Further developments remain undisclosed.
