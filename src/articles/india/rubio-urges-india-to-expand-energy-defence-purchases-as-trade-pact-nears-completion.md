US Secretary of State Marco Rubio visited India for his second trip in less less than five months and held wide-ranging talks with External Affairs Minister S Jaishankar in Mumbai.

The discussions focused on trade, energy, technology, and defence cooperation as the two countries work toward finalizing a trade pact.

Despite ongoing strains over trade and immigration policies, the United States and India moved to strengthen cooperation in energy and defence.
