Defence Minister Rajnath Singh attended the multinational air combat exercise Tarang Shakti-2026, which was held in Jodhpur, where he declared that future military power will be defined by the speed of sensing, understanding, deciding and acting.

He stressed the growing importance of technological superiority, operational agility and indigenous defence production in shaping future military power, and pointed out that the exercise showcased a range of Indian Air Force capabilities.

The highlight of the event was a precision aerial display by the Sarang helicopter, and the report was filed by Indiandefensenews.
