In India, credit card default rates are declining as consumers grow more conscious of timely repayments. Many borrowers now approach credit card usage with greater caution, viewing these cards more like debit cards thanks to extended grace periods.

This shift in behavior is creating a dilemma for financial institutions. While default rates are dropping, spending continues to increase, leading banks to worry about declining interest income as they face a challenging environment for lending.
