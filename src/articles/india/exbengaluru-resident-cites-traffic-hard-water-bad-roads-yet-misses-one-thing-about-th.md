A woman who spent almost three years in Bengaluru told The Times of India that the city’s traffic congestion, hard water supply and deteriorating road conditions were daily frustrations during her stay.

Despite those difficulties, she said the one element of life in Bengaluru that she now misses the most became evident only after she left, underscoring how personal experiences shape city memories.
