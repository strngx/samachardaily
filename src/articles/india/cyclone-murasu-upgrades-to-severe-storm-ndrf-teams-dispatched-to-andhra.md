Cyclone Murasu has intensified into a severe cyclonic storm as it nears the Andhra coast, with forecasts of winds reaching 120 kmph and heavy rain expected to lash Chennai tonight.

The National Disaster Response Force has rushed teams from Chennai to Andhra for emergency support; Chennai flights are disrupted and Odisha officials have begun evacuations ahead of the storm's arrival.

Murasu is the first tropical cyclone to receive a Tamil name, marking a new naming milestone.
