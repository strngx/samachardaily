A day after taking over as the 29th Chief of the Air Staff, Air Chief Marshal Ashutosh Dixit unveiled his strategic vision for the Indian Air Force. His agenda emphasizes rebuilding fighter strength, accelerating indigenous aircraft development, and deepening integration with the Army and Navy.

He stressed rebuilding fighter strength, accelerating indigenous combat aircraft development, and deepening integration with the Army and Navy to modernise the force and enhance joint operations. This approach aims to modernise the force and improve operational readiness.

This focus on indigenous production and multi‑domain synergy aims to modernise the IAF’s combat capability, strengthen domestic defence manufacturing, and support national defence objectives.
