Prime Minister Narendra Modi chaired the 54th PRAGATI meeting at Seva Teerth, reviewing critical infrastructure projects worth more than ₹16,000 crore across 31 states and Union Territories.

The ICT-enabled multimodal interaction brought together top central and state government leadership to resolve inter-agency bottlenecks and accelerate welfare delivery.

PRAGATI, which stands for Pro-Active Governance and Timely Implementation, is used to coordinate agencies and unblock projects stalled between different levels of government.
