On Monday, Nov 2, the Supreme Court warned a petitioner against air travel and refused to list urgently a plea that claimed airfares had risen by almost 700 %.

The plea, presented by an advocate, sought immediate hearing over the alleged surge, but the bench declined to prioritize it, leaving the issue unresolved.

The court’s admonition came as the petitioner highlighted the steep price increase, though no further judicial action was ordered.
