R Ashwin has urged the Indian cricket team to show compassion toward Mohammed Siraj following the fast bowler's repeated exclusions from the playing eleven. The veteran off-spinner said Siraj requires emotional understanding and a guiding figure in the dressing room during this challenging period.

Ashwin suggested that a sports psychologist could help Siraj navigate his feelings after being left out multiple times. Despite the disappointment, Ashwin noted that Siraj has remained professional and continues to support his teammates from the sidelines.
