President Donald Trump participated in a Diwali celebration at the White House on Friday, marking the latest observance of the Indian festival on the executive residence.

India’s ambassador to the United States, Vinay Kwatra, was among the guests in the Oval Office, joining officials for a ceremony that has been held annually since President George W. Bush introduced the tradition in 2003.

The event underscores cultural diplomacy and reinforces the United States’ engagement with India, highlighting the importance of bilateral relations in the region.
