State-owned oil refiners in India are actively evaluating their crude procurement strategies in response to heightened maritime transit risks and logistical bottlenecks across key Middle Eastern shipping corridors.

Public sector refiners, including Indian Oil Corporation Limited, Bharat Petroleum Corporation Limited, and Hindustan Petroleum Corporation Limited, are reviewing operational inventories and assessing supply alternatives to mitigate potential cargo delays. While immediate domestic refined fuel supplies remain stable, refiners are closely tracking elevated maritime insurance premiums and volatile tanker freight rates for shipments transiting the Strait of Hormuz.

The Middle East remains a primary source of crude oil for Indian refineries, supplying over half of the country's overall petroleum imports. To maintain uninterrupted processing schedules at coastal and inland refining facilities, state refiners are coordinating with shipping logistics providers, reviewing long-term supply contracts, and assessing spot procurement options from alternative producing regions, including West Africa and the Americas.

Energy officials continue to coordinate with maritime security authorities to monitor vessel passage and ensure supply chain continuity for critical hydrocarbon infrastructure.
