Law enforcement officers in the capital have taken a minor into custody in connection with a fatal collision involving a high-speed luxury vehicle in Kishangarh.

The fatal event took place on October 4 near the red light directly opposite the local CNG pump. Preliminary investigations conducted by Delhi Police indicated that the driver lost control of the sedan as a result of rash and negligent driving, resulting in the victim's death.
