Union Railways Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw said on Friday that an analysis had been undertaken to identify the peak rush days of the ongoing festive season, in New Delhi. The analysis was carried out to pinpoint the busiest travel days.

Accordingly, special trains will be run on those identified peak days to accommodate the heightened travel demand during the holiday period, ensuring smoother journeys for commuters.

The special trains are being operated during the ongoing festive season, providing extra capacity for passengers traveling home for celebrations.
