Suvendu Adhikari attended a Kali Puja organised at Kalighat, where the host received him personally and openly backed him in front of those gathered at the religious event.

The host was Swapan Banerjee, the estranged brother of Mamata Banerjee, who greeted Adhikari with the words 'We are with you' and told him to 'keep moving forward.'

Swapan's decision to welcome Adhikari and voice support publicly placed a long-running political and personal rift within the Banerjee family on open display at a religious occasion.
