Nitesh Tiwari’s epic Ramayana opened in cinemas today, pulling in roughly Rs 44 crore and ranking as the fourth‑biggest opening of 2026.

The figure is notable because it was achieved on a regular weekday during the pre‑Diwali lull, whereas the three films ahead – Dhurandhar 2 (Rs 76 crore), Drishyam 3 (Rs 62 crore) and Toxic (Rs 53 crore) – all opened on holidays.

Analysts say the film’s true test will be the weekend and the Monday‑to‑Wednesday holiday stretch; sustained earnings will reveal its box‑office potential.
