Pregnant actress Samantha Ruth Prabhu was spotted glowing outside a Bandra salon in Mumbai, wearing a bright pink floral dress, and confirmed her pregnancy together with her husband Raj Nidimoru.

She released a motivating video showcasing her fitness routine while expecting and marked the occasion with a baby shower at Sadhguru's Isha Foundation in Coimbatore, filling the venue with cheers before her return to the spotlight, hoping to inspire healthy living for mothers everywhere.

Following delivery, Samantha will host Celebrity MasterChef Tamil, promising continued media presence and family celebration for fans.
