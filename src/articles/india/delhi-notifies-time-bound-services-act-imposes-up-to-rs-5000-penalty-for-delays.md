Delhi government led by Chief Minister Rekha Gupta notified the Delhi (Right of Citizens to Time-Bound and Easy Delivery of Services) Act, 2026 on November 9, 2026, in New Delhi, to ensure timely delivery of government services and fix accountability for unnecessary delays.

The legislation replaces the earlier time‑bound provisions and sets a penalty of up to Rs 5,000 for officials who exceed prescribed service timelines, aiming to curb chronic delays in public service delivery across the capital. The penalty applies to any official who fails to meet the stipulated time limits, reinforcing the government's commitment to efficient public service.
