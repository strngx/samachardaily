The India Meteorological Department has issued a yellow alert for October 28 across Sonbhadra, Mirzapur, Chandauli, Varanasi, Bhadohi, Ghazipur and Ballia as Cyclone Arnab approaches, forecasting winds of 30 to 40 kilometres per hour. An orange alert follows for October 29 with heavy rain and winds of 40 to 50 kilometres per hour.

State authorities have activated relief and rescue preparations, and the Relief Commissioner's Office has issued safety guidelines for the public and farmers ahead of the expected weather impact.

People have been told to take shelter in buildings, stay away from trees and electricity poles, and keep livestock safe. Daytime temperatures may fall by 5 to 8 degrees Celsius.
