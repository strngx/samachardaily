Hyderabad: On Thursday, BRS leaders marked three years since Rahul Gandhi visited Bawarchi restaurant in Hyderabad's Ashok Nagar. They staged a 'biryani protest' there, opposing the Congress government's promises of two lakh government jobs and a job calendar in Telangana. The BRS linked the demonstration to those employment assurances.

The gathering later saw a face-off that turned into a scuffle involving Congress and BRS workers at the restaurant. The clash followed the protest focused on jobs. The scuffle broke out during the protest, as Congress and BRS workers confronted each other.
