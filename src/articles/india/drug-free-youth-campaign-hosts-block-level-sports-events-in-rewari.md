Mera Yuva Bharat, Rewari, conducted a block-level sports competition at Harjipur village in the Khol block on November 14 and 15. The event was held under the guidance of the Union Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports.

These competitions are part of the 'Drug-Free Youth for a Developed India Campaign,' a broader 100-week initiative. Organizers stated that the primary objective of the sports events was to promote a drug-free lifestyle among the youth.
