The BJP on Wednesday sharply countered the Congress in Ahmedabad, comparing the opposition party to the Bollywood films "Chor Machaye Shor" and "Bunty Aur Babli" and accusing it of repeatedly undermining the Constitution during its tenure in power.

The ruling party's attack focused on the Congress's alleged record of subverting constitutional norms, framing the opposition's current stance as hypocritical. References to the 1974 and 2005 films were deployed to depict the Congress as disorderly and fraudulent.

The confrontation centers on disputes regarding the Constitution and the SIR, with both sides trading accusations amid heightened political tensions.
