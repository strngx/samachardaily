A Jana Sena Party (JSP) candidate has been elected chairman of the Tenali municipal council, securing the top post in the latest local election.

Congress contender Dara Ramakrishna received 1,528 votes, while independent Venkatesh Konatala garnered 1,597 votes, narrowly trailing the winner. BSP representative Mattalapudi Sambasiva Rao obtained 342 votes.

The JSP victory places the party in charge of Tenali’s municipal administration, influencing decisions on local services and development projects.
