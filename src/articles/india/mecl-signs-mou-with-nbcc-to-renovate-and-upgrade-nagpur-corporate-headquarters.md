On 11 September 2026, Mineral Exploration and Consultancy Limited (MECL) entered into a memorandum of understanding with NBCC (India) Limited to undertake a comprehensive overhaul of its corporate headquarters in Nagpur.

The agreement outlines NBCC’s responsibility for the repair, renovation and upgradation of the building, aiming to modernise facilities and improve the functional layout for MECL’s staff and operations.

NBCC, a government‑linked construction and engineering firm, will apply its expertise in large‑scale infrastructure projects to deliver the revamp, signalling a strategic partnership that could enhance MECL’s operational efficiency and corporate image.
