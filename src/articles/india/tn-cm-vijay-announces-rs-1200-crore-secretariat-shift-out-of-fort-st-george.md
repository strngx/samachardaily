In a major administrative overhaul, Tamil Nadu Chief Minister C. Joseph Vijay has officially announced plans for a modern, integrated Secretariat and Legislative Assembly complex in Chennai. The ambitious state project carries a projected outlay of Rs 1,200 crore and will relocate the core seat of executive governance and lawmaking away from the historic Fort St George premises.

The decision to shift out of Fort St George comes as part of a strategic push to streamline public administration and upgrade state infrastructure. The colonial-era fort has served as the administrative epicenter of Tamil Nadu for generations, but its age, spatial limitations, and heritage status have created significant logistical challenges for accommodating expanded bureaucratic departments and high-tech administrative tools.

To address these challenges, the Vijay administration is planning a green-building compliant facility that incorporates modern architectural design, energy-efficient infrastructure, and optimized workspaces. By bringing the Legislative Assembly chambers and executive Secretariat offices under a single unified complex, the government aims to centralize administrative workflows, enhance internal communications, and eliminate operational redundancies.

The investment of Rs 1,200 crore highlights a substantial financial commitment toward building purpose-designed infrastructure for Tamil Nadu's governance. The state government intends for the new complex to serve as a high-capacity operational hub capable of supporting digital governance initiatives, streamlined public access, and modern legislative proceedings without the heritage preservation constraints associated with Fort St George.

As the administration prepares to initiate the project, the move marks one of the most significant structural realignments of Tamil Nadu's administrative footprint in recent memory. The new integrated campus in Chennai is expected to redefine the state's governance architecture, establishing a contemporary, environmentally sustainable home for the government while preserving Fort St George's heritage legacy.
