New Delhi announced on Aug 29 that Union Parliamentary Affairs Minister Kiren Rijiju will chair the Indian delegation for the High‑Level Commemorative Meeting of the Non‑Aligned Movement (NAM) scheduled for Aug 31‑Sep 1 in Serbia’s capital, Belgrade.

The gathering marks the 65th anniversary of the NAM summit, a forum of more than 120 developing nations that was founded during the Cold War to promote collective self‑reliance and resist alignment with major power blocs. India, a founding member, has traditionally used the platform to advance South‑South cooperation and articulate an independent foreign‑policy stance.

Rijiju’s team is expected to include senior officials from the Ministry of External Affairs and representatives from key economic ministries. While a detailed agenda has not been released, discussions are likely to focus on trade, climate action, technology transfer and coordinated positions on global governance issues, with Serbia hosting the event as a bridge between Europe and the Global South.
