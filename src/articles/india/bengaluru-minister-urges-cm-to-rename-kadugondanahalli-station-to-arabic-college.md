Housing Minister B Z Zameer Ahmed Khan has urged Chief Minister Shivakumar to rename Bengaluru's Kadugondanahalli metro station to Arabic College. The minister said the new name would better reflect the local identity of the area surrounding the station.

The proposal was raised in a communication involving BMRCL, which operates the station on Bengaluru's metro network. The minister cited local identity as the reason for the proposed name change.

The dispatch did not provide a timeline for the renaming or indicate whether BMRCL or the state government has responded to the request.
