The Aam Aadmi Party (AAP) on Sunday accused the BJP-led Delhi government of planning to sell prime Delhi Jal Board (DJB) properties located in areas such as Greater Kailash, Lajpat Nagar and Jhandewalan, questioning the administration's claim that it could make the utility profitable through better governance.

AAP Delhi president Saurabh Bharadwaj addressed the issue, alleging that the government's promise of improving the utility is being contradicted by the proposed sale.
