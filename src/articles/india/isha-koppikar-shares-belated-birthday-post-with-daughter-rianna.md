Indian actress Isha Koppikar released a belated birthday post on social media, sharing a series of photos and videos from her recent celebration. The images show a decorated bed with red heart‑shaped balloons and a “HAPPY BIRTHDAY” banner, the actress cutting her cake among family and friends, and a group shot that includes her daughter Rianna. The gathering was described as intimate rather than a large Bollywood event.

Koppikar, who turned 50 after being born in Mumbai in 1976, marked the occasion on September 19. In an earlier post she expressed gratitude for the blessings of Maa Shantadurga, and she has previously performed a Rudrabhishek at home and visited Lalbaugcha Raja during Ganeshotsav.
