On Friday, October 2, Prime Minister Narendra Modi paid tribute to Mahatma Gandhi on his birth anniversary, which is observed as Gandhi Jayanti, in New Delhi.

In his tribute, the prime minister recalled the core ideals of Mahatma Gandhi, including truth, non‑violence, simplicity, and Swadeshi, and stated that Bapu's timeless message would continue to guide society toward a better future.

The tribute was published as a post on SocialNews.XYZ, and included the phrase, 'May Bapu’s timeless message keep guiding us towards a better society'. The post appeared on the news outlet's website.
