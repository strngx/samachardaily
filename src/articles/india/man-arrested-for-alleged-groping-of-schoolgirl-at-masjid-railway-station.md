On October 12, a woman identified as a schoolgirl was returning home after appearing for an examination when she was allegedly assaulted. The Gujarat Rural Police (GRP) reportedly made an arrest within 24 hours of the case being filed.

Authorities have taken swift action following the incident, bringing the accused man into custody. The arrest comes as authorities investigate the alleged sexual misconduct against a minor in a public railway station area.
