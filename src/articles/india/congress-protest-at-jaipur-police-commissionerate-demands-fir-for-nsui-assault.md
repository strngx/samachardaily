Congress workers held an overnight protest outside the Jaipur police commissionerate on October 20, with the demonstration beginning on the evening of October 19 and continuing through the night. The protest has been ongoing since the evening.

The protest demands a first‑information report after NSUI leader Ritu Barala was assaulted by Delhi Police, and it is taking place amid heightened political tensions in the region. The protest is a response to the assault.
