Simran Behl, known for Roadies 20, Splitsvilla 16 and Playground 5, told IANS on Sep 13 that her recent eviction from the Amazon MX Player‑streamed series was a “cruel twist of luck” that exposed the fragility of friendships formed in the show’s “reel world”.

She recounted standing by fellow contestant Shrishti Parashar during an illness and supporting Mannat Kulharia through emotional moments, noting that her loyalty to those bonds, she believes, became a factor that kept her from reaching the finale.

Behl said trust can be both strength and vulnerability, adding that when “real people enter a reel environment, those lines can get blurred.” She said the experience taught her a lesson, that outside support now fuels her optimism and that she has a surprise planned for fans, without revealing details.

‘Playground’ blends gaming, entertainment, competition and influencer culture, bringing together young creators, gamers and internet personalities who compete in challenges while navigating rivalries, friendships and team dynamics.
