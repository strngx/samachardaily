BJP candidate Pinaki Chakraborty and CPIM candidate Bijoy Biswas went together to polling stations in Ramnagar constituency, Agartala, on Friday (November 27), riding one scooter. The joint visit took place during the municipal elections.

The two former classmates called for peace, brotherhood and cooperation in the interest of local development. Their shared ride was presented as a demonstration that political rivalry need not undermine personal friendship.

The Agartala visit happened during the municipal elections. The pair's joint activity was framed around friendship and development rather than immediate electoral combat, the report noted.
