Addressing a gathering in Pune on Sunday, Maharashtra Minister Ashish Shelar asserted that he had never opposed any decision taken by Prime Minister Narendra Modi, and that he had not torn any book. He said he did not support the tearing of books.

Shelar trained his attack on the Opposition, alleging hypocrisy in its criticism of the electoral system. The same parties, he claimed, had remained silent over irregularities in earlier elections and were questioning the process only now.
