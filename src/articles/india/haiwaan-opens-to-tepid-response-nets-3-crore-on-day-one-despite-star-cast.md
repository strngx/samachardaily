Haiwaan, starring Akshay Kumar and Saif Ali Khan, opened to a sluggish box‑office response, collecting roughly ₹3 crore net across India on its first day.

Directed by Priyadarshan, the film recorded an occupancy of about 11% in theatres, underscoring the modest audience turnout despite the high‑profile cast.
