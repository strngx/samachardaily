An apple malpua recipe now pairs traditional flavours with health-conscious ingredients. The sweet features apple slices and protein-packed rabdi, with the recipe promising zero sugar and 28g protein. Mumbai nutritionist Vanshika Khurana and Bengaluru food influencer Adhya Subramanian are behind the dish.

The dish incorporates zero-calorie sweeteners, which the pair say keep sugar levels low. It allows indulgence during Diwali without the guilt of calorie concerns, offering a wholesome version of the traditional malpua. The final sweet is positioned as a low-sugar dessert option. The recipe's aim is indulgent flavour with less sugar.
