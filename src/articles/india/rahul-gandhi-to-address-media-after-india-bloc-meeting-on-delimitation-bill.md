Nineteen opposition leaders and representatives of INDIA bloc parties are currently meeting to prepare a united strategy against the government's proposed Delimitation Bill. Rahul Gandhi is expected to address the media with other opposition leaders once the discussions conclude.

The talks focus on gathering the required numbers, presenting their position in Parliament, and coordinating a response. Gandhi has previously argued that women's reservation is being used as a pretext for delimitation and alleged the government aims to change the Constitution through the exercise. The press conference is expected to outline the alliance's next course of action.
