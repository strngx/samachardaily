Delhi High Court has suspended the three‑year prison sentence of a person convicted under the POCSO Act and certain provisions of the Indian Penal Code. The suspension takes effect immediately and will remain while the appeal is pending.

The court ordered that the convict be released on bail, subject to the deposit of a fine and the furnishing of a Rs 20,000 bail bond with one surety. The bond must be executed in accordance with the court's requirements.

The conviction involved charges under the POCSO Act and specific IPC provisions, resulting in the three‑year term that is now stayed.
