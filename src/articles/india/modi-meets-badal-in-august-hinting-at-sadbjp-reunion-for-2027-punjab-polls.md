Prime Minister Narendra Modi met Shiromani Akali Dal chief Sukhbir Singh Badal in August in Chandigarh, marking the first public sign of a potential rapprochement between the two parties ahead of the 2027 Punjab assembly election.

Both the BJP and SAD subsequently downplayed the encounter, describing it as routine, yet analysts noted it hinted at a possible reunion of the former allies that split years earlier.

The speculation arrives as both parties calibrate strategies for the upcoming poll, where a united front could reshape Punjab’s political landscape.
