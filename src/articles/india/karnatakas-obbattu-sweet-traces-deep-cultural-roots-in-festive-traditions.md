Karnataka's beloved festive sweet obbattu carries a layered history rooted in regional culinary traditions, according to a recent feature by Deccan Herald. The flatbread, typically stuffed with jaggery and lentils, remains a staple during celebrations across the state.

The preparation of obbattu varies widely across regions, with fillings ranging from sweet potato to peanut, reflecting local tastes and ingredient availability. Each variation adds a unique twist to the traditional recipe passed down through generations.

Beyond its taste, obbattu symbolizes cultural continuity, bringing families together during festivals and reinforcing Karnataka's rich food heritage through every rolled and pan-fried layer.
