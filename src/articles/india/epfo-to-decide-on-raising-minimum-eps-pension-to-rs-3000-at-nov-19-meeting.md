The Employees' Provident Fund Organisation will hold its 240th Central Board of Trustees meeting on November 19 at its New Delhi headquarters, starting at 10:15 a.m., after moving the venue from Gujarat.

Neither the Labour Ministry nor the Finance Ministry has officially confirmed the proposal, so any increase remains pending formal announcement. The current minimum pension has stayed at Rs 1,000 since September 2014, while unions have long demanded higher amounts to match rising living costs. The EPS, a social security programme for organised sector workers, is funded by an 8.33% employer contribution to the Employees' Provident Fund and calculates pensions based on salary and service length.
