The 2025-26 Swachh Survekshan Awards have named Navi Mumbai India’s cleanest city, ending Indore’s previous hold on the top position. Surat took second place and Vijayawada came third in the overall standings.

Rewa in Madhya Pradesh was honoured as the cleanest riverfront town this year, while Rudrapur received recognition as the cleanest Himalayan city this year.

The awards spanned various categories based on city population, recognising urban centres across different size brackets in addition to the overall cleanest city honour. Significant mentions also included other population-specific categories across the country.
