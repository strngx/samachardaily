A fiery multi-vehicle crash on Sheikh Khalifa bin Zayed Street near Fujairah’s entrance claimed 14 lives, including Indian nationals, and left 30 people injured late Tuesday.

The accident involved 23 vehicles, including five trucks, shortly before midnight. A truck lost control, collided with another vehicle, overturned and ignited a fire that spread to surrounding traffic. Emergency services, police and civil defence teams responded, rescuing victims and extinguishing the blaze.

The Indian Consulate in Dubai expressed deep sorrow, reached out to bereaved families and is coordinating assistance with local authorities. Police are conducting DNA tests to identify the victims, while half of the injured have been discharged and the rest remain in medical care.
