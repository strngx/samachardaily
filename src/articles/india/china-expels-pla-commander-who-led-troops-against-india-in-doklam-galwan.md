Beijing announced on Thursday that General Zhao Zongqi, former commander of the PLA Western Theatre Command, has been stripped of his Communist Party membership. Zhao oversaw the deployment of Chinese troops during the 2020 Galwan clash with India, a confrontation that resulted in casualties on both sides.

In the same disciplinary wave, two additional senior commanders were removed from the Party's Central Military Commission, the highest military decision‑making organ. The moves were confirmed by the Standing Committee of the Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference, signalling a coordinated internal purge.

Analysts say the timing coincides with renewed border standoffs and ongoing diplomatic talks between New Delhi and Beijing, suggesting the purge may be intended to demonstrate accountability and recalibrate the PLA’s approach to frontier management.
