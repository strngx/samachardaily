Four people were killed and 18 injured in a bus collision near Tarn Taran in Punjab. Two pilgrimage buses were involved in the crash on the Jammu-Kashmir-Rajasthan National Highway.

The buses were travelling between Ferozepur and Amritsar when the collision happened. The accident involved two pilgrim buses and resulted in four fatalities and 18 injuries.

The fatal crash in Punjab killed four people and injured 18 others. The two buses were on the highway at the time of the collision. The crash occurred in Tarn Taran.
