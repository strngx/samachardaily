Parvinder Singh Jhota's mother said her son was murdered in Punjab. She said the family had not sought a government job or financial compensation after the killing.

She added that the family wanted an end to the drug menace so that no more young lives would be lost. She said there was no other demand. The remarks were made on Friday, according to The Tribune India.

The outlet also noted that netizens had been trolling the case online. The mother's statement centered on the loss of her son and rejected compensation.
