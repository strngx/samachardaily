BITS Hyderabad students Naman Kasliwal and Shiv Patil have engineered an indigenous GPS‑free navigation system for drones, originally conceived to operate where satellite signals are unavailable, such as on Mars.

The prototype has undergone testing by the Indian Army, positioning it as a defence‑grade technology with potential applications for autonomous aerial platforms on Earth.

By enabling drones to navigate without external positioning data, the system addresses a critical challenge for both extraterrestrial exploration and contested battlefield environments.
