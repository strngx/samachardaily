A Janata Dal (Secular) delegation led by Union Minister HD Kumaraswamy met newly appointed Karnataka Governor Sudha Yadav in Bengaluru on Saturday. The meeting included JD(S) lawmakers and focused on governance and state affairs.

Participants said the discussion deliberately avoided political topics, concentrating instead on administrative matters and state development issues. The courtesy call marked the first formal interaction between the regional party leadership and the new governor since her appointment.
