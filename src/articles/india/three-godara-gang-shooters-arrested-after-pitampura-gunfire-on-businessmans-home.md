The Delhi Police Crime Branch arrested three men on Thursday, identifying them as shooters tied to gangster Rohit Godara. The arrests relate to a gun‑fire incident on Oct. 28 outside the home of businessman Sahil Jain, known locally as “Google,” in the Pitampura area of northwest Delhi.

During the operation, officers recovered two semi‑automatic pistols, a locally‑made country pistol, twelve live cartridges and a motorcycle that police say was used by the suspects. All items were seized as evidence.

The police have not disclosed further details, and the case remains under investigation.
