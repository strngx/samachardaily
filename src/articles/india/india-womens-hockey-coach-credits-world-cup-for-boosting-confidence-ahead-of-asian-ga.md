India women’s hockey team chief coach Sjoerd Marijne has highlighted the squad's strong performance at the 2026 World Cup as a crucial turning point, emphasizing that their fifth-place finish has provided a significant surge in momentum and self-belief. Speaking on the team's progress, Marijne noted that competing against top-tier international opponents and securing a top-five ranking on the global stage has laid a solid foundation for their upcoming campaign at the Asian Games.

The Indian squad delivered a series of disciplined performances during the 2026 World Cup tournament, demonstrating marked tactical maturity and enhanced physical conditioning. According to Marijne, finishing fifth in a field composed of the world's premier hockey nations served as concrete proof that the team can match the intensity and skill levels of high-ranking opponents, clearing psychological hurdles as the team shifts focus toward regional success.

As the squad prepares for the Asian Games, the head coach underscored the importance of translating their international form into continental dominance. The coaching staff has structured high-intensity training modules aimed at addressing defensive consistency and penalty corner execution, two key areas identified during the World Cup as critical for securing podium finishes in tight tournament formats.

Success at the Asian Games remains a central target for the national setup, carrying major implications for qualification and seeding in subsequent international cycles. Marijne emphasized that the players have internalized the lessons from their World Cup matches, building a resilient collective mindset that will be vital when facing traditional continental rivals.

With the World Cup campaign establishing a strong performance benchmark, the national team management is looking to capitalize on the positive environment within the camp. The squad's recent results have bolstered internal expectations, setting a clear trajectory as they enter the final phase of technical and tactical preparation.
