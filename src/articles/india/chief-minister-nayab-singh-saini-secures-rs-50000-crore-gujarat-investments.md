The chief minister of Haryana, Nayab Singh Saini, has signed memoranda during a two-day visit to Gujarat. He held one-to-one meetings with business leaders and investors in Gandhinagar, while Industries and Commerce Minister Rao Narbir Singh was also present. The chief minister held discussions with investors. The stay in Gujarat lasted two days.

The memoranda primarily include investment proposals worth around Rs 50,000 crore for Haryana. Saini's Gujarat trip has now yielded these memoranda. The proposals were signed on Wednesday. The MoUs were signed during the visit. The proposals were for Haryana.
