Actress Parineeti Chopra opened up about her experience filming the OTT thriller "Shaque: Trust No One" while she was pregnant, speaking at the series' trailer launch in Mumbai on September 10. She said the pregnancy, far from being a hindrance, provided her with a unique emotional reservoir that enriched her performance and allowed the crew to adapt shooting schedules in ways that benefited the overall production.

Chopra explained that the production team, led by director Sidharth Sengupta, restructured the shooting calendar to accommodate her condition, opting for shorter, more focused shoots and prioritising indoor locations that required less physical strain. This flexibility, she noted, prevented the usual delays that can arise when a lead actor faces health constraints, and the crew reported a smoother workflow as a result.

The actress also highlighted how her personal journey added authenticity to the series' themes of trust, betrayal, and vulnerability. "When you are carrying new life, every scene that deals with fear or hope feels more real," she said, noting that the heightened emotional stakes resonated with co‑star Raghav Juyal, who praised her professionalism and the collaborative spirit on set.

Industry observers have pointed out that Chopra's openness about working while pregnant reflects a broader shift in Indian entertainment, where productions are increasingly accommodating female talent's health and family considerations. The series, produced by Balaji Telefilms and streaming on Disney+ Hotstar, is slated for release later this month, and its promotional campaign is already leveraging the actress's candid remarks to generate buzz.

Chopra's comments also sparked discussion on social media about workplace inclusivity in Bollywood, with many fans and fellow actors applauding the production's proactive approach. The actress concluded by thanking the crew, her co‑star, and her family for their support, emphasizing that the experience has made her appreciate the industry's evolving attitudes toward motherhood on set.
