A car toppled after a section of Chennai's OMR Road collapsed during ongoing metro construction. Authorities are actively working to recover the vehicle and determine the cause of the sudden cave-in.

The incident occurred amid continuous metro works, prompting emergency response efforts to assess the damage and ensure public safety in the busy urban corridor.

Times Now News reports that officials remain focused on retrieving the stranded vehicle and investigating whether the metro project activities contributed to the structural failure.
