A British couple completed a 4,218‑kilometre trek across India in a customised Bajaj auto‑rickshaw, spending 75 days on the road. The vehicle, originally designed for city use, was modified to endure the long distances and varied conditions encountered during the journey.

During the expedition the couple grew emotionally attached to the three‑wheeler, choosing to preserve it as a memento rather than leave it behind in India.

They arranged to ship the auto‑rickshaw to the United Kingdom, where it arrived six months later, marking the conclusion of a trans‑continental adventure that turned a local Indian transport mode into an international travel souvenir.
