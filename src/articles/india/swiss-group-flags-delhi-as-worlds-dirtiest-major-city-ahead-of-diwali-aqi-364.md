A Swiss monitoring group said Delhi recorded an Air Quality Index of 364 on the day before Diwali, classifying the capital’s air as ‘very poor’. The agency’s data placed the city as the world’s most polluted major metropolis at that time.

The alarm comes as the city prepares for Diwali festivities, when firecracker use typically spikes emissions. Health officials warn that an AQI above 300 poses severe risks, especially for children, the elderly and those with respiratory conditions.

Authorities have not announced specific mitigation measures for the upcoming holiday.
