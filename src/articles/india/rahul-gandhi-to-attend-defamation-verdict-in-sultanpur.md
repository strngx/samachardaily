Rahul Gandhi is traveling to Sultanpur for a special MP-MLA court hearing on Monday, where a defamation case over his 2018 remarks against Amit Shah will be adjudicated. The case stems from comments made during the Karnataka Assembly election campaign.

Security has been tightened around the court in Sultanpur to manage the high-profile proceedings. The verdict is expected to be delivered during the hearing, marking a key legal moment in the political dispute.

The outcome may have implications for the ongoing political narrative surrounding the BJP leader and the Congress party. No further details were provided beyond the court appearance and security arrangements.
