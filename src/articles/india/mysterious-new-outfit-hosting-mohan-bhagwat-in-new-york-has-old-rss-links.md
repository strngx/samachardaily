The Rashtriya Swayamsevak Sangh (RSS) chief Mohan Bhagwat is slated to address the Universal Oneness Celebrations in New York on August 29, marking his first public appearance abroad since assuming the RSS helm. The event, billed as a global interfaith gathering, is being held at the Manhattan Convention Center and is expected to draw diaspora leaders, spiritual figures, and policy analysts.

Investigations reveal that the hosting entity, registered in the United States just three months ago as "Universal Oneness Celebrations, Inc.," lists three directors who previously held senior positions within RSS-affiliated cultural wings. Corporate filings show the organization received seed funding from a charitable trust linked to long‑time RSS volunteers, and its website mirrors language used in RSS publications, raising questions about the group’s independence.

RSS officials have declined to comment on the specific organizers, while the Indian Ministry of External Affairs issued a brief statement affirming Bhagwat’s right to engage with overseas communities. U.S. authorities confirmed standard security protocols are in place but did not elaborate. Analysts note the event could serve as a soft‑power outreach platform for the RSS, potentially influencing Indian diaspora politics and U.S.–India cultural ties.
