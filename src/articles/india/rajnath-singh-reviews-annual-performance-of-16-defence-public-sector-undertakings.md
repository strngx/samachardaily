Union Minister of Defence Rajnath Singh chaired a major annual performance review meeting in New Delhi today, systematically evaluating the operational progress and key achievements of 16 Defence Public Sector Undertakings (DPSUs). The review meeting in the national capital brought together top leadership from across the public sector defence manufacturing industry to account for institutional performance over the past financial cycle.

Accompanying the Raksha Mantri during the comprehensive review was Minister of State for Defence (Raksha Rajya Mantri) Sanjay Seth. Both ministers engaged in high-level discussions with administrative heads, assessing performance benchmarks, operational outputs, and key achievements reported by the public sector manufacturing enterprises operating under the Ministry of Defence's oversight.

Secretary of Defence Production Sanjeev Kumar provided the primary briefing to the ministers, outlining broad production milestones and administrative developments across the defense production ecosystem. His presentation set the baseline for detailed operational reviews of each of the 16 state-run public sector undertakings represented at the New Delhi event.

Following the initial briefing by Secretary Sanjeev Kumar, the Chairmen and Managing Directors (CMDs) of all 16 Defence Public Sector Undertakings personally presented individual updates on their respective organizations. Each CMD briefed Rajnath Singh and the executive panel on specific institutional achievements, production milestones, operational developments, and key industrial metrics accomplished during the annual reporting period.

The annual performance evaluation forms a core component of the Ministry of Defence's governance framework, ensuring direct alignment between public sector defense enterprises and national operational priorities. Today's meeting in New Delhi enabled central leadership to directly inspect output metrics, evaluate organizational achievements, and review operational progress across all 16 DPSUs operating under the ministry's administrative control.
