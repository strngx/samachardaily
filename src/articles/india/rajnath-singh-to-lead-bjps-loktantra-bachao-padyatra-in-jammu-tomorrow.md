Defence Minister Rajnath Singh will travel to Jammu on Saturday to lead the Bharatiya Janata Party’s Loktantra Bachao, Arajakta Hatao Padyatra, a foot march that seeks to save democracy and curb anarchy.

The procession is scheduled for November 28, beginning at 10 AM and concluding at the Parade Ground near Brahman, according to a BJP leader speaking to Kashmir News Observer.

A BJP leader said the event will be held in Jammu city, with participants expected to walk the route as part of the party’s outreach effort, as reported by the same source.
