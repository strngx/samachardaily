Domestic institutional investors have sold more than ₹47,662 crore in equities. The selling took place during the recent market upswing and extends their longest selling streak in four years, reflecting their inclination to lock in profits after substantial buying.

Meanwhile, foreign portfolio investors have returned vigorously, adding about $8 billion to Indian stocks since mid‑October. This inflow marks a strong re‑entry of foreign capital into the market.

The developments represent a notable change in market behavior, with domestic investors trimming positions while foreign investors increase exposure. The data shows a clear divergence between domestic and foreign flows.
