Mumbai police have arrested four people for allegedly transporting a firearm to Mumbai and helping coordinate the operation tied to the Tiger Shroff firing case, according to The Hindu.

The detainees are suspected of moving the weapon into the city and assisting in the planning of the attack, authorities said. The arrests mark a key breakthrough in the investigation that has kept the actor’s family and law enforcement agencies on high alert.

No further details on the suspects’ identities or charges were released, and the investigation continues to trace the shooters’ network. The case remains unresolved as Tiger Shroff’s team has not issued any official statements.
