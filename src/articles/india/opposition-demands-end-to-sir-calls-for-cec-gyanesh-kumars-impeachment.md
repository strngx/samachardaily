On Wednesday, opposition parties in New Delhi demanded that the Special Intensive Revision (SIR) exercise be scrapped and launched a fierce attack on Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar, accusing him of running the poll panel as a “one‑man autocracy” and calling for his impeachment and arrest.

The opposition’s demand follows objections raised by election commissioners over the SIR process, intensifying pressure on the Election Commission as it contends with internal criticism of the voter‑list revision exercise.
