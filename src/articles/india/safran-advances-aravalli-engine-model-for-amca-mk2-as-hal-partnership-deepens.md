Safran is promoting its Aravalli model for the AMCA MK‑2 engine, underscoring a deepening partnership with Hindustan Aeronautics Ltd (HAL).

HAL and Safran Helicopter Engines signed a contract through their 50:50 joint venture, SAFHAL Helicopter Engines Pvt Ltd, marking a major step in India‑France aviation cooperation.

The agreement tasks SAFHAL with the design, development, manufacture, supply and lifecycle support of the new Aravalli helicopter engine intended for India's future military rotorcraft fleet.
