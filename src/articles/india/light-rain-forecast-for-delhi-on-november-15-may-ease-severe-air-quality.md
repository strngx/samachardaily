Light drizzle is forecast in parts of Delhi on November 15, according to the India Meteorological Department outlook, giving the capital's residents a possible break from severely polluted air.

The relief, if it arrives, would likely be short-lived. The central question raised by the forecast is whether the expected rainfall will be substantial enough to bring down pollution levels across the city.

Delhi is currently in the grip of severe air quality, and rainfall is one of the few near-term weather factors that can pull pollutant concentrations down.
