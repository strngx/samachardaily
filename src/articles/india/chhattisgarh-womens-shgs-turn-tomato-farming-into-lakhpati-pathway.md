Eighteen women-led self-help groups in Patrapara village, Balrampur district, have transformed tomato and vegetable farming into a profitable enterprise, with over 100 tribal women now earning between ₹3-4 lakh each per season. The groups, operating since a 2017 grant of ₹20,000, currently harvest 20-25 trays of tomatoes per cycle sold at local mandis, alongside chillies, beans and cauliflower.

Many members have already achieved the government's 'Lakhpati Didi' benchmark of ₹1 lakh annual income, while others are rapidly approaching it. Savitri Shandilya coordinates operations across three gram panchayats, and participants report cumulative sales worth several lakh rupees with more harvests pending.
