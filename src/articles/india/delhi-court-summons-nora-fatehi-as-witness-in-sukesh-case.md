A Delhi court has summoned actress Nora Fatehi to appear as a prosecution witness in the Rs 200 crore high‑profile extortion case involving Sukesh Chandrashekhar, and she is required to appear before the bench.

Nora Fatehi has been named in the third supplementary chargesheet, which is a key part of the case file and the ongoing investigation. The chargesheet was filed in the case. The investigation continues.

She is expected to record her testimony in the high‑profile proceedings, and the court is examining the extortion allegations as part of the case.
