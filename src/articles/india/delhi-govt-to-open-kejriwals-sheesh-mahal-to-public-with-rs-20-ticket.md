Delhi's public works minister Parvesh Verma said the Sheesh Mahal bungalow owned by Chief Minister Arvind Kejriwal will be opened to the public, with an online ticketing portal and an entry fee of about Rs 20.

The government is considering fee waivers for senior citizens and college students, aiming to promote broader access, and a private hospitality firm may be contracted to handle the property's maintenance and operations.

The house is noted for a Rs 11.56 lakh chandelier and Rs 5.69 lakh curtains; an exact opening date has not been announced.
