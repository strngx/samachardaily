AIMIM chief Asaduddin Owaisi on Sunday confirmed that Indian national Mohammad Yousuf Ali, a resident of Hyderabad, died in the attack at Riyadh Airport in Saudi Arabia. His wife and children sustained injuries during the incident.

Owaisi took to social media to announce the tragic development, expressing grief over the loss of life and praying for the speedy recovery of the injured family members. The attack at the Saudi Arabian airport sent shockwaves among the Indian diaspora.

The incident has raised concerns over the safety of Indian nationals traveling abroad, particularly in conflict-prone regions. Authorities are likely to review security protocols for outbound passengers in the coming days.
