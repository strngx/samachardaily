The Communist Party of India (Marxist) has attacked the Centre over corporate policies, calling Prime Minister Narendra Modi's remarks at FICCI's centenary convention a confession.

Modi had asked industry not to take 140 crore Indians for granted as a market where anything will sell, and questioned whether companies raised hiring and salaries after corporate tax was cut from 30% to 22% and income tax was waived up to Rs 12 lakh.

The CPI(M) and other opposition voices criticised the government, with Congress leader Jairam Ramesh saying Modi repeated what the opposition long said about wages, investment and jobs. Government supporters said the PM held industry accountable and urged better quality and an India First approach.
