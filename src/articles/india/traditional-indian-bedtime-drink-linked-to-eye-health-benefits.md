Living in urban environments can adversely affect eye health and lead to various vision problems. A traditional Indian bedtime drink, rooted in Ayurveda, offers a natural approach to supporting vision and overall well-being.

This remedy combines fennel seeds, almonds, and rock sugar simmered in milk. It is said to nourish the retina, enhance vision, and aid digestion while supporting brain function.

The blend's combination of ingredients targets multiple health areas, from retinal support to cognitive function. While promising, users are reminded that medical consultation remains essential for any eye-related concerns.
