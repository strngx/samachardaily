India A declared their first innings at 464 for 5 after 146 overs, giving them a 428-run lead over New Zealand A in the third unofficial Test at Lincoln on Day 2.

New Zealand A managed 36 runs for two wickets when play was halted for the day, leaving them far behind the massive deficit.

The match, the third unofficial Test between the two A sides, will resume with New Zealand A attempting to recover from the steep target.
