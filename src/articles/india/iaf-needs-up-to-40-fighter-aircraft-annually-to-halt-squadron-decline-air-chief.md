The Indian Air Force must induct between 36 and 40 new fighter jets every year to prevent a further drop in its squadron strength. Air Chief Marshal AP Singh highlighted the requirement ahead of Air Force Day, calling attention to the critical need for fleet modernization.

The statement points to the immense operational challenge facing the service as it seeks to stabilize its fighter numbers and rebuild combat readiness across its operational units.
