India has narrowed its overall power gap with China to the smallest level recorded in the past decade, according to the Lowy Institute's Asia Power Index 2026, a regional ranking of national power.

The report credits the improvement to gains in defence capability, deeper strategic partnerships and economic strength, factors that have together reinforced India's position in the regional order.

BrahMos exports and a wider reach of Indian soft power have added further momentum to the country's climb, according to the index.
