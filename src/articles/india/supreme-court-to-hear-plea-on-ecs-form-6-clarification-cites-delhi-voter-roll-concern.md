The Supreme Court has scheduled a hearing on a petition that seeks clarification of the Election Commission’s Form 6, amid concerns that Delhi’s voter‑roll revision may be inaccurate, and argues that the current Form 6 does not reflect the latest updates to the voter list. The petition also requests that the Election Commission provide a detailed explanation of the Form 6 revisions.

Chief Justice of India indicated the bench may ask the Election Commission to extend the deadline for filing objections, stressing the importance of a fair and timely electoral process. The bench will review the petition as part of its docket.
