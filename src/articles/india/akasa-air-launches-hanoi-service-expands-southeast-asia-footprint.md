Akasa Air, India's fastest-growing airline, has officially commenced operations in Vietnam, inaugurating a direct service that links Mumbai with Hanoi. The new route represents the airline's seventh international destination and signals a strategic push into Southeast Asia. By adding Hanoi to its network, Akasa Air continues to build on its reputation for rapid growth and ambitious expansion. The inaugural flight departed from Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport in Mumbai at 0535 hours IST and arrived at Noi Bai International Airport in Hanoi at 1200 hours ICT.

The airline will operate four weekly direct flights on the Mumbai-Hanoi sector, ensuring a consistent flow of passengers between the two cities. Each flight is scheduled to depart from Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport at 0535 IST and to land at Noi Bai International Airport at 1200 ICT, providing travelers with a convenient early‑morning departure and a midday arrival.

The addition of Hanoi marks a significant milestone for Akasa Air, as it becomes the carrier's seventh international destination and expands its footprint into Southeast Asia. This move is part of the airline's broader strategy to increase its international reach and to strengthen economic and cultural ties between India and Vietnam.

As India's fastest-growing airline, Akasa Air has been rapidly expanding its domestic and international network since its inception. The launch of the Hanoi service underscores the carrier's commitment to connecting Indian travelers with key global destinations and reflects its competitive positioning in the regional aviation market.

The new Mumbai-Hanoi link is expected to enhance travel convenience for passengers, reducing journey times and offering a direct alternative to connecting flights. By providing four weekly services, Akasa Air ensures that travelers have multiple options throughout the week, supporting both business and leisure travel between India and Vietnam.

The early morning departure at 0535 IST enables travelers to begin their journey from Mumbai well before the typical rush, while the scheduled arrival at 1200 ICT in Hanoi ensures that passengers can commence their activities by midday, making efficient use of travel time.

With four weekly services spread across the week, Akasa Air offers flexibility for both business commuters and leisure travelers, allowing them to choose a flight that best fits their itinerary.
