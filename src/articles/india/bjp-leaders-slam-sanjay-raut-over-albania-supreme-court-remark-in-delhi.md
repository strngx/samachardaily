As reported by IANS from New Delhi, BJP leaders on Tuesday, October 20 criticised Shiv Sena (UBT) MP Sanjay Raut over his social media post referencing the reported burning of Albania’s Supreme Court.

The post’s reference to the reported burning of Albania’s Supreme Court became the focal point of a sharp exchange between ruling and opposition benches in New Delhi.

Ruling figures invoked the slogan 'People of India will not tolerate anarchy' in response to the controversy, emphasizing institutional respect.
