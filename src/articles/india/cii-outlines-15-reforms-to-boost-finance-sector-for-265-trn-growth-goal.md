CII has tabled 15 financial sector reforms aimed at underpinning India's goal of a $26.5 trillion economy. The proposals target key bottlenecks in credit flow and risk management.

Key measures include a permanent, rule‑based liquidity facility for well‑governed non‑banking financial companies, revisions to priority‑sector lending norms, wider investment in lower‑rated corporate bonds, and the creation of a national catastrophe and climate risk pool.

CII says the reforms are intended to deepen market resilience, broaden funding sources and embed climate‑risk mitigation, creating a more robust financial ecosystem for the projected economic expansion.
