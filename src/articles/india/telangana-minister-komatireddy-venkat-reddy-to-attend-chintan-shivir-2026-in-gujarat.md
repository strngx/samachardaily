Reporting on October 5, 2026, Telangana minister Komatireddy Venkat Reddy is scheduled to attend the Chintan Shivir 2026 in Gujarat.

During the event, he plans to hold a meeting with Union Minister Nitin Gadkari to seek support for key road projects.

This engagement represents a direct appeal to central leadership for infrastructure backing. The minister's presence at the gathering signals ongoing coordination between the state administration and the Union government on transport development. The Telangana government is prioritizing road connectivity as a key development goal for its citizens.
