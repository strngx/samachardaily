The Regional Passport Office has introduced a specialized outreach campaign named 'Passport First, Opportunities Next' to assist young individuals in Uttar Pradesh applying for their first passport.

Under this initiative, Passport Seva Kendras across the state will organize a focused passport drive on October 10. The campaign aims to facilitate documentation for those planning to pursue educational or professional avenues abroad.

Slot bookings for the special drive open on October 6. Eligible candidates must secure their appointments online through the official Passport Seva Portal prior to attending.
