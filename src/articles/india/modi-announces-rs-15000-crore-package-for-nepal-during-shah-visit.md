Prime Minister Narendra Modi announced a Rs 15,000 crore development package for Nepal on Monday during the state visit of Nepalese Prime Minister Balendra Shah to New Delhi. The commitment marks a substantial financial pledge aimed at deepening bilateral cooperation.

The announcement comes as India seeks to reset ties with its Himalayan neighbour amid growing Chinese influence in the region. Shah's visit represents the first major high-level engagement since he assumed office, signalling renewed diplomatic momentum between the two capitals.
