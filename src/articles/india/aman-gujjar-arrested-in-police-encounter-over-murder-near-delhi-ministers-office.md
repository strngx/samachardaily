New Delhi police arrested Aman Gujjar following an encounter on November 26, taking him into custody over the murder of a 26-year-old outside Delhi Minister Kapil Mishra's office.

Gujjar had been wanted in connection with the killing near the minister's office, and officers apprehended him after the encounter, according to the report.

He is now in police custody, and the report did not provide further details about the encounter or the victim's identity.
