Prime Minister Narendra Modi felicitated four trainees from Odisha’s industrial training institutes at the 5th Kaushal Deekshant Samaroh in Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh, on November 22, 2026, a moment of pride for the state.

These individuals were honored for their outstanding performance in the All India Trade Test 2026, a national assessment that evaluates skill proficiency, with Abhijit Das of Government ITI, Kataka highlighted as a top performer.

The ceremony underscored the growing importance of vocational education in India’s workforce development and reinforced the government’s pledge to expand opportunities for youth through technical institutes.
