Prime Minister Narendra Modi addressed the nation from New Delhi on Thursday, stating that cities such as Pune would have endured decades of delay for metro connectivity had the expansion continued at its pre‑2014 tempo.

He made the remarks in a recent post, emphasizing that the rapid rollout of metro lines since 2014 has dramatically shortened expected wait times and accelerated metro projects in cities such as Pune.

Modi's statement underscores the government's focus on fast-track infrastructure development as a key driver of modernizing India's growing cities and supports the broader objective of improving connectivity for residents.
