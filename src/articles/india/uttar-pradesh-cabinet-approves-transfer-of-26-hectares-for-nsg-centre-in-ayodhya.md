The Uttar Pradesh State Cabinet, presided over by Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath, has formally approved the transfer of 26 hectares (approximately 65 acres) of land free of cost to the Union Ministry of Home Affairs for establishing a permanent National Security Guard (NSG) Regional Centre in Ayodhya.

The decision was finalized during a cabinet meeting held at the state secretariat in Lucknow. According to official government notifications, the allocated parcel consists of uncultivated government revenue land located in the Milkipur tehsil of Ayodhya district, positioned along strategic transit corridors connecting the district with regional highway networks.

The transfer enables the central government to construct a dedicated operational hub for the NSG, India’s premier federal contingency counter-terrorism and anti-hijacking commando force. Operating under the administrative oversight of the Ministry of Home Affairs, the NSG maintains regional hubs across major metropolitan regions—including Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata, Hyderabad, and Gandhinagar—established following the 2008 Mumbai attacks to minimize mobilization and transit times during emergency hostage or counter-insurgency operations.

The establishment of an NSG garrison in Ayodhya forms part of a broader security architecture overhaul in the district following the inauguration of the Ram Janmabhoomi temple complex, which has resulted in a substantial increase in daily religious tourism and civil transit. The regional centre will house specialized commando units, bomb disposal squads, and rapid-intervention teams trained for urban counter-terror operations, providing defensive coverage across northern and eastern Uttar Pradesh.

With the cabinet approval in place, district revenue authorities in Ayodhya have been instructed to execute the land mutation records in favor of the Ministry of Home Affairs. The central ministry will subsequently oversee site demarcation, perimeter infrastructure, and institutional housing construction. State officials noted that the facility will strengthen defensive readiness across eastern Uttar Pradesh without placing financial demands on local civic bodies.
