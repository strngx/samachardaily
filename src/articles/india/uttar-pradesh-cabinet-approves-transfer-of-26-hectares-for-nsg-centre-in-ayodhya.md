The Uttar Pradesh Cabinet has cleared the free handover of 26 hectares (approximately 65 acres) of land to the Union Ministry of Home Affairs for the establishment of a National Security Guard (NSG) Regional Centre in Ayodhya.

The decision was taken in a cabinet meeting chaired by Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath, approving the transfer of barren land located in Milkipur tehsil of the Ayodhya district.
