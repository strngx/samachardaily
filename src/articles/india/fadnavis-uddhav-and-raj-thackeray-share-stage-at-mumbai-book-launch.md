Maharashtra Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis shared a podium with Shiv Sena (UBT) leader Uddhav Thackeray and Maharashtra Navnirman Sena chief Raj Thackeray at a book launch in Mumbai.

Fadnavis described the unexpected alignment as 'part of Maharashtra's political tradition' and the three leaders turned the occasion into a forum for the art of cartooning, unveiling a proposal for a dedicated cartoonists' gallery.

The gathering underscored a significant cultural push to preserve satirical expression, with the proposed gallery intended to give cartoonists a permanent public platform in the state’s artistic landscape.
