Amazon is offering the IRIS Instant White Rice 10‑Pack at a steep $12.59, inclusive of shipping, after shoppers apply a digital coupon and opt into the retailer’s Subscribe & Save program. The deal appears on the Hip2Save platform, which flags it as a limited‑time discount for members.

The rice pack normally retails for $19.99, meaning the current price reflects a discount of roughly 37%. A 5% off digital coupon is available, bringing the price down to $13.99 before any subscription benefits are applied. The coupon can be clipped directly on the Amazon product page and is automatically applied at checkout.

Subscribe & Save adds an additional layer of savings, offering a further 5‑15% discount on top of the coupon. When shoppers combine the 5% coupon with the maximum 15% subscription discount, the net price falls to $12.59. This tiered discount structure is typical of Amazon’s recurring‑delivery model, which rewards customers for committing to regular shipments.

The offer includes free shipping, a notable advantage in the online grocery segment where delivery fees can erode savings. Consumers who enroll in the subscription will receive the rice on a schedule of their choosing, ensuring a steady supply while maintaining the reduced price. Such deals are part of a broader trend of e‑commerce platforms leveraging subscription models to lock in customer loyalty.

Hip2Save notes that the promotion is "ONLY AVAILABLE IN PAID PLANS," indicating that the deal is highlighted for users who subscribe to the site’s premium service. While the discount is accessible to any Amazon shopper who follows the coupon and subscription steps, the visibility of the offer is limited to Hip2Save’s paying members.
