An aircraft operated by Trans Bharat Aviation disappeared shortly after taking off from Badrinath toward Dehradun, with six occupants on board, and is suspected to have crashed near Chayi village in the Joshimath area. The helicopter was traveling from Badrinath to Phata when contact was lost, according to local media reports.

Search operations are hampered by persistent rain and poor visibility, making it difficult to locate the wreckage. Two additional helicopters have been deployed to support the effort, but the adverse weather continues to impede progress. Authorities say the six individuals remain unaccounted for as the search persists.
