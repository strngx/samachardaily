Jackson, Mississippi – Law enforcement officials disclosed on Friday that they have taken a man into custody in relation to the fatal hanging of Tasia Fortune.

Fortune, identified as a Black woman, was discovered hanging from a tree in the state last month, prompting a homicide investigation that has now led to an arrest.

Authorities said the investigation remains active but provided no further details about the suspect, charges, or the circumstances surrounding the case.
