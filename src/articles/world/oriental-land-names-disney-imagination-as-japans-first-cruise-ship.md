On October 14, 2026, in Urayasu, Chiba, Japan, Oriental Land Co., Ltd. and ORIENTAL LAND CRUISE Co., Ltd. announced that the name of their new Disney cruise ship is Disney Imagination. The vessel is set to commence operations as Japan's first Disney cruise ship.

The joint announcement was made by both companies. Oriental Land Co., which operates the Tokyo Disney Resort, is developing the cruise venture through its subsidiary ORIENTAL LAND CRUISE Co. The Disney Imagination's operational details and launch timeline are expected to be released by the companies as the vessel's debut approaches.
