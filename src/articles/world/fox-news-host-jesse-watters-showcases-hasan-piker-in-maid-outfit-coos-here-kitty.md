On September 29, Fox News host Jesse Watters broadcast footage of left‑leaning Twitch streamer Hasan Piker dressed in a French maid outfit with cat ears, captured during a January 2025 trip to Japan.

Watters, noting Piker’s support for Michigan Senate candidate Abdul El‑Sayed, cooed “Here, kitty, kitty” and referenced the streamer’s earlier controversial 9/11 remarks while linking the clip to his political affiliations.

The video, originally posted on YouTube from a maid‑café visit, remains publicly accessible and was highlighted on Fox News’ Primetime program.
