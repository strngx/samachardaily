Iran has called on the United States to abide by the terms of the diplomatic framework agreement reached in June, describing the Pakistan-mediated pact as a sovereign decision endorsed by the country's highest leadership, Anadolu reported.

The statement was delivered by Iranian Interior Ministry spokesman Ali Zainvand, who noted that diplomatic consultations remain ongoing regarding implementation mechanisms. Zainvand also referenced a scheduled official visit to Tehran by Pakistan’s Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi, according to Iranian state media broadcasts.

The diplomatic framework, known as the Islamabad Memorandum of Understanding, was negotiated with Pakistani mediation to establish structured communication channels, address regional security protocols, and outline reciprocal commitments regarding sanctions relief and compliance monitoring.

Zainvand stated that multiple regional and international governments continue to support active diplomatic channels, emphasizing that constructive engagement through established multilateral frameworks remains the preferred path for resolving bilateral issues.
