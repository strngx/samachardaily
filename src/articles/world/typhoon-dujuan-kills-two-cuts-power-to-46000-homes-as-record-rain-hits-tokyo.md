Typhoon Dujuan has killed two people in Japan and knocked electricity out for roughly 46,000 households, according to local authorities.

The storm weakened as it tracked northeastward, but still unleashed record‑breaking rainfall across parts of the Tokyo metropolitan area.

The heavy downpours combined with the power loss have disrupted daily activities, prompting emergency services to respond to flooding and restore electricity.
