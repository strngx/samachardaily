Mayor Sheila Dixon Sheffield of Detroit publicly rejected accusations made by her former boyfriend that she used her office to award municipal contracts to companies linked to him. The ex‑partner circulated a series of photographs on social media that he claimed showed Sheffield meeting with contractors and reviewing bid documents. In a press briefing on Monday, Sheffield asserted that the images were taken in 2022, before she assumed the mayoral office, and that they do not reflect any official actions taken under her administration. She emphasized that all city contracts are subject to a competitive bidding process overseen by the Detroit Procurement Department, and that no evidence has been presented to suggest any deviation from those procedures.

Sheffield’s office also released a statement indicating that the city’s ethics commission has been notified of the allegations and will conduct a routine review of the contracts in question. The mayor highlighted recent audits that showed no irregularities in the awarding of the contracts cited by her ex‑boyfriend. While she declined to comment on the personal nature of the dispute, Sheffield stressed that personal relationships should not be used to undermine public confidence in municipal governance. The controversy has drawn attention from local media and civic groups, prompting calls for transparency and a reaffirmation of the city’s procurement safeguards.
