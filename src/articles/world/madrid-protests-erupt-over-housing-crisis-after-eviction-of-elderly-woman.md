Housing protesters gather in Madrid's iconic square as authorities prepare a response to a growing crisis. The demonstrations follow the forced eviction of 87-year-old María del Carmen Abascal, who was removed from her apartment, sparking widespread anger among residents demanding affordable housing solutions. Local leaders are calling for immediate action to prevent further displacement.

The government has been mobilized to address the escalating housing situation, with officials outlining plans to tackle the root causes of displacement and improve living conditions for vulnerable citizens across the city. Authorities aim to implement urgent measures to stabilize the neighborhood and protect residents from further evictions.
