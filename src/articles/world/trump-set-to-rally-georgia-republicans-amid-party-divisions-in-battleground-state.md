President Donald Trump is scheduled to appear at a rally in Georgia alongside top Republican candidates, marking another stop in his midterm campaign blitz despite growing party tensions.

The visit occurs despite noticeable divisions within the Georgia Republican Party, as Trump seeks to consolidate support in a battleground state that has been central to his unsubstantiated election fraud allegations and remains a focal point of his false claims about the 2020 election.

Observers note the rally aims to energize the party base and reinforce Trump’s influence in a key electoral battleground ahead of the upcoming midterm elections, while political analysts say the event could test Trump’s ability to unite fractured GOP factions and drive voter turnout.
