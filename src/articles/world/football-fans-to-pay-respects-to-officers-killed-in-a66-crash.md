Two police constables, Matthew Blades and Tom Clough, lost their lives after their patrol vehicle was involved in a serious collision on the A66, a major trans‑Yorkshire route. The incident, which prompted an extensive emergency response, has left the local policing community in mourning.

In response, Middlesbrough Football Club issued a statement urging fans attending the upcoming home fixture to stand for a minute’s silence before kickoff and to wear black armbands as a sign of respect. Club officials said the tribute will also be highlighted on the stadium’s big screen, with a brief video recalling the officers’ service.

Family members of the constables, as well as senior police representatives, have expressed gratitude for the football community’s solidarity. The North Yorkshire Police Chief confirmed that a formal investigation into the crash is underway, and that safety reviews of patrol routes on the A66 will be conducted.
