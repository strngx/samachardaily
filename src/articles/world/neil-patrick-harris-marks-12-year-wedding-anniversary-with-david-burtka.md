Neil Patrick Harris marked the 12th anniversary of his marriage to David Burtka on September 7, 2026, revisiting the pivotal moment when Burtka said "I do" during their wedding ceremony. The actor, best known for his role as Barney Stinson on "How I Met Your Mother," shared a nostalgic Instagram post featuring a photograph from their wedding day, expressing deep gratitude for Burtka's commitment.

Harris's relationship with Burtka began in April 2004, following a prior romantic connection with actress Christine Taylor between 1997 and 1998. Over the past decade-plus, the couple has built a long-term partnership, culminating in the birth of twin children—son Gideon and daughter Harper—in October 2010 through a surrogate mother arrangement. Their bond solidified further when they wed in Italy during September 2014, adding another chapter to their public narrative.

The couple recently reunited at Disneyland on August 14, 2026, where they spent the day exploring iconic attractions including Minerva and Mickey, wandering through Phantom Manor, and navigating thrilling hedge mazes. The visit concluded with a spectacular fireworks display, which both spouses described as a memorable experience. Harris captured the moments online, highlighting the joy of being together and the magic of their shared adventures.
