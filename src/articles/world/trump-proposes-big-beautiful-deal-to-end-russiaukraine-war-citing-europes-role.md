Donald Trump has floated a major agreement to end the Russia‑Ukraine war, saying the solution could involve the whole of Europe. The U.S. president made the comment after a White House meeting with Russian envoy Kirill Dmitriev.

Separately, U.S. officials are holding talks with Ukraine, NATO and European partners ahead of upcoming discussions in Abu Dhabi. The coordinated diplomatic effort aims to explore a comprehensive settlement that includes European stakeholders.

The proposal marks a shift toward a broader European role in resolving the conflict, though no concrete terms have been disclosed. Observers note the timing aligns with intensified diplomatic outreach across the Atlantic.
