California health officials confirmed Tuesday a death connected to an E. coli outbreak traced to a pumpkin farm in San Luis Obispo County. All affected individuals visited the same farm during October, the county public health department said in a statement obtained by the San Luis Obispo Tribune.

Authorities did not release details about the deceased or the total number of cases. The farm has not been publicly named as the investigation continues.
