Investigators have located the two Pierre‑Auguste Renoir paintings that were stolen from the Renoir Museum in France, confirming the artworks have been recovered after the high‑profile robbery.

Police apprehended six suspects believed to be part of the gang that carried out the heist last month, a theft estimated at €9 million. Authorities previously said at least five people were involved.

The swift arrests demonstrate French law enforcement’s commitment to protecting cultural heritage and may deter future art‑theft operations targeting major museums.
