Dhruv Rathee, an Indian YouTuber, used X on Friday night to call for a protest in London targeting what he describes as “vote chori”.

He urged followers to gather at noon on Saturday outside Parliament Square, specifically near the Mahatma Gandhi statue, to demonstrate against the alleged vote theft.

The planned rally aims to draw attention to the issue among the Indian diaspora and British public, with Rathee inviting anyone in the capital to join the demonstration.
