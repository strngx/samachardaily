A catastrophic fuel tanker explosion along a major highway in western Iran has claimed the lives of at least 10 people and left at least six others injured, official sources confirmed. The incident occurred on the Hamedan-Sanandaj highway, triggering an immediate emergency response as regional rescue crews mobilized to the scene.

According to official reports, the blast involved a heavy fuel transport vehicle traveling along the busy transit corridor in western Iran. The intensity of the explosion caused severe damage along the roadway, resulting in high casualties among those present in the immediate vicinity of the tanker.

First responders and medical personnel rushed to the affected location shortly after the explosion was reported to attend to the victims. Official casualty figures indicate that at least six survivors sustained injuries of varying severity and were evacuated to nearby medical facilities for treatment.

The Hamedan-Sanandaj highway serves as a key regional transportation route in western Iran, facilitating the daily transit of commercial cargo, passenger vehicles, and industrial tankers. The major disruption caused by the explosion highlights the acute hazards associated with transporting volatile bulk fuel along major public thoroughfares.

Authorities have secured the site along the Hamedan-Sanandaj corridor to manage the aftermath and assess the total damage. Official evaluations remain ongoing as regional emergency services work to address the impact of the fatal highway explosion.
