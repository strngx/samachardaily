Supreme Court rejected a push to set aside thousands of California mail ballots that the Trump administration claimed were cast by noncitizens. The decision was issued on the eve of the midterm elections.

The request, backed by the administration, sought to invalidate the ballots pending verification of citizenship status. The court’s denial leaves the ballots in place for counting.

The ruling comes as both parties gear up for the pivotal midterms, where California’s vote could influence national outcomes.
