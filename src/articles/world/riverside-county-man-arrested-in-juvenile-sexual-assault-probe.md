Riverside County authorities arrested a 23‑year‑old Murrieta resident on suspicion of sexually assaulting a juvenile. The suspect, identified as Reece Thomas Kennedy, was taken into custody after the Murrieta Police Department linked him to the alleged crime.

Police opened the case on Sept. 2 following a report that a young girl had been assaulted in July. Investigators pursued the lead, gathered evidence and ultimately secured the arrest, though no further details on charges or court proceedings were released.
