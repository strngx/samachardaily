Gisele Sugimoto, 46, was filming her husband's high‑speed drift demonstration at a public car show when the performance vehicle accelerated and struck her, sending her several feet backward.

The vehicle mowed her down as she attempted to capture the stunt on camera.

The impact flung her onto the dirt track of the exhibition, and the footage captured the moment the vehicle hit her as the stunt continued, with the car remaining in motion.

The high‑speed drift was performed as part of a staged exhibition for spectators.

The car, a modified performance vehicle, accelerated rapidly before making contact.
