Cornell has opened its 2026-27 call for nominations for the A.D. White Professors-at-Large Program, inviting faculty members, academic departments and groups of departments to submit nominations for the upcoming academic year.

The invitation covers scholars in the arts, humanities, life sciences and physical sciences, allowing nominations for any eligible candidate across these disciplines, and the program’s scope is limited to these four fields.

No deadline has been announced, but the program will review submissions and appoint selected scholars for the 2026-27 academic year, and the call remains open for submissions with no set deadline.
