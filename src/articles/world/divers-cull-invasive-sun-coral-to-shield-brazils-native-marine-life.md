Divers are actively removing sun coral (Tubastraea coccinea and T. tagusensis) from Brazil’s coastal waters as a direct effort to safeguard native marine ecosystems.

The sun coral, an Indo‑Pacific species first observed in Brazil in the 1980s on an offshore oil platform near Rio de Janeiro, has spread along the shoreline, outcompeting indigenous corals.

Conservation teams say the targeted culling is intended to halt the invasion and give native species a chance to recover.
