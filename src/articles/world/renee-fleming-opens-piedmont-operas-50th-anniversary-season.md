Piedmont Opera announced that world‑renowned soprano Renée Fleming will open its 50th Anniversary Season with a pair of specially curated events. The company, in partnership with regional health system Novant Health, will use the performances to explore how music can act as a therapeutic and connective force, underscoring the growing dialogue between the arts and science.

The first of the two events is scheduled for Sept. 18, when Fleming will lead a program that blends operatic repertoire with commentary on music’s health benefits. While the exact program details remain under wraps, organizers say the concert will illustrate the “transformative power of music to heal, inspire and connect,” a theme that aligns with Novant Health’s community‑wellness initiatives.

Piedmont Opera, founded five decades ago, has built a reputation for presenting both classic and contemporary works to audiences across the region. Reaching its half‑century milestone, the company sees the anniversary season as an opportunity to reaffirm its mission of cultural enrichment while forging new partnerships that extend beyond the traditional performing‑arts sphere.

Renée Fleming, a multiple‑Grammy‑winning soprano and former Metropolitan Opera star, brings a global profile to the celebration. Her involvement signals the season’s ambition to attract wider attention and to position Piedmont Opera as a hub where artistic excellence meets public health advocacy. The collaboration with Novant Health, a major health‑care provider, reflects a broader trend of institutions leveraging the arts to promote holistic well‑being.

In addition to the Sept. 18 opening, a second event will follow later in the season, though the date and format have not yet been disclosed. Both concerts are expected to feature a mix of arias, orchestral interludes, and spoken insights from medical experts, creating a multidisciplinary experience designed to resonate with both opera lovers and community members interested in health and wellness.
