Puerto Rico's Department of Family is losing its secretary, who announced Monday in San Juan that she is resigning from the post. The announcement was reported by The Associated Press from the island capital.

The secretary had been described as embattled as corruption allegations swirled around her, and her departure removes the head of a Puerto Rico government department from office.

No further detail on the allegations or on the terms of her exit was immediately established in the announcement.
