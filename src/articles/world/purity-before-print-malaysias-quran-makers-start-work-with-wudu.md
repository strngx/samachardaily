Inside Malaysia's largest Quran production center, the making of the sacred text begins with the maker, not the machine. Before entering the printing room in Putrajaya, every worker must perform 'wudu,' a rule that admits no exceptions.

The ablution is meant to prepare Muslims mentally and physically before they pray. Placing it at the printing room's threshold gives each production day a devotional starting point.

From the first application of ink to the completed scripture, the center presents its work as a fusion of printing craft and religious faith.
