The United States Air Force has taken home all of its bomber aircraft that were stationed at the RAF Fairford airfield in England. The base, operated by the United States, had been a launch point for strikes against Iran.

The withdrawal follows an investigation that uncovered a planned terrorist attack targeting the RAF Fairford facility. Security officials acted to remove the bombers while the probe continues, leaving the base temporarily without its bomber fleet.
