Virginia Tech opened its season with a statement victory, defeating the Virginia Military Institute 73-3 in the inaugural game at the newly built Franklin stadium. The Hokies' offense exploded for 73 points, while the Keydets managed only a field goal, underscoring a stark disparity between the two programs.

Running back Marcellous Hawkins Jr. was the catalyst for the offensive onslaught, rushing for three touchdowns. His ability to break through VMI's defensive front repeatedly highlighted the depth of Virginia Tech's backfield and set the tone for a dominant first half.

Jeffrey Overton Jr. complemented Hawkins' performance by amassing 108 rushing yards and finding the end zone twice. Overton's balanced attack, mixing power runs with quick cuts, kept the VMI defense off balance and contributed significantly to the Hokies' 73-point tally.

Beyond the standout individual efforts, Virginia Tech's entire unit executed at a high level. The offensive line opened lanes for both backs, the quarterback distributed the ball efficiently, and the defense limited VMI to a single field goal, forcing multiple three-and-outs. The comprehensive nature of the win reflects the Hokies' preparation and depth across all three phases of the game.

The victory not only marks a successful start to the season but also serves as a showcase for the new Franklin stadium, signaling that Virginia Tech intends to leverage its upgraded facilities to sustain competitive advantage. With such a decisive opening performance, the Hokies have set a high benchmark for the remainder of their schedule.
