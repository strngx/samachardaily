Prominent climate activist Greta Thunberg arrived Tuesday at the United Nations climate summit in Antalya, Turkey, urging delegates to prioritize people and the planet over profits and power. The activist framed the gathering as a moment of urgent consequence, stating that 'everything we love is at stake.'

The summit, known as COP31, convenes against the backdrop of a year marked by climate-induced heat waves and other extreme weather events. Thunberg's appearance at the conference underscores the continued mobilization of youth climate movements as world leaders gather to negotiate emissions reductions and adaptation measures.
