Attorneys for former FBI director James Comey plan to petition a federal judge to block the Justice Department from moving forward with its third criminal case against him. The filing seeks a court order that would halt any further prosecution while the request is considered.

Comey, known for his outspoken criticism of President Donald Trump, has repeatedly been the focus of Republican anger. The attempt to stop the case highlights the intersection of high‑profile legal actions and the political disputes surrounding the former bureau chief.
