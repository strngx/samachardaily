Officials in Gaza said a Palestinian infant died after a sharp drop in body temperature, and a forensic examination confirmed severe hypothermia. The child, identified as Kinan Ahmed Arqiq, was moved from Kuwaiti Hospital to Nasser Medical Complex on Thursday and arrived dead, according to the Gaza Health Ministry. The ministry said Friday.

The death reported by the Gaza Media Office is the enclave’s first cold-related fatality this winter, officials said. The ministry added that 10 people died from cold exposure in Gaza during the 2025 winter season.
