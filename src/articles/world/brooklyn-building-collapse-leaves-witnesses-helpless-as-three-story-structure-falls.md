Dervie Mapp, a 67-year-old retiree, was jolted awake by loud popping sounds outside her Brooklyn apartment and ran to her kitchen window overlooking a neighboring construction site. From there she watched helplessly as a three-story building on the far side of the worksite came crashing down.

The Associated Press reported Mapp's account of the sudden collapse, which unfolded in full view of residents living adjacent to the active construction zone. Details on the cause, timing, and any injuries were not immediately available in the initial witness report.
