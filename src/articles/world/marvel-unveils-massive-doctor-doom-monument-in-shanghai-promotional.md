Marvel has added a giant Doctor Doom statue to its promotional campaign, unveiling the monument in Shanghai, China. The installation follows earlier Doctor Doom displays and is the latest in a series of large‑scale villain monuments created to build anticipation for Avengers: Doomsday.

The massive figure quickly became a talking point among netizens, who shared images and debated the design online. The statue's arrival has sparked widespread discussion among fans on social media. Marvel continues its promotional rollout, using the statue to highlight the villain ahead of the movie's release.
