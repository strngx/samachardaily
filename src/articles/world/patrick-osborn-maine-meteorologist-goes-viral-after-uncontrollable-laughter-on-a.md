On a Wednesday afternoon, WABI-TV in Bangor, Maine, aired a weather forecast that quickly diverged from the usual calm delivery. As Osborn began describing a high-pressure system moving across the state, a sudden bout of laughter erupted, causing him to lose his train of thought. The clip, captured by the station's own cameras, shows Osborn trying to regain composure while his co‑anchor attempts to keep the segment on track. The laughter, which lasted several seconds, was not a rehearsed gag; Osborn later explained that a joke shared off‑air with a colleague triggered the reaction, and the humor lingered longer than expected. The station cut to a commercial break, but the moment had already been uploaded to social media platforms. Within hours, the video amassed millions of views on Twitter, TikTok, and YouTube, with users sharing the clip under hashtags like #PatrickOsborn and #WeatherLaughs. The meteorologist himself posted a brief apology on his personal Instagram, acknowledging the slip and thanking viewers for the support. While the incident was light‑hearted, WABI-TV issued a brief statement affirming its commitment to professional broadcasting standards, noting that live TV can sometimes produce unexpected moments. The station also highlighted that Osborn is a veteran meteorologist with over a decade of experience, suggesting that the episode was an anomaly rather than a reflection of his usual performance.
