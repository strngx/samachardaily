Prominent singer and politician Babul Supriyo has released a heartfelt musical tribute dedicated to the late vocalist Zubeen Garg, offering a specialized rendition of the iconic song 'Mayabini'. The tribute highlights the deep professional camaraderie and personal bond shared between the two acclaimed musicians.

Speaking on the release of the song, Supriyo reflected on his connection with Garg, stating that music remains perhaps the purest way to express his love and respect for the late artist. The homage centers on 'Mayabini', a composition closely associated with Garg's distinguished career and wide artistic appeal.

Zubeen Garg's expansive body of work across regional and national music landscapes left a lasting imprint on the Indian entertainment industry. Supriyo’s tribute brings renewed focus to Garg's musical contributions, re-engaging audiences with his timeless compositions.

The release of 'Mayabini' continues a long-standing tradition within the music community where peers pay homage to legendary artists through artistic reinterpretation. The gesture has resonated among listeners and members of the music fraternity honoring Garg's creative legacy.

As fans and fellow artists across the country reflect on the tribute, the song stands as a formal tribute to Garg's enduring impact on Indian music, keeping his repertoire vibrant for contemporary audiences.
