On September 9, the International Atomic Energy Agency's Board of Governors took the step of referring Iran to the United Nations Security Council over alleged non-proliferation breaches, marking the first such referral in two decades. Following the referral, Donald Trump issued a warning to Tehran on September 10 regarding renewed activity near the Pickaxe Mountain complex located close to Natanz.

Alongside escalating nuclear pressure, diplomatic engagements are unfolding in the region. Oman is preparing to host talks with Iran and Gulf states concerning the strategic Strait of Hormuz. Additionally, Iran and the United Arab Emirates have united with other BRICS members to call for political resolution and restraint across the broader regional conflict.
