Two months after Spain’s worst border crisis, migrants in a Ceuta tent camp face a worsening humanitarian crisis and are reassessing their options. Allegations of mistreatment by Spanish forces have heightened their uncertainty.

A 27‑year‑old Moroccan artisan says soldiers beat him and tied his face to a tree, accusing them of abuse. He has lodged a complaint and wants the soldiers held responsible.

Spanish prosecutors have launched an inquiry into the claims, pressuring authorities to improve camp conditions. The investigation will assess the allegations and decide if disciplinary or criminal action is needed.
