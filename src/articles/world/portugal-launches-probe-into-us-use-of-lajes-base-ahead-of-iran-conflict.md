Portugal’s Public Prosecutor’s Office has opened an inquiry into the legality of U.S. military use of Lajes Air Base as the United States prepares for operations against Iran.

The investigation follows a complaint filed by the Left Bloc in April that asked the Attorney General to examine whether the base’s use violates Portuguese and international law, calling it a “gross violation of International Law.” Left Bloc coordinator Joss Manuel Pureza said the government appears to be complicit.

RTP reported that the probe will assess compliance with domestic statutes and treaty obligations.
