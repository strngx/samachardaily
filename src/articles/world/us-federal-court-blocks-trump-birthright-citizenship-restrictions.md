In Nathan v Trump, a federal court in Washington has blocked the administration's attempt to create further exceptions to birthright citizenship. The ruling concerns an August executive order targeting additional categories of US-born children after the Supreme Court rejected earlier restrictions in June.

The August 6 order directed agencies to withhold citizenship from children whose parents included certain foreign-government employees, individuals defined as alien enemies, and those in commercial birth arrangements including paid surrogacy. The court said the president cannot create new exceptions to the constitutional guarantee for children born in the United States.

ACLU partners including the Asian Law Caucus, NAACP Legal Defense and Educational Fund, and Democracy Defenders Fund filed the Washington lawsuit on September 28.
