Chief Executive John Pfeifer said Oshkosh Corp.'s third-quarter 2026 results fell short of expectations as supply chain inefficiencies for thousands of fire truck parts reduced builds and deliveries in the vocational segment. The company reported consolidated sales of $2.8 billion and adjusted earnings of $2.41 per share.

The company cut its full-year 2026 adjusted earnings per share forecast to $8.50 to $9.50 from $11. Consolidated operating income fell 24.3 percent year over year, while the transport segment dropped $25.9 million after last year's one-time $25 million Pentagon intellectual property sale.

Oshkosh still expects 25 to 30 percent growth through 2028 and strong demand for fire trucks and postal vehicles, Pfeifer said.
