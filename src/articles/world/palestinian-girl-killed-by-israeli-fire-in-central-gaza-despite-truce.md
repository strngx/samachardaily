A Palestinian girl, Misk Hazem al-Dalou, died from wounds sustained when Israeli forces opened fire toward tents housing displaced Palestinians in the Maghazi refugee camp in central Gaza, medical sources said, WAFA reported.

The fatal shooting occurred on Sunday despite a ceasefire in effect in Gaza since Oct. 10, 2025, amid continued Israeli violations of the agreement, according to the report.

Separately, Israeli military vehicles opened heavy fire toward tents sheltering displaced Palestinians in the al-Mawasi area of Rafah in southern Gaza, and Israeli artillery shelled areas northeast of Khan Younis, the report added.
