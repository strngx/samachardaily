During the opening minutes of today's BBC Breakfast, host announced a personal tribute to Dolly Parton, who passed away earlier this year, expressing how her music shaped his own upbringing.

Co‑presenters confirmed that the programme is exploring several ways to commemorate the legend, including a dedicated feature segment, live performances by UK artists, and a partnership with the Dolly Parton Foundation for a charitable fundraiser.

BBC officials said any tribute will be carefully coordinated with Parton's estate to ensure authenticity, and that the final format will be announced in the coming weeks, aiming to celebrate her legacy while supporting music education initiatives.
