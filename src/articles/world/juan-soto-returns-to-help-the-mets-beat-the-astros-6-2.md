Soto was cleared to play just before the game after a brief stint on the 10‑day injured list, where he was treated for a right‑leg contusion sustained in a previous outing. The Mets announced his activation early Saturday, allowing him to line up in the leadoff spot.

In the fourth inning, Soto fouled a ball off his right leg but stayed in the box and, on the very next pitch, drove a go‑ahead single that plated the tying run. The Mets added four more runs, holding the Astros to two and securing a 6‑2 win that snapped a brief slump.

The victory improves the Mets’ standing in the National League East and provides a morale boost as they chase a playoff berth. Soto’s quick return also eases concerns about his health, with the club indicating he is cleared for full participation moving forward.
