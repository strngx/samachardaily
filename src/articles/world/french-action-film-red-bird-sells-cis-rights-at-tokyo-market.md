The CIS region, covering Russia and other former Soviet states, is now served by Kinologistika LLC for the French action film "Red Bird." Cappu Films closed the sale at the Tokyo International Film Festival's TIFFCOM market.

The American indie romance "Step Back, Doors Closing" is bowing at the Tokyo market, and Cappu Films has taken on its international sales. Both titles are available to international buyers at the festival's TIFFCOM market.

Both the French action feature and the American indie romance are being offered to international buyers at TIFFCOM during the festival.
