A new report offers fresh insight into Haitian gangs, revealing they are backed by political and economic actors as the country gears up for its first general elections in more than a decade.

The findings highlight the gangs' operational tactics and raise concerns about electoral security, underscoring the potential influence of armed groups on the democratic process. Analysts say the exposure could shape international aid and security planning for Haiti's fragile democracy.

The report, released ahead of the vote, maps the networks linking gang leaders to local officials and businesses, offering authorities a roadmap for intervention.
