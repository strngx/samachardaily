Yemeni government forces said on Saturday they carried out 875 operations against the Houthis in recent hours, according to military spokesman Majed al-Nazili. The operations targeted Houthi positions, reinforcements and military equipment as part of Operation Dawn of Yemen. Warplanes, drones and artillery supported the operations, which destroyed or damaged 50 military vehicles and pieces of equipment, including missile launchers.

In southwestern Taiz province, government troops and allied Popular Resistance fighters continued to repel Houthi attacks and target the group's positions and movements.

The statement was posted on X, the US social media company.
