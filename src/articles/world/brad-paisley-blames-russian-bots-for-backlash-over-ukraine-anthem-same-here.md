Brad Paisley, 54, released the solidarity song Same Here in 2023, which included a recording of Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky. The song was intended as a show of support for Ukraine's people. The singer says he has noticed the online reaction to the track begin to shift.

He now attributes the backlash to "Russian bots," claiming they are attacking his Ukraine anthem on social media. Paisley's statement highlights the ongoing digital warfare surrounding cultural support for Ukraine.

The incident underscores how artistic expressions of solidarity can become battlegrounds in geopolitical tensions, with online manipulation influencing public perception of humanitarian gestures.
