The upcoming Far Cry television adaptation for FX is taking shape as co‑showrunners Rob Mac and Noah Hawley confirm a high‑profile ensemble for the first season, featuring Lizzy Caplan, Steve Buscemi, Brendan Gleeson, Jennifer Jason Leigh, Hamish Linklater, Jamie Demetriou and Sam Spruell.

Leigh arrives fresh from acclaimed roles in *The Hateful Eight* and *Fargo*, while Spruell was recently seen in *Game of Thrones: A Knight of the Seven Kingdoms*. Demetriou is known for *Only Murders in the Building*, Linklater lends his voice to the Dark Knight in *Batman: Caped Crusader*, and Gleeson, celebrated for *Spider‑Noir* and *The Banshees of Inisherin*, is best remembered as Knuckles McGinty in *Paddington 2*.

The anthology format mirrors the video‑game series’ tradition of new settings each season, meaning the cast may rotate in future installments. Ubisoft, which has been quiet on the Far Cry franchise since the 2021 release of *Far Cry 6*, is developing *Far Cry 7* under Creative House 1 (Vantage Studios), the same studio handling *Assassin’s Creed* and *Rainbow Six*. The studio benefits from a significant $1 billion investment by Tencent, underscoring strong financial backing for the franchise’s expansion.
