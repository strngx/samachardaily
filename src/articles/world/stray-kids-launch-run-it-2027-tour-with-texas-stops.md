Stray Kids' 2027 'Run It' tour kicks off in Bangkok on January 16, 2027, launching a 25-city, seven-month global journey across Asia, the U.S., Canada, and Europe. The tour supports their new album 'This & That,' which debuted at No. 1 on the Billboard 200 in August 2026, making Stray Kids the first act to debut nine charting albums at the top spot.

The group will stop in Arlington, Texas at AT&T Stadium on June 3, and Houston at Reliant Stadium on June 5. Lee Know will be absent from the North American and European legs as he begins mandatory military service. Tickets go on sale November 18, 2026, through the Stay Membership Presale.
