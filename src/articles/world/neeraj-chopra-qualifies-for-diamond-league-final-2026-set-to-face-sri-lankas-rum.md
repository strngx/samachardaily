At the Lausanne Diamond League meeting on [date], India's leading javelin thrower Neeraj Chopra recorded a season‑best distance of 88.05 metres, finishing second behind Sri Lanka's Rumesh Pathirage. The throw, achieved in the fifth round of the competition, not only marked Chopra's longest effort of the 2025‑26 season but also secured the automatic qualifying mark for the Diamond League final scheduled for 2026. The Swiss venue, known for its fast runway and supportive crowd, saw a tight duel between the two athletes, with Pathirage edging Chopra by a narrow margin of 0.30 metres. By finishing among the top two, Chopra met the World Athletics‑set criteria that grant direct entry to the season‑ending Diamond League final, which will be contested in Zurich later next year. His rival, Rumesh Pathirage, who threw 88.35 metres to win in Lausanne, also secured his place, setting up an anticipated showdown between the two Asian javelin specialists. Both athletes have been on an upward trajectory this season, with Chopra improving from his 86.70m mark earlier in the year and Pathirage breaking the 88‑metre barrier for the first time. The final will feature the world’s elite, including Olympic champion Johannes Vetter and Czech prodigy Jakub Vadlejch, making the Chopra‑Pathirage clash a focal point for Asian representation. Chopra, the 2020 Olympic gold medallist and former world champion, entered the 2025‑26 season seeking to reclaim the top spot after a modest start that saw him miss the early Diamond League meets. His 88.05m effort places him within striking distance of the 90‑metre mark that has become the benchmark for medal contention at major championships. The performance also boosts his world ranking, positioning him among the top five javelin throwers globally. As the Indian athletics federation looks to build on his success, the upcoming final offers a platform to gauge his form ahead of the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics.
