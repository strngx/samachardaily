London police detained 139 demonstrators outside the US Embassy after they voiced support for the banned Palestinian advocacy group Palestine Action. The operation resulted in the apprehension of all 139 individuals, highlighting a major enforcement action against pro‑Palestinian activism in the capital.

The arrests were carried out ahead of a forthcoming Supreme Court appeal that will review the legality of the group's activities. Authorities linked the timing of the detentions to the pending judicial review, emphasizing the ongoing legal scrutiny of the organization.

The mass arrests underscore the escalating tensions surrounding pro‑Palestinian protests in the UK and may influence the court's considerations in the appeal. No further details on charges or next steps have been released.
