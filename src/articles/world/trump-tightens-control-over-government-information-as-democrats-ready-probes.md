Donald Trump has issued an order tightening his administration's control over government information, a directive reported by Hindustan Times and described as part of a wider effort to shape what reaches Congress.

Democrats are preparing investigations into the administration, and the order is presented as a means of limiting those inquiries by restricting the official material lawmakers can obtain.

The directive is not an isolated step. It sits within a broader push to control the flow of government information, with congressional access to executive branch records the point of contention.
