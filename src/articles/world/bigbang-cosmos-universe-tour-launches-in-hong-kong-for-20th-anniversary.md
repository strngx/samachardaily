BIGBANG's COSMOS universe tour kicks off in Hong Kong at Kai Tak Stadium on November 13, 14, 15, and 17, 2026. Presented by TME live and YG Entertainment with ELF ASIA, FWD Insurance, Visa, Trip.com Group, Klook, and Samsung as joint sponsors, the event brings the 20th anniversary of the group's debut to life with immersive performances and fan experiences.

The production includes two drone light shows over Victoria Harbour on November 12 and 14, plus a COSMOS BIGBANG 20TH ANNIVERSARY MEDIA EXHIBITION opening November 18 through December 27. Attendees at the Hong Kong stop can collect commemorative postcards and fridge magnets, while interactive installations throughout the venue encourage fan engagement.
