A court in Aix-en-Provence has opened a criminal investigation into Ateliers Hugo, a celebrated workshop in southern France, following a complaint from the Picasso family estate. The inquiry centres on silver plates and gold medallions attributed to Pablo Picasso that were produced under licence.

Legal sources confirmed the probe targets the workshop's handling of the licensed editions. The Picasso estate's complaint triggered the judicial scrutiny, though specific allegations were not disclosed.
