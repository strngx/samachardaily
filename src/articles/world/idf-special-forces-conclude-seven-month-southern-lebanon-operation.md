IDF special forces have successfully concluded seven months of intensive combat in southern Lebanon, marking the formal end of a prolonged military campaign against Hezbollah forces operating in the region.

During the operation, Israeli forces destroyed more than 900 Hezbollah terror sites while simultaneously seizing RPGs, explosives, drones, and Kalashnikovs positioned along the Litani River corridor.

This conclusion follows weeks of sustained ground engagement and represents a significant phase in efforts to degrade Hezbollah's operational capacity in the southern border zone.
