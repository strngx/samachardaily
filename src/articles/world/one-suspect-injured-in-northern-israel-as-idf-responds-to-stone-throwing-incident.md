Israeli military forces intervened in northern Israel following a confrontation where suspects threw stones near a grazing area. An initial assessment indicated that a shepherd was present in the location when the stone-throwing began and subsequently withdrew from the site.

The Israel Defense Forces dispatched personnel to manage the security disturbance. During the military response to the stone-throwing incident, one suspect sustained injuries.
