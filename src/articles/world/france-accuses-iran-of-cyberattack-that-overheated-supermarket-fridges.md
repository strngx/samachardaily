France’s Foreign Ministry accused Iran of a cyber intrusion on October 12 that caused supermarket refrigeration units to overheat, impacting roughly 7% of France’s supermarkets.

The breach affected roughly 700 of France’s 10,000 supermarkets, raising temperatures dangerously and disrupting normal cooling operations, and Paris summoned the Iranian ambassador on Monday to protest. The affected stores represented about 7% of the nation’s retail network.

Authorities have not disclosed any further details about the attackers or the motive behind targeting retail cooling systems. Paris summoned the Iranian ambassador on Monday, signaling a formal protest.
