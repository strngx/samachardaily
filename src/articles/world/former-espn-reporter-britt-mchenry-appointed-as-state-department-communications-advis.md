The U.S. State Department announced that former ESPN reporter Britt McHenry will join its communications team as a press advisor, focusing on media strategy and outreach.

McHenry’s appointment does not make her the department’s official spokesperson; that role remains separate and will continue to be filled by the existing senior official.

The State Department described the new position as one centered on developing communications plans, coordinating with journalists, and shaping the department’s public messaging.

McHenry’s background includes years of experience in sports journalism at ESPN, where she covered major events and conducted high‑profile interviews, giving her a strong foundation in media relations.

Officials said the hire reflects a broader effort to modernize the department’s outreach and to bring fresh perspectives from the private media sector into diplomatic communications.

The announcement did not include details about McHenry’s specific projects or the length of her tenure, but it emphasized that her role will be advisory rather than operationally authoritative.
