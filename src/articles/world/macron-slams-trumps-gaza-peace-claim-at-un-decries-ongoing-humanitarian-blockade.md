French President Emmanuel Macron rebuked President Donald Trump’s portrayal of his Gaza peace plan as a diplomatic success, insisting that humanitarian access to the enclave has yet to be restored.

Delivering his last address to the United Nations before leaving office, Macron warned that escalating violence and settlement activity in the West Bank are eroding the prospects for a viable Palestinian state.
