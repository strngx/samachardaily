Spire Motorsports has suspended NASCAR Cup Series driver Carson Hocevar from Sunday's season finale at Homestead-Miami Speedway following racing incidents. The team announced the sidelining and removed Hocevar from the season-ending event.

In a statement, Spire said "racing at the Cup level is a privilege, and it comes with an expected level of professionalism." The organization tied the suspension to on-track conduct standards.

Hocevar will miss the season finale as a result of the team's decision.
