Officials confirm Ukraine has resumed drone and missile strikes on a Russian oil refinery. This escalation comes despite prior negotiations for a short-lived ceasefire, marking a return to kinetic operations against Russian energy infrastructure in the region.

The targeted facility is situated in Russia's eastern industrial corridor, where the refinery serves as a major energy production hub. Ukrainian military sources describe the campaign as part of sustained pressure on Russian strategic assets during the ongoing conflict, aiming to degrade enemy logistics and supply chains.
