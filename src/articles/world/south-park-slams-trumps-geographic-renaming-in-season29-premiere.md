The season‑29 premiere of Comedy Central’s South Park aired Wednesday night, targeting Donald Trump with a fresh wave of satire that mocked his controversial geographic name changes and again depicted the former president naked.

In a tongue‑in‑cheek move, the show announced it was renaming itself to “South America,” a direct jab at Trump’s late‑August executive order that renamed Lake Ontario as Lake America.

Creators Trey Parker and Matt Stone issued a statement that referenced Apple and Google “bowing down” to Trump’s renaming, and they thanked parent company Paramount with the phrase “a Skydance Capitulation.”
