President Donald Trump continued his midterm campaign swing with a Friday rally in Alabama. The appearance marked a return to the venue where he originally drew his first large-scale crowd, even though the state is rarely classified as a key competitive battleground.

With just weeks remaining in the midterm election cycle, the campaign stop underscores ongoing efforts to stump for candidates. Addressing the crowd, Trump stated that he intends to return to the state again next week.
