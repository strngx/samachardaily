Former Cuban President Raúl Castro has made a rare public appearance at an official military event in Havana, joining senior officials and service personnel to celebrate the anniversary of a key army branch. The specialized military division is directly responsible for safeguarding Cuba's top political and state leadership.

The commemorative event brought together military personnel and officials to mark the founding anniversary of the specialized executive protection unit. Castro, who previously served as president and commander-in-chief before stepping down from formal executive duties, attended the official proceedings held in the capital.

The military unit being recognized holds a vital operational mandate within Cuba's national security apparatus. Tasked with providing personal security and operational defense for high-ranking state leaders, the elite formation serves as a foundational pillar of internal protection and political stability.

Public appearances by Castro have become increasingly infrequent following his transition out of official executive office. His presence at high-level state functions, particularly those involving the armed forces and executive security, highlights the historic revolutionary figure's continued symbolic importance within the nation's political and military structures.

The gathering emphasized the deep institutional ties between Cuba's political authority and its military forces. By attending the anniversary ceremony, the former president highlighted the central role of specialized military branches in maintaining state continuity and protecting executive leadership.
