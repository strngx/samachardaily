On Saturday, Iraq's government moved quickly to manage the repercussions after Iran‑backed militias were blamed for striking and shutting a key Saudi oil pipeline that runs through Iraqi territory.

Baghdad officials confirmed the sabotage originated inside Iraq and announced a formal investigation to identify those responsible and prevent further disruptions.
