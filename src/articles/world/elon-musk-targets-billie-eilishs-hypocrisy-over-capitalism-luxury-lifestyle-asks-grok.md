On X, Elon Musk posted a thread accusing Billie Eilish of hypocrisy, pointing to her recent comments about capitalism and juxtaposing them with images of her wearing high‑end designer items and driving expensive cars. Musk framed the critique as a broader rebuke of artists who, in his view, profit from the very system they denounce.

In the same thread, Musk invited his AI chatbot Grok to generate a "vulgar roast" of the singer, asking the model to produce language he described as "raw" and "unfiltered." The request quickly trended, with screenshots of the AI‑generated text circulating among users, though the full output was not publicly released by Musk.

The post sparked a flurry of reactions: fans of Eilish condemned the remarks as a personal attack, while some commentators highlighted Musk's pattern of using provocative language to dominate social media discourse. Eilish’s publicist has not issued a statement, and the singer’s official channels remain silent.
