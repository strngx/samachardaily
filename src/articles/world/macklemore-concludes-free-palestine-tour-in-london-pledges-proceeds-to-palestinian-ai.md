Macklemore concluded his Free Palestine Tour with a concert in London on Friday, Anadolu reports.

He pledged to donate the proceeds to organizations supporting the Palestinian people, and was joined onstage by prominent singers, poets and activists, including survivors of the Holocaust and the Nakba.

The London appearance followed his removal from Ed Sheeran's tour after he voiced support for Palestine, and featured performers and speakers who took the stage before Macklemore's set.
