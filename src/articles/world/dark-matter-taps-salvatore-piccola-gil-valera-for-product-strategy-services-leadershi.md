Dark Matter, a provider of technology solutions for the mortgage and broader financial services sectors, announced on Monday that it has elevated two industry veterans to senior leadership roles. Salvatore Piccola will assume responsibility for product strategy, while Gil Valera will head professional services. Both appointments are part of a broader effort to sharpen the company’s roadmap and improve how clients implement its tools.

Piccola brings more than two decades of experience in mortgage‑originated technology, having previously overseen product development at several large lenders. Valera, meanwhile, has a track record of scaling enterprise‑technology services across multiple verticals, including fintech and cloud‑based platforms. Their combined expertise is expected to deepen Dark Matter’s insight into the operational challenges faced by its customers and translate that knowledge into more intuitive, adaptable solutions.

In his new role, Piccola will lead a cross‑functional team tasked with defining feature priorities, aligning development cycles with market demand, and ensuring that the product suite remains competitive against emerging alternatives. Valera will focus on professional services, overseeing implementation, integration, and post‑deployment support to help clients extract maximum value from Dark Matter’s offerings. Together, they will report directly to the company’s CEO and work closely with engineering, sales, and customer success groups.

The appointments come at a time when the mortgage technology landscape is undergoing rapid digitisation, with lenders seeking faster, more secure ways to process applications and manage data. By strengthening its product strategy and services capabilities, Dark Matter aims to reduce implementation friction and accelerate time‑to‑value for its users. Industry observers note that such leadership moves often signal a company’s intent to capture greater market share and deepen client relationships.

Both Piccola and Valera will begin their duties immediately, with initial priorities including a review of the existing product roadmap, the rollout of a new client onboarding framework, and the launch of a series of best‑practice workshops for existing customers. Their appointments have been welcomed by Dark Matter’s board, which described the hires as “critical to delivering on our commitment to innovation and customer success."
