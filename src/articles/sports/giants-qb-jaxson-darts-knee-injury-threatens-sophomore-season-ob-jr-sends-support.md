New York Giants quarterback Jaxson Dart sustained a brutal knee injury during the team’s Week 2 matchup on Monday Night Football. Early reports indicate the damage is worse than initially feared, raising the possibility that his sophomore campaign could be cut short.

Dart joins a recent string of Giants players who have endured serious knee injuries, with several teammates suffering similar setbacks last season. Following the incident, wide receiver Odell Beckham Jr. posted an inspiring message to Dart, urging him to stay strong.
