Harry Kane, England’s leading striker, has indicated that he may pursue an NFL kicking position once his soccer career ends, according to The New York Times.

Reuters, ESPN, CBC and Yahoo Sports report that Kane is also contemplating a dual‑sport future, potentially playing in Major League Soccer while training as a placekicker.

Analysts remain skeptical, noting the considerable differences in technique and physical demands between field soccer and professional kicking, and stress that no official commitment has been made.
