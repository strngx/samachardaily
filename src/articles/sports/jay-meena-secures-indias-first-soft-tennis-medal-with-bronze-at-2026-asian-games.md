Jay Meena, an Indian soft tennis player, won the bronze medal in the men’s singles event at the 2026 Asian Games held in Aichi‑Nagoya.

The medal represents India’s first ever podium finish in soft tennis at the Asian Games, giving the nation its inaugural success in the sport at the continental multi‑sport event.
