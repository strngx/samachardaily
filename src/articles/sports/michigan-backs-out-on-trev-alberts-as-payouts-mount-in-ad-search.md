The University of Michigan has backed out on Trev Alberts, the man it had expected to install as its next athletic director. The sudden reversal has left fans asking questions about how the school reached the decision.

Writing for ESPN, Wetzel described a continuing tailspin at Michigan, with payouts piling up after the failed appointment. The potential cost of the episode has been called staggering.

The fallout could also damage Michigan's search for an athletic director, according to analysts. Toledo Blade's Briggs was blunter still, saying the school's administrative incompetence 'knows no bounds.'
