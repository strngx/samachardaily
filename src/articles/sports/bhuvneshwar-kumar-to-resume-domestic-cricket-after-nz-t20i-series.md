Bhuvneshwar Kumar, the 36‑year‑old India pacer, said he did not chase his recent comeback. He explained that he is taking the situation one step at a time, emphasizing a gradual return. His comments came after he recently rejoined the Indian team.

The bowler is keen to return to domestic cricket after the New Zealand T20I series, despite having only recently rejoined the Indian side. He plans to play in domestic cricket after the series.

He plans to take things one step at a time, focusing on consistent performances in domestic matches.
