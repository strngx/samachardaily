Raffaele Palladino voiced regret after Bologna squandered a lead in a 3‑1 defeat to Inter, warning that leaving the game in balance invites trouble, and quoted, “When you leave it in the balance, you are asking for trouble.”

Bologna entered the fixture on an unbeaten streak, but Inter’s second‑half surge erased their advantage, producing a 3‑1 loss that underscored the risk of hesitating when the scoreline is tight.

Palladino’s comment highlights that a single lapse can alter momentum, reminding clubs that composure in close moments is crucial for avoiding defeat. The defeat also halted Bologna’s unbeaten run, emphasizing the need for steadiness in tight contests.
