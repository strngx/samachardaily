On Sept 10, 2026, Ludhiana Lions wicketkeeper‑batter Salil Arora spoke to IANS from New Delhi, calling the Sher‑E‑Punjab T20 League a ‘big opportunity’ for aspiring cricketers in the state to catch the eye of Indian Premier League (IPL) franchises and secure professional contracts.

The Sher‑E‑Punjab T20 League, launched earlier this year, brings together regional sides from across Punjab in a fast‑paced, Twenty‑20 format. Organisers designed the tournament to fill a gap between state‑level cricket and the national spotlight, giving emerging talent a platform to perform under conditions similar to the IPL and to be evaluated by scouts who attend the matches.

Arora emphasized that strong performances in the league can open doors to the IPL, noting that franchises routinely monitor domestic T20 competitions for fresh prospects. He said the league’s visibility, combined with its competitive intensity, allows players to demonstrate the skill set IPL teams value – power hitting, agile fielding and the ability to finish games under pressure.

As a regular member of the Ludhiana Lions, Arora has been using the tournament to showcase his dual role as a reliable wicket‑keeper and a middle‑order batter. While he refrains from naming personal statistics, he highlighted that consistent contributions with both the gloves and the bat are essential to stand out among the pool of hopefuls vying for a limited number of IPL slots.

The broader cricketing community in Punjab sees the league as a catalyst for talent development. By providing a high‑profile stage, the Sher‑E‑Punjab T20 League not only raises the standard of local competition but also aligns the region’s player pipeline with the demands of the IPL, potentially increasing the number of Punjab‑born cricketers entering the premier league in coming seasons.
