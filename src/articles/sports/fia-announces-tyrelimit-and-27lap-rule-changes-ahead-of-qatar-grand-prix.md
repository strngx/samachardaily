The FIA issued a key regulatory decision before the Qatar Grand Prix at Lusail International Circuit, introducing a 2026 tyre‑limit rule and extending the race distance to 27 laps.

The tyre limit is set to influence team tactics across practice, qualifying and the race, with McLaren pointing out a possible early‑stop advantage under the longer lap count.

Officials also published the weekend timetable, including practice, qualifying and race start times, together with a Lusail weather forecast to aid team preparations.
