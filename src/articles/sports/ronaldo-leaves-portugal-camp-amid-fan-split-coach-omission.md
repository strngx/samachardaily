Cristiano Ronaldo confirmed his departure from Portugal’s training camp on Wednesday, shortly after speaking with Portuguese Football Federation president Pedro Proenca. The exit followed coach Jorge Jesus’s statement that he would not start the veteran against Denmark.

Fans have reacted sharply, splitting between those defending the five‑time World Cup winner and critics who say he should have walked away from international football years earlier. The episode has been described as “always drama” by supporters.

The split underscores a broader debate over Ronaldo’s international future, with supporters debating whether his departure marks the end of an era or a long‑overdue decision. The controversy continues to dominate Portuguese football discourse.
