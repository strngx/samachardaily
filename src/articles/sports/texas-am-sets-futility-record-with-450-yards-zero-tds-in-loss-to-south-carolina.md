Texas A&M made unwanted history Saturday, becoming the first FBS program to amass at least 450 total yards with zero turnovers and zero touchdowns in the same game, yet still losing to South Carolina.

The Aggies controlled the ball for over 34 minutes and converted 26 first downs but could not reach the end zone, settling for field goals and stalled drives in a statistical anomaly that underscored their red-zone struggles.
