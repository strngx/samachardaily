Carlo Ancelotti revealed Brazil's starting eleven for the upcoming international friendly against Australia, confirming that Raphinha, Vinicius Junior and 17‑year‑old Endrick will spearhead the attack.

Midfielders Matheuzinho and Vitor Reis are set to earn their senior debuts, while seasoned campaigners Bruno Guimaraes and Marquinhos provide experience across the spine of the side.

The match forms part of Brazil's post‑World Cup schedule, offering the coach a chance to integrate new talent ahead of future competitive fixtures.
