On Monday, the Senate voted overwhelmingly to pass legislation that would establish federal standards for how college athletes are compensated. The measure also aims to shield the NCAA from antitrust lawsuits concerning eligibility rules and other restrictions, and to limit mid‑season transfers of coaches.

If enacted, the law would bring federal oversight to athlete pay, reduce legal exposure for the NCAA, and alter the dynamics of coaching moves during a season.
