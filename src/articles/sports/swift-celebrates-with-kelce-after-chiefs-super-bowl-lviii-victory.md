Taylor Swift joined Travis Kelce on the Allegiant Stadium field moments after the Kansas City Chiefs secured a 25-22 overtime win over the San Francisco 49ers in Super Bowl LVIII on February 11. The Chiefs tight end, wearing No. 87, embraced and kissed his girlfriend amid the confetti celebration in Paradise, Nevada.

Photographer Mark J. Rebilas captured the postgame scene as Swift, who attended the championship game, shared the victory moment with Kelce following Kansas City's second consecutive Lombardi Trophy.
