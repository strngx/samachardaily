A five-match winless run is over for Mexico after a 1-0 victory against Jamaica. Jesús Corona struck in the first half to settle the game and restore winning form for the national side.

Corona's goal carried added significance as he returned to the national team after a four-year absence. His first-half finish proved the only goal of the match and settled the outcome.

The result secures Mexico's place in the CONCACAF Nations League semifinals, allowing the team to move beyond a difficult stretch of form and back into knockout contention.
