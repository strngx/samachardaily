Ohio State secured a 19-15 win over Indiana in a tightly contested game. The turning point came early in the fourth quarter when quarterback Julian Sayin connected with Jeremiah Smith on a 38-yard touchdown pass, keeping the lead intact despite a potential comeback.

However, the victory was bittersweet as Ohio State's starting quarterback faced a setback when his left foot was ruled out during the pivotal moment. The injury to the starter marked a significant hurdle for the team heading into the postgame period.
