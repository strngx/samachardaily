Caitlin Clark’s appearance on ESPN’s College GameDay in Iowa City pulled in 3.7 million viewers, a figure nearly triple the average audience for the WNBA’s first‑round games and underscoring her strong pull on sports audiences.

The broadcast came just two days after Clark lost a Thursday‑night contest in Las Vegas, returning to her old home turf in Iowa City to show her quick turnaround from the court to the studio.

In Iowa City she joined the GameDay crew to pick college football games, speaking before a crowd that voiced its opinions on the selections.
