At the Asian Games in Nagoya, the Indian women's shooting team earned a silver medal in the trap event. Neeru Dhanda delivered standout individual performance, posting a record-setting qualification score that propelled the squad to the podium.

On the men's side, the team faced a tough run, ultimately placing sixth. Despite strong individual efforts, including Shapath Bharadwaj's successful qualification for the final round, the overall result reflects ongoing competition challenges.
