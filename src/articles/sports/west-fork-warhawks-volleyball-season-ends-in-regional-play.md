West Fork's volleyball season has ended after the Warhawks competed in Top of Iowa Conference and Class 2A regional play. The team traveled to West Hancock on Tuesday night for a conference match and overcame an early deficit to win in four sets.

That four-set victory completed their conference tournament outing, but regional play brought the Warhawks' campaign to a close. The Warhawks are based in the Britt/Clarion area of Iowa.

Spencer Reporter published the report from Britt/Clarion, Iowa.
