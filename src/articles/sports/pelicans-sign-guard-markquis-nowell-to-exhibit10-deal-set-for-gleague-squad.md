The New Orleans Pelicans have officially signed 5-foot-7 point guard Markquis Nowell to an Exhibit 10 contract, positioning the playmaker to participate in training camp before joining the franchise's NBA G-League affiliate, the Laketown Squadron.

An Exhibit 10 agreement is a standard one-year, minimum-salary NBA contract that includes specific affiliate bonus clauses. Under the terms of the deal, if Nowell is waived by the Pelicans at the conclusion of the preseason and subsequently spends at least 60 days with the Laketown Squadron, he will qualify for an agreed financial retention bonus while remaining eligible for NBA call-ups.

Nowell joins the Pelicans organization following previous professional stints, including a two-way contract with the Toronto Raptors and their G-League affiliate Raptors 905, as well as time with the Rio Grande Valley Vipers. Most recently, the former Kansas State NCAA standout competed in Europe with Spanish club Baskonia, where he averaged 5.9 points and 3.1 assists per game in international competition.

The transaction provides New Orleans with additional guard depth and developmental continuity as the franchise prepares its roster for the upcoming season.
