Meenu Dahiya Johal, the wife of the TOPS chief executive, has been named coach‑cum‑manager for Indian sailor Priyanshi Patil at the Youth Olympics. The appointment has sparked a conflict‑of‑interest controversy within the sailing community.

Critics have raised questions about Johal’s qualifications in sailing, arguing that young athletes need expert coaching to ensure safety and performance. The sports ministry has defended the selection, pointing to Johal’s own sailing achievements and administrative background.

Despite the ministry’s support, concerns about coaching quality and potential conflicts continue to dominate discussions among sailors and officials, highlighting the broader debate over governance and athlete development in the sport.
