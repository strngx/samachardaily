Team India secured the Asia Cup title, but Pakistan’s board chief chose to skip the subsequent trophy presentation ceremony.

The BCCI described the chief’s absence as an “institutional decision”, fueling discussion over whether the move was a principled stance or a display of political optics.

In the Nation Wants To Know episode hosted by Shreya Dhoundial, the controversy was placed within the wider India‑Pakistan cricket dispute, touching on future match prospects and the ongoing “Cup Chori” debate over the trophy.
