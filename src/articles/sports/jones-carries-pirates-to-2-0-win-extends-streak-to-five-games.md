Jared Jones limited the St. Louis Cardinals to a single hit across seven innings, retiring the first 16 batters and preserving a perfect game into the sixth inning before the lone hit arrived.

The 2-0 victory gave Pittsburgh its fifth straight win, moving the club nearer to its first winning season in eight years.

Nathan Church broke up Jones' perfect bid with a double, accounting for the only hit the Cardinals recorded during the game.
