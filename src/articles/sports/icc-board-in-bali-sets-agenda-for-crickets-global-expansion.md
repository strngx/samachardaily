The ICC Board convened in Bali, Indonesia, to shape cricket’s future, tackling governance reforms, franchise league structures, and event planning for the sport’s global trajectory and strategic direction. Key topics included governance reforms, franchise league structures, and event planning, laying the groundwork for cricket’s long-term expansion.

ICC Chairman Jay Shah emphasized collaboration and inclusivity as essential to expanding cricket’s global footprint, while highlighting Indonesia’s rapid growth in infrastructure and participation, supported by significant investments.

The agenda focuses on enhancing sustainability and competitiveness, especially for emerging nations, offering a comprehensive overview of upcoming developments in the sport and outlining future strategic priorities.
