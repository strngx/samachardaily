Ben Shelton will represent the United States in Sunday’s US Open men’s final, where he will meet Germany’s Alexander Zverev after advancing through the tournament’s draw.

His appearance revives American hopes for a men’s Grand Slam title, the country’s first in 23 years, prompting fans to rally behind the flag‑bearing contender.
