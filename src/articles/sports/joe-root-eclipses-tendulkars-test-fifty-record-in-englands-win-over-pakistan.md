England’s Test captain Joe Root broke Sachin Tendulkar’s long‑standing record for the most half‑centuries in Test cricket.

The milestone came in the third and final Test of the series at Edgbaston, where Root remained unbeaten on 62 runs from 39 balls, striking nine fours.

His innings helped England chase down Pakistan’s target of 130, securing an eight‑wicket victory and sealing the series.
