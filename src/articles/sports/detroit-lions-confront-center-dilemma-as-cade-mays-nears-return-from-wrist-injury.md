The Detroit Lions are navigating an unexpected challenge at the center position. Cade Mays, signed to a three-year, $25 million contract to bolster the offensive line, is currently sidelined with a fractured wrist.

In Mays' absence, Scruggs has stepped up and delivered performances that have convinced many he deserves to keep the starting role. His solid play has created a competitive dilemma for the coaching staff.

As Mays nears his return, the Lions face a crucial choice: revert to their high-signing or continue with the in-form Scruggs. This decision will shape their offensive strategy and lineup stability.
