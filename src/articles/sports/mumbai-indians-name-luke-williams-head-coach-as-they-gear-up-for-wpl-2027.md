On Thursday the Mumbai Indians franchise announced that Luke Williams will take over as head coach, succeeding Lisa Keightley who has decided to move on from the role.

Keightley, appointed in September 2025, guided the two‑time champions to WPL titles in 2023 and 2025, but the side slipped to a fourth‑place finish in the five‑team tournament in 2026 – its poorest showing in four seasons – after an Eliminator exit in 2024.

Williams said the appointment offers "a great opportunity to build something together, play an exciting brand of cricket and compete for success" as the team prepares for the 2027 WPL campaign.
