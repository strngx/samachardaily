The show marked the first boxing event presented by its promoter. The report notes that fighters require bravery to enter the ring, while a first-time promoter staging an outdoor autumn show also needs courage. No venue, date, participant, or beneficiary details were disclosed.

The coverage framed the promoter's decision to hold the event outdoors in autumn as an act of courage comparable to stepping into the ring.
