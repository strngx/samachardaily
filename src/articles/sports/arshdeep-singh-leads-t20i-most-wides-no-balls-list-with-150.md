India fast bowler Arshdeep Singh has conceded the most wides and no-balls in T20 Internationals, with a combined total of 150 deliveries comprising 132 wides and 18 no-balls.

Bangladesh left-arm pacer Mustafizur Rahman follows with 146 such deliveries, while Ireland's Mark Adair is third at 143. India all-rounder Hardik Pandya and Zimbabwe's Richard Ngarava are tied at 116 each.

Tim Southee, Romario Shepherd, Jason Holder, Kagiso Rabada and Mitchell Starc complete the top 10 with totals between 113 and 101. The statistics show only one aspect of bowling, with wickets, economy and consistency also key to judging effectiveness.
