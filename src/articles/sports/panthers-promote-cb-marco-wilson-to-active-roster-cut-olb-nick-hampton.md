The Carolina Panthers are promoting cornerback Marco Wilson from the practice squad to the 53-man roster, according to Adam Schefter. Wilson has started the past three games for Carolina after injuries depleted the cornerback room and has exhausted his standard practice-squad elevations.

To make room, the team will release outside linebacker Nick Hampton, per Mike Kaye. The move locks Wilson onto the active roster for the remainder of the season as Carolina addresses ongoing secondary depth concerns.
