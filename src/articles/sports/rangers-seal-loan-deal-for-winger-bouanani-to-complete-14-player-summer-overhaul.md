Rangers have finalized their summer transfer window business with the loan acquisition of winger Bouanani, bringing an end to an extraordinarily active period of player recruitment. The arrival of the winger marks the 14th and final addition to the squad during a window characterized by extensive reshuffling across the roster.

The temporary transfer for Bouanani provides the Scottish side with additional attacking options along the flanks as they prepare for a heavy schedule of domestic and European fixtures. Club officials confirmed that the agreement completes their recruitment objectives for the current window, capping weeks of targeted negotiation.

The decision to complete 14 separate player deals represents a major rebuilding effort designed to increase squad depth and tactical flexibility. By securing Bouanani on a loan agreement late in the window, management satisfied its requirement for wide attacking cover while maintaining strategic financial flexibility.

Throughout the summer transfer window, Rangers focused on revamping multiple areas of the pitch to construct a dynamic, competitive squad. The completion of this final deal officially closes incoming transfer activity at the club, allowing coaching staff to fully pivot toward squad integration and upcoming match preparation.

With the transfer deadline now passed and the incoming roster locked, the immediate challenge lies in integrating Bouanani and the other 13 new arrivals into the tactical framework. Observers will be assessing how quickly this overhauled unit can build chemistry and deliver results in competition.
