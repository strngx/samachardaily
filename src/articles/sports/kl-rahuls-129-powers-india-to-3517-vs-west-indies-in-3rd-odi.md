KL Rahul's explosive unbeaten 129 off 87 balls anchored India's dominant batting performance, helping the team post 351/7 in the third ODI against West Indies in Mullanpur on Saturday.

Opening the innings, Rohit Sharma contributed a fluent 92, forming a strong foundation before departing. The hosts' bowlers struggled to contain the Indian batsmen throughout the innings.

Set a massive target of 352 runs under lights, West Indies will need a near-perfect performance to chase down the total in the remaining overs.
