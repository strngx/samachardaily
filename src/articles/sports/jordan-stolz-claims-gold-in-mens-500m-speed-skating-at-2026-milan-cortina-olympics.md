Jordan Stolz of the United States won the men's 500m speed skating gold medal at the Milan-Cortina 2026 Winter Olympic Games, held at the Milano Speed Skating Stadium in Milan on February 14, 2026.

Following his victory, Stolz raised the U.S. flag on the podium, a visual salute to his country after crossing the finish line first.

The triumph adds to the United States' overall medal tally and highlights the nation's strength in short‑distance speed skating at the Games.
