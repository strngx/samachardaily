Three Dallas Mavericks players have been identified as the NBA’s hottest trade candidates as the franchise prepares for its 2026‑27 training camp.

The speculation arrives while the Mavericks, still in a rebuild after the February 2025 trade of Luka Doncic, aim to build around second‑year cornerstone Cooper Flagg, whose presence fuels optimism that Dallas could rise soon.
