The Bulldogs opened their conference slate on Saturday at Stillwater, facing a hostile crowd in the stadium that has become a fortress for the home side. Both squads entered the matchup with high expectations, the Bulldogs hoping to capitalize on a revamped offense while Stillwater aimed to defend its home turf record.

Lucas, making his first start at quarterback, completed 18 of 27 passes for 215 yards, two touchdowns and one interception. He also contributed a 32‑yard scramble that set up the Bulldogs’ opening score. Despite his promising debut, the Bulldogs struggled to contain Stillwater’s rushing attack, which amassed 180 yards on the ground, ultimately outgaining the visitors 375‑340 in total offense.

Coach Mark Daniels praised Lucas’ poise but emphasized the need for quicker decision‑making under pressure. The loss drops the Bulldogs to 0‑1 in conference play, and the coaching staff announced an extra‑day film session before the team’s next road game against the River City Raiders next week.
