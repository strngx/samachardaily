Both Gray and Robinson have, by their own admission, performed relatively well to keep their teams in contention up to the middle of September, yet they now confront uncomfortable questions about their future roles.

This surge of fan unrest adds a layer of uncertainty to the clubs' preparations, making the managers' job security a central narrative as the quarter-final fixtures approach.
