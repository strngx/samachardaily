The American federation is seeking a new coach for its men's national team. The organization is consulting within its national setup as it works through the coaching vacancy and leadership change.

The American federation is leaning on U.S. women's national team coach Emma Hayes to help find the new men's team coach. Hayes will offer insight on what to look for in a new leader, according to the development. She will speak to the criteria for the appointment.

The men's team coaching position remains open as the federation weighs its next leader.
