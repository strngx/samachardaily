Asian Games 2026 gold medallist Lovlina Borgohain has joined Delhi Police women personnel for a foot patrol operation in Rajouri Garden. The boxer highlighted that such community initiatives aim to strengthen women's safety and foster empowerment in local neighborhoods.

By participating alongside police officers during the patrol, Borgohain demonstrated commitment to public service and gender-focused security outreach. Her involvement underscores efforts to build confidence and enable women to move freely without fear in shared spaces.
