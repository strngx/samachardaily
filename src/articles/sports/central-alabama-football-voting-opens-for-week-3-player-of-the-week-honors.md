Fan voting has officially opened for the Central Alabama high school football Player of the Week following an action-packed Week 3 on the gridiron. Organized in partnership with Southlake Orthopaedics, the weekly award recognizes standout individual performances across regional prep football programs in Central Alabama.

High school sports enthusiasts, students, and local community members are invited to cast their votes online through Alabama Local News. The voting window remains active until the strict cutoff deadline at noon on Wednesday, giving supporters a designated period to rally behind their chosen nominees.

The Southlake Orthopaedics Player of the Week initiative aims to highlight exceptional athletic talent across Central Alabama schools throughout the regular season. Each week, impressive field performances earn student-athletes a featured spot on the official ballot, sparking spirited online engagement among regional fanbases.

Weekly recognition programs of this nature serve as a key element of local sports coverage, drawing attention to outstanding offensive, defensive, and special teams plays. As Week 3 concludes, fan participation will directly determine which standout athlete earns the top regional accolade for this round of game action.

All ballots must be submitted electronically prior to the Wednesday noon deadline to be included in the official tally. The final results and the winner of the Week 3 Southlake Orthopaedics Central Alabama top football player award will be announced shortly after voting concludes.
