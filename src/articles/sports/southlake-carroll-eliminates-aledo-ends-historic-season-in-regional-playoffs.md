Southlake Carroll advanced in the regional tennis playoffs by defeating Aledo on Oct. 12. The match took place at the Bayard H. Friedman Tennis Center on the Texas Christian University campus. Carroll’s victory secured its place in the next round of the competition.

The defeat marked the end of Aledo’s historic season, which had featured a series of notable achievements throughout the year. With the loss, the Aledo program’s run concluded at the regional level, closing a memorable campaign for the Texas high‑school team.
