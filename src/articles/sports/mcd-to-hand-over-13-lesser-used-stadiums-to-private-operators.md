The Municipal Corporation of Delhi is preparing to hand over 13 low-use stadiums to private managers. The civic body announced the plan, which would bring private administration into the oversight of these sports venues.

The MCD has set eligibility criteria for the operators. Candidates must have experience coaching multiple sports, directly operate coaching centres, and develop sports curricula. The criteria focus on coaching and curriculum development.

The arrangement includes no-cost coaching for school students. The MCD has linked the operator selection process to those eligibility criteria. The free coaching is part of the plan.
