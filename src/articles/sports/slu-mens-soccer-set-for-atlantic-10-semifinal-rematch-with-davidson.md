SLU men's soccer will face Davidson in the Atlantic 10 semifinal on Tuesday, giving the Billikens a chance to avenge a late‑season setback.

Davidson's decisive goal in the final regular‑season game denied SLU the Atlantic 10 title, creating a rematch that could reshape the conference standings. That goal, scored in the closing minutes, turned a potential title for SLU into a disappointment, and now the teams will meet again under the bright lights of the A‑10 semifinals.

The outcome will determine which squad moves forward in the tournament, with the victor poised to pursue the conference tournament.
