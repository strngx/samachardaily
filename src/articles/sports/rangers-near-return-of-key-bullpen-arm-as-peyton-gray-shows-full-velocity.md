In Arlington, Texas, right‑hander Peyton Gray threw a 20‑pitch session on Saturday, and, according to manager Skip Schumaker, his velocity matched the level he had before his injury.

Schumaker’s remarks indicate the Texas Rangers are edging toward restoring a key bullpen arm, a development that could strengthen their relief corps as they pursue a postseason berth.
