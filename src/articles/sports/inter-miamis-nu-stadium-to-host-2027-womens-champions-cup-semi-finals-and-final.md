Inter Miami announced on Saturday that its newly‑opened Nu Stadium will host the semi‑finals, bronze‑medal match and final of the 2027 Women’s Champions Cup, scheduled for January 2027.

The venue selection follows a decision by the FIFA Council in April to award Miami the final phase of the competition, which pits the reigning continental club champions from UEFA, CAF, CONCACAF, CONMEBOL, OFC and AFC against each other in a single calendar year.

The agreement, confirmed jointly by FIFA and Inter Miami, marks the first time the tournament’s concluding matches will be staged at an MLS club’s purpose‑built stadium, underscoring the growing profile of women’s club football in North America.
