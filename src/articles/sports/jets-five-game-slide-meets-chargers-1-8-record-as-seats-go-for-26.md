The New York Jets, coached by Aaron Glenn, have lost five consecutive games, dropping to a losing record. The visiting Los Angeles Chargers sit at 1-8, making the matchup a contest between two struggling teams.

Ticket prices for the game have plummeted to roughly $26, barely more than a cheeseburger at SoFi Stadium, underscoring the lack of fan interest. Both franchises are already looking toward the upcoming NFL Draft, treating the contest as a low‑priority affair.

The cheap seat availability highlights the broader context of a season where neither club expects much from the remainder of the year, with the Jets' slide and the Chargers' dismal record limiting attendance and revenue.
