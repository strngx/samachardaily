UC Irvine began its season by defeating Chapman 92-66, and the scoring was spread among the starters with three players reaching double-digit points. The win marked the Anteaters' first victory of the season.

Luke Isaak posted 16 points and three steals, while Olivier Rioux logged a double-double with 13 points and 12 rebounds, showcasing strong offensive and defensive contributions. The performance highlighted the team's depth and early-season momentum.

Tama Isaac added 12 points, showing the depth of the Irvine roster as the team posted a strong early performance. The victory gave the Anteaters early confidence.
