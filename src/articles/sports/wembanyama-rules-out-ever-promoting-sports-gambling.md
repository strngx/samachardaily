San Antonio Spurs star Victor Wembanyama has drawn a firm line in the sand when it comes to sports gambling endorsements. When asked by the San Antonio Express-News whether he would ever promote a sports betting or prediction-market company, Wembanyama responded without ambiguity: 'Absolutely not.'

The 7-foot-4 forward, who has become one of the NBA's most marketable young stars, made clear that while he is open to other business ventures and sponsorships, sports gambling will not be among them. His stance comes as the league continues to grapple with the growing presence of betting partnerships across teams and players.

Wembanyama's decision reflects a personal boundary that could influence future endorsement choices, particularly as athletes navigate increasing opportunities in the sports-betting space. The Spurs star did not elaborate further on his reasoning behind the decision.
