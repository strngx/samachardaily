No. 3 Florida escaped with a 76-67 victory against Milwaukee on Tuesday night in Gainesville, Fla., as Rueben Chinyelu delivered a milestone performance.

Chinyelu posted his first double-double of the season. The milestone came as Florida defeated Milwaukee by nine points in the 76-67 final on Tuesday night in Gainesville.

He also made the first 3-pointer of his college career. The shot added a career first to Chinyelu's performance in No. 3 Florida's 76-67 victory against Milwaukee.
