Delhi Capitals are assembling a revised coaching setup ahead of the 2027 Indian Premier League season. According to a report by ESPNcricinfo, former Sri Lanka pace bowler Chaminda Vaas is positioned to join the franchise as its new bowling coach.

The strategic restructuring also features changes at the top of the management hierarchy, with former India captain Sourav Ganguly expected to assume duties as head coach. Additionally, former Indian spinner Amit Mishra is tied to the incoming coaching staff changes as the Delhi-based franchise reshapes its backroom team.
