Notre Dame released an injury report Monday, ruling out captain Drayk Bowen, the team's leading tackler and radio helmet communicator, for Saturday's clash with No. 1 Miami. The news removes a key defensive anchor ahead of the high-stakes contest.

The Fighting Irish are shaping their linebacker corps to fill the void, though no replacement has been announced. The coaching staff will adjust game plans and rotations to compensate for the loss of a player who leads the tackles and receives coordinator signals.

The absence of Bowen, who recorded 45 tackles this season, could impact Notre Dame's defensive rhythm against Miami's potent offense. No confirmed next steps reported yet.
