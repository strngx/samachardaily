India A's top order crumbled on day one of the first unofficial Test against New Zealand A, losing wickets in quick succession. The visitors were bowled out for a modest total, handing the hosts a 22-run advantage at the close of play.

The 22-run lead gives New Zealand A a platform to build on as the match moves into day two, with both sides looking to consolidate their positions in the four-day encounter.

India A's very tough outing on the opening day underscores the challenge of adapting to New Zealand conditions.
