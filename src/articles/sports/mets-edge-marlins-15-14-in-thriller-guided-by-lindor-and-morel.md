Francisco Lindor delivered a pivotal hit in the ninth inning while José Morel provided crucial relief pitching, steering the New York Mets to a 15-14 victory over the Miami Marlins on Tuesday night.

The Mets overcame a five‑run deficit early in the game, rallying with a series of timely hits that included a two‑run double by Lindor and a clutch single by Jeff McNeil that set up the go‑ahead run.

Morel entered the game in the eighth inning with the score tied at 13-13 and retired two batters before issuing a walk that forced a single‑run rally by the Marlins, prompting the Mets to bring in closer Edwin Díaz for the final out.

The win marks the Mets' third consecutive triumph this week, improving their record to 45-38, while the Marlins fall to 38-45, keeping both clubs in the tight race for the National League East playoff spots.

Manager Buck Showalter praised Lindor’s leadership at the plate and Morel’s composure under pressure, noting that the team's depth and resilience were key factors in securing the narrow win.
