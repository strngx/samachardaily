The No. 20 Swampscott girls soccer team saw its Div. 3 state tournament hopes end in a 3-0 defeat on Tuesday night, sealing an early exit from the competition and bringing the season’s campaign to a close.

Joey Barrett delivered a competitive performance, but the offensive gap proved decisive as the opposition capitalized on mistakes to secure the shutout victory.

The loss marks the second consecutive season Swampscott failed to advance beyond the opening round, leaving the program to regroup for the upcoming season. Coach’s feedback highlighted areas for improvement, and the roster is already planning drills to address defensive vulnerabilities.
