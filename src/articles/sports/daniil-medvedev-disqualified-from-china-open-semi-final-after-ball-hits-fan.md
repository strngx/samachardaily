Officials disqualified Daniil Medvedev from his China Open semi-final fixture against Novak Djokovic following an on-court incident involving a spectator.

Medvedev hit a ball into the stands while trailing 7-5, 5-3 against the Serbian. The rebounding ball struck a spectator directly in the eye, resulting in his prompt default from the tournament match.

The event mirrored Novak Djokovic's own default during the 2020 US Open, which took place when an accidentally hit ball struck a line judge.
