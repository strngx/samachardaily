Virat Kohli made history in Wellington by shattering Ricky Ponting’s long‑standing massive ODI record, a breakthrough that marks a new benchmark for Indian batting. The feat came in the second ODI of the series.

The achievement also highlights Kohli’s unique status as the only batter to average 50 or more in all four SENA countries, and India secured a win over New Zealand on Wednesday. His consistent scoring across Australia, New Zealand, South Africa and England has set a new standard for international batsmen.

India’s victory reinforces the team’s ODI dominance and cements Kohli’s legacy as one of cricket’s all‑time greats. The win also boosted India’s confidence ahead of upcoming tournaments.
