Amen and Ausar Thompson have become the first brothers in NBA history to each sign a contract extension worth at least $100 million, marking a new financial milestone for sibling athletes.

Ausar secured a fully guaranteed five‑year, $155 million deal with the Detroit Pistons, while Amen agreed to a five‑year, $208 million extension with the Houston Rockets, reflecting the clubs’ long‑term commitment to the twins.

Together, the extensions bring the Thompson twins’ combined career earnings to $439 million, establishing them as the highest‑paid twin pair in NBA history.
