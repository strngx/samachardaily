Dmitry Bivol is gearing up for his second outing of 2026, fulfilling a WBO mandatory by traveling to face Liverpool‑born Callum Smith in a December bout.

Should Bivol defeat Smith, a high‑profile fight is projected for the first half of 2027, described as a potential blockbuster on the light‑heavyweight calendar.

The upcoming contest follows Bivol’s two recent legendary clashes with fellow light‑heavyweight Artur Beterbiev, after which the Russian champion returned home to Russia.
