Former India all‑rounder Sandeep Patil said there would be no problem if Rohit Sharma or Virat Kohli were part of India’s squad for the 2027 ODI World Cup.

Patil also highlighted the side’s strong bench depth, indicating that the team can accommodate either star without compromising overall balance.
