Manchester United are set to kick off their UEFA Champions League league phase campaign under manager Michael Carrick against Azerbaijani side Sabah FK, fielding a drastically transformed team. A sweeping squad makeover orchestrated by Old Trafford hierarchy in recent transfer windows has left just four players remaining from the club’s previous appearance in Europe’s premier club competition.

The Red Devils were handed a demanding fixture schedule when the Champions League draw was conducted last month. Alongside their opening fixture against minnows Sabah FK, United have been paired against major continental heavyweights including Bayern Munich and Atletico Madrid, testing the depth and competitive readiness of Carrick's newly assembled group.

The extreme turnover highlights an aggressive rebuilding process conducted by Manchester United leadership ahead of their return to top-tier European football. With only four stalwarts surviving the structural roster overhaul since their last Champions League outing, Carrick faces the immediate tactical challenge of forging quick cohesion within a revamped playing group on the elite stage.

The upcoming match against Azerbaijan-based Sabah FK serves as a critical entry point for United in the tournament's revamped league phase. While facing underdog opposition presents a distinct opportunity to secure three points early, the broader fixture list ensures Carrick’s refreshed squad will soon face stern tests against traditional European powerhouses.
