Ireland head coach Andy Farrell is set to announce the Nations Championship squad amid a No‑8 shortage. He will be without Caelan Doris and Jack Crowley, who are unavailable for the autumn fixtures, intensifying the selection challenge for the back‑row position.

Benson and McNabney have earned their first caps, while the returns of Porter, Baird and Hansen add experience to the lineup. Their inclusion is expected to bolster Ireland’s options as Farrell finalises the roster for the upcoming Nations Championship matches. The blend of fresh talent and seasoned players aims to address the depth concerns highlighted by the coach.
