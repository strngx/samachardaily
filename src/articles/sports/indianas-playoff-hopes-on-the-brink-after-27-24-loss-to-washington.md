Indiana's College Football Playoff hopes were dealt a blow on Saturday as the Hoosiers lost 27-24 to Washington. The defeat left the team on the brink of elimination from the postseason race.

Special teams struggles resurfaced when kicker Nico Radicic had a 46-yard field goal blocked in the second half. The Hoosiers subsequently benched Radicic and turned to backup kicker McAteer.

The loss underscored Indiana's special teams struggles, which have at times been a challenge for the team. The Hoosiers will need to address their kicking woes to remain in playoff contention.
