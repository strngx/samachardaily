Durban's Super Giants have appointed Kane Williamson as their new head coach for SA20 Season 5, handing him charge of the franchise's coaching set-up for the campaign.

Williamson was originally named in the Super Giants squad for the season, but the franchise has now elevated him to a coaching capacity. That shift leaves a vacancy in the playing roster, which Durban intend to fill.

A replacement player will be named in due course, the franchise said. The Super Giants will therefore enter Season 5 with a new head coach and a squad still to be finalised.
