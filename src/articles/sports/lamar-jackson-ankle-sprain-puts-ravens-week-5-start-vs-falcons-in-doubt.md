Lamar Jackson sustained a sprained left ankle, leaving his availability for the Ravens’ Week 5 matchup against the Atlanta Falcons uncertain. According to NFL Network’s Ian Rapoport, Jackson has only an outside chance to suit up for the Sunday night game.

Rapoport added that the quarterback could miss multiple games, which would place backup Tyler Huntley in line to start if Jackson cannot return in time. The Ravens now face a potential quarterback dilemma heading into the contest.
