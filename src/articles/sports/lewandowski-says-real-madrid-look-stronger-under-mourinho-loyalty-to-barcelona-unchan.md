Polish forward Robert Lewandowski, who spent three seasons at Camp Nou, told reporters that Real Madrid appear "stronger" with veteran manager Jose Mourinho at the helm. The comment came during a post‑match interview in which Lewandowski was asked to weigh in on the shifting power dynamics in La Liga after Mourinho’s recent appointment.

Lewandowski, a former Barcelona striker who scored 68 goals in 115 appearances, emphasized that the Portuguese coach’s tactical discipline and experience have added a new dimension to Madrid’s play. He cited the team’s improved defensive organization and sharper counter‑attacking transitions as evidence of Mourinho’s impact.

Despite the compliment to his former rivals, Lewandowski was quick to underline where his personal loyalties lie. He reiterated his enduring connection to Barcelona, noting that his most cherished memories remain tied to the Catalan club’s recent successes and the fans who supported him during his tenure.

Mourinho, a four‑time Champions League winner with different clubs, took charge of Real Madrid earlier this season, inheriting a squad that had struggled for consistency. Lewandowski’s assessment reflects a broader sentiment among pundits that the manager’s pragmatic approach could restore Madrid’s competitiveness in both domestic and European competitions.

The former striker’s remarks arrive as the league approaches a crucial phase, with Barcelona, Real Madrid, and Atletico Madrid locked in a tight title race. Lewandowski’s perspective adds a rare voice from a player who has experienced both sides of the rivalry, offering insight into how managerial changes can reshape team dynamics.
