Gurugram will host the Women’s Indian Open from October 22 to 25, gathering prominent international players for the high-profile Asian leg of the Ladies European Tour.

Defending champion Shannon Tan of Singapore will headline the competition. Joining her in the field are former tournament winners England’s Liz Young, who claimed the title in 2024, and Germany’s Olivia Cowan, the 2022 winner.

Contestants will compete over four days to claim the coveted trophy in one of the region's major professional golf championships.
