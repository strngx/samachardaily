NFL Week 1 2026 viewership fell 13% from the previous year and 9% from 2024, yet the opening week remained the third‑most‑watched in the past decade, trailing only the two prior seasons.

The league’s revised schedule featured a rare Wednesday kickoff between the New England Patriots and Seattle Seahawks, a Thursday game in Australia between the San Francisco 49ers and Los Angeles Rams, an 11% drop for NBC’s kickoff broadcast, flat Netflix coverage of the Melbourne match, and a double‑header Sunday format with Fox and CBS sharing 1 p.m. and 4:25 p.m. ET slots; CBS’s late‑afternoon broadcast of the Vikings‑Packers game fell 22% while NBC’s Sunday Night Football posted a 4% gain, the best since 2015, and ESPN recorded its highest‑rated Monday Night Football game in 20 years, ranking third overall.

Despite the overall decline, the NFL still dominated the top ten programs, claiming eight spots, and Week 2 will see Amazon Prime Video broadcast the Detroit Lions‑Buffalo Bills matchup at the new $2.2 billion Highmark Stadium on Sunday, while primetime showcases for the Kansas City Chiefs and a Monday Night Football clash between the Rams and Giants are slated for the upcoming week.
