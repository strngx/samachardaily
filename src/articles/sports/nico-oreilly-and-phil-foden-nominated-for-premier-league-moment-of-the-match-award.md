Manchester City’s emerging talent Nico O'Reilly and established star Phil Foden have each been placed on the shortlist for the Premier League’s Adobe Express Moment of the Match award for the month of August. The nominations were announced by the league on Tuesday, highlighting the two players’ decisive contributions in the club’s early‑season fixtures.

The Moment of the Match award, introduced as part of the Premier League’s partnership with Adobe Express, recognises a single instance of brilliance that captures the drama of a game. Fans and a panel of experts vote on a curated list of moments, with the winner receiving a cash prize and prominent placement on the league’s digital platforms. The award aims to celebrate individual skill and the narrative power of football’s most memorable flashes.

Both O'Reilly and Foden earned their spots after delivering standout actions in August’s matches. While the league has not disclosed the exact incidents, the nominations reflect moments that resonated with supporters and analysts alike – from a decisive pass to a game‑changing finish. Their inclusion underscores City’s depth, with a teenager and a senior international both capable of producing match‑defining quality.

City’s forward Rayan Cherki also features in the Premier League’s Goal of the Month shortlist, further illustrating the club’s offensive potency. Cherki’s nomination, announced alongside the Moment of the Match list, adds another layer of competition for the English side as it seeks to dominate individual accolades as well as the league table.

The final voting period will run over the next two weeks, during which fans can cast their votes via the Premier League’s official website and social channels. The winner will be revealed in a live announcement later this month, with the league promising extensive coverage across its broadcast and digital partners.
