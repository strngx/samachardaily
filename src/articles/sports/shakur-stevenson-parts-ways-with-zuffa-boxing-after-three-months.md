Shakur Stevenson has officially parted ways with Dana White's Zuffa Boxing. The undefeated four-division world champion announced Monday that he has left the promotion just three months after signing a multi-fight agreement.

The boxing promotion is backed by UFC CEO Dana White. Stevenson's departure marks an abrupt end to a partnership that was announced just three months prior.

Stevenson is an undefeated four-division world champion. His departure from Zuffa Boxing comes just months after the promotion launched.
