Rising ATP star Ben Shelton and United States women’s soccer forward Trinity Rodman have forged a mutual support system that is drawing attention at the US Open. The pair, who met through mutual friends in the sports circuit, have been spotted exchanging words of encouragement on and off the court, with Shelton crediting Rodman’s optimism for helping him stay focused after his high‑profile victory over world No. 2 Carlos Alcaraz.

Shelton’s win against Alcaraz, a match that thrust him into the tournament’s spotlight, was followed by a brief, awkward moment when he forgot to thank Rodman for her presence in the locker room. Rodman’s reaction – a playful, “What about me?” grin captured by on‑site photographers – quickly went viral, underscoring the light‑hearted chemistry between the two athletes.

Rodman, who is making her US Open debut in the women’s singles draw, has spoken publicly about Shelton’s influence on her mindset. In a recent interview with the Olympics website, she described him as “pretty inspiring,” noting that his self‑belief after a tough five‑set battle helped her push through a challenging practice session. Their friendship, she said, is built on a shared understanding of the pressures that come with rapid ascents in their respective sports.

Both athletes have also used their platforms to highlight the importance of mental health and peer support. Shelton, who entered the ATP Tour as a qualifier, has been vocal about the loneliness of the tour and how Rodman’s candid conversations about anxiety have helped him develop coping strategies. Conversely, Rodman has credited Shelton’s willingness to listen without judgment for giving her confidence to speak out about gender expectations in professional sports.

The partnership extends beyond personal encouragement; they have jointly appeared in charity events promoting youth sports in their hometowns of Atlanta and San Diego. Their collaborative efforts are being watched closely by sponsors, who see the duo as a fresh, cross‑sport marketing narrative that resonates with younger audiences.
