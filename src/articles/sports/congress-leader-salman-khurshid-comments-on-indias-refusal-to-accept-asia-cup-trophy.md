On Monday, Congress leader Salman Khurshid reacted to the Indian women's cricket team's decision not to accept the Women’s T20 Asia Cup trophy, which was to be presented by Asian Cricket Council President and Pakistan Cricket Board chief Mohsin Naqvi.

Khurshid described the refusal as a symbolic gesture meant to convey a message during a period of strained relations between the cricketing bodies.
