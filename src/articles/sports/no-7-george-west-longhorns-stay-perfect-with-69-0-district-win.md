The Longhorns defeated the Falfurrias Jerseys 69-0 on Friday, Oct. 9 at home in George West, carrying their perfect record into District 16-3A Division II play this season. The game served as the District 16-3A Division II opener for both teams.

The shutout win marks the Longhorns' first district contest of the season, keeping their unbeaten record intact as district play begins early for the seventh-ranked squad. George West now moves forward within the new official district standings following the decisive victory, while Falfurrias starts its district schedule with a loss on Friday.
