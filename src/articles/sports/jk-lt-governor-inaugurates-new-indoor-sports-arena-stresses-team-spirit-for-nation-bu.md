Lieutenant Governor Manoj Sinha inaugurated the newly built Multi-Utility Indoor Sports Arena at Sainik School Nagrota on Wednesday, addressing cadets, faculty and guests. He emphasized that sports cultivate leadership, resilience and team spirit, which he described as vital for nation-building.

The arena, completed in Jammu, offers modern facilities to enhance athletic training and promote teamwork among students. Sinha’s remarks linked these qualities to broader national development objectives.

The inauguration underscores the administration’s focus on integrating sports into educational institutions to strengthen communal cohesion. It reflects a strategic push to use athletics as a tool for leadership development across the union territory.
