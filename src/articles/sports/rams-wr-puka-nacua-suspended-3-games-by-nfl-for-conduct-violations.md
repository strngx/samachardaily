Los Angeles Rams wide receiver Puka Nacua has been suspended for three games by the NFL for violating the league's personal conduct policy.

Nacua will serve the ban while already on injured reserve, meaning the suspension overlaps with his injury absence rather than starting after he returns.

The NFL's personal conduct policy governs player conduct, and the league determined Nacua's actions warranted a three-game suspension while he remains on injured reserve.
