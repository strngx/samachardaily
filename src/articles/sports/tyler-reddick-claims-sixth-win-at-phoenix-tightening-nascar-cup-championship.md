Tyler Reddick earned his sixth win of the season in a NASCAR Cup Series playoff race on Sunday at Phoenix Raceway, near Avondale, Arizona. The victory positions Reddick among several drivers who made significant gains in the championship standings.

The win came on a difficult day for series leader Kyle Larson, who struggled at the Arizona track. Reddick's performance tightened the playoff picture, highlighting the competitive nature of the Cup Series.

The result also elevated Reddick higher in the points list, while Larson's poor performance cost him ground in the playoff hunt. Both drivers remain key contenders as the series continues.
