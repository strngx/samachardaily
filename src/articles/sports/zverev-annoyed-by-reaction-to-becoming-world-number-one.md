Alexander Zverev became the world number one after a Paris Masters defeat, but he said he is the only player who has had to explain why it's OK for him to be at the top of the rankings.

The German tennis player expressed frustration with the reaction to his new status, suggesting that other players have not faced the same scrutiny or criticism.

Zverev's comments came after a loss at the tournament, where he was still ranked highest in the world despite the result.
