In Eugene, Oregon, the Summit Storm boys continued their historic season by capturing the 5A cross country state championship, finishing first with a low score of 28 points and placing all five of their top runners inside the top nine. The victory marked a continued historic run for the Storm this season.

Landon Kjorstad led the charge as the team’s top runner, while Caldera’s Maddie Carney placed second individually. Their depth was evident as each of the top five runners finished within the top nine positions, underscoring the Storm’s dominance at the meet.
