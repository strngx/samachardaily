New York Giants players say the toughest training camp they have experienced in the NFL, overseen by John Harbaugh, equipped them to defeat Dallas in the season opener.

The victory puts the Giants at 1‑0 for the first time since 2022, even though only three members of that opening‑day lineup remain on the roster this year.

Harbaugh stresses that the rigorous camp has built a lasting foundation, and he does not anticipate a letdown as the team travels to face the Los Angeles Rams on Monday night.
