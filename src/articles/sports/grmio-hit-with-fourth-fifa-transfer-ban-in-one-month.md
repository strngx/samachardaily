Grêmio has been sanctioned with a fourth FIFA transfer ban in the span of a single month, according to a report from Brazilian sports outlet ge. The Rio Grande do Sul club now faces another window of restrictions on registering new players.

The rapid succession of bans compounds the club's squad-planning difficulties ahead of the domestic season. FIFA typically imposes such sanctions over unpaid transfer debts or contractual disputes, though the specific triggers for each ban were not detailed in the initial report.
