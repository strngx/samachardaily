Liverpool academy graduate Kieran Morrison has been handed a forthright assessment after his initial weeks with Sheffield Wednesday, with the club’s coaching staff underscoring that "hard work comes first" in his development plan.

The 20‑year‑old midfielder joined Wednesday on a season‑long loan from Liverpool in early August, aiming to gain regular first‑team minutes in the Championship. In his first training block, he impressed the coaching team with his intensity but was reminded that raw talent must be matched by consistent effort.

Head coach Darren Moore said Morrison’s technical ability is evident, yet the club expects him to "show up every day, give 100 per cent, and let the work speak for itself" before any further praise is offered. The manager added that the youngster’s attitude will determine how quickly he can become a regular starter.

Sheffield Wednesday’s recent influx of new signings has seen several players make an immediate impact, and Morrison is expected to follow suit if he adheres to the hard‑work mantra. The club’s sporting director, Paul Cook, noted that the loan arrangement is designed to accelerate Morrison’s growth through competitive minutes and a demanding training environment.

Morrison, who featured for Liverpool’s U23 side last season, expressed gratitude for the candid feedback, stating that he is “eager to prove myself and earn my place by putting in the work every day.” His first appearance for Wednesday is slated for the upcoming league fixture against Bristol City.
