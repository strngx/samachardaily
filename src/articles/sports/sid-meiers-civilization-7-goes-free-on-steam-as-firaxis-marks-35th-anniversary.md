Steam users can currently play Sid Meier's Civilization 7 at no cost for a full week following a promotional event launched on the platform. Strategy developer Firaxis Games has opened complete trial access to the title through September 10, allowing players worldwide to download and test the release.

The limited-time offer coincides with a major milestone for the historic grand strategy franchise. Firaxis is officially celebrating 35 years of the Sid Meier's Civilization series, marking three and a half decades since the original turn-based strategy title first launched.

Characterized as one of the most divisive entries in the long-running franchise, Civilization 7 has generated widespread debate among strategy enthusiasts. The temporary free period offers players a direct opportunity to evaluate the title's controversial elements and design choices without a financial commitment.

The special promotion is available exclusively via Valve's Steam store for PC players. Gamers looking to take advantage of the week-long trial have until September 10 to access the title before standard store access returns.

According to details highlighted by gaming outlet Polygon, the event serves as a focal point for the franchise's ongoing anniversary campaign. Firaxis continues to leverage high-visibility platform promotions to re-engage veteran fans and attract new players across global gaming communities.
