South Africa's top T20 league has launched its fifth campaign. The competition described the upcoming season as an incredible cricketing spectacle. The league said the competition will return from January 17, 2027.

The campaign is set to run through February 21, 2027. Its opening fixture will feature Sunrisers Eastern Cape against Pretoria Capitals in the opener. The league will play its first match of the season.

Tickets for the season went on sale starting at $3. The league launched its fifth campaign with the schedule and ticket details.
