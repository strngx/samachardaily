Benedictine College will open its season with a non‑conference game at Virginia Academy of Science and Journalism (VASJ) on Oct. 16, kicking off at 7 p.m. on Gutbrod Field.

VASJ enters the contest with a 6‑2 record and sits seventh in Division II, while Benedictine holds a 4‑3 mark and ranks 12th in Division III, Region 9. Both programs are positioning themselves for postseason berths.

The outcome could shift each team's standing as the playoff race intensifies.
