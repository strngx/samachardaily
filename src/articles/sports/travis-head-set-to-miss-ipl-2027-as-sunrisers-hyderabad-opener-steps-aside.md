Travis Head has informed Sunrisers Hyderabad that he will skip IPL 2027 to manage his workload, with Australia facing a packed international schedule. He was retained for Rs 14 crore ahead of the 2025 mega auction and will be released once the IPL ratifies the decision.

Head scored 567 runs in IPL 2024, 374 in 2025 and 410 in 2026 at a strike rate above 160 in both recent editions. Australia faces the Ashes from June to August and the ODI World Cup 2027 in October-November.
