Indian women's cricket head coach Amol Muzumdar asserts that the side can dominate world cricket within two years, citing emerging talent, heightened fitness levels and professional training as the key drivers of future success.

Speaking at an SJAM event, he praised the BCCI Centre of Excellence and stressed discipline, a winning mentality and dressing‑room unity, while crediting team spirit for India's historic ODI World Cup triumph and emphasizing that sustained preparation will cement the team's global rise.

If the current trajectory holds, the women's team could become a benchmark for other emerging nations, reshaping the global cricket landscape.
