The Cleveland Guardians avoided an AL Division Series sweep with a 9-3 victory over the Chicago White Sox in Game 3. Jo Adell and José Ramírez powered Cleveland with a pair of two-out triples, delivering the offensive spark needed to secure the win.

The result cools off the Chicago White Sox and keeps the Guardians alive in the American League playoffs. Cleveland now avoids elimination in the divisional round, shifting the series dynamic after the White Sox threatened to end their season. Ramírez and Adell were central to the victory.
