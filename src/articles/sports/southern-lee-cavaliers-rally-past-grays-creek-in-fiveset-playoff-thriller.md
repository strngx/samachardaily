Sixth‑seeded Southern Lee Cavaliers edged No. 11 Gray’s Creek 3‑2 in the second round of the NCHSAA 6A East volleyball state playoffs on Tuesday night. The match went the distance, with set scores 22‑25, 25‑20, 19‑25, 25‑15 and 15‑12.

The Cavaliers rallied after early setbacks, a comeback the team described as a test of mental toughness, and the victory keeps their postseason campaign alive.

The win positions Southern Lee for a potential quarterfinal slot as the playoffs progress.
