Madalyn Bucy delivered a six‑goal performance to spearhead Allegany’s 10‑0 demolition of Hampshire on Saturday in Romney, West Virginia.

She opened the scoring with the team’s first two goals and had already netted four by halftime as Allegany built a 5‑0 lead.

In the second half, Bucy contributed to four of the five additional goals, adding two assists to round out her involvement in six of the ten tallies; the win leaves her with 16 goals on the season.
