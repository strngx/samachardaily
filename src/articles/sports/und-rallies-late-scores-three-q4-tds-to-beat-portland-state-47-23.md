On Sep. 13 in Hillsboro, Ore., No. 6 North Dakota (UND) Fighting Hawks defeated Portland State 47‑23, handling a late‑night start to secure a decisive win.

The Hawks surged in the fourth quarter, adding three touchdowns while quarterback Jerry Kaminski threw for a career high, cementing the comeback.

UND’s backups entered the field for the final minutes, marking the third consecutive game the team has given reserve players meaningful playing time.
