Delhi U-19 coach Nayyar affirmed that Aaryavir Sehwag has the quality to play for India, but stressed the batsman must take things one step at a time to reach senior international cricket, reflecting a measured development approach recently.

The assessment was delivered in New Delhi, where Nayyar outlined the incremental pathway required for promising U-19 talent to transition to the national setup without rushing the process.

The update, circulated by Social News XYZ on Oct 25, reports on Sehwag's progression as he works toward potential selection in the senior ranks.
