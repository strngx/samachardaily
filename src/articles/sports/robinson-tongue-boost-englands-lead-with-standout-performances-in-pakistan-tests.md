Dubai, Sep 2 (IANS) – England secured a commanding 2-0 advantage in their ICC World Test Championship series against Pakistan after the second Test, thanks to decisive contributions from their pace attack. The series, part of the multi‑year championship that determines the world’s top Test side, now sees England in a strong position to accumulate points toward the final, while Pakistan looks to regroup.

Fast bowler Ollie Robinson was the standout figure, being named Player of the Match in both Tests. His disciplined line and length, combined with the ability to generate movement off the seam, consistently troubled the Pakistani batting line‑up, leading to crucial breakthroughs at pivotal moments in each innings.

Josh Tongue complemented Robinson’s efforts with a series of incisive spells that helped England maintain pressure throughout the matches. Tongue’s ability to extract bounce on the pitches and his disciplined follow‑through forced several key dismissals, contributing significantly to England’s dominance and the clean sweep of the series so far.

Both bowlers saw their ICC Men’s Test Bowling Rankings rise sharply after the series, reflecting the impact of their performances on the global scale. The rankings, which factor in recent results and the quality of opposition, now place Robinson and Tongue among the top tier of Test bowlers, underscoring England’s depth in pace resources.

The 2-0 lead not only gives England a points cushion in the World Test Championship but also strengthens their strategic options ahead of upcoming fixtures. With the fast‑bowling unit in form, England’s selectors have a broader pool to draw from for future series, while Pakistan will need to reassess its batting strategies to counter England’s renewed pace threat.
