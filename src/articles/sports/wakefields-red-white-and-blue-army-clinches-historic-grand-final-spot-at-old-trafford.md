Wakefield’s ‘red, white and blue army’ secured a place in Super League Grand Final history on Oct 3, 2026, as the club qualified to play at Old Trafford. The side, famed for its red, white and blue strip, roared through the playoffs to earn the berth.

The author, who has walked to Old Trafford for the Grand Final countless times – first as a child fan and later in a professional capacity – says none of those visits felt like this one.

The milestone adds Wakefield to the elite roster of clubs that have reached the championship decider at the iconic venue.
