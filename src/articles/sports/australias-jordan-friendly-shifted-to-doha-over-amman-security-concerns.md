A soccer friendly between Australia and Jordan, scheduled for next Tuesday, will no longer be played in Amman. Football Australia has shifted the fixture to Doha, Qatar, after raising security concerns about the Jordanian capital, where the match was originally due to be held.

The governing body confirmed the arrangement on Friday, saying the Jordan Football Association had approved its request to move the game. With that approval secured, the friendly can proceed on its planned date, though at a venue outside Jordan.
