The Los Angeles Lakers have ranked among the league’s strongest defensive teams through the first three quarters of games this season, yet repeated fourth‑quarter collapses have emerged as a glaring weakness.

The latest lapse occurred against the Phoenix Suns, where a fourth‑quarter breakdown left Dallas Mavericks star Luka Doncic visibly frustrated, prompting him to grab a coaching board during the game.

Former Laker Derek Fisher responded by offering a key fix, aiming to steady the Lakers’ closing minutes and translate early‑game defensive strength into full‑game consistency.
