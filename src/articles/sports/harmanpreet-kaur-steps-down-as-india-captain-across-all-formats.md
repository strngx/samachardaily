Harmanpreet Kaur has ended her tenure as India's skipper across all formats, announcing the decision herself. The World Cup-winning captain confirmed the decision through a lengthy Instagram post, ending her leadership while clarifying her playing future with the national side. The news was first reported by Northeast Live.

Despite relinquishing the captaincy, the batter stated she will continue to remain part of the Indian team. The report does not name an immediate successor to the role. Kaur led India to a World Cup title during her tenure as captain, announcing the change via social media.
