Prakash Padukone expressed disappointment over India's recent Asian Games badminton performance. The former world champion noted that while the team showed considerable talent and received strong support, there is a pressing need for greater consistency across the squad to match expectations. His comments reflect broader concerns about maintaining momentum after initial success.

He cautioned that identifying deeper systemic issues is premature, stressing that the current level of play demonstrates solid foundations. Nonetheless, he highlighted PV Sindhu's unwavering commitment as an inspiring example for other players to emulate in their own efforts.
