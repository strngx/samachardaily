Akash Deep marked his return to Test action with three wickets in the first innings of India's opening Test against New Zealand, then revealed the support that shaped his recovery. The bowler recalled how Gambhir would check in during his rehabilitation, asking a simple question: 'Kaisa chal raha?' — 'How's it going?'

Those conversations took place during a rehabilitation spell that kept him out of the side. By singling out Gambhir's regular check-ins, Akash Deep offered a glimpse of the quieter, behind-the-scenes support that often accompanies a player's return from injury.
