Liverpool are reportedly considering a move to re-sign Trent Alexander-Arnold, 18 months after the defender left Anfield for Real Madrid. The club is weighing the possibility of bringing the England international back to the Premier League. The defender's future has been the subject of speculation in recent weeks.

The England international has struggled for consistent game time under Jose Mourinho at the Spanish giants, enduring a difficult period. This situation has sparked rumours that a Premier League return could be on the cards as early as the January transfer window.
