Night two of the 4-Crown Nationals unfolded Friday at Eldora Speedway in Rossburg, Ohio, with the half-mile track hosting a stacked card of sprint car racing. The evening featured High Limit Racing Sprint Cars, Great Lakes Super Sprints, and USAC Silver Crown competitors battling on the dirt surface.

The 4-Crown Nationals continue through the weekend at Eldora, with racing action scheduled for both Saturday and Sunday. Full results from Friday's events are available through the track's official channels.

Eldora Speedway's 4-Crown Nationals represent one of the premier dirt track racing events in the United States, drawing top-tier sprint car talent from multiple sanctioning bodies to compete on the historic half-mile oval.
