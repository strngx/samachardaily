The inaugural ICC Women's Champions Trophy will debut in India early next year, marking a historic moment in women's cricket. The high‑octane T20I tournament will feature six powerhouse nations: Australia, England, India, New Zealand, South Africa and Sri Lanka.

Six standout players are set to headline the event, promising fierce competition across the six participating teams. The tournament positions India as host of a premier women's cricket showcase.

With top‑ranked sides converging, the Champions Trophy is expected to elevate the global profile of women's cricket and set new benchmarks for T20I excellence.
