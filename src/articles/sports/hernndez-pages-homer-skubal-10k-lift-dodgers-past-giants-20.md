Teoscar Hernández and Andy Pages each launched a home run in the second inning, providing all the offense the Los Angeles Dodgers needed to defeat the San Francisco Giants 2‑0.

Tarik Skubal worked seven innings, surrendering just three hits while fanning ten batters; the outing marked his tenth start for Los Angeles since the Detroit trade and continued his streak of six consecutive victories.

Relievers Roki Sasaki and Tanner Scott closed the game, with Scott notching his 24th save, as the Dodgers recorded their sixth straight win over the Giants and secured the season series for the fifth year in a row.
