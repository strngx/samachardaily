Defending champion Aryna Sabalenka expressed her frustration following the 6-4, 5-7, 6-2 final defeat, smashing her racquet beside the court bench and shouting at an approaching cameraperson. The visible outburst occurred as Elena Rybakina celebrated her victory feet away, capping off a decisive performance that prevented Sabalenka from securing a historic three-peat at Flushing Meadows.

Beyond the major trophy, the outcome reshaped the summit of women's tennis as Rybakina dethroned Sabalenka from the world No. 1 position. Sabalenka had held the top spot for 99 consecutive weeks during her second stint at number one, having previously occupied it for eight weeks in 2023. The defeat also leaves Sabalenka without a Grand Slam title in a single season for the first time since 2022, ending a streak that began when she defeated Rybakina in the 2023 Australian Open final.

Addressing reporters after the match, Sabalenka credited Rybakina's aggressive strategy and exceptional serving while admitting dissatisfaction with her own level of play. The outgoing number one added a lighthearted comment regarding her opponent's ascension, challenging Rybakina with a laugh to see if she could surpass her 99-week tenure at the top of the WTA rankings.
