Holger Rune made his long‑awaited comeback to the ATP Tour today, stepping onto the court at the Japan Open after an 11‑month absence. The Danish star, who had already played a competitive match winning two singles matches at the Davis Cup in front of his home fans, issued an apology, simply stating, “I’m sorry.”

The brief statement follows a period of rehabilitation and marks Rune’s first official action since his injury‑related layoff. While details of his performance at the Japan Open were not disclosed, the apology signals a focus on learning from the setback and a commitment to returning to form.
