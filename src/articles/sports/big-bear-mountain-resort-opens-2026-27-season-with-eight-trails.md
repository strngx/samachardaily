Skiers and snowboarders are back on Southern California slopes after Big Bear Mountain Resort launched its 2026-27 winter season on Wednesday. Snow Summit and Bear Mountain both began operating Nov. 25, putting the opening inside the Thanksgiving holiday window.

Across the two mountains, two chairlifts and eight trails are running, according to Visit Big Bear. That is the full extent of the terrain reported open at the resort so far this season.
