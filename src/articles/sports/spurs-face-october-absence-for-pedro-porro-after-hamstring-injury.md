Spanish defender Pedro Porro was taken off less than 20 minutes into Tottenham Hotspur’s home defeat by Aston Villa on Saturday after sustaining a hamstring injury, prompting the club to confirm his unavailability for multiple matches in October.

The injury comes shortly after Porro had rejoined the first‑team squad following an earlier spell on the sidelines, and a new report now hints at a potential comeback date, though the exact timing has not been disclosed.

Tottenham will therefore need to reshuffle their defensive options for the upcoming October fixtures, relying on other squad members until Porro is cleared to play again.
