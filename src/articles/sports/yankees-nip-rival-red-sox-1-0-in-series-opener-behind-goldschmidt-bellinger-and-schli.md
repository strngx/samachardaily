The decisive moment arrived in the seventh inning when Boston loaded the bases with two outs. The win gives New York a 1‑0 edge in a pivotal four‑game series that could shape the AL East race. Both clubs entered the matchup with identical records, and the narrow margin underscores the importance of timely hitting and defensive execution in a tightly contested division.
