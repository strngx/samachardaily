India has confirmed a 30‑shooter roster for the 2026 Asian Games in Nagoya, covering rifle, pistol and shotgun disciplines.

The squad is anchored by Olympic medalist Manu Bhaker and Asian Games champion Aishwary Pratap Singh Tomar, and also includes other Olympic and Asian Games medallists together with rising talent.
