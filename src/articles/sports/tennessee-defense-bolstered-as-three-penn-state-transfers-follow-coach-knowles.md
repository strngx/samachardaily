The transfer portal remains a key source of talent for the Tennessee Volunteers, delivering three defensive standouts this season. Defensive lineman Xavier Gillman, linebacker Amare Campbell and safety Dejuan Lane all transferred from Penn State, following defensive coordinator Jim Knowles to Knoxville.

Since arriving, the trio has been credited with strengthening the Vols' defensive unit, adding depth and playmaking ability across the line, linebacker corps and secondary. The Daily Beacon notes their contributions have been immediate. Their impact underscores the strategic value of coordinated transfers in shaping Tennessee's defensive performance.
