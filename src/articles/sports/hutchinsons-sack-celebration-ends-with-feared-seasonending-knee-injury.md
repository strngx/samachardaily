Detroit Lions defensive end Aidan Hutchinson made an impact on the opening drive against the New England Patriots. On third down he broke through the line and sacked rookie quarterback Drake Maye, then leapt into the air to celebrate the play.

Moments after the jump Hutchinson landed awkwardly, clutching his knee. Trainers rushed to the field and escorted him to the locker room for evaluation. Early reports suggested a possible season‑ending knee injury, though a definitive diagnosis has not been released.
