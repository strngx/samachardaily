Major League Baseball's awards season concluded on Tuesday night with the announcement of the 2026 Silver Slugger Award winners. The New York Yankees placed two players on the finalist list, but neither came away with the victory.

Ben Rice was nominated as a designated hitter, while Cody Bellinger was named in the outfield. Louisville Slugger had previously announced the finalists, which included both Yankees. Both players finished as nominees in their respective categories.
