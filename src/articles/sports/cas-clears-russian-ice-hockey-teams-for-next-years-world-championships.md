On Friday, Oct. 30, the Court of Arbitration for Sport ruled that Russian men's and women's ice‑hockey teams are eligible to compete in next year’s world championships, reversing the International Ice Hockey Federation’s exclusion.

The decision upheld appeals filed by the Russian Ice Hockey Federation, effectively nullifying the IIHF’s earlier bans. The arbitration panel’s judgment is binding and final.

With the ruling, both Russian squads regain the right to enter the tournaments, though the IIHF has not detailed procedural steps for their re‑entry. The championships are scheduled for the following season, and the teams will now be able to register.
