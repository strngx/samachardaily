The Vikings announced Kyler Murray as the starting quarterback, prompting immediate speculation that backup J.J. McCarthy may be released before the team finalizes its 53‑man roster. Pundits have floated the possibility of a waiver as the club trims its squad.

McCarthy saw action in two preseason contests, completing nine of 17 attempts for a total of 112 yards and recording no touchdowns.
