The Texas A&M women's basketball team will observe Senior Day on Saturday as they face Oklahoma in Norman, with tip‑off scheduled for 2:45 p.m.

Fans can follow the action via the Texas A&M Sports Network: a 12:30 p.m. Spec’s 12th Man Tailgate Show from Kyle Field hosted by Giovanni Heater and Shea Walker, followed by a 2:00 p.m. booth broadcast from Norman featuring Andrew Monaco, Dave Elmendorf and Will Johnson.

The audio feed does not carry the video stream; viewers must tune to ESPN2 or the ESPN app for live television.
