Jalen Brunson delivered the decisive blow late, draining a four-point play with 1:25 remaining in the fourth quarter to snap a tie and put New York in front. The Knicks went on to close out a 111-102 road win over the Toronto Raptors on Tuesday night.

Brunson finished with 31 points, and Karl-Anthony Towns added a double-double of 16 points and 16 rebounds to support the Knicks.

The result was New York's 15th consecutive win over the Raptors, a streak that has run across repeated meetings between the two teams.
