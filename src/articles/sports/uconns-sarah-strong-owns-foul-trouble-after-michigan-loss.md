UConn women's basketball suffered a reality check against Michigan in its second game of the season, with sophomore Sarah Strong acknowledging her early foul trouble hampered the team. Strong picked up quick fouls and spent much of the first half on the bench as the Huskies struggled.

The forward said she "can't be stupid like that" while taking accountability for the performance. UConn now prepares for a clash with the Iowa Hawkeyes.
