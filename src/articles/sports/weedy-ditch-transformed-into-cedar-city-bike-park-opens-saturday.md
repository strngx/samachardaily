A former weedy ditch has been converted into a public cycling venue, concluding three months of building on the Cedar City Bike Park. Bicyclists celebrated the grand opening, gathering to ride the new terrain.

The project repurposes a low-lying drainage area into a structured riding environment, offering access to features designed for different skill levels. Cedar City News reported the launch, noting the shift from an unused ditch to an active recreational destination for local cyclists.

The facility now serves as a dedicated space for cycling within the city limits.
