The Rogue River Jr. Sr. High girls’ soccer team completed a pivotal four-day stretch featuring two league matches in Class 3A/2A/1A District 3. The Chieftains opened the pair of games with a 2-1 victory over Sutherlin, earning the win on hot artificial turf.

Following the initial victory, Rogue River fell to the Bruins in their subsequent contest. The split outcome leaves the Chieftains with an overall record of 6-4 and a 5-2 mark in league competition, providing a clear benchmark for their potential placement within the district standings.
