The New York Jets unveiled their leadership group for the 2026 NFL season on Tuesday, naming six players as season‑long captains. Veteran quarterback Geno Smith and right guard Joe Tippman will serve as the offensive captains, while linebacker Demario Davis and safety (name omitted) anchor the defensive side. The full roster also includes two additional leaders whose identities were confirmed in the team’s official release.

The announcement came as part of the league‑wide captain‑selection process that sees each franchise designate its on‑field leaders before the opening weekend. For the Jets, the move signals a clear intent to build continuity around Smith’s veteran presence and Davis’s defensive acumen, both of whom have been pivotal in the team’s recent resurgence.

Wide receiver Garrett Wilson, who posted 1,200 receiving yards and 10 touchdowns last season, was notably absent from the list. In a brief statement posted to his Instagram story, Wilson thanked fans for their support and hinted at “more to come,” sparking speculation about his feelings toward the snub. The cryptic reply has drawn attention from analysts who view the omission as a potential test of his leadership aspirations.

Jets head coach Robert Saleh praised the selected captains, saying they embody the “hard‑working, resilient culture” the organization wants to reinforce. He also emphasized that leadership is not limited to the formal captain group, noting that “any player who steps up in the locker room is a leader for us.” The coaching staff has not commented on Wilson’s reaction, but sources close to the team say the wideout remains focused on his on‑field performance.

The Jets will open their season against the Buffalo Bills on September 13, with the newly appointed captains expected to lead the team’s preparations. Wilson’s role in the offense remains unchanged, and he is slated to start alongside veteran receivers as the Jets aim to improve on last year’s playoff run.
