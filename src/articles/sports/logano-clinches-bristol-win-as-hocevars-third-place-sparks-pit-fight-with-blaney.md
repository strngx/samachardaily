Joey Logano won the NASCAR event at Bristol Motor Speedway on Saturday night, seizing the lead for the final seven laps to secure the victory.

He took the lead from 23‑year‑old Carson Hocevar, who finished third and has drawn attention for an aggressive driving style that has earned the ire of veteran competitors.

After the checkered flag, Hocevar was involved in a physical altercation with Ryan Blaney in the pit area, with both drivers trading punches.
