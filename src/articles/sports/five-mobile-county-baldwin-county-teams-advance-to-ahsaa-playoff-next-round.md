Five Mobile County-Baldwin County high school football programs have advanced to the next round of their respective AHSAA playoff classifications, securing postseason berths in the tournament.

Two of the advancing squads will contest the state championship game, the decisive final that crowns the overall state champion. Their state championship match will decide the overall champion for the classification they represent.

The AHSAA playoff structure spans multiple classifications, and these five local teams illustrate the region's competitive breadth as they pursue higher titles. The remaining three teams will continue competing in their respective classification rounds.
