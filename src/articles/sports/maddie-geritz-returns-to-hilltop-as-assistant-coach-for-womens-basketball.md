Maddie Geritz, a celebrated former player from Carroll College, has been appointed as an assistant coach for the Hilltop women’s basketball team. The hiring was confirmed by Hilltop’s athletic department on Monday, noting that Geritz will work alongside the existing coaching staff to prepare the squad for the upcoming campaign. Her return to Hilltop is described as a homecoming, linking her playing roots with a new role in coaching.

Geritz earned a reputation at Carroll by contributing to multiple conference titles and earning All‑Conference honors during her four‑year playing career. Her on‑court leadership helped the program secure its first regional tournament berth in several years, and she finished her collegiate tenure as one of the school’s all‑time leading scorers. Those achievements cemented her status as a “Carroll great” and laid the foundation for a future in basketball mentorship.

After graduating, Geritz spent the past season with Montana State, where she served in a support role on the women’s basketball staff. In that capacity she assisted with player development, scouting, and game‑plan preparation, gaining valuable experience in a Division I environment. Colleagues at Montana State praised her work ethic and ability to translate her playing experience into effective coaching techniques, positioning her as a rising talent in the coaching ranks.

Hilltop’s women’s basketball program is entering a pivotal phase, seeking to build on recent improvements and elevate its competitive standing within the conference. The addition of Geritz is expected to bolster the team’s defensive schemes and player‑development initiatives, areas identified by the head coach as priorities. Her familiarity with the local basketball landscape and recent exposure to high‑level collegiate operations are seen as assets that could accelerate the squad’s growth.

Geritz expressed enthusiasm about rejoining the Hilltop community, emphasizing her commitment to mentoring student‑athletes both on and off the court. She highlighted the importance of creating a supportive environment that balances academic rigor with athletic ambition. The coaching staff anticipates that her fresh perspective and proven track record will translate into tangible improvements as the team prepares for preseason training.
