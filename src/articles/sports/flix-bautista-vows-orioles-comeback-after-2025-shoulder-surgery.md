Félix Bautista has pledged to rejoin the Baltimore Orioles for the current season after a shoulder operation in August 2025 that repaired both his labrum and rotator cuff.

Having already navigated a demanding rehabilitation process before, Bautista said he understood the sacrifices required and expressed that finally stepping back onto the field felt “incredibly satisfying.”
