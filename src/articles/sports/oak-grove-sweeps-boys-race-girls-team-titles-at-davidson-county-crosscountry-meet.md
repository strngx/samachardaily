Oak Grove emerged as the standout program on Tuesday at the Davidson County cross‑country championships hosted at Denton FarmPark, securing both the boys individual victory and the girls team championship.

The Grizzlies, representing Oak Grove's girls squad, placed four runners inside the top ten and amassed a total of 35 points, comfortably outpacing second‑place Central Davidson, which finished with 61 points.

In the boys race, Oak Grove's Bryson Vann crossed the finish line first in 16:17, while teammate Mason Moore followed closely in second place with a time of 16:25.
