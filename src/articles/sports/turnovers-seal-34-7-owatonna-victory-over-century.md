On Thursday night in Owatonna, the Century football team was defeated 34-7 by the Owatonna Huskies, with turnovers playing a pivotal role in the outcome.

The defeat comes after a September meeting in Rochester last fall, where Century had upset Owatonna, delivering the program its first win since 2023.

Century turned the ball over four times—three interceptions and one fumble—while Owatonna capitalized on a third‑quarter interception returned for a touchdown, fueling a 20‑point surge.
