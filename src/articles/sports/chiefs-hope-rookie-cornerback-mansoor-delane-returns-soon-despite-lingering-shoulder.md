Kansas City Chiefs are eager to get rookie cornerback Mansoor Delane back, but he is coping with a lingering shoulder injury that, while not serious, remains unresolved.

The issue was highlighted after the Chiefs’ loss to the Denver Broncos on Sunday, when head coach Andy Reid gave reporters an uncertain outlook on when Delane might suit up again.

Reid’s comments left the timeline open, underscoring the team’s need to address depth concerns in the secondary as they prepare for upcoming games.
