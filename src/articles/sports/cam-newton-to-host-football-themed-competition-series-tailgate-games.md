Former NFL MVP Cam Newton will host "Tailgate Games," a new 60-minute competition series produced for Business Wire from Bethlehem, Pennsylvania. Newton will be joined by co-host Kinsey Wolanski for the football-inspired format.

The series pits everyday male and female fans against each other in outrageous challenges that merge traditional game-show mechanics with the energy of game-day tailgating. Obstacle-course elements and tailgate-themed tasks are designed to capture the communal spirit of stadium parking lots.
