Manchester United officials said they were presented with statements from Max Dowman and former player Wayne Rooney that shed light on JJ Gabriel’s recent request to depart the club.

Gabriel, a United academy graduate, flew to Barcelona last week, prompting media speculation about a possible move, although sources confirm he is not in direct negotiations with the Spanish side.

At the same time, Tottenham Hotspur is reported to be ahead in the chase for a Brazilian prospect, while United have earmarked Mezian Mesloub as a potential wonderkid to fill the gap left by Gabriel.

Adding to the mix, Gabriel’s father – a former Arsenal first‑team player – is taking an increasingly active role in guiding his son’s career decisions.
