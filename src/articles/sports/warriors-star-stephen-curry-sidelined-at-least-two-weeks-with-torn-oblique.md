The Golden State Warriors confirmed Stephen Curry will miss at least two weeks following a torn oblique muscle injury. The announcement arrives while the NBA’s all-time 3-point leader is on pace for one of the best seasons of his 18-year career.

Curry’s absence removes a key offensive weapon for the Warriors during the early part of the campaign. The team will need to navigate the next two weeks without their star shooter while he recovers from the abdominal strain. This setback interrupts a campaign where the veteran guard has been among the league’s most productive players.
