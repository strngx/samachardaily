The Clay Panthers secured a 3-2 win over the New Boston Glenwood Tigers in an SOC I soccer match, completing an 80‑minute battle that highlighted the tenacity of both small‑school programs.

Both schools, known for modest enrollments, often require athletes to juggle multiple fall sports, limiting roster depth. Nevertheless, the Panthers and Tigers entered the contest determined to compete fiercely regardless of the scoreline.

Clay’s victory came after a back‑and‑forth exchange of goals, with the Panthers pulling ahead in the final minutes. The Tigers fought back twice, but Clay’s defensive resilience held firm to preserve the narrow margin.

Coach Mark Reynolds praised his players’ stamina and adaptability, noting that many had been balancing soccer with football and track commitments throughout the season. "Our guys gave everything for 80 minutes, and that’s the hallmark of this program," he said.

The win improves Clay’s standing in the SOC I league table, while New Boston Glenwood remains in contention for a playoff spot, setting up a potential rematch later in the season.
