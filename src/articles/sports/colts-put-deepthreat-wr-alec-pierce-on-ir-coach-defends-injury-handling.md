The Indianapolis Colts announced that wide receiver Alec Pierce will be placed on injured reserve following a heel injury, ending his season participation. Head coach Shane Steichen said the team’s handling of the injury was appropriate and prioritized Pierce’s health.

Pierce’s absence removes a deep‑threat target from the passing game, prompting the Colts to look to other speed assets such as Darius Slayton, while quarterback Daniel Jones and safety Cam Bynum remain integral to the offense’s rhythm. The team indicated the move allows Pierce to focus on rehabilitation.
