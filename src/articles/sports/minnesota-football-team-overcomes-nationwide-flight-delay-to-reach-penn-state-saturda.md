Minnesota's football squad faced a travel snag on Saturday, Nov. 14, when a nationwide ground stop halted flights out of Minneapolis‑St. Paul (MSP), cancelling and delaying numerous departures, including the team's charter.

The ground stop was later lifted, allowing the team to resume its journey to State College. Game officials confirmed that the Penn State vs. Minnesota contest is still slated to kick off as scheduled on Saturday.

The swift resolution means the scheduled kickoff proceeds without further delay, despite earlier concerns about the team's ability to arrive on time.
