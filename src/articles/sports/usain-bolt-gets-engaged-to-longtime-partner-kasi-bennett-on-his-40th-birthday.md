Jamaica’s most celebrated athlete, Usain Bolt, turned 40 on August 21 and used the milestone to mark a new chapter in his personal life. The former eight‑time Olympic gold‑medalist gathered close friends and family at a birthday celebration in Kingston, where he slipped a ring onto the finger of his long‑time partner, Kasi Bennett. The proposal, described by attendees as intimate yet festive, took place after a short speech from Bolt thanking supporters for “standing by me through every race and every moment.”

Bolt’s athletic résumé reads like a record‑breaking history book: three consecutive Olympic 100‑metre and 200‑metre titles (2008, 2012, 2016), a total of eight gold medals, and a world‑record 9.58 seconds in the 100 m. Since retiring from competition in 2017, he has remained a global brand ambassador, ventured into business, and occasionally appeared in exhibition races, but his on‑track dominance continues to define his public image.

Kasi Bennett, a Jamaican entrepreneur and mother of two, has been linked to Bolt for several years, though the couple kept their relationship largely private. The pair have been spotted together at events such as the 2022 World Athletics Championships and have occasionally shared glimpses of their life on social media. The engagement, confirmed by close friends at the birthday gathering, signals a formal step forward for a partnership that has weathered the intense scrutiny that comes with Bolt’s fame.
