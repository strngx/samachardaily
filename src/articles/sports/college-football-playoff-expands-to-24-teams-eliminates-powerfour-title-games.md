College football leaders announced on Monday that the College Football Playoff will double to 24 teams beginning next year, ending a three‑decade era of Power Four conference title games.

Five teams will earn automatic berths, with the remaining slots selected by a panel. ESPN will add 11 new games under a revised five‑year contract that adds $1.7 billion to the existing $7.8 billion deal through 2031.

The overhaul broadens postseason access and reshapes the national championship pathway for college programs.
