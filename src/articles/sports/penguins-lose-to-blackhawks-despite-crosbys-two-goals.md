The Penguins faced one of the NHL's weakest teams, and while many things went right early, Chicago's defense held firm. The team had hoped to secure a win early in the road trip, but Chicago's goaltending limited Pittsburgh to just those two goals. The game was played in Chicago as part of a tough stretch for the Penguins.
