The Court of Arbitration for Sport has restored Senegal as champions of the 2025 Africa Cup of Nations, overturning the Confederation of African Football's decision to hand the tournament to Morocco.

Algerian commentator Hafid Derradji, who works for beIN Sports, responded to the ruling with a pointed jibe, calling Senegal the first team to win the same title twice.

His remark pointed to the unusual sequence in which the continental crown was awarded to Morocco and then returned to Senegal by the arbitration panel's decision.
