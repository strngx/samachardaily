Max Verstappen secured pole position for the Singapore Grand Prix, placing him at the front of the field as he aims to extend his current winning streak. He enters the event with a strong car and a clear target to add to his recent successes.

Although he leads the grid, Verstappen has recorded horrendous starts from pole in recent races, losing positions early and prompting a need to refine his launch technique. His recent pole performances have been inconsistent, highlighting the importance of a clean start.

The Singapore GP will therefore be a crucial test of his ability to convert pole into a race win, keeping his championship momentum alive.
