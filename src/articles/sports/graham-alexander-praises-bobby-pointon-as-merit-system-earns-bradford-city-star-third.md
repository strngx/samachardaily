Bradford City manager Graham Alexander has delivered an exceptionally glowing assessment of player Bobby Pointon following the youngster's third start of the current football season. Addressing the media after the team's latest match, the Bantams boss expressed immense satisfaction with Pointon's ongoing overall development, explicitly noting to reporters that he could talk about the player all the time due to his consistently positive attitude and noticeable impact on the rest of the squad.

Pointon's journey toward securing a regular place in the starting eleven has required significant tactical patience and personal resilience under Alexander's leadership. The manager revealed that Bradford City operates strictly under a clear merit system for matchday team selections, meaning that starting places are never guaranteed to anyone in the squad but must instead be continuously earned through daily effort, high training standards, and operational discipline.

Because of this rigorous selection policy, Pointon was forced to bide his time on the bench while waiting for his specific opportunity to break through into the starting lineup. Alexander pointed out that the meritocratic environment forces every member of the Bantams squad to demonstrate unyielding focus, with Pointon eventually forcing his way into the manager's starting plans through steady dedication and impressive work rate when given chances.

The manager emphasized that Pointon's recent inclusion for his third start of the campaign serves as an ideal template for how the club’s internal selection process ought to function. By staying patient, avoiding frustration, and maintaining top physical and mental preparation during periods of limited playing time, Pointon proved to the coaching staff that he was entirely worthy of a starting role when called upon.

Ultimately, Alexander’s enthusiastic commentary underscores the robust health of the competitive squad environment currently established at Bradford City. By publicly lauding Pointon's patience and strict adherence to the team's selection criteria, the manager sent a clear, direct signal to the entire locker room that sustained hard work, humility, and individual merit will consistently be rewarded with places on the pitch.
