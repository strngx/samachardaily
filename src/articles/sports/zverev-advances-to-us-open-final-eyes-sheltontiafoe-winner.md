Alexander Zverev has moved into the US Open men’s final and will meet the victor of the all‑American semifinal featuring Ben Shelton and Frances Tiafoe.

Zverev noted that his own journey to a Grand Slam title has been marked by a prolonged wait, underscoring that he has yet to capture a major championship.

He added that he hopes the current drought for American men’s tennis will persist, signalling his desire to see the US players fall short in the championship match.
