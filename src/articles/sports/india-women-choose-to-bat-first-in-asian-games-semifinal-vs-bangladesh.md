India's women's cricket side won the toss at Korogi Sports Park on Sunday and elected to bat first in the Asian Games semifinal against Bangladesh, keeping the same XI that played in the quarter‑final.

Bangladesh, making their tournament debut, face an Indian team that entered the match fresh from a dominant quarter‑final victory over hosts Japan, a win that prompted the unchanged lineup.

Choosing to set a target underscores India's confidence after the lopsided win, while the decision to retain the same players signals trust in the squad's balance ahead of a medal‑contending encounter.
