Arsenal were held to a 1-1 draw by Borussia Dortmund in their Champions League league-phase meeting. Viktor Gyokeres opened the scoring for Mikel Arteta's side before halftime, but Maximilian Beier equalised after the restart.

Joane Gadou created Beier's equaliser, while Jobe Bellingham's close-range goal was ruled out for offside. Arsenal missed further chances through Christos Tzolis, Noni Madueke and Declan Rice, and Gyokeres headed wide in the six-yard area.

The draw left Arsenal fifth with 11 points from five matches, while Dortmund stayed seventh with 10. Arteta had made six alterations to his starting XI following the weekend's draw against Newcastle United.
