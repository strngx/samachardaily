The Professional Golf Tour of India is stepping up efforts to raise the profile of Indian golf internationally by combining financial assistance with upgraded practice infrastructure.

PGTI covers key entry costs for qualifying overseas events and extends direct financial aid to promising players, aiming to increase their exposure in global competitions.

The tour also encourages young golfers to balance their professional careers with education, viewing academic development as part of long-term athlete growth.
