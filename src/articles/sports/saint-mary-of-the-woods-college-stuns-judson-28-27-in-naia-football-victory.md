Saint Mary-of-the-Woods College delivered a dramatic upset Saturday, defeating Judson 28-27 to claim their first NAIA football victory in program history. Adonimi Parker was instrumental, blocking an extra point and returning it 97 yards for two points, while Carter Hall sealed the win with a go-ahead touchdown with 31 seconds remaining.

The victory marks a historic milestone for the Saint Mary-of-the-Woods College football program, ending a long quest for their first NAIA championship. Parker's 97-yard return stands out as a standout play, and Hall's late touchdown provided the decisive edge in this thrilling contest.
