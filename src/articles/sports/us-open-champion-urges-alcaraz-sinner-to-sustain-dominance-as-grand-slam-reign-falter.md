A US Open champion has advised Carlos Alcaraz and Jannik Sinner that the key to remaining at the summit of men's tennis is to keep dominating their game.

Alcaraz and Sinner have been the leading forces in men's tennis over the past few seasons, yet their recent Grand Slam supremacy has been upended.

The champion’s counsel highlights the importance of sustained performance as the two stars confront the erosion of their Grand Slam streaks.
