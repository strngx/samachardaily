Dusty May announced that the amount of playing time Santi Aldama receives in his Dallas Mavericks debut Friday night against the Utah Jazz will be determined once the game begins.

The debut takes place at the American Airlines Center, and May emphasized that the coach‑player decision will unfold live during the contest, with the exact minutes to be settled once play starts.

May made the remarks before tip‑off, noting that Aldama's role will be adjusted as the game progresses, making this debut a live experiment for the team's rotation overall.
