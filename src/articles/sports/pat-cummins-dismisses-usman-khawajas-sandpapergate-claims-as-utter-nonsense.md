Sky Sports reported that Australian Test captain Pat Cummins rejected Usman Khawaja's claims over the ball-tampering scandal, calling them 'utter nonsense.' The remarks came during a press conference focused on sandpapergate.

Coverage highlighted abrasive, arrogant and ugly moments from the session, while David Warner was reported to be furious at Cummins for 'chucking Usman Khawaja under the bus.' The session was described as one of Australian sport's most memorable press conferences.

The episode has revived debate over Australian cricket's past, with commentary saying sandpapergate is back and reopening wounds in a 'nation of good sports.'
