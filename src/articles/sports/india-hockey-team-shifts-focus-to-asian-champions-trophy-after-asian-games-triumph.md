India's men's hockey team will quickly shift focus from their Asian Games triumph to the Asian Champions Trophy, which will be held in Punjab, the team management announced on Tuesday.

The continental title defence at the Asian Games concluded recently, and the squad now turns attention to another key test of their regional dominance in the upcoming Champions Trophy.

The tournament in Punjab will serve as a platform to assess the team's consistency and readiness ahead of future international commitments.
