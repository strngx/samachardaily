The sudden and tragic news unfolded shortly after the former player posted an alarming, deeply distressing message on his personal Facebook account, triggering immediate concern among family members, former teammates, coaches, and football supporters across the country who sought to reach out to him.

Smith, who played as a defensive back for the Denver Broncos during his professional career, was confirmed dead following the heartbreaking social media update. Major news outlets including the New York Post, Yahoo Sports, Broncos Wire, Us Weekly, and The Spun subsequently reported his passing. The news sent shockwaves through the American football ecosystem, where Smith was remembered as an energetic secondary player who competed at the highest levels of collegiate and professional gridiron football.
