Miami Heat added roster depth on Monday by securing Lester Quinones on an Exhibit 10 contract. The move brings in a player who spent last year competing for G League United.

Quinones brings previous experience across 54 games in the NBA, where he posted a career average of 4.8 points per game. He has previously suited up for the Warriors, Sixers, and Pelicans.

The Heat's current player structure also includes Tre Donaldson, Vlad Goldin, and Keshad Johnson, who are all actively signed to two-way deals.
