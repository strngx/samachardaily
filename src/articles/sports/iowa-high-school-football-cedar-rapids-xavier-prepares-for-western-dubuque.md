The Iowa high school football season moves into its second week of competition with several high-profile matchups scheduled across the state. Among the prominent fixtures, Cedar Rapids Xavier is set to clash with Western Dubuque in a key early-season test that brings together two established regional programs.

Cedar Rapids Xavier enters the contest building on recent state-level success, having previously competed in the Class 4A state football championship game at the UNI-Dome in Cedar Falls, Iowa. During that championship appearance on November 20, 2025, key performers such as Ty Weiss carried the offensive workload against the Pella Dutch, highlighting Xavier's presence on the state's biggest high school stage.

The upcoming Week 2 slate also features another notable matchup between top Iowa programs, as Sergeant Bluff-Luton hits the road to take on Lewis Central. These early non-district games provide teams with an essential proving ground to test squad depth, refine schemes, and build momentum before district play begins.

As Cedar Rapids Xavier shifts focus to Western Dubuque, the team aims to build upon the championship experience gained during their UNI-Dome run. With crucial early-season wins at stake, both Xavier and Sergeant Bluff-Luton face demanding assignments that will help shape the competitive narrative in Class 4A football.
