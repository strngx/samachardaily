Westchester Country Day rallied to a 2-1 victory over rival Caldwell on Friday at the school's Kennedy Field in the PTAC boys soccer contest. Miles Glover netted the winner in the 75th minute after an assist from Eduardo Juarez Garcia, while Ben Covington added the opening goal; Preston Kendrick recorded the win in goal.

The triumph lifted the Wildcats to a perfect 9-0 overall record and 4-0 in conference play, propelling them past the Eagles to claim the No. 1 position in the NCISAA 2A rankings.
