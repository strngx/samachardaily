Rams head coach Sean McVay announced Friday that Myles Garrett, the reigning NFL Defensive Player of the Year, will be activated off injured reserve for Sunday's matchup with the Washington Commanders. The move ends a wait of more than eight weeks after Garrett underwent microscopic surgery on his left side.

Garrett's return adds depth to the Rams' pass rush and gives the team its top defensive star back in action. The Commanders will face a fully healthy opponent who has been sidelined since early season. McVay's decision signals confidence in Garrett's recovery.
