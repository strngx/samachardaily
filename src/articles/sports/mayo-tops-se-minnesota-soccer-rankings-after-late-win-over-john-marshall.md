The Southeast Minnesota boys and girls soccer power rankings were released on September 19, with team records updated through Wednesday, September 16. Mayo, now 5‑2‑1, claimed the No. 1 spot, moving ahead of its regional rivals.

Mayo earned the ranking after a 3‑2 victory over rival John Marshall on Tuesday. Finn Slama scored the decisive goal with just 17 seconds remaining, heading home a corner delivered by senior Amani Gupta.

Gupta, a senior who continues to be a key playmaker for the Spartans, contributed throughout the match, reinforcing the leadership that helped Mayo secure the top position in the latest rankings.
