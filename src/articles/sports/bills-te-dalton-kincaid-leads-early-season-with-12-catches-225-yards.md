In April, Bills tight end Dalton Kincaid volunteered as the anchor for a series of team‑building tug‑of‑war contests, putting lingering knee concerns to rest and signaling his readiness for the season.

Two games later, Kincaid tops Buffalo’s receiving chart with 12 catches that have amassed 225 yards and one touchdown, confirming his role as a primary offensive weapon.

The production mirrors the promise he displayed in 2023, when he set the franchise rookie record with 73 receptions, after two injury‑marred seasons that saw him play on compromised knees.
