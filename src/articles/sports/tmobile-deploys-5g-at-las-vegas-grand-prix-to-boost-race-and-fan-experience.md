T‑Mobile announced it will activate its Formula 1 regional partnership at the Las Vegas Grand Prix, using its 5G network to enhance race operations and fan experiences.

The company says the event’s demanding environment serves as a proving ground for its advanced 5G, allowing testing of new capabilities at scale while delivering members additional ways to engage with the sport. T‑Mobile also noted its network helps the race run smarter and gives fans and members more ways to experience the Grand Prix.

The release originated from Bellevue, Washington, and was distributed through Business Wire.
