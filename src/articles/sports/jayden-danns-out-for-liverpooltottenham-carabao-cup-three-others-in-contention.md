Midfielder Jayden Danns has been ruled out of Liverpool's Carabao Cup tie against Tottenham Hotspur at Anfield on Tuesday, with the club indicating that three other players remain under consideration for the vacant spot.

In a pre‑match news conference, Liverpool manager Andoni Iraola said the team would welcome a strong cup run but emphasized that balancing the competition with an already intense fixture list is a delicate juggling act.

Captain Virgil van Dijk reflected on the Carabao Cup's memorable moments for Liverpool and affirmed the squad's ambition to progress further, adding that the side must adapt quickly to the demanding schedule ahead.
