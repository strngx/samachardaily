Curt Cignetti capped last year’s campaign with a flawless record, granting his returning squad a brief five‑day respite before the next cycle of drills and meetings. The short break underscored the coach’s confidence in the group’s readiness, while also fueling speculation about how much continuity will translate into 2026 success.

Key contributors such as quarterback J. Miller and defensive end L. Hernandez are slated to return, bolstered by a nationally ranked recruiting class that adds depth at linebacker and wide receiver. The Hoosiers’ schedule now features two top‑ten opponents and a revamped non‑conference slate, raising the bar for consistency and execution.

Experts project Indiana to remain a contender for the conference crown, but warn that injuries, turnover in the secondary, and the heightened strength of schedule could derail the magic. Cignetti’s play‑calling adaptability and his ability to integrate freshmen will be decisive factors as the Hoosiers chase a repeat of their perfect season.
