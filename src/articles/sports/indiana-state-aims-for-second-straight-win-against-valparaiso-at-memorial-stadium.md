Indiana State’s football team will try to extend its winning run on Saturday, when the Sycamores host Valparaiso at Memorial Stadium. The matchup is scheduled to begin at 1 p.m., and a victory would give ISU back‑to‑back wins.

Valparaiso competes in the Pioneer Football League for football, although the school participates in the Missouri Valley Conference for most other sports. A similar arrangement applies to Drake, which also belongs to the MVC in non‑football competitions.
