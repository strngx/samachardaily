The Board of Control for Cricket in India's selection panel has convened to name the squad for the forthcoming One‑Day International series against the West Indies, with captain Rohit Sharma, now 39, at the centre of debate.

Rohit’s inclusion is being weighed against concerns about his ability to sustain performance through the 2027 ODI World Cup, scheduled to be held across South Africa, Zimbabwe and Namibia.

The outcome of the meeting will determine whether the veteran batsman leads the side in the West Indies tour, a series that could serve as a litmus test for his World Cup prospects.
