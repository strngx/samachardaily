The Election Commission of India on Friday allotted new names and symbols to the rival factions of the Trinamool Congress led by West Bengal Chief Minister Mamata Banerjee and Arup Roy.

Mamata Banerjee’s faction received the football player symbol, while Arup Roy-led rebels were allotted the envelope symbol.

The decision came a day after the ECI barred both groups from using the party’s original name “All India Trinamool Congress” and its Flowers and Grass symbol, ahead of the October 6 bypoll.
