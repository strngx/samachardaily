The NFL's international series opened Sunday morning with a game in Germany between the New England Patriots and the Detroit Lions. The Patriots drove the field on the opening drive, entering the red zone and setting up a third-and-goal from the Lions' 13-yard line.

On that play, Aidan Hutchinson sacked quarterback Drake Maye. While celebrating the sack, Hutchinson appeared to suffer a nasty-looking knee injury, drawing immediate concern on the field.
