The Duke men's soccer team suffered a decisive 3-0 defeat against sixth-ranked Georgetown on Sunday, August 30, at Freeman Field at Koskinen Stadium. The non-conference fixture saw the Blue Devils struggle to contain a disciplined visiting attack, ultimately yielding three goals on home turf in front of their home supporters.

Playing at Koskinen Stadium in Durham, North Carolina, Duke sought to secure a statement victory against one of the top-ranked programs in collegiate soccer. However, the visiting Hoyas demonstrated their tactical maturity and offensive sharpness early on, establishing control over key phases of play and cleanly converting their scoring opportunities to build a commanding three-goal margin.

The result highlights early-season tests for the Blue Devils as they navigate a challenging schedule against upper-echelon national competition. Facing an opponent holding the No. 6 national ranking provided an immediate benchmark for Duke, exposing defensive gaps and areas requiring swift tactical adjustments.

For Georgetown, the shutout victory on the road reinforces their status as a major national championship contender. Earning a comprehensive 3-0 win away from home at Freeman Field gives the Hoyas significant momentum and solidifies their standing among the nation's elite collegiate squads.

Duke will evaluate match footage to correct structural mistakes following the three-goal deficit. As the Blue Devils regroup from the weekend contest, competing against high-caliber non-conference opposition will serve as a crucial learning opportunity for the squad's ongoing development this season.
