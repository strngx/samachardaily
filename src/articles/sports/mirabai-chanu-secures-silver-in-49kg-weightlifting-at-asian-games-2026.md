India’s Mirabai Chanu earned a silver medal in the women’s 49kg weightlifting competition at the 2026 Asian Games, posting a total lift of 198kg. She trailed North Korea’s Ri Song Gum, who took gold.

The medal marks Chanu’s first podium finish at these Games and comes after she battled past injuries and other challenges, underscoring her perseverance and commitment to the sport.
