The Denver Nuggets entered Monday’s road game with a 5‑4 record and fell 110‑100 to the Houston Rockets, marking their fourth defeat in nine contests.

Jamal Murray and Nikola Jokic together accounted for 63 of Denver’s points, and assistant coach David Adelman publicly praised Murray, saying his play resembled that of Luka Doncic.

Adelman’s endorsement highlights Murray’s scoring importance as the Nuggets look to steady their performance in the early 2026‑27 season.
