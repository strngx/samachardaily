The Oklahoma State Cowgirls will host Arizona State this Friday night at Neal Patterson Stadium, marking the first of three consecutive home matches for the team. The Cowgirls sit 5-6-3 on the season and remain tied for 12th place in the Big 12 conference with a record of 1-4-1.

This homestand comes after a season where the Cowgirls have struggled defensively, posting a 1-4-1 record overall. Their upcoming match against Arizona State represents a shift toward securing vital points in the Big 12 race.
