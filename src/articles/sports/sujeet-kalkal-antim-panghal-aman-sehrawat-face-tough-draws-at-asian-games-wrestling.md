All three athletes are expected to test their form against strong opponents in their respective weight categories. Sujeet Kalkal's recent form has been notable, Antim Panghal aims to regain attention, and Aman Sehrawat brings Olympic experience. Their performances will be closely watched as India seeks to improve its wrestling medal tally at the Games.
