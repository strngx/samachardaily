Jamie Carragher, the ex‑Liverpool defender turned pundit, remarked that Arsenal boss Mikel Arteta is "becoming the Sir Alex Ferguson of the Premier League now" following the manager’s outspoken criticism of a referee.

Arteta’s rant centred on a contentious penalty decision involving Sunderland, which he described as a clear error and publicly lambasted the official for the call.

In response, Arsenal have indicated they will contact the Professional Referees' Association to formally raise concerns over the penalty ruling.
