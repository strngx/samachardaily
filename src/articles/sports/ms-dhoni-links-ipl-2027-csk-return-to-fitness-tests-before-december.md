MS Dhoni has tied his IPL 2027 availability with Chennai Super Kings to fitness tests scheduled before December. He will rule himself in or out only after assessing his physical condition.

He made the remarks at a recent event, where he also acknowledged that CSK has struggled in the past two seasons.

His comments leave his participation in the upcoming edition uncertain pending medical clearance and a final personal review of his fitness.
