The 21-year-old boxer, who prepared at the Subhash Chandra Foundation Boxing Training Centre in Gangwa, delivered a dominant performance to claim the top podium spot. He earned a clear 4-0 decision over South Korea's Kim Minseong in the championship bout of the men's 80kg category.

This triumph marks a landmark achievement in Panghal's competitive career, adding another major continental honor to his expanding international resume. His gold-medal result in the tournament highlights his athletic progress while spotlighting the Gangwa-based training center where he developed his skills.
