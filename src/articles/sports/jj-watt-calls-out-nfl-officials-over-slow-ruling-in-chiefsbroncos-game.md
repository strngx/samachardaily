The Kansas City Chiefs hosted the Denver Broncos for the second time this season on Sunday, and the Broncos claimed a 28‑24 victory. The contest was overshadowed by controversy, continuing a pattern of debated officiating in Chiefs games this year. The matchup was the second meeting of the season between the two teams.

CBS Sports analyst J.J. Watt criticized a particular referee ruling that took an unusually long time to be decided, calling out the officials for the delay. His remarks highlighted concerns about the pace of reviews.

J.J. Watt's criticism has drawn attention to the slow ruling during the game.
