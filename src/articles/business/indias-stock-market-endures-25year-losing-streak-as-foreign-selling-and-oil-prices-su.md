India's equity markets have entered their longest losing streak in 25 years, with the Nifty 50 and BSE Sensex falling sharply. Record foreign selling and soaring crude oil prices are identified as the primary drivers of the downturn.

Market analysts warn that investors face a difficult path ahead as inflation concerns grow. The Reserve Bank of India is preparing for a potential interest‑rate hike, reflecting worries about price stability and economic growth.
