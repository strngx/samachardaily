The Florida Beef Council announced the conclusion of a spring‑time digital e‑commerce program it funded with $50,000, partnering with one of the nation’s largest retailers.

Council spokesperson Deanne Maples said the council received the campaign’s final results this week and described the outcomes as “pretty exciting,” though specific performance metrics were not disclosed.
