UK stocks ended higher on Wednesday, with healthcare and consumer staples sectors posting notable gains as a weaker sterling provided critical support to export-focused companies navigating shifting trade dynamics.

The upward movement coincided with economic data showing US inflation came in hotter than expected, which weighed on broader market confidence even as sector leaders maintained their strength.

Investors observed how currency shifts and differing inflation trajectories continue to shape equity performance across major economies, underscoring the interconnected nature of global financial markets.
