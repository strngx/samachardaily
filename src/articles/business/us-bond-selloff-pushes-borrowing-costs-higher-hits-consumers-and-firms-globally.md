A recent selloff in U.S. government bonds has driven Treasury yields upward, raising borrowing costs across the broader economy.

Higher yields are translating into steeper mortgage and auto‑loan rates, directly tightening household finances.

Companies are now facing increased financing costs, which dampens the incentive for new investments and expansion projects.

The surge in interest expenses also strains the U.S. government's fiscal position, while global markets adjust to the shifting benchmark yields.
