EverestIMS Tech has made a strong debut on the Bombay Stock Exchange as a Small and Medium Enterprise (SME). The company's initial public offering (IPO) was met with robust market response, with shares trading at a 48% premium to the offer price during the market debut.

Analysts and investors highlighted the successful launch as a notable moment for the BSE SME segment. The premium listing reflects strong demand and confidence in the company's prospects following its recent IPO.
