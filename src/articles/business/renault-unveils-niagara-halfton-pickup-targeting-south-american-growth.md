Renault has launched the Niagara, a half‑ton pickup, marking the French automaker's newest model aimed at the South American market.

The introduction of the Niagara is part of Renault's broader strategy to grow its footprint in the region, where the company has been seeking to increase sales volumes.

By adding a commercial‑grade pickup to its lineup, Renault hopes to attract business customers and compete more directly with established rivals in the South American light‑truck segment.
