The Karpacz Economic Forum 2026 convened policymakers, business leaders and analysts to assess Poland’s economic direction, the nation’s internal political divisions and the prospects for the Visegrád Group (V4).

During the event, Prime Minister Mateusz Morawiecki was interviewed directly, offering his perspective on the country’s growth, the political landscape and the future cooperation among V4 members.
