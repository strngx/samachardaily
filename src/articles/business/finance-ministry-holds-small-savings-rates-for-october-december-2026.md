The Finance Ministry has kept interest rates on all major small savings schemes unchanged for the October-December 2026 quarter. The decision leaves the existing percentages in place, with no new rate revision announced for the period.

The Public Provident Fund will continue to earn 7.1%, while the Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana and Senior Citizens' Savings Scheme will both remain at 8.2%. These are the rates that will apply to the schemes during the quarter.

For small savers, the unchanged rates mean interest earnings on PPF, Sukanya Samriddhi and SCSS will stay the same through December 2026.
