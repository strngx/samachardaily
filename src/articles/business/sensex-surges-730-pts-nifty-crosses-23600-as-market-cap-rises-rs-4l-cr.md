The Indian equity market closed higher on Tuesday, with the Sensex advancing roughly 730 points and the Nifty 50 gaining more than 190 points. The rally lifted overall market capitalisation by about Rs 4 lakh crore, underscoring a sharp rise in investor confidence. The surge also saw several sectoral indices posting double‑digit gains, especially in banking and consumer goods.

Traders cited expectations of possible LTCG tax reforms and a decline in global oil prices as the primary drivers of the gain. The combination of policy anticipation and cheaper energy costs helped push the benchmark indices to fresh levels, reflecting robust sentiment across the board. Analysts note that the positive momentum could persist if oil remains subdued and tax‑policy talks progress.
