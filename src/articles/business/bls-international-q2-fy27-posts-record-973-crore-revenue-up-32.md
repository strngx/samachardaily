BLS International delivered a record Q2 FY27 performance, reporting revenue of ₹973 crore—an increase of 32% year‑on‑year. The growth was driven by stronger Visa and Consular Services activity combined with expanding Digital Services. For the first half, total revenue climbed 28.7% to ₹1,863 crore, underscoring a robust start to the fiscal year.

Quarterly EBITDA rose 16.6% and profit after tax climbed 8.6%, reflecting improved margins despite heightened operational costs. The company says the surge in digital solutions and streamlined visa processing fueled the top‑line expansion. While the results signal strong momentum, market observers will watch whether the earnings growth can translate into share‑price recovery in the coming quarters.
