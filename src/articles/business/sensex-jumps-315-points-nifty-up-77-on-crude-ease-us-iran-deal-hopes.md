Indian equity markets rebounded on Friday, with the Sensex climbing 315 points and the Nifty rising 77 points, driven by easing crude prices and optimism over a potential US-Iran agreement.

Analysts noted that while investor sentiment improved, trading remained selective due to ongoing global economic risks.

The gains reflect renewed confidence in domestic markets despite broader uncertainties, with crude oil price movements playing a key role in shaping investor appetite.
