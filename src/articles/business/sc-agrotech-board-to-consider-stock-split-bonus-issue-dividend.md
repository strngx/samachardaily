SC Agrotech will hold a board meeting on November 13, 2026, to consider a stock split of up to ten shares for each existing share, a bonus issue of up to four shares for every five held, and a dividend of up to 100 percent of the face value. The filing notes these are proposals, not final approvals.

The company's standalone equity capital stood at roughly ₹75.995 crore at the end of September, while cash and cash equivalents were about ₹13.17 lakh. In the same quarter, revenue rose to ₹45.26 crore from ₹5.02 crore a year earlier, and net profit reached ₹10.21 crore.
