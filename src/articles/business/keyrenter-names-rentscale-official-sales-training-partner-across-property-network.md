Keyrenter Property Management, a prominent national franchise network, has officially selected RentScale as its designated sales training and coaching partner. Announced on September 3, 2026, out of Boca Raton, Florida, the strategic collaboration is structured to equip Keyrenter franchise operators with structured, industry-specific sales systems designed to accelerate revenue growth.

Under the newly established partnership, Keyrenter franchise owners will gain direct access to RentScale’s specialized sales methodologies and targeted executive coaching. A primary focal point of the initiative includes dedicated training programs for Business Development Managers (BDMs), providing standardized playbooks to help individual franchise locations systematically acquire new management contracts.

For property management organizations, scaling operational footprints requires robust client acquisition strategies that go beyond traditional property maintenance and tenant management. By formalizing this relationship, Keyrenter aims to standardize sales execution across its entire franchise network, offering owners actionable frameworks to convert leads into long-term management agreements.

The integration comes as property management groups increasingly turn to specialized external consulting to optimize local market performance. RentScale’s focus on the residential property management vertical enables Keyrenter operators to implement sales strategies crafted specifically for real estate investors and rental property owners.

With the partnership now official, Keyrenter franchise locations will begin incorporating RentScale’s training modules into their active business development frameworks, seeking to establish consistent, scalable organic growth metrics across their national network of independent franchise units.
