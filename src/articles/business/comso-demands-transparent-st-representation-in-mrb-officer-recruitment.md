The Confederation of Meghalaya Social Organisations (CoMSO) has formally petitioned the Meghalaya Rural Bank (MRB) and the state government, requesting full disclosure of recruitment procedures and outcomes.

CoMSO’s statement points to data from recent MRB hiring cycles that reveal a shortfall in Scheduled Tribe (ST) candidates, particularly for officer‑level positions, raising concerns about equitable representation.

The body is calling for immediate corrective measures, urging MRB and authorities to review selection criteria and implement steps that ensure ST candidates receive fair opportunity in future recruitments.
