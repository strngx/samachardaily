PW Consulting projects that worldwide revenue from Dolby Vision‑enabled televisions will total $26.98 billion in 2025 and climb beyond $67 billion by 2032.

The forecast reflects a broader shift in the television market, where manufacturers and consumers are prioritising high‑dynamic‑range (HDR) performance to deliver cinema‑like picture quality at home.

Dolby Vision, once marketed as a premium differentiator, has become a near‑essential feature for mid‑to‑high‑end TV models, prompting industry players and investors to factor the technology into product roadmaps and capital allocation.
