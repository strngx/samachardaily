Worldwide sales revenue generated by Dolby Vision‑enabled televisions is projected to expand from $26.98 billion in 2025 to exceed $67 billion by 2032, according to a display industry research forecast published by PW Consulting.

The multi-year outlook reflects an ongoing shift across the global television market, where consumers and manufacturers increasingly prioritize advanced High-Dynamic-Range (HDR) formats capable of delivering scene-by-scene dynamic metadata optimization and enhanced color accuracy for home entertainment.

Historically treated as an exclusive differentiator restricted to top-tier flagship OLED and Mini-LED screens, Dolby Vision technology is rapidly moving into mainstream mid-range 4K and 8K smart television lineups. This adoption curve is heavily reinforced by content ecosystem dynamics: leading subscription video-on-demand platforms, including Netflix, Apple TV+, and Disney+, now routinely master original films and episodic series in Dolby Vision, creating direct consumer pull for compatible playback hardware.

As display original equipment manufacturers (OEMs) integrate dedicated HDR processing silicon into standard consumer models, industry analysts anticipate sustained commercial demand. However, analysts note that long-term realization of the $67 billion forecast remains subject to broader consumer electronics replacement cycles and regional macroeconomic spending conditions.
