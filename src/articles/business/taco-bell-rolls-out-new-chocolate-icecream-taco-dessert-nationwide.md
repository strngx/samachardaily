Taco Bell announced that a new chocolate‑topped ice‑cream taco dessert will be available at every U.S. restaurant. The product is the latest collaboration between the fast‑food chain and Salt & Straw, the Portland‑based artisanal ice‑cream maker, and is being marketed as a novel chocolate treat that blends the brand’s taco format with premium ice cream.

The partnership builds on a prior limited‑edition offering that paired Taco Bell’s menu with Salt & Straw’s flavors. That earlier collaboration proved popular among younger diners and generated significant social‑media buzz, prompting the two companies to develop a second joint dessert. By re‑uniting, both brands aim to capitalize on the momentum created by the first release.

Salt & Straw, founded in Portland, has earned a reputation for inventive flavor combinations and small‑batch production. Its involvement brings an artisanal credibility that Taco Bell hopes will attract consumers seeking premium dessert experiences within a fast‑food setting. The ice‑cream taco leverages Salt & Straw’s expertise in texture and flavor while retaining the recognizable taco shape that defines the chain’s menu.

Industry analysts note that fast‑food operators are increasingly turning to specialty partners to differentiate their dessert lines. The chocolate ice‑cream taco arrives at a time when consumers are gravitating toward indulgent, shareable treats that can be enjoyed on‑the‑go. By offering a dessert that merges familiar fast‑food branding with upscale ice‑cream craftsmanship, Taco Bell is positioning itself to capture discretionary spending that might otherwise flow to dedicated dessert chains.

The new dessert will roll out across all Taco Bell locations nationwide, with the company indicating that the product will be stocked in the coming weeks. Customers can expect the chocolate‑coated ice‑cream taco to be displayed alongside other limited‑time offerings, and the chain plans to monitor sales and social‑media response to gauge the partnership’s success.
