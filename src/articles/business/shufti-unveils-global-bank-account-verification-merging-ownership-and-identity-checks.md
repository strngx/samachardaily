Global identity verification and regulatory compliance provider Shufti has officially launched its Bank Account Verification capability, integrating real-time account ownership checks and customer identity validation onto a unified compliance platform.

The newly deployed service validates bank account ownership details directly against banking network records across more than 70 countries. The system supports core payment operations, including instant account linking, merchant payout setup, automated direct debits, and high-value beneficiary modifications.

By querying official financial rails and open banking interfaces, the platform confirms that the individual or corporate entity undergoing identity verification matches the verified legal owner of the destination bank account. The system operates without requiring end-user configuration or manual document uploads, processing verification requests automatically during customer onboarding or payout initiation.

The feature is accessible globally to enterprise users on Shufti’s subscription plans, designed specifically for fintech platforms, marketplace operators, gig-economy employers, and cross-border payment processors requiring automated compliance with global KYC and anti-fraud mandates.
