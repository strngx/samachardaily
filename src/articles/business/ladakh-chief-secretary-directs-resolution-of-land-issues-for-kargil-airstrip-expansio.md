Ladakh Chief Secretary Ashish Kundra on Saturday, Oct 31, directed the deputy commissioner of Kargil to conduct a joint site inspection with the Indian Air Force to resolve land acquisition issues and objections for the Kargil airstrip expansion at the earliest.

The inspection, ordered from Leh/Jammu on Saturday, Oct 31, aims to expedite resolution of land-related hurdles and facilitate early clearance for the airstrip project, an official said.

Officials reported that the chief secretary’s directive requires the deputy commissioner of Kargil and the Indian Air Force to jointly inspect the site and resolve land acquisition issues for the Kargil airstrip expansion at the earliest.
