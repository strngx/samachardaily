Vijay Kedia, managing director of Kedia Securities Pvt Ltd, addressed the ET Wealth Expo in Mumbai on what it takes to survive the stock market. The veteran investor pointed to management, knowledge, courage, and patience as the essential qualities for investors.

Kedia shared hard-earned lessons on picking winning stocks and learning from bear markets during the session. The masterclass combined market insights with music, as Kedia performed a song or two along the way.

The Economic Times hosted the wealth expo event in Mumbai, where Kedia spoke as MD of Kedia Securities Pvt Ltd.
