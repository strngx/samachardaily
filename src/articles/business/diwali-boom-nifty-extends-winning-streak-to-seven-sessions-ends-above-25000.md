Indian equity benchmarks extended their winning streak to seven straight trading sessions on Friday, marking the first such run in three years, as heavy buying pushed the benchmark index past the 25,000 level.

The rally came amid broader domestic strength that saw indices defy global market routs and benefit from festive-season corporate earnings momentum.

Analysts noted that the streak reflects sustained investor confidence ahead of key economic data releases and policy decisions expected in coming weeks.
