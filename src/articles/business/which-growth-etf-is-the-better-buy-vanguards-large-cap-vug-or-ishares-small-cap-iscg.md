Vanguard's Large‑Cap Growth ETF (VUG) has posted higher cumulative returns than iShares' Small‑Cap Growth ETF (ISCG) across a five‑year horizon, reflecting the broader market tilt toward large‑cap growth stocks during that period.

However, ISCG has surged 26.5% over the past twelve months, outpacing VUG's recent performance as investors rotate into smaller, higher‑growth companies amid improving earnings outlooks and a more accommodative monetary stance.

Both funds offer low‑cost exposure to growth equities, but their differing market‑cap focus means portfolio managers must weigh long‑term stability against short‑term momentum when allocating capital.
