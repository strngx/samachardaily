A seven-session drop in Taiwan’s equity benchmark ended on Monday. The decline had reached more than 7,275 points, or 16.8 percent. Trading last stood just above the 41,660-point mark after the selloff.

Monday’s advance stopped the decline, and the market may extend those gains on Tuesday. The benchmark had entered the losing streak before the halt, according to the report.

The exchange now sits near the 41,660-point level. The report said the market may add to its winnings on Tuesday. The halt followed the recent crash and the seven-day run.
