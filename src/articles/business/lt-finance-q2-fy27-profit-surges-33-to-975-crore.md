L&T Finance Ltd (LTF) posted a 33% jump in consolidated profit after tax to ₹975 crore for the second quarter of financial year 2027, compared to the same period last year.

The company's consolidated book expanded by 28% to ₹1,36,635 crore during the quarter, reflecting strong growth in its lending portfolio.

The profit surge was driven by robust retail demand, with the retail book growing 29% to ₹1,34,600 crore, underscoring LTF's focus on consumer-focused financing.
