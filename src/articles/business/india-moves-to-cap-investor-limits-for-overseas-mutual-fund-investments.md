India is set to introduce an individual investor cap for overseas mutual fund investments, targeting the longstanding privilege that limits foreign exposure to affluent investors and seeks to democratize access.

The proposal will impose a per‑person limit on foreign mutual fund purchases, creating a regulated, low‑cost channel for small investors to gain diversified international exposure, while addressing the current gap where high entry barriers restrict participation.

Regulators say the cap will ensure compliance with foreign investment norms and prevent market distortion, while enabling middle‑income households to allocate assets abroad without excessive financial hurdles.
