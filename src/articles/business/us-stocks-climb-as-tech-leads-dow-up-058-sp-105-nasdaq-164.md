United States equity markets closed higher on Wednesday, driven by a broad-based rally across mega-cap technology and semiconductor companies as investors analyzed economic data and macroeconomic indicators.

The Dow Jones Industrial Average gained 238 points, or 0.58%, to settle at 41,391, while the S&P 500 rose 1.05% to finish at 5,626. The tech-heavy Nasdaq Composite outperformed the broader market, advancing 1.64% to close at 17,683.

Semiconductor manufacturers and large-cap software enterprises led the session's gains, supported by sustained institutional interest in artificial intelligence infrastructure and cloud computing hardware. The rally in technology and communication services sectors offset modest declines in energy and consumer defensive shares.

Market participants also monitored fixed-income markets, where the benchmark 10-year US Treasury yield traded in a narrow range. Trading desks noted measured positioning across major exchanges as market participants prepared for upcoming Federal Reserve economic projections and monetary policy deliberations.
