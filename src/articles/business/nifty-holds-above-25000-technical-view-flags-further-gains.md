Trading above the 25,000 mark, the Nifty is holding in positive territory, according to a technical analysis shared on the TradingView platform.

That analysis expects the Nifty to extend its bullish momentum as long as it stays above a supply zone that has now turned into a demand zone. A slip below this level could weaken the positive outlook.

Traders have been advised to plan their trades around this level and keep a close watch on the index.
