The Centre's review petition aligns it with the Reserve Bank of India and Yes Bank, both of which had already sought reconsideration of the decision. With the government now on board, three parties are pressing the Supreme Court to revisit its October 15 verdict.

The October 15 judgment had set aside the write-off of Yes Bank's Rs 8,415-crore AT-1 bonds. The review petitions leave that decision open to re-examination by the top court.
