Wealth management firm Three Seasons Wealth LLC has established a brand-new position in TechnipFMC plc (NYSE: FTI), acquiring 31,843 shares during the second quarter of the fiscal year. According to the firm’s latest Form 13F disclosure filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC), the total market value of the newly disclosed equity stake stands at approximately $2,111,000.

The transaction represents a strategic addition to Three Seasons Wealth LLC's investment portfolio, marking a targeted allocation into the energy services and technology equipment provider. The purchase reflects institutional capital deployment into oil and gas market participants as asset management firms adjust portfolio exposures across major equity sectors.

Under federal securities regulations, institutional investment managers exercising investment discretion over $100 million or more in equity securities are required to submit quarterly Form 13F reports. These public disclosures provide institutional transparency and serve as a primary window for market participants tracking where institutional asset managers are directing capital.

TechnipFMC plc, traded on the New York Stock Exchange under the ticker FTI, functions as an energy technology provider specializing in subsea and surface solutions for the global oil and gas industry. The entry of Three Seasons Wealth LLC adds to the roster of institutional investors taking equity positions in the company during the recent quarter.

Because Form 13F filings reflect portfolio holdings at the close of the reporting period, market observers closely track these institutional position updates to gauge institutional confidence in specific energy sector stocks and evaluate underlying institutional liquidity trends.
