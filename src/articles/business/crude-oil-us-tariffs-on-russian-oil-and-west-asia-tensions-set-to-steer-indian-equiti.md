Indian equity markets are set to navigate market cues shaped by crude oil price movements, global bond yields, and West Asia geopolitical tensions this week, according to market analysts. Investor sentiment comes into focus following the enactment of the Sanctioning Russia and Iran Act, signed by United States President Donald Trump to expand executive tariff authority.

Under the legislation, the United States administration is granted the authority to levy tariffs of up to 100% on goods imported from major international buyers of Russian oil and natural gas. Because India relies on sizable import volumes of Russian crude, the tariff provision introduces significant trade and policy considerations for domestic markets.

Market analysts note that potential tariff actions or energy price surges could elevate domestic import costs, pressure corporate profit margins, and shift trade balances across sectors. Alongside energy developments, market participants and domestic traders are tracking foreign institutional investor flows and global market trends for direction on broader equity benchmarks.
