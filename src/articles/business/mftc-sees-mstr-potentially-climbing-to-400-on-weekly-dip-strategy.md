MFTC analysis maintains a bullish outlook for Strategy Inc. (NASDAQ: MSTR), projecting a potential rise to $300‑$400 if the weekly time‑frame structure holds.

The stock trades near $160 while Bitcoin is around $85,000, with both showing initial reversal structures on monthly and weekly charts; MSTR’s weekly supply/resistance zone lies at $170‑$175, and a dip to $150‑$155 is seen as a risk‑defined buying opportunity.

A break above $175 could lead to the $200‑$210 zone, while the MFTC view warns the bullish thesis fails if MSTR sustains weekly weakness below $145‑$150; Citi’s $240 target and the analyst consensus near $258 are noted as separate from the technical projection.
