The U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC) has issued a recall covering hundreds of thousands of children's finger‑light toys that were sold on Amazon.

The agency warned that the button‑cell batteries powering the toys pose a swallowing hazard and can cause burns if punctured, prompting the recall.
