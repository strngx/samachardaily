The Press Democrat reports that a single‑family home in Windsor fetched $822,000, making it the most expensive residential transaction in the city over the past seven days.

The sale sits atop a list of the seven highest‑priced homes sold during the same period, each exceeding six‑figures. While the exact prices of the remaining six properties were not disclosed, they collectively illustrate a surge in high‑value activity within Windsor’s real‑estate market.

Industry observers note that such top‑tier sales signal robust demand among affluent buyers, even as overall inventory remains constrained, potentially nudging median home prices upward in the short term.
