Saudi Arabia has reactivated crude oil loading operations at the Yanbu port along the Red Sea. The East-West onshore pipeline, which enables the kingdom to bypass the Strait of Hormuz, resumed partial service recently.

While partial flow has been restored, full resumption of approximately 4 million barrels per day is expected to take several weeks. The pipeline's recovery marks a critical adjustment to maintain energy supply routes around regional security challenges.
