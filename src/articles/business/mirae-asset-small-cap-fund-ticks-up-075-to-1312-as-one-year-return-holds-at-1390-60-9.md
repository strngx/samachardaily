Mirae Asset Small Cap Fund Direct Growth closed at a net asset value of ₹13.12 on 3 September 2026, marking a 0.75% rise on the day. The small-cap mutual fund has delivered a one-year return of 13.90%, according to data reported by 5paisa.

The fund, managed by Mirae Asset Investment Managers, is structured as a direct-growth plan — meaning investors buy units directly from the asset management company without distributor intermediation, and returns are reinvested rather than paid out as dividends. This structure typically results in a lower expense ratio compared with the regular plan of the same scheme.

Small-cap funds invest predominantly in companies ranked beyond the top 250 by market capitalisation on Indian exchanges. They are generally considered higher-risk, higher-reward instruments, with returns subject to greater volatility than large-cap or multi-cap peers. The 13.90% one-year return places the fund's recent performance in a context where Indian small-cap indices have seen mixed momentum amid broader market fluctuations.

The reported NAV of ₹13.12 reflects the per-unit market value of the fund's underlying portfolio as of the close of trading on 3 September 2026. NAV movements are driven by the daily price changes of the fund's constituent holdings, which typically include a basket of small-cap equities across sectors. The 0.75% single-day gain indicates a broadly positive session for the fund's holdings.

Investors can access the Mirae Asset Small Cap Fund through systematic investment plans or lump-sum purchases, subject to the scheme's minimum investment threshold.
