U.S. stock futures extended gains after Democrats swept the midterm elections, securing control of Congress. The Dow rallied 1,500 points while the S&P 500 and Nasdaq also posted strong gains, reflecting heightened investor optimism.

The rally, estimated at about $750 billion, underscored a broad tech surge across data‑center and tariff‑sensitive sectors, as analysts linked the momentum to the Democratic victory and its potential policy shifts.

The outcome sets the stage for legislative debates on technology regulation and trade, while the lingering influence of a lame‑duck Trump administration remains a focal point for global markets.
