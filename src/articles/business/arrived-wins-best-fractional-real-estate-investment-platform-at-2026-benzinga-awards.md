The announcement was made on 22 November 2026 at 02:00 CET/CEST by Menafn.

Arrived was named the winner of Best Fractional Real Estate Investment Platform at the 2026 Benzinga Awards, a recognition that highlights its ability to let investors purchase fractional stakes in real estate assets, lowering the capital required for property exposure. The award, part of the 2026 Benzinga Awards, underscores the platform’s innovative approach to real estate investment and reflects growing demand for diversified, low‑entry solutions in today’s financial landscape.

The award highlights Arrived’s growing influence in the fractional investment sector. This recognition positions Arrived for further expansion in the fractional investment market.
