The Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO) announced a revision to its partial withdrawal framework, aiming to make access to savings more straightforward for members facing specific life events.

Under the new guidelines, eligible members may withdraw up to 75% of their EPF balance, subject to category‑specific ceilings that apply to education, marriage, medical illness and housing purchases.

The reform seeks to reduce procedural complexity that previously deterred members from tapping their provident funds for urgent or planned expenditures, aligning EPFO's practices with broader financial inclusion goals.

While the announcement outlines the broadened withdrawal ceiling, it does not disclose the exact monetary limits attached to each purpose, leaving members to consult EPFO's official portal or service centres for detailed figures.

Industry observers note that the change could improve liquidity for workers, particularly in sectors where wage delays are common, and may encourage greater participation in the EPF scheme.

The EPFO has urged members to review the updated rules on its website and to ensure that any withdrawal complies with the stipulated documentation and eligibility criteria.
