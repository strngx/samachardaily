India Post has announced the official schedule for the Gramin Dak Sevak (GDS) 2026 Session II recruitment drive. Registration for prospective candidates is set to open on August 31, 2026, marking the commencement of the second major intake cycle for rural postal positions this year.

Following the initial registration opening, the formal submission of online applications will begin on September 2, 2026. All eligible applicants must register their profile and submit their completed forms through the official India Post recruitment portal within the designated timelines.

Gramin Dak Sevaks play a crucial role in maintaining India Post’s expansive reach across semi-urban and rural territories. The Session II recruitment initiative aims to fill operational vacancies and reinforce daily postal, financial, and logistical services delivered by frontline staff.

Candidates planning to apply for the GDS 2026 Session II cycle are advised to keep their academic certificates, identification documents, and personal details prepared ahead of the portal's submission launch on September 2 to avoid technical delays.

The step-by-step application workflow on the central portal is designed to streamline registration, allowing applicants across all postal circles to complete their submissions efficiently once the window opens.
