Peñoles and Atlas Renewable Energy have signed a 20-year power purchase agreement to supply the Mexican mining group with approximately 550 GWh of renewable electricity per year. The project, located in Coahuila state, combines 269 MWp of solar generation with battery storage to support Peñoles' decarbonization targets.

Atlas said the contract expands its portfolio of large industrial offtakers in Mexico. The solar-plus-storage facility will strengthen renewable supply reliability for Peñoles' operations while reducing carbon intensity across its mining and metallurgical assets.
