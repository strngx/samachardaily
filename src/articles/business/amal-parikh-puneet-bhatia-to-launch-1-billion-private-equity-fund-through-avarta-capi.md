Amal Parikh and Puneet Bhatia plan to launch a $1 billion private equity fund through Avarta Capital, with Parikh as managing partner and Bhatia guiding from TPG Capital.

The fund targets consumer, healthcare, and financial services sectors, aiming for annualized returns of 20-25%. Already ₹7,500 crore in soft commitments has been secured toward the closing goal.

The managers target finalizing the fund by March, closing a significant new vehicle for India-focused private equity.
