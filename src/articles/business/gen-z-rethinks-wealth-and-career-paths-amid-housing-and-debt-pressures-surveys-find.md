Recent surveys indicate a notable shift in how India's Gen Z views wealth accumulation and career advancement, challenging the stereotype of a generation indifferent to traditional professional trajectories.

While still aiming for financial security and career growth, respondents report adjusting their pathways because rising housing prices, mounting debt and higher living costs make conventional ladders feel less attainable.

The findings nuance the common portrayal of Gen Z as less loyal to employers, suggesting that economic pressures, rather than a lack of ambition, are driving the re‑evaluation of career and wealth strategies.
