Taiwan's stock market recovered on Friday, one day after a three-day winning streak came to an end. That rally had added almost 3,550 points, or 8.6 percent, to the benchmark.

The Taiwan Stock Exchange now sits just above the 43,675-point plateau, holding near that level after the steep advance drove the index higher across three consecutive trading sessions.

The rebound may prove short-lived, however, with technology shares expected to pull the market lower again on Monday.
