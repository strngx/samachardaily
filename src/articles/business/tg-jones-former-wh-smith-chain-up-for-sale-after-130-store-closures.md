TG Jones, the former WH Smith high street chain, is being lined up for sale less than 18 months after owner Modella Capital completed its takeover, with an auction of the retailer planned for next year.

The 233-year-old business employs about 3,500 people and has closed 130 stores. Modella, whose other holdings include Hobbycraft, bought the chain for £76m in June last year.

A completed auction would end a brief ownership of the retailer, which traded under the WH Smith name before the takeover.
