The centre has raised dearness allowance from 60% to 63%, a 3% jump that can lift the retirement gratuity of central government employees by as much as ₹50,000.

The exact increase varies with each employee’s basic salary and length of qualifying service, because gratuity is calculated on a formula that multiplies a portion of basic pay by years served.

The benefit is also capped by the statutory ₹25 lakh gratuity ceiling, meaning only staff whose projected payout falls below that limit will see the full ₹50,000 boost.
