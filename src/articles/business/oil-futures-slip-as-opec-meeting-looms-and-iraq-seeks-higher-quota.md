Oil futures settled lower today as markets awaited the upcoming OPEC+ policy meeting, with both WTI and Brent prices showing little movement.

Iraq's oil minister said the country is in talks with OPEC about its production ceiling, pressing for a higher quota and adding pressure on the group.

Sources told Reuters that OPEC+ is expected to roll over its Q1 output targets, citing ongoing capacity disputes that suggest limited changes at the meeting.
