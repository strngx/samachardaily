Indian equity benchmarks have posted stronger gains than global peers so far this November, with the Sensex advancing 12.71% and the Nifty rising 11.92%. Foreign institutional investors have poured a record Rs 1.12 lakh crore into Indian markets during this period.

Over the same period, South Korea's KOSPI declined 15.02% and Hong Kong's Hang Seng fell 3.79%. Attention now turns to next week's trading sessions, with Nifty eyeing the 26,000 level and the durability of the banking rally in question.

Market participants are monitoring whether the banking sector's recent advance can extend further in the coming sessions.
