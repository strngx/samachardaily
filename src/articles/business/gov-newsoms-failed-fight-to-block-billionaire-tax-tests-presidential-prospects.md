California Governor Gavin Newsom worked to prevent a proposed billionaire tax from appearing on the state ballot, but his effort did not succeed.

With the tax now slated for voter consideration, the ensuing campaign will serve as a barometer for Newsom’s broader political ambitions, including a potential run for the U.S. presidency.

The outcome of the ballot measure could influence both California’s fiscal policy and Newsom’s standing among voters and party leaders as he navigates his national profile.
