Adroit Industries made a strong market entry on Thursday, with its shares opening at ₹250 on the Bombay Stock Exchange. The debut price marked a significant jump from the issue price, signaling robust investor interest in the newly listed company.

On the National Stock Exchange, the shares also launched at a premium of 75.37% relative to the issue price. Combined, the BSE listing reflected an even higher premium of 86.57%, underscoring the enthusiasm among investors for Adroit Industries' IPO.

The steep premiums indicate strong demand and confidence in the company's growth prospects, potentially setting a positive tone for upcoming listings in the sector. No confirmed next steps reported yet.
