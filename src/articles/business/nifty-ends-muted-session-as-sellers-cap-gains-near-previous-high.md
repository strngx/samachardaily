NIFTY opened slightly lower on Thursday and closed with a relatively muted performance on a closing basis, despite notable intraday swings that marked the overall trading session.

During the day, buyers and sellers engaged in an intense tussle for control, with buyers initially attempting to seize the initiative while sellers effectively capped the upside as prices neared the previous session's high.

This resistance triggered a gradual drift, leaving the index slightly lower by the close. The muted outcome underscored the difficulty buyers faced in breaking above the prior session's high.
