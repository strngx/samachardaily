Friday saw Indian equity benchmarks close sharply higher, extending gains for a second consecutive session. Banking and financial stocks led the advance, with the Sensex rising 897.96 points, or 1.08 per cent, to settle at 83,919.71, while the Nifty closed above 25,800.

The rally was driven by expectations of improving foreign institutional investor inflows, as documented in the Mumbai report. Sectoral focus remained on banking shares, which underpinned the broader market uptick.

The report, filed from Mumbai on November 20, 2026, by Yes Punjab News, captured the benchmarks' closing levels and the session's sectoral performance.
