The Jersey City Council has scheduled public hearings and a formal vote for Wednesday regarding two proposed 30-year property tax abatements for the Canal Crossing residential development.

Planned along Garfield Avenue, the Canal Crossing project consists of a 500-unit residential complex that includes 102 designated affordable housing apartments. Proponents of the measure maintain that the long-term tax abatements are necessary financial mechanisms to offset high construction costs and ensure the viability of the affordable housing allocation.

During the same legislative meeting, council members are set to determine the municipal status of adjacent parcel land currently operated as a community garden. The dual items have prompted public debate among local residents and housing advocates, balancing the preservation of neighborhood green spaces and municipal tax revenue against the expansion of multi-family housing developments in the city.
