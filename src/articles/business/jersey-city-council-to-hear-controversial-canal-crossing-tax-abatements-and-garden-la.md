The Jersey City Council has scheduled public hearings and a vote for Wednesday on two 30‑year property tax abatements that supporters say are required to move forward with the Canal Crossing development, a proposal that has generated controversy.

Canal Crossing is planned as a 500‑unit residential complex on Garfield Avenue and will incorporate 102 affordable apartments; the proposed abatements are intended to offset costs associated with constructing the project.

In the same session, councilmembers will decide the fate of land currently used as a community garden, a measure that has also drawn public opposition.
