NEW YORK, Sept. 06, 2026 – Rosen Law Firm, a global investor‑rights practice, issued a formal reminder on Thursday to anyone who bought Wise Group plc (NASDAQ: WSE) securities between May and the present date. The reminder stresses that investors who have suffered losses exceeding $100,000 must secure legal representation before an imminent deadline in a securities class action that Rosen has filed on their behalf.

The class action, the first of its kind brought by Rosen against Wise Group, alleges that the company misrepresented key financial metrics, leading to a sharp decline in its share price. Purchasers who invested during the May‑June window are the focus of the suit, as that period coincides with the alleged disclosures. The firm notes that the aggregate losses among affected shareholders surpass the $100,000 threshold, a figure that triggers heightened legal scrutiny and potential recovery avenues.

Rosen Law emphasizes that the deadline is a statutory filing cut‑off that, if missed, could bar affected investors from participating in the collective recovery. In securities litigation, such deadlines are typically set by the court to preserve the integrity of the claims process and to ensure that counsel can adequately prepare evidence and expert testimony. The firm advises investors to act promptly, as counsel engagement after the deadline may limit their ability to claim damages.

Founded in 2005, Rosen Law has built a reputation for representing retail investors in complex securities disputes across multiple jurisdictions. The firm’s strategy often involves aggregating similarly situated investors to amplify bargaining power against publicly traded companies. By filing this class action, Rosen seeks to hold Wise Group accountable for alleged misstatements and to recover losses for its client base, many of whom lack the resources to pursue individual lawsuits.

Investors who meet the loss criteria are instructed to contact Rosen’s dedicated intake team no later than the stated deadline, provide documentation of their purchases, and retain independent counsel to evaluate their claim. The firm will then coordinate the collective filing, manage discovery, and negotiate any settlement offers. Failure to act before the deadline could result in forfeiture of legal rights under the class action framework.
