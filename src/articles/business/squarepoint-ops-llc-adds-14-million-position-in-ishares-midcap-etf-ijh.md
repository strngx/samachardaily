Squarepoint Ops LLC purchased 18,202 shares of the iShares Core S&P Mid‑Cap ETF (ticker IJH) during the second quarter, a transaction valued at roughly $1.404 million, according to the firm’s most recent 13F filing with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission.

The SEC filing shows the firm adding a fresh position in the mid‑cap ETF, joining a wave of recent institutional activity in the fund, though the filing does not disclose the identities or sizes of those other investors.
