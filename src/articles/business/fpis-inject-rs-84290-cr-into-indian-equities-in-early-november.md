Foreign portfolio investors placed Rs 84,290 crore into Indian equities in the first fortnight of November, marking a sharp reversal after two months of significant capital outflows that had persisted despite global market volatility. The infusion represents a substantial injection of foreign capital into the Indian equity market.

The turnaround was propelled by falling crude oil prices and attractive valuations in large‑cap stocks, which together reversed the recent trend of capital flight.

The benchmark Nifty index responded by surpassing the 25,000 level, with key sectors rallying as the infusion boosted investor sentiment and lifted the overall market.
