On Thursday, Indian stock markets opened strongly, with the Sensex climbing 1,610 points and the Nifty 50 rising 1.74%.

The rally was driven by crude oil falling below $100 and by renewed buying from foreign investors, which together lifted market sentiment.

The positive momentum came amid improving global sentiment following the November 2026 U.S. midterm elections, where the Democratic Party won control of both the Senate and the House, delivering a significant political setback to President Donald Trump's administration. The political shift in Washington added to the optimistic tone globally.
