Toronto, Ontario – On September 6, 2026, Alex Rajak, founder of Profundity Inc., announced the launch of a new service called the Operational Value Assessment. The offering is positioned as a diagnostic tool for private‑company owners who seek to sharpen their operational performance and increase the ease with which their businesses can be transferred, sold, or succeeded.

The Operational Value Assessment is designed to map a company’s core processes, financial health, and strategic assets against industry benchmarks. By quantifying operational efficiencies and identifying gaps, the assessment provides a roadmap for owners to enhance productivity, reduce risk, and build a more defensible market position. The methodology combines data analytics, on‑site reviews, and stakeholder interviews to produce a detailed report with actionable recommendations.

Business owners in Canada’s mid‑market segment often face challenges when preparing for succession or exit, including opaque value drivers and fragmented operational practices. A clear, transferable business model can command higher valuations and attract a broader pool of buyers. Profundity’s new service seeks to address these pain points by delivering a standardized, evidence‑based evaluation that can be used in negotiations, financing discussions, or internal strategic planning.

Profundity Inc. is a Toronto‑based advisory firm that has been serving companies across the Greater Toronto Area with operational improvement, cost‑optimization, and growth‑strategy projects. The firm’s experience with a diverse client base—from manufacturing outfits to technology service providers—has informed the design of the Operational Value Assessment, ensuring it reflects real‑world complexities while remaining scalable for firms of varying sizes.

According to the company’s announcement, the Operational Value Assessment will be available to clients immediately, with onboarding handled through Profundity’s dedicated advisory team. Interested owners can request an initial consultation via the firm’s website, where they will receive a preliminary scope of work and timeline for the assessment process.
