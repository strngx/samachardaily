The Employees’ Provident Fund Organisation announced a proposed Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP) that will let members who have retired withdraw their savings at regular intervals—monthly, quarterly or half‑yearly. Union Minister Mansukh Mandaviya presented the scheme as a “gift for workers”.

The plan targets former PF contributors who prefer a steady post‑retirement cash flow instead of a lump‑sum payout. EPFO said detailed eligibility criteria and the rollout timeline will be shared in upcoming communications.
