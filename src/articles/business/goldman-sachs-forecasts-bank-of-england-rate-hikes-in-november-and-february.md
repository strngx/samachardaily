Goldman Sachs predicts the Bank of England will raise interest rates by 25 basis points next week, followed by an additional quarter-point hike in February 2027. The financial institution attributes these projections to a combination of stronger economic data and increasing energy prices.

The Bank of England is scheduled to meet on November 5. According to LSEG data, money markets currently price in an approximately 92% probability that the central bank will proceed with a 25-basis-point increase during this upcoming session.
