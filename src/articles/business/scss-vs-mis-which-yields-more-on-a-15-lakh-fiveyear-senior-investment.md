The Economic Times ran a piece that pits the Senior Citizens Savings Scheme (SCSS) against the Post Office Monthly Income Scheme (MIS), two government‑backed small‑savings options available to senior citizens, each maturing after five years.

The article outlines how the schemes differ in interest rates, payout frequency and investment limits, and runs a side‑by‑side calculation of the income a ₹15 lakh investment would generate, helping seniors gauge which product may deliver higher regular returns.
