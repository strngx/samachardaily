Foreign portfolio investors have been net buyers in every trading session this month except Muhurat Trading, according to NSDL data cited by analysts. Cumulative purchases till November 20 marked a record monthly inflow.

Indian equity benchmarks rose nearly 1% in early trade on Monday. Nifty IT was the top sectoral gainer, up over 1%, while banking, financial services and auto indices also traded positive. Nifty Metal declined about 1%.

In the previous session, foreign institutional investors bought equities worth Rs 10,308 crore, while domestic institutional investors sold Rs 4,859 crore.
