Yes Group Management Berhad has formalised an underwriting agreement with Malacca Securities Sdn. Bhd. as it prepares to list on Malaysia's ACE Market, the growth enterprise segment of Bursa Malaysia.

## What the exchange records show

The underwriting agreement was disclosed through Bursa Malaysia's official company announcement system. Under the arrangement, Malacca Securities will act as the lead underwriter for Yes Group's forthcoming initial public offering on the ACE Market — the Bursa Malaysia segment designed for emerging companies with strong growth potential. The underwriting covers 75 million IPO shares, according to The Edge Malaysia's coverage of the deal.

## Background

Yes Group Management operates the Yes Travel brand, a corporate travel management company specialising in customised incentive travel solutions and comprehensive MICE (meetings, incentives, conferences, and exhibitions) event planning services. The company has built its client base in the Malaysian corporate sector, where managed travel programmes are a standard operational service.

## Why it matters

The underwriting agreement clears a key regulatory and commercial hurdle in the ACE Market listing process. With a named underwriter in place, Yes Group can proceed to finalise its prospectus and regulatory filings with the Securities Commission Malaysia. For investors, listing on the ACE Market raises the company's profile among institutional and retail participants in the regional capital market, while also providing the company with publicly-tracked capital to expand its incentive travel and MICE operations.
