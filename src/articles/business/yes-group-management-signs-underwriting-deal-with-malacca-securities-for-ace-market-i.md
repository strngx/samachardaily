Yes Group Management Berhad has formalised an underwriting agreement with Malacca Securities Sdn. Bhd. as it prepares to list on Malaysia’s ACE Market.

The company, best known for operating the Yes Travel brand, specialises in customised incentive travel solutions and comprehensive meetings, incentives, conferences and exhibitions (MICE) event planning services.

Malacca Securities will act as the lead underwriter, a role that will help Yes Group access public capital and meet regulatory requirements for its forthcoming IPO.
