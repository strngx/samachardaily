Indian equity benchmarks stayed shut as trading paused for the festival day. The session was closed, and the trading pause followed the holiday period.

The Sensex and Nifty moved in a narrow range before ending higher, according to the report. The close followed Sunday's special Muhurat trading session, which snapped the benchmarks' seven-session winning streak.

The recent winning run therefore ended with the special session's result, while the holiday closure kept the benchmarks closed. The benchmark move left the indexes higher before the pause, and the trading halt followed the festival.
