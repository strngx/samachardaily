The Nifty 50 closed at 25,299.35, up 44.30 points (0.18%), while the BSE Sensex finished at 81,911.01, gaining 31.61 points (0.04%). Both indices ended Thursday essentially flat as investors held back ahead of the October consumer price index due later in the day.

Buying pressure was observed in financial stocks, providing some support amid the broader market caution.

The pending CPI figure is expected to shape market sentiment and could influence future trading direction.
