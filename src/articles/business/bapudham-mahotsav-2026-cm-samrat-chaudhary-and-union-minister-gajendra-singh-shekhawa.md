The historic city of Motihari in Bihar's East Champaran district is preparing to host the Bapudham Mahotsav 2026, marking a significant developmental milestone for the region. As part of the grand celebrations, Bihar Chief Minister Samrat Chaudhary alongside Union Minister Gajendra Singh Shekhawat will jointly unveil and dedicate developmental projects valued at ₹285 crore.

Local administrative authorities in East Champaran have finalized extensive operational and security arrangements for the high-profile event. The Bapudham Mahotsav serves as a major annual platform celebrating the socio-cultural heritage of East Champaran—a district deeply tied to India's historical legacy—while simultaneously serving as a launching pad for new state and central public works initiatives.

The multi-crore infrastructure package scheduled for rollout includes targeted investments in municipal infrastructure, rural connectivity, public utilities, and civic amenities across Motihari and neighboring areas. Officials noted that the joint deployment of funds reflects a coordinated push by state and central ministries to upgrade public utility frameworks in North Bihar.

With final preparations under way in Motihari, the unveiling of the ₹285-crore project suite signals an intensified focus on regional capital expenditure. District administrators confirm that ground-level execution and foundation laying for the newly slated projects will proceed immediately following the official inaugurations at the festival.
