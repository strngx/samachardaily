Fast-growing property and casualty insurance provider Orion180 has officially priced its initial public offering (IPO) at $12.00 per share, offering 20 million shares of common stock to raise approximately $240 million in gross proceeds prior to underwriting expenses.

The pricing announcement, reported by financial news platform San Francisco Star, positions the Florida-headquartered insurtech firm for trading on the Nasdaq Global Market. Net proceeds from the offering are earmarked to support statutory capital requirements, fund geographical expansion across coastal and southern state markets, and enhance proprietary underwriting algorithms designed for climate-resilient homeowners coverage.

Founded to modernize coastal property insurance, Orion180 utilizes automated risk-scoring models that integrate satellite imagery, meteorological data, and property construction assessments to underwrite policies in coastal regions where traditional national carriers have curtailed exposure. The firm's business model combines direct-to-consumer digital distribution with independent agency networks across states including Florida, North Carolina, and South Carolina.

Insurance industry analysts view the successful public listing as an indicator of investor appetite for specialized property-casualty underwriters capable of maintaining profitable combined ratios amidst volatile hurricane and severe convective storm seasons. The transaction was managed by a consortium of major institutional investment banks.
