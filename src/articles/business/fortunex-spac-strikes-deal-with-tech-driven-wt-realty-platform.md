FortuneX Acquisition Corporation (NASDAQ: FXAC), a special purpose acquisition company, announced on Sept. 18, 2026 in New York that it has entered into a definitive business combination agreement with WT Realty Group Inc.

WT Realty Group is an integrated, technology‑enabled real estate platform offering brokerage, escrow, lending, mortgage, title, commercial real estate services and technology solutions for professionals and consumers.
