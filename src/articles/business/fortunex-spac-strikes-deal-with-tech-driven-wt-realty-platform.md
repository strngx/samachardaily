Special purpose acquisition company (SPAC) FortuneX Acquisition Corp. has finalized a definitive business combination agreement with WT Realty Platform, a technology-driven real estate brokerage and digital asset management firm, valuing the combined enterprise at approximately $280 million.

The transaction, announced via Globe Newswire, provides WT Realty Platform with public listing access to U.S. capital markets to fund the expansion of its proprietary digital transaction software and automated property evaluation tools. Under the terms of the merger, the combined operating entity will trade under a new ticker symbol on the Nasdaq Stock Market following shareholder and regulatory approval.

WT Realty Platform operates a cloud-based real estate platform that integrates automated property underwriting, digital transaction escrow, and algorithmic property valuation for residential and commercial real estate brokers. By replacing traditional, labor-intensive paperwork with workflow automation, the platform aims to reduce closing timelines and improve commission visibility for real estate professionals.

The merger occurs as SPAC market activity undergoes structural recalibration following heightened regulatory oversight from the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC). Market analysts note that successful blank-check combinations are increasingly concentrated on established technology firms with demonstrated revenue models and identifiable pathways to operational profitability.
