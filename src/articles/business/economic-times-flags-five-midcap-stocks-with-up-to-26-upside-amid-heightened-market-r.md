The Economic Times identifies five Indian mid‑cap stocks that could deliver upside of as much as 26%, but stresses that investors should prioritize a watchlist over a buy list in today’s risk‑laden market.

The outlet notes that the protective cushion that softened weak earnings a few months ago has vanished, leaving Q2 results exposed to heavier scrutiny. Consequently, the probability of a steep decline following any disappointment is higher than in recent months.

Market participants are therefore urged to scrutinise earnings forecasts closely and be prepared for heightened volatility when assessing these mid‑cap opportunities.
