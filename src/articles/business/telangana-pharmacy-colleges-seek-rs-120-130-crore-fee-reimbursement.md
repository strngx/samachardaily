Pharmacy colleges in Telangana have urged the state government to settle their outstanding fee reimbursement payments, with final-year students' dues placed at the front of the queue.

The institutions put the total arrears at Rs 120-130 crore, an amount they say covers roughly 70,000 students enrolled in pharmacy courses across the state.

The pending reimbursements form the core of the colleges' request, and the prioritisation of final-year dues indicates where the institutions want the release to begin.
