Hindustan Aeronautics Limited (HAL) has announced its audited financial results for the second quarter and half-year ended September 30, 2026. The defence manufacturer reported a consolidated net profit of Rs 1,935.64 crore for the second quarter of fiscal 2027.

The company's share price is in focus following the disclosure of these figures. Audited results indicate verified financial performance rather than preliminary estimates. The period covered ends in September, marking the middle of the fiscal year. Investors are monitoring the stock after the announcement. This financial update was reported by the India Tv outlet.
