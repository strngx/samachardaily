Brokerages have issued fresh calls on several major Indian stocks ahead of October 7 trading, updating their ratings and share price targets. Analysts are reassessing fundamentals and market positioning. These revisions reflect ongoing market assessment of each company's performance and outlook.

The stocks under review include Titan, Reliance Industries, Airtel, IEX, Dabur, and RR Kabel. Each has seen its ratings adjusted and target prices modified by brokerage analysts seeking to provide more precise investment guidance for investors. The updates aim to offer clearer directional signals to traders and portfolio managers considering these equities.
