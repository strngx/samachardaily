The Chinese yuan climbed to a four‑year high against the US dollar, reaching its strongest level in four years and marking a significant appreciation for the currency.

This move was underpinned by the People’s Bank of China’s firm guidance and by the Federal Reserve’s decision to keep interest rates unchanged, as the central bank continued to manage the currency’s value and maintain stability.

Market observers are now awaiting the European Central Bank’s upcoming policy decision, which is expected to influence the future direction of the currency market and shape investor sentiment.
