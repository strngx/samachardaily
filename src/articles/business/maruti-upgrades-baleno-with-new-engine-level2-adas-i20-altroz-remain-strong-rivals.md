Maruti Suzuki has refreshed its Baleno premium hatchback, adding a new engine, extra features and a Level‑2 advanced driver‑assistance system (ADAS).

The updated Baleno is priced at roughly Rs 6.10 lakh, placing it squarely against Hyundai’s i20 and Tata Motors’ Altroz in the closely fought premium hatch segment.

Although the Baleno now enjoys a technology edge, the i20 and Altroz continue to be recognised for their own strengths within the same market space.
