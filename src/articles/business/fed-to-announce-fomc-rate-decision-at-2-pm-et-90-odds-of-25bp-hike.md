The United States Federal Reserve is scheduled to release its Federal Open Market Committee (FOMC) interest rate decision today at 2:00 PM Eastern Time, with financial markets anticipating another adjustment to monetary policy. Money market pricing tracked by CME Group's FedWatch tool reflects greater than 90% odds of a 25-basis-point (quarter-percentage point) rate increase.

Following the policy announcement, Fed Chair Kevin Warsh is scheduled to conduct a press conference at 2:30 PM ET to explain the central bank's economic assessment and policy rationale.

A 25-basis-point rate hike would further increase borrowing costs across the United States economy, directly influencing commercial lending rates, consumer mortgages, and corporate financing. Market participants, institutional investors, and economists are monitoring the committee's forward guidance for signals regarding future policy meetings, particularly as policymakers weigh prevailing price pressures, inflation expectations, and national employment data.
