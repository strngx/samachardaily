Domino’s has launched a major marketing offensive targeting some of the fast-food industry's most recognizable icons, including McDonald's Big Mac and Burger King's Whopper. The aggressive promotional push marks a direct effort by the pizza chain to position its menu against established flagship products across the quick-service restaurant sector.

The creative execution was developed in close collaboration with Domino’s agency of record, Work In Progress. Rather than relying on traditional food industry advertising tropes, the team opted for a distinct creative direction characterized by extreme hyperbole and dark humor designed to cut through saturated media channels.

Domino’s Chief Marketing Officer Kate Trumbull explained that the decision to adopt a satirical, darkly comedic tone was driven by a desire to disrupt standard fast-food messaging. By directly referencing iconic competitor items like the Big Mac and Whopper, the campaign seeks to reframe how consumers evaluate fast-food choices during key meal times.

Direct brand-on-brand competition has long been a staple of quick-service marketing, but Domino’s latest initiative relies on exaggerated narrative framing to make its point. The partnership with Work In Progress underscores a calculated move to leverage unconventional creative storytelling to drive brand engagement.

The campaign represents a high-profile strategic positioning for Domino's as major restaurant chains continuously vie for market share. By leaning into hyperbolic storytelling, the company aims to establish a memorable narrative contrast between its offerings and traditional burger staples.
