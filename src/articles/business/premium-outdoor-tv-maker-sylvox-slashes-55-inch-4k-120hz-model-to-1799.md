Specialized display manufacturer Sylvox has announced a $200 price reduction on its 55-inch 4K outdoor television, bringing the updated retail cost down to $1,799. The promotional discount specifically targets homeowners and commercial property operators seeking to install high-performance television hardware in semi-enclosed exterior spaces, such as covered patios, outdoor verandas, and sheltered lounge areas where traditional indoor display units cannot safely operate due to environmental risks.

A central selling point of this 55-inch model is its integration of dedicated gaming specifications, which are historically rare in weatherproof exterior displays. The television features a fast 120Hz refresh rate alongside Auto Low Latency Mode (ALLM). Together, these features work to eliminate image tearing, minimize input lag, and ensure fluid motion rendering, making the unit optimized for next-generation video game consoles and fast-action sports viewing in outdoor settings.

To maintain high visual fidelity in bright ambient conditions, the display combines 4K Ultra HD screen resolution with Dolby Vision high dynamic range technology. This advanced image processing delivers high contrast ratios, deep black levels, and enhanced color accuracy. These visual capabilities help ensure that video content remains crisp and legible even when exposed to fluctuating light levels typical of shaded patio structures during daytime hours.

Physical protection is central to the design, with the television housing engineered to achieve an IP56 weatherproofing rating. This industrial standard certifies that the cabinet and internal electronics are shielded against heavy dust exposure and high-pressure water streams from any angle. Consequently, the screen is protected against rain, windborne debris, and atmospheric moisture commonly encountered in covered outdoor residential environments.

Positioned at $1,799 following the discount, the 55-inch model addresses a growing market demand for feature-rich exterior entertainment solutions. By combining structural weather resistance with high-end display technologies—including 120Hz refresh rates and Dolby Vision—Sylvox provides a dedicated outdoor solution that competes directly with traditional indoor home theater setups adapted for covered patio installations.
