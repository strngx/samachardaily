Next week, five companies will bring their shares to Dalal Street between October 26 and October 29, aiming to raise roughly Rs 2,600 crore through their initial public offerings. The list includes EAAA India Alternatives, SFC Environmental Technologies, M K Sons Fine Jewels, BLS Polymers, and Bombay Coated and Special Steels. Each firm plans to allocate the proceeds to business expansion, boost working capital, and settle existing debts.

The wave of listings reflects continued investor appetite for fresh equity in India’s primary market. Meanwhile, additional companies are preparing for IPO launches slated for November 5, signaling further activity ahead. Analysts view the upcoming offerings as a gauge of market confidence heading into the year‑end.
