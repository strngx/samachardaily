Sheikh Khaled bin Mohamed bin Zayed, Crown Prince of Abu Dhabi, arrived in Athens on an official visit at the invitation of Greek Prime Minister Kyriakos Mitsotakis. The trip commemorates 50 years of diplomatic relations between the United Arab Emirates and Greece.

The visit underscores the strategic partnership between the two nations, which has deepened across trade, energy, and investment sectors in recent years. Both countries have been strengthening economic cooperation, with Greece serving as a key gateway for UAE investments into Europe.

During his stay, the Crown Prince is expected to hold bilateral meetings with senior Greek officials, discussing avenues to expand collaboration in areas including renewable energy, infrastructure, and financial services.
