The Bangalore‑based EV maker Ather launched its next‑generation Konarc scooter in a streamed event, positioning it as the company’s newest offering in the premium two‑wheeler segment. The rollout follows the success of the Ather 450X and reflects the firm’s ambition to broaden its portfolio amid accelerating demand for electric mobility in India.

Carandbike’s live feed is delivering incremental updates – from battery capacity and motor output to price brackets and high‑resolution images – as Ather’s spokesperson releases each data point. While full specifications remain pending, the outlet notes that the Konarc is expected to feature a larger battery pack and enhanced connectivity compared with earlier models, aligning with market trends toward longer range and smart features.

The launch arrives at a pivotal moment for India’s electric two‑wheel market, which has seen a surge in consumer interest and manufacturer entries. Ather’s entry with the Konarc could intensify competition among domestic players such as Ola Electric and TVS, potentially driving price adjustments and faster technology roll‑outs across the sector.
