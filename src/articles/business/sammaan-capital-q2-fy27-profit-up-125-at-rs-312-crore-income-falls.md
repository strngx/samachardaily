Non-bank lender Sammaan Capital on Saturday posted a 1.25% rise in September-quarter net profit to Rs 312.34 crore, from Rs 308.47 crore a year earlier and Rs 243.30 crore in the June quarter, even as total income fell to Rs 1,850.85 crore from Rs 2,260.52 crore.

Core interest income dropped to Rs 1,161.26 crore from Rs 1,449.02 crore, while total expenses declined to Rs 1,426.28 crore from Rs 1,835.49 crore, according to the regulatory filing.

Assets under management rose 5% to Rs 59,188 crore as of end-June 2026, with disbursements jumping 62% to Rs 6,278 crore. It targets AUM of Rs 70,000 crore by March 2027 and plans to grow its branch network from 245 to 270 by fiscal year-end.
