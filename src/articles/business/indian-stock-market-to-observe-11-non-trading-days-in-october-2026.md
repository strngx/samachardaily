The month includes 11 non-trading days, with trading suspending on Friday, October 2, for Mahatma Gandhi Jayanti, alongside Dussehra on Tuesday, October 20, according to the supplied holiday schedule.

The remaining closures fall on weekends. Saturdays and Sundays are marked off for October 3-4, 10-11, 17-18, 24-25, and October 31. Investors planning transactions must account for these dates, as market activity remains suspended on all listed dates.

Trading resumes based on the regular market schedule following each respective holiday closure, so investors should verify the next session date.
