India’s stock market has staged a sharp turnaround, becoming the best-performing equity market in the world over the past month after a period of weakness, according to The Economic Times. The performance marks a reversal from the market’s earlier weak position in global rankings.

The recovery has been driven largely by a fresh wave of foreign investment, which has improved liquidity and boosted confidence among investors. That inflow has been a major driver of the rebound.

Domestic institutions have also provided support, while a clearer outlook on corporate earnings has helped sustain the market’s upward momentum.
