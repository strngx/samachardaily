Google unveiled Gemini 4 Argon on Wednesday, September 30. The flagship model is billed as the company's most advanced system to date, specifically designed for coding, cybersecurity defense, and complex professional work.

Access was initially limited to vetted cybersecurity partners and Google's internal teams. This controlled distribution suggests a measured approach to deploying the powerful model across enterprise environments and ensuring robust security standards.

The model remains available exclusively through paid plans, aligning with Google's strategy to monetize cutting-edge AI infrastructure for businesses and specialized security operations requiring advanced computational resources.
