OpenAI announced on Monday it will not release its latest AI system, GPT‑6.1 Astra, after internal testing flagged safety risks. The company said the decision reflects its commitment to responsible AI development.

The cancellation follows a broader industry trend of slowing frontier‑model rollouts as regulators and experts debate the technology’s potential harms. Other leading AI firms have similarly delayed advanced model releases, citing safety apprehensions.

OpenAI did not detail the specific concerns, but the move underscores a growing preference for safety reviews before public deployment. The statement arrived amid ongoing debate over AI’s potential societal impacts.
