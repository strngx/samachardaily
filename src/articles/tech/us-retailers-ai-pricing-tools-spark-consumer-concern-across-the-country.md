U.S. retailers and restaurant chains are rolling out AI-assisted pricing systems that automatically adjust prices based on predicted demand, prompting unease among shoppers. The technology aims to optimize sales by responding to real-time market signals.

The shift is evident in grocery stores where digital price tags are replacing traditional paper labels, allowing real-time price changes driven by algorithms. These tags can update prices instantly without manual intervention.

Consumer groups say the technology reduces price transparency and raises concerns about fairness, while businesses argue it improves inventory management and promotional efficiency. The debate highlights growing tension between convenience and visibility in modern retail.
