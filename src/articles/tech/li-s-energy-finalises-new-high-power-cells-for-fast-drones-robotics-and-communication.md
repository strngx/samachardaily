LI-S Energy has finalised a new high-power cell technology built on lithium-sulfur chemistry that delivers up to 50 times the power of traditional battery systems. The breakthrough opens new market opportunities across multi-rotor drones, soldier-carried systems, robotics and communications equipment.

The company's new Power Cell is positioned as a next-generation energy solution designed to support fast-moving unmanned systems and field-deployable robotics requiring sustained high performance.

Industry analysts note that the development signals growing commercial viability for lithium-sulfar chemistries in applications where conventional batteries fall short in energy density and discharge rates.
