Samsung has started the official rollout of One UI 9.0, its latest Android interface, to the newly released Galaxy S26, S26+ and S26 Ultra smartphones.

The same update wave also covers earlier flagships, with Samsung confirming that the Galaxy S25 and the Galaxy Z7 series will receive One UI 9 as part of the rollout.

Delivered as a free upgrade, One UI 9 brings 11 new features to the supported devices, broadening the Galaxy experience across the lineup.
