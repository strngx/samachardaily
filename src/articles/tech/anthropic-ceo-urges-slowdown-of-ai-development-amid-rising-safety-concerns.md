Dario Amodei, co-founder and chief executive of artificial intelligence laboratory Anthropic, has publicly urged technology companies and researchers to moderate the rapid deployment pace of frontier AI systems, warning that rapid scaling without corresponding safety evaluations increases the risk of catastrophic failures.

Speaking on the trajectory of advanced foundation models, Amodei emphasized that developers must establish concrete safety benchmarks and rigorous empirical red-teaming before releasing next-generation models to the public. Anthropic, which develops the Claude series of language models, operates under a formal Responsible Scaling Policy (RSP) that defines specific capability thresholds requiring enhanced physical cybersecurity, automated threat monitoring, and internal governance reviews.

Amodei noted that while competition among leading AI laboratories has accelerated model capabilities in coding, reasoning, and multimodal processing, the development of interpretability tools and alignment safeguards has lagged behind raw computational scaling. He advocated for standardized third-party audits and coordinated safety testing frameworks across the industry to evaluate biological, cyber-offense, and autonomous risk factors prior to deployment.

The appeal comes amid broader discussions in Washington and Brussels regarding legislative oversight for frontier model developers, with policymakers evaluating mandatory safety reporting standards and compute thresholds for high-risk artificial intelligence systems.
