Nvidia and its chief executive Jensen Huang are the subject of a proposed shareholder class‑action lawsuit that alleges the company misled investors about the involvement of a suspected Chinese intelligence operative.

The filing claims Huang concealed potential espionage risks, prompting shareholders to seek damages for alleged breaches of disclosure duties. The controversy has drawn widespread online attention and could pressure Nvidia’s governance and market reputation.

The suit, filed in a U.S. federal court, remains untested, and Nvidia has not commented publicly on the allegations.
