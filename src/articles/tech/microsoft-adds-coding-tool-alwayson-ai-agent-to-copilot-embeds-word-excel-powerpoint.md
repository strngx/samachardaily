On Friday, Microsoft announced a suite of upgrades to its Copilot app, introducing a dedicated coding tool and an always‑on AI agent while integrating Word, Excel and PowerPoint directly inside the AI interface.

The enhancements aim to turn Copilot into a one‑stop shop for office workers, expanding its role beyond chat and seeking higher adoption as the company competes with AI chatbots such as ChatGPT and Claude; the new capabilities are limited to paid‑plan subscribers.
