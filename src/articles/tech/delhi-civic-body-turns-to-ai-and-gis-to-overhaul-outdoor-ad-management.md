The Municipal Corporation of Delhi is weaving artificial intelligence and geographic information system (GIS) mapping into the way it handles outdoor advertising. The technology will be trialled through a new 180-day pilot project, the civic body said.

The initiative is designed to improve revenue generation, prevent losses, and ensure better management of advertising assets, according to the corporation. Pairing AI with GIS mapping is intended to give officials a clearer, data-driven view of outdoor advertising assets and the revenue they generate.
