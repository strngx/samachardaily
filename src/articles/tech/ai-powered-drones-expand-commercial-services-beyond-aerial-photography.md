A report dated October 22 from New York says artificial intelligence is expanding Drones-as-a-Service beyond aerial photography and basic data collection. AI-powered drones can analyze images in real time and identify potential problems.

The technology relies on intelligent automation, autonomous flight and real-time data analytics. These capabilities are part of a billion-dollar AI drone revolution reshaping the global industry.

New commercial opportunities are emerging across agriculture, energy, infrastructure and defense.
