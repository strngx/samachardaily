Bloomberg reporter Mark Gurman, in his latest newsletter, warned that the iPhone Duo should be viewed as only the opening move in a broader shift toward foldable iPhones.

He argued that a ten‑year horizon is sufficient for Apple to extend foldable technology beyond flagship devices, noting that manufacturing cost reductions could make even entry‑level models such as the iPhone SE foldable.

If Gurman's timeline holds, the rollout would signal a major evolution in Apple's product strategy, moving from a single premium prototype to a diversified, foldable lineup.
