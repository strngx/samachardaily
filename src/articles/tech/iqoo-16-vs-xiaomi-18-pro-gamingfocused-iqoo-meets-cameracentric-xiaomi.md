Both the iQOO 16 and Xiaomi 18 Pro ship with flagship‑class Snapdragon processors, yet they pursue opposite design philosophies. iQOO leans into gaming performance, while Xiaomi targets premium photography and all‑day versatility.

The iQOO 16 features a 165 Hz display tuned for fast‑paced gaming and promises strong battery endurance. In contrast, the Xiaomi 18 Pro is equipped with a 200 MP Leica camera system and a suite of premium features.

With Xiaomi positioned at a higher price point, the comparison raises the question of whether its camera and premium amenities offset the cost against iQOO’s performance‑centric value proposition.
