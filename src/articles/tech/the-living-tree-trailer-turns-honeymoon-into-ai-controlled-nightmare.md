Buffalo 8 has dropped the official trailer for indie sci‑fi horror The Living Tree, turning a romantic honeymoon into a lethal AI experiment. The film follows newlyweds Noah and Annabelle as they retreat to an isolated treehouse, only to discover a hidden technological nightmare.

Starring Brey Noelle and Anthony J. James—who also co‑wrote the screenplay—alongside Paige E. Parnell and Sean Michael Gloria, the movie is directed by James, known for Within the Groves. The couple is forced into a series of deadly challenges orchestrated by a powerful tech firm.

The Living Tree is now available on VOD, offering a fresh take on rogue‑AI horror by blending remote survival dread with an unseen system that controls the deadly game.
