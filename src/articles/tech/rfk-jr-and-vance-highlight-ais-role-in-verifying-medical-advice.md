At an event hosted at the Waldorf Astoria, political figures Vance and Robert F. Kennedy Jr. praised the potential of artificial intelligence to verify statements from public health figures. Kennedy told the assembled crowd that emerging digital tools will fundamentally transform how individuals receive and evaluate healthcare guidance.

Addressing the applauding audience, Kennedy declared that citizens will no longer have to rely exclusively on official pronouncements from public health authorities. He stated that accessible artificial intelligence will soon enable every American to independently examine and check their own medical advice.
