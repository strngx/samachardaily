Apple is preparing to turn Siri into an AI agent that can understand what users want and handle tasks without requiring constant direction. The new system will interpret requests and act on its own, providing a more autonomous experience.

This change is intended to boost Apple's standing in the fast‑growing artificial intelligence market. By developing an AI agent, the company aims to compete directly with services such as ChatGPT and Meta.
