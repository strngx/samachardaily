OpenAI said on Thursday that its latest system, GPT-6 Astra, has overtaken competitor Anthropic and is now the "world’s most intelligent" model. The claim was made in a formal statement released by the company and was subsequently reported by the Financial Times.

Anthropic, a San Francisco‑based AI startup founded by former OpenAI researchers, has been one of the few firms able to challenge OpenAI’s dominance in large‑language‑model development. By positioning Astra as a step ahead of Anthropic’s offerings, OpenAI is signalling a renewed competitive edge in a market where performance benchmarks and real‑world utility are closely watched by enterprise buyers and developers alike.

According to the Financial Times, OpenAI described GPT-6 Astra as market‑leading across three critical domains: software engineering, scientific research, and cybersecurity. In software engineering, the model is said to generate, debug, and optimise code with higher accuracy than previous generations. In scientific contexts, Astra purportedly assists researchers in data analysis, hypothesis generation and literature synthesis. For cybersecurity, the model is claimed to identify vulnerabilities and suggest mitigations faster than existing tools.

If the assertions hold up, Astra could accelerate adoption of AI‑assisted workflows across tech firms, research institutions and security operations centres. Companies that rely on rapid code development, complex data modelling, or threat detection may see cost reductions and speed gains, potentially reshaping procurement strategies that have so far favoured a handful of established AI providers.

OpenAI’s announcement arrives without independent benchmarking at this stage, and analysts note that the company’s self‑assessment will likely be tested by third‑party evaluations in the coming weeks. Nonetheless, the public positioning of Astra as a market‑leading solution underscores OpenAI’s intent to maintain its leadership role as the sector moves toward more specialised, high‑stakes applications.
