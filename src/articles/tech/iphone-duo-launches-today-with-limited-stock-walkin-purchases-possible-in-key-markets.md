Apple’s first foldable iPhone Duo officially launched on Friday, October 23, with limited walk‑in inventory available in the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, Mexico and several European nations, while customers in France, China, India and other regions must secure a reservation.

The device starts at $1,999 for 256 GB in Star White or Night Sky, with higher tiers up to $3,199 for 2 TB, and Apple limits purchases to two per customer in the U.S.; early stock at Edinburgh suggested stronger supply than expected, but availability varies by location, and online reservations can be refreshed Saturday, October 24.
