FEVM has teased its upcoming FNPH1 ultra‑slim mini PC ahead of launch. The device occupies just 0.4 liters yet houses a 16‑core Intel CPU, positioning it among the most compact high‑performance machines.

Equipped with up to 64 GB of LPDDR5X RAM, the FNPH1 packs a lot of punch despite its diminutive size, offering desktop‑level performance in a space‑saving form factor.

The specifications are currently visible only to FEVM’s paid‑plan subscribers, with a wider release expected later.
