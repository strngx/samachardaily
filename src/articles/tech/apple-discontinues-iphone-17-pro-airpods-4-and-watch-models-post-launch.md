Apple has officially discontinued six older hardware devices following the debut of the flagship iPhone 18 Pro series, restructuring its product catalog for late 2026 buyers. The sunsetted products include the flagship iPhone 17 Pro, select Apple Watch models, and the fourth-generation AirPods 4, clearing shelf space for newer silicon and updated hardware standards.

The decision to drop the previous-generation flagship, the iPhone 17 Pro, aligns with Apple's established retail strategy of phasing out high-end predecessors to prevent internal competition with newly introduced Pro models. While standard base models often remain in the catalog at reduced prices, removing the iPhone 17 Pro encourages prospective premium buyers to transition directly to the latest iPhone 18 Pro hardware.

Alongside the previous flagship smartphone, Apple is retiring older wearable and audio hardware. The phasing out of specified Apple Watch units and the AirPods 4 marks a broader hardware refresh, shifting consumer focus toward updated health sensors, improved battery efficiency, and newer audio processing chips integrated into current-generation accessories.

These catalog modifications directly reshape purchasing options across global retail channels and authorized resellers. Existing stocks of the discontinued items will typically remain available through third-party vendors and clearance promotions until inventories are fully depleted, though official Apple Store locations and online platforms have already updated their storefronts.

By eliminating these six devices immediately after the iPhone 18 Pro reveal, Apple reinforces a streamlined hardware hierarchy. The portfolio adjustment ensures clear differentiation between entry-level offerings and top-tier devices while optimizing supply chain operations for the upcoming holiday shopping period in late 2026.
