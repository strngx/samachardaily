Apple SouthGate store in Bath, England has reopened following more than three months of renovations, arriving just in time for the iPhone Duo launch day. The venue received a refreshed design that includes a dedicated Apple Pickup area and a more accessible Genius Bar featuring varied table and seat heights.

Additional upgrades were made throughout the space as part of the comprehensive refresh. The store is now ready to welcome customers during the highly anticipated iPhone Duo debut.
