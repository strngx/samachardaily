Microsoft’s $175 billion capital expenditure, aimed at AI computing power, is reported to be hurting its business. The outlay fuels its internal Copilot service and supports OpenAI’s ChatGPT.

The firm remains one of the three largest cloud infrastructure providers and is consistently listed among AI hyperscalers, reflecting its aggressive push for massive AI capacity.

Analysts warn that the scale of the spend could pressure profitability even as Microsoft expands its AI product portfolio.
