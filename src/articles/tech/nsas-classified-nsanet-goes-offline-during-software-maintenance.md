NSANet, the US National Security Agency's system for securely exchanging classified information, experienced an outage while the agency was installing a software patch, CNN reported on November 17, citing six people familiar with the matter. A US official described a temporary degradation during routine maintenance, and a person familiar told The Wall Street Journal the network was expected to stay offline only temporarily.

The incident led the NSA to introduce staggered arrival times for non-emergency personnel on November 17 as officials investigated the problem. The agency said mission-critical capabilities remained fully operational and its ability to carry out national security responsibilities was not compromised. A person familiar with the incident told the Journal there was no evidence of a cyberattack.
