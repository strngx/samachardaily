Warehouse automation firm Tutor Intelligence Inc. announced today that it has closed a $75 million Series A financing round, marking a major capital raise for the robotics startup.

The round was led by venture capital firm Andreessen Horowitz, with participation from Union Square Ventures and Neo, the latter having led Tutor’s two preceding funding rounds.

The new capital is slated to accelerate Tutor’s development of robotic systems that handle palletized goods, aiming to expand its automation offerings across more warehouse operations.
