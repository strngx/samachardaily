More than 700 government officials, enterprise technology leaders, and academic researchers from China and the Association of Southeast Asian Nations (ASEAN) convened in Nanning, Guangxi, for the fourth China-ASEAN Artificial Intelligence Forum, according to coverage by Forever News.

The summit focused on deepening cross-border digital economy partnerships, regional computing infrastructure integration, and coordinated artificial intelligence governance frameworks. Bilateral discussions centered on deploying localized multilingual large language models (LLMs) to facilitate cross-border trade, automated customs logistics, and smart agricultural monitoring across member states.

Delegates highlighted the operational significance of the China-ASEAN Information Harbor, a joint initiative aimed at building terrestrial and subsea fiber-optic connectivity between southern China and Southeast Asian economic corridors. Officials underscored the need for standardized data-sharing protocols and ethical AI guidelines that account for the diverse legal systems and digital maturity levels of participating nations.

The forum concluded with the signing of multiple commercial memoranda of understanding between Chinese cloud providers and ASEAN telecommunications operators. Analysts observe that regional technical cooperation reflects ASEAN's broader strategy to expand its digital trade footprint while maintaining digital sovereignty through diversified technological infrastructure.
