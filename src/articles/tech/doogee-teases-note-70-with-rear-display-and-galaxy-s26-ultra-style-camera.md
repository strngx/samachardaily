Doogee has teased its upcoming Note 70 smartphone, revealing a 1.8-inch rear display and a camera design similar to the Galaxy S26 Ultra. The handset will run Android 17 and carry a 6,500mAh battery.

The device is powered by the Unisoc T7250 processor and supports up to 6GB of RAM and 256GB of storage. Notebookcheck published the teaser details based on Doogee's announcement.

The teaser did not include a launch date or pricing for the Note 70. The rear panel carries the 1.8-inch display and camera array.
