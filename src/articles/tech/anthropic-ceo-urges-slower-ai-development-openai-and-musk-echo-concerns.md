Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei released an essay urging the artificial‑intelligence industry to decelerate the pace at which it improves model capabilities.

Within hours of the essay’s publication, OpenAI chief Sam Altman and entrepreneur Elon Musk issued public statements aligning with Amodei’s view.

Amodei clarified he is not demanding a halt or a training moratorium; instead, he calls for any slower pace to be used to strengthen safeguards, advance alignment work, and enable independent oversight.
