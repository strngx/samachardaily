On Oct. 7, 2026, Hanwha was recognized on Fast Company's 2026 Next Big Things in Tech list for its EnergyFluo™ agentic AI energy management system. The platform orchestrates power from diverse sources including grid electricity, battery storage, and on-site generation.

Designed for data centers requiring flexible power distribution, EnergyFluo™ enables autonomous optimization of facility loads. By balancing supply and demand across multiple inputs, the system helps facilities adapt dynamically to fluctuating energy conditions while maintaining efficiency.
