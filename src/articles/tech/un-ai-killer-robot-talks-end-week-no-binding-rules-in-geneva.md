A week-long UN conference on weapons closed Saturday in Geneva. It had looked at AI-powered killer robots and armed drones.

Scores of countries later agreed to elaborate a possible accord on lethal autonomous weapons systems by 2028. They did not commit to the binding rules sought by UN Secretary-General Antonio Guterres, many countries, and activists. The outcome was softened. The talks had sought firm limits.

The Geneva talks ran aground as efforts to set firm rules stalled. The two-year extension set a later timeline for talks. Negotiators agreed to keep talking.
