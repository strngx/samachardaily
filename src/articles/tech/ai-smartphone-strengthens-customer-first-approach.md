Srinagar: Ai+ Smartphone announced a strengthened customer-first approach today, with real-world user feedback now driving software enhancements, product testing, and improvements in after-sales support across its smartphone portfolio.

The initiative specifically targets common consumer concerns including camera stability, connectivity reliability, and overall device performance, aiming to translate direct customer experiences into meaningful product improvements.

Notably, the enhanced support features are currently limited to paid plans, reflecting the brand's focus on delivering tangible value to paying customers.
