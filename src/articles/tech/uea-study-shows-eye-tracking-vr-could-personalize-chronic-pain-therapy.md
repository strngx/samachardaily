A world‑first study from the University of East Anglia shows that combining AI with eye‑tracking in VR can adapt pain‑relief therapy to individual patients. The system responds to a user’s gaze patterns, offering more effective, personalized treatment for chronic pain sufferers.

The findings suggest millions with persistent pain could benefit from therapies that adjust in real time to where they look, moving away from a one‑size‑fits‑all approach. By interpreting eye movements, clinicians may fine‑tune interventions to each patient’s specific needs.

While still early, the work marks a key step toward using advanced sensing with VR for medical use. No confirmed next steps reported yet.
