E-commerce platforms Amazon India and Flipkart have launched their 2026 festive shopping promotions, offering wide-ranging discounts on consumer electronics. The Amazon Great Indian Festival 2026 features mega deals on smartphones, accessories, and home tech.

To drive purchases, platforms have partnered with ICICI Bank, State Bank of India, and Axis Bank to provide dedicated credit card offers. Major mobile brands slated for price cuts across Flipkart and Amazon include Apple's iPhone series, Samsung, OnePlus, and Realme devices.

Additional product categories are also seeing promotions, with companies such as Dreame announcing deals on vacuum cleaners and lifestyle goods. Amazon India reported early customer interest leaning toward AI-driven tech and premium hardware upgrades ahead of the sale.
