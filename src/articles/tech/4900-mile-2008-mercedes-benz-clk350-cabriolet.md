Equipped with a 3.5‑liter V6 mated to a seven‑speed automatic, the car features a power‑operated blue soft top, 17‑inch five‑spoke alloy wheels, heated power‑adjustable front seats, navigation, and dual‑zone automatic climate control. The exterior is finished in Capri Blue Metallic over Stone Nappa leather, a color combination that is rare among surviving CLK350 cabriolets.

The listing includes a clean Carfax report confirming no accidents or major damage. Bring A Trailer’s platform, known for attracting serious collectors, will host the auction, allowing bidders to assess the car’s condition through high‑resolution photos and a detailed description.
