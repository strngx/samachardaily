The European Union asserts that its AI Act can effectively counter rogue AI agents, according to tech chief Henna Virkkunen, who said Europe is well‑equipped to handle safety risks from advanced AI models.

Virkkunen highlighted the comprehensive regulatory scope of the AI Act, noting recent incidents at major tech firms as evidence of its necessity. The recent incidents referenced involve leading technology companies.

She added that the Act’s broad framework aims to manage safety risks and ensure responsible AI deployment across the European bloc. This approach reflects the EU’s commitment to proactive AI governance.
