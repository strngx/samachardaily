OpenAI, Anthropic and the Simons Foundation have announced a $15 million collaborative initiative aimed at integrating artificial intelligence into mathematical research. The program will provide funding and resources to support mathematicians seeking to employ AI tools in their work.

The partnership’s core objectives are to improve how AI‑generated mathematical contributions are evaluated and to establish uniform standards for verifying such results. By creating consistent assessment frameworks, the project seeks to ensure that AI‑assisted proofs meet rigorous scholarly criteria.
