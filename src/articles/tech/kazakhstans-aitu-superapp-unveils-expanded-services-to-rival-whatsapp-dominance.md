At the AI & Digital Bridge 2026 forum, Kazakhstan's national messenger Aitu received a major update that transforms the platform into a multi‑service hub. The new features enable users to obtain government documents, transfer money, and sign paperwork digitally, positioning the app as a Swiss‑Army‑knife for everyday life.

President Kassym‑Jomart Tokayev inspected the enhancements and linked them to broader plans for the Data Center Valley. While WhatsApp remains dominant with over 80% of the population—roughly 13 million users—Aitu has already attracted more than one million monthly active users by focusing on practical digital services rather than sheer messaging volume.

Developers are prioritizing integration of real‑world utilities into the platform, aiming to reduce reliance on foreign messaging apps and provide a locally owned alternative for Kazakh citizens.
