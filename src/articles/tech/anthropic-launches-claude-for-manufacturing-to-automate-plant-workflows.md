Anthropic's Claude for Manufacturing made its debut on November 12, the company said. The AI software product is designed to handle routine administrative and engineering work for manufacturers.

It combines 17 manufacturing-specific skills with four software connectors. Those connectors allow Claude to work with information stored in the systems manufacturers already use across their operations.

Tasks the offering is built to perform include preparing quotations, investigating quality problems, diagnosing equipment failures and managing supplier relationships, according to the company.
