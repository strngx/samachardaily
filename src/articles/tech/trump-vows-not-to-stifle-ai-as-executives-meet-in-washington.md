President Donald Trump has pledged that his administration will not restrict the growth of artificial intelligence, asserting that the technology should be allowed to evolve without government interference.

In a parallel move, senior executives from AI firms that have previously called for delays in development are convening in Washington for a lunch meeting with the president. Their call for delays reflects concerns about the potential risks of rapid AI development.

The gathering underscores the ongoing debate between fostering innovation and addressing safety concerns, as industry leaders seek to influence policy while Trump promotes an unregulated approach.
