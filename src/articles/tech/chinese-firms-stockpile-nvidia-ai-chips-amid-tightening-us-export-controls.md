Chinese companies are aggressively stockpiling Nvidia artificial intelligence chips and securing computing racks in overseas locations. This surge in procurement follows efforts by Washington to close existing loopholes in export controls and coordinate with allies to protect sensitive technologies.

Concerns within China suggest that a personal affair involving Nvidia founder Jensen Huang may prompt the US government to intensify crackdowns on chip smuggling. There are further fears that Washington may introduce new legislation to regulate the flow of these critical components.
