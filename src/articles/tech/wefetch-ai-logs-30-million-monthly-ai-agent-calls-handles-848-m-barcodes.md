WeFetch AI Corp. announced that its gtinfetch.ai platform logged 30 million AI agent interactions in the past month, marking a new usage milestone.

The service registers 12 AI agent calls each second, about 700 per minute and one million per day, processing data on 84.8 million barcodes, and is offered only through paid plans.

The press release was issued from the company’s Vancouver, BC and London offices on Nov. 26, 2026.
