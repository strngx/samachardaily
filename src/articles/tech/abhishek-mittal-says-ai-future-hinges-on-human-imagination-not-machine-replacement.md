Abhishek Mittal contends that the central question surrounding artificial intelligence concerns how humans can sustain imagination and independent thinking rather than whether machines will supplant cognition. He maintains that the unique attributes of human intelligence remain essential as AI systems evolve.

A classroom episode at the Indian Institute of Foreign Trade in Delhi demonstrated this perspective, as students tackled AI topics while actively exercising critical reasoning and collaborative discussion despite AI tools.

Mittal asserts that nurturing these distinct human qualities will determine the future direction and impact of AI development, ensuring AI augments rather than replaces human judgment.
