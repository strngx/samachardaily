Foundation Future Robotics, an Eric Trump‑backed firm, became the first company to field humanoid combat robots in an active war zone when it dispatched its Phantom MK 1 units to Ukraine in February.

The CEO addressed public “Terminator” concerns, stressing the robots’ specific operational role and the testing of technology under battlefield conditions.

The company is now in talks to deliver additional Phantom MK 1 robots to Israel, potentially extending its presence to a second theater of conflict.
