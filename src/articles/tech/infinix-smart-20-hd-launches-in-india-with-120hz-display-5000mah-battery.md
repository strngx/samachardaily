Infinix has launched the Smart 20 HD in India, featuring a 6.67-inch HD+ display with a 120Hz refresh rate and 700 nits of high-brightness mode brightness. The budget handset runs on a Unisoc T7250 chipset paired with 3GB of RAM and 64GB of internal storage, expandable via microSD card and virtual RAM.

The device sports identical 8-megapixel cameras on the front and rear, dual speakers with a 300 percent Ultra Volume mode, and a 5,000mAh battery. Infinix has not yet disclosed pricing or sale dates for the Indian market.
