OpenAI has terminated Saudi national Abdulrahman Alfozan and reported the case to federal authorities after a covert listening device was found in a company conference room. The FBI confirmed the arrest of Alfozan on Friday, linking the incident to a security breach that prompted immediate dismissal and law‑enforcement involvement.

The discovery of the device raises fresh concerns about workplace surveillance and data theft risks at major AI firms. OpenAI and the FBI are investigating the circumstances surrounding the installation, while no further details about the device’s origin or purpose have been released. The incident underscores the growing need for robust physical security measures in tech campuses.
