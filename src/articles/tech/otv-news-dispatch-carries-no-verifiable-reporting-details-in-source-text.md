The item listed the outlet's WhatsApp channel, YouTube, Facebook, Telegram, Twitter and Instagram handles, along with its website, live-stream and mobile app addresses, and noted that the substantive text was available only in paid plans.

No names, events, figures, decisions or statements were included that could be independently verified or published as a news report.
