A recent survey shows that only 4% of container port operators have documented earnings gains from artificial intelligence, despite 44% already scaling AI deployments across their terminals. The survey covered major Indian and global terminals, emphasizing limited tangible benefits despite high deployment rates.

The disparity reflects a measurement failure rather than a technology issue, as most firms do not systematically track efficiency gains against profit margins. Without consistent data collection, firms cannot correlate AI-driven process improvements with actual margin changes.

Consequently, the true financial impact of AI in port operations remains uncertain, highlighting the need for standardized metrics to evaluate return on investment. Until robust measurement frameworks are adopted, AI's contribution to port earnings will stay difficult to quantify.
