A joint team from Germany’s Helmholtz Centre for Environmental Research (UFZ) and the Leipzig‑based German Centre for Integrative Biodiversity Research (iDiv), together with several partners, has created an automated, AI‑based pollen analysis system for honey.

The new method identifies the pollen types that single‑flower honeys must contain, delivering results that closely align with conventional laboratory testing while eliminating the need for specialist expertise and lengthy procedures.

If adopted, the technology could speed up authenticity checks and support producers in meeting pollen‑content regulations.
