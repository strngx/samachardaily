MSI’s recent BIOS updates add native support for Intel’s 12‑core and 10‑core Bartlett Lake processors on Z690 and Z790 motherboards, removing the need for custom BIOS modifications and unlocking power limits.

The Bartlett Lake chips, originally sold for the embedded market, can now operate on consumer LGA 1700 platforms straight from the box, a capability that previously required a BIOS hack on ASUS boards.

This compatibility boost simplifies deployment of Bartlett Lake silicon in standard desktop builds.
