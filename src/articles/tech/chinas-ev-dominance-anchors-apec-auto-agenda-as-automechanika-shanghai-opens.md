China's automotive sector is anchoring discussions at the APEC China 2026 meeting in Shenzhen, with the country's NEV sales share reaching just over 53% in the first nine months of 2026, totalling 12.47 million units. Chinese manufacturers also command 73% of the global EV battery market, while BYD's upgraded Blade Battery now charges from 10% to 97% in nine minutes.

Electric heavy-duty trucks captured nearly half of China's new registrations by mid-2026 and over 90% of the global electric truck market. Meanwhile, ASEAN-6 automotive volume grew just over 11% in the first half of 2026, with EV adoption rising 32% and production projected to exceed six million units by 2030.

Automechanika Shanghai opens December 2 at the National Exhibition and Convention Center, featuring 7,600 exhibitors across 16 halls and three new zones covering air-space-ground connectivity, quality standards, and the semiconductor supply chain. The previous edition drew over 250,000 visitors from 190 countries.
