A United States senator has introduced new legislation designed to protect American workers as artificial intelligence rapidly integrates across industries. The initiative aims to proactively address emerging labor and economic shifts driven by automated technologies.

Artificial intelligence has increasingly taken center stage on Capitol Hill, prompting lawmakers to evaluate federal oversight and public safeguards. The proposed measure focuses on ensuring that technological advancements benefit citizens while mitigating potential risks to employment security.

By seeking to establish legislative protections ahead of widespread industry transformation, the bill reflects growing efforts in Congress to guide how AI tools are deployed across the national workforce.
