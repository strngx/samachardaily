India has advanced its digital infrastructure with the launch of new indigenous artificial intelligence models, including systems designed to operate without internet access. This initiative aims to accelerate local innovation and deliver accessible technology across remote areas.

Concurrently, the national cybersecurity agency has issued an urgent advisory to banks regarding sophisticated hacking threats. Officials have urged financial institutions to strengthen their digital defenses against the rising use of AI-driven attacks on sensitive monetary systems.
