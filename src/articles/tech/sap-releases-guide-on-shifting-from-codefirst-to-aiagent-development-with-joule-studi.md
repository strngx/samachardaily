SAP has published a practical guide titled “From Business Intent to AI Agents: A Practical Guide to Getting Started with SAP Joule Studio.” The guide outlines how enterprise application development is evolving.

It contrasts the traditional linear process—gathering requirements, designing architecture, identifying APIs, writing code, testing and deploying—with the emerging agentic AI approach that reshapes each stage.

By providing step‑by‑step guidance, SAP seeks to accelerate adoption of AI‑driven agents across enterprise workloads using Joule Studio.
