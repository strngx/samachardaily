KLH Aziznagar Campus in Hyderabad has formally entered into a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with the Data Security Council of India (DSCI). The strategic collaboration is aimed at expanding high-tier cybersecurity education, fostering specialized skill development, and deepening industry engagement for students and faculty.

The institutional agreement was officially signed on campus by Dr. A. Ramakrishna, Principal of KLH Aziznagar Campus, and Dr. Sriram Birudavolu, Chief Executive Officer of the Cybersecurity Centre of Excellence (CCoE), Hyderabad—an initiative established under DSCI. The pact marks a significant effort to align academic learning directly with practical cybersecurity challenges.

Under the partnership framework, the entities plan to establish joint skill-building initiatives, faculty development programs, and industry-oriented technical training. By utilizing the resources and domain expertise of the CCoE, the institute aims to address India's expanding demand for workforce-ready digital security professionals.

The collaboration will focus on creating structured avenues for practical learning, research projects, and technical workshops. By providing students direct exposure to modern cybersecurity frameworks, the alliance seeks to cultivate talent capable of protecting complex digital infrastructures against evolving threats.

Through this tie-up, KLH Aziznagar Campus reinforces its commitment to technical education and industry relevance, while DSCI's CCoE continues its mission to strengthen India's cybersecurity ecosystem through robust institutional partnerships and targeted capability building in key technology hubs like Hyderabad.
