Apollo Atomics, MaxQ Medical, Pixelgen Technologies, along with several other early‑stage firms, disclosed that they have closed new financing rounds backed by a mix of venture capital houses such as FCVC, Atlantic Blue Ventures and Flat Capital.

The deals span a range of high‑impact sectors: Apollo Atomics is advancing compact nuclear power solutions, MaxQ Medical focuses on AI‑driven cardiac monitoring, while Pixelgen Technologies is developing next‑generation imaging sensors. The involvement of specialized investors signals confidence in the commercial potential of these emerging technologies.

Neither the companies nor the investors disclosed specific valuation figures or capital amounts. However, founders highlighted that the fresh capital will accelerate product development, expand regulatory pathways and support go‑to‑market strategies in the coming months.
