realme has quietly launched the P4s Lite smartphone in India, setting the starting price at ₹16,499. The device is positioned as an entry-level model in the company’s expanding P-series lineup.

The phone packs a 7,000mAh battery, a 120Hz refresh rate display, and is powered by the Unisoc T7250 chipset. These specifications are highlighted as straightforward for users seeking long endurance and smooth visuals.

By adding the P4s Lite, realme continues to fill its budget segment with another option, though the source indicates there is a catch associated with the launch, though specifics are not disclosed in the excerpt.
