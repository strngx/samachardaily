Bose introduced the second‑generation QuietComfort (QC 2) headphones in early 2026, aiming to refresh a flagship that has long been praised for its plush fit and reliable active‑noise‑cancellation (ANC). The new model retains the signature over‑ear design and soft ear‑cup padding, but it incorporates a revised acoustic architecture that mirrors the higher‑end Ultra series. According to Bose, the updated drivers deliver a clearer, more balanced soundstage, while the ANC algorithm now adapts more quickly to changing ambient noise, closing the gap with the Ultra’s industry‑leading cancellation. Battery life remains a strong point, with Bose quoting up to 20 hours of playback on a single charge, and a quick‑charge feature that provides an hour of listening after just 15 minutes on the charger.

On the connectivity front, the QC 2 adds Bluetooth 5.3 support, enabling a more stable link and lower latency for video and gaming use. The headphones also support multipoint pairing, allowing users to switch seamlessly between a phone and a laptop. The on‑device controls have been streamlined; a single button now toggles ANC modes, while a touch‑sensitive area on the right earcup adjusts volume. Bose has priced the QC 2 at roughly ₹24,999 in India, noticeably lower than the Ultra’s ₹34,999 tag, positioning it as a more accessible entry into Bose’s premium audio ecosystem.

In real‑world testing, reviewers noted that the QC 2’s ANC performs admirably in both office environments and noisy public transport, cutting out a broad range of frequencies without the occasional hiss sometimes heard in older models. The sound signature leans toward a neutral profile, with enough bass presence to satisfy mainstream listeners yet without overpowering mids and highs. Comfort remains a hallmark; the lightweight frame and pressure‑relieving ear cushions allow for marathon listening sessions without fatigue. Overall, the QC 2 is presented as a well‑rounded upgrade that captures the best of the Ultra’s technology while retaining the brand’s hallmark comfort and a more approachable price point.
