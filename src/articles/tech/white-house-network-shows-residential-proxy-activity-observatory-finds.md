The Residential Proxy Observatory, created by Layer3 Intel, reported proxy exit traffic on AS2650, a network registered to the US President’s executive office and operated from the White House.

Residential proxies run on personal devices to mask IP addresses and are often used by malicious actors for illicit actions such as DDoS attacks or malware distribution. Their presence in the White House aligns with Infoblox research showing widespread enterprise use.

The observatory also detected similar activity on the remote island of St Helena, underscoring that no location is immune to proxy‑based internet interference.
