A security flaw that potentially impacts 70% of the world’s websites was publicly disclosed this week, sparking immediate turmoil online. The vulnerability was meant to be patched quietly, but a researcher released the details before a fix was in place.

Cybersecurity leaders speaking to Biztoc said the incident has amplified an already rising demand for security professionals as firms rush to remediate the exposure and strengthen defenses.

The episode highlights the talent shortage in the sector, prompting companies to accelerate hiring and training programs to guard against future large‑scale vulnerabilities.
