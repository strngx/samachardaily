The US-China Economic and Security Review Commission’s 2026 report, released on Nov 18, says Beijing is trying to evade Washington’s export controls to acquire advanced Nvidia semiconductors for AI‑powered military systems.

A parallel probe reveals Kang Wei invested more than $9 bn between 2021 and 2024 in a nationwide data‑centre network, a venture tied to his wife Alice Huang, who is alleged to have moved Nvidia chips from her Singapore firm Megaspeed into China.

In response, the Trump administration tightened semiconductor restrictions on Nov 17 and is considering a rule that would force U.S. companies to disclose any use of Chinese AI models.
