A detailed new analysis of flagship smartphone repair costs demonstrates that Android devices consistently offer lower repair costs than the Apple ecosystem. Headtopics published the study, which examined screen, battery, and back-glass repair fees for the iPhone 18 Pro Max, Google Pixel 11 Pro XL, and Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra.

Results show the Android handsets offer lower repair expenses across the measured categories. The report notes these cost differences influence long-term ownership value for consumers choosing between the platforms, the description states.

The study focused on three specific flagship models released recently.
