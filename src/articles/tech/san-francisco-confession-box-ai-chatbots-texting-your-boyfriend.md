A purple box on a folding table in San Francisco's Alamo Square draws attention as part of an interactive public art installation. The box, which could be mistaken for a tissue container, is labeled "Confession box: Everyone has an AI secret." Oliva Tai was nearby watching as curious passersby engage with it, reading and leaving their own confessions.

Among the admissions are people confessing about using AI chatbots to communicate with romantic partners. The installation raises questions about where personal relationships intersect with artificial intelligence. Visitors share stories of farming out personal messages to machines, prompting ethical questions about authenticity in dating.
