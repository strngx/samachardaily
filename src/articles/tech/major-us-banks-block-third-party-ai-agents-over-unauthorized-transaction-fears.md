Major US banks have begun blocking third-party AI agents from accessing customer accounts, citing concerns that the software could initiate transactions without explicit user authorization.

The restrictions reflect growing alarm among banking giants that autonomous AI tools may bypass security protocols and take financial actions customers never approved.

Industry leaders warn the trend could undermine account security and expose consumers to uncontrolled automated activity across their finances, raising questions about the safety of deposits and payments.
