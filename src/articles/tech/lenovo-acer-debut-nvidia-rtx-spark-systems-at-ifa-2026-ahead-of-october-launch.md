Hardware giant Lenovo officially expanded its premium Yoga lineup at IFA 2026, showcasing two new laptops powered by Nvidia’s RTX Spark platform alongside two new Android tablets. The announcements at the consumer technology trade show highlight a major hardware refresh for Lenovo’s creator and productivity portfolio, integrating specialized computing architecture into portable form factors.

Taiwan-based Acer also showcased new hardware powered by Nvidia RTX Spark at IFA 2026, unveiling a tiny PC design aimed directly at competing with Apple's Mac Studio. Acer’s compact desktop system leverages Nvidia’s Blackwell architecture combined with 128GB of unified memory, bringing heavy-duty workstation computing power and local artificial intelligence capabilities into an ultra-small desktop footprint.

Details revealed during the Berlin trade show confirm that Nvidia RTX Spark personal computers from both Lenovo and Acer are scheduled for a worldwide commercial launch in October 2026. The new RTX Spark laptops will hit the market in two distinct configurations, giving buyers multiple options across power efficiency and graphics performance thresholds.

The simultaneous announcements from Lenovo and Acer demonstrate a broader PC industry push toward integrating workstation-grade GPU acceleration and unified memory structures directly into personal hardware. By bringing high-capacity memory architecture and Blackwell technology into consumer laptops and small-form-factor desktops, manufacturers are aiming to meet growing demand for local AI processing and complex creative tasks.

Both technology giants are positioning these new systems ahead of the key autumn retail season. With dual configurations prepared for the initial wave of RTX Spark laptops and desktops, October's product rollout marks a significant expansion for Nvidia’s platform across leading Windows hardware partners.
