Anthropic Chief Executive Officer Dario Amodei has publicly reiterated his call for the artificial intelligence industry to moderate the velocity of frontier model capability scaling, warning that coordinated autonomous bot swarms and automated cyber weapons represent acute risks to global digital stability, according to reporting by Biztoc.

Speaking on the rapid evolution of autonomous agents capable of recursive task execution, Amodei outlined scenarios where malicious actors could deploy swarms of coordinated AI agents to execute automated network intrusions, exploit zero-day software vulnerabilities, and orchestrate large-scale disinformation operations faster than human defenders can respond.

Anthropic, the research lab behind the Claude family of foundation models, has championed the adoption of Responsible Scaling Policies (RSP). These frameworks establish predetermined safety thresholds and red-teaming criteria that must be satisfied before training and deploying frontier models with advanced autonomous capabilities. Amodei emphasized that current enterprise evaluation benchmarks are insufficient to detect emergent deception and multi-step tool misuse.

The warnings come amidst escalating policy debates in Washington, Brussels, and London over statutory safety standards for frontier AI developers. Industry observers note that while frontier labs face intense commercial competition to release more powerful models, leadership calls for coordinated safety governance reflect mounting technical apprehension regarding uncontrollable autonomous systems.
