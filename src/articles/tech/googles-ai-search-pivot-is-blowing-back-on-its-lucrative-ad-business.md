The report casts Google's AI pivot as self-inflicted damage, with AI features now placed at the very top of its popular search engine and the blowback landing on the company's lucrative advertising business.

The AI Overview tool answers user queries directly, so people can get information without clicking further into results. That change is the channel through which the rapid AI embrace is hurting the ad business, per the account.

No figures quantifying the impact were included in the available material, which frames the issue as a threat to Google's advertising revenue rather than a flaw in the technology itself.
