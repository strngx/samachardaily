BloodCounts! is the first healthcare project hosted by the Cambridge Centre for Advanced Research and Education in Singapore, according to an announcement dated October 26, 2026 from Media OutReach Newswire.

The announcement described a plan to use artificial intelligence for blood testing, with the goal of detecting diseases earlier in the body. Mathematicians and clinicians are involved, the announcement said.

The effort will create advanced artificial intelligence models for routine blood tests, the announcement said. The source reported the post first appeared on Forever News. The organization is based in Singapore.
