Apple has introduced an aggressive festive retail promotion for its flagship iPhone 17 Pro Max, bringing the effective purchase price down to Rs 74,990 across Croma physical stores and its online platform in India. The limited-period offer represents a notable retail adjustment designed to attract premium smartphone buyers ahead of major festive shopping periods.

The promotional structure combines an upfront price cut from the smartphone's official Rs 1,34,900 launch price with a structured trade-in scheme. Under the exchange arrangement, buyers trading in eligible previous-generation iPhone 15 Pro and iPhone 16 Pro handsets receive an additional exchange bonus of up to Rs 10,000 over standard device valuation. Furthermore, transactions completed using select partner bank credit cards, such as ICICI Bank and HDFC Bank, qualify for an instant 10% discount of up to Rs 5,000. Croma is also including a complimentary certified protective case with each qualifying handset.

The bundled offer aims to stimulate demand in India's competitive high-end smartphone segment, where consumer interest in top-tier hardware remains high. While reaching the headline Rs 74,990 price requires fulfilling all trade-in and banking criteria, the promotion provides one of the steepest aggregate discount packages currently available for Apple's flagship device through organized electronics retail chains.
