On Sept. 17, 2026, in Abu Dhabi, global AI‑native cybersecurity firm Cyble and the United Arab Emirates' Cyber Security Council formalised a Memorandum of Understanding aimed at strengthening the nation’s threat‑intelligence capabilities.

The agreement creates a framework for Cyble to supply the Council with advanced threat intelligence, early‑warning alerts and actionable insights targeting ransomware, phishing, fraud and other cyber risks that affect government bodies and critical‑infrastructure operators across the UAE.

Cyble CEO and Co‑Founder Beenu Arora called the pact a strategic validation of the company’s platform, while H.E. Dr. Mohamed Al Kuwaiti, Head of Cyber Security for the UAE, highlighted the partnership as a step toward bolstering the country’s digital resilience through proactive, intelligence‑led defence.
