On Sept. 17, 2026, in Abu Dhabi, global AI-native cybersecurity company Cyble and the United Arab Emirates Cyber Security Council signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) to significantly enhance the nation's proactive cyber defense and national threat-intelligence capabilities.

Under the partnership framework, Cyble will integrate its AI-powered threat-intelligence platform, Cyble Vision, into the Council's national monitoring infrastructure. The deployment provides government ministries, public sector entities, and designated critical national infrastructure operators with automated early-warning alerts, real-time dark-web surveillance, and actionable telemetry on emerging ransomware strains, credential theft, and coordinated phishing campaigns targeting UAE digital assets.

Cyble Chief Executive Officer and Co-Founder Beenu Arora characterized the bilateral agreement as a strategic validation of the company's continuous threat-exposure management technology, emphasizing the shared objective of pre-empting state-sponsored and criminal cyber intrusions. H.E. Dr. Mohamed Al Kuwaiti, Head of Cyber Security for the UAE Government, affirmed that collaborating with global threat-intelligence specialists strengthens the Emirates' digital resilience through proactive, intelligence-driven incident response and cross-sector information sharing.

The initiative aligns with the UAE's broader national cybersecurity strategy, designed to establish automated defense perimeters, protect financial and energy infrastructure, and reduce vulnerability exposure across connected municipal networks.
