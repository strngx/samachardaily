AI is turning ad tech into a fight for the brain behind the buy, forcing brands to race ahead with machine-learning tools before rivals step in and erode traditional media trust and threaten brand credibility worldwide.

Mergers and acquisitions in ad tech have climbed steadily as 2026 approaches its end, while earlier this week LUMA Partners hosted the Digital Marketing Summit to gauge which AI breakthroughs will drive audience purchase decisions across markets.

Summit participants debated the next wave of AI-enabled targeting, warning that without decisive moves, buying budgets could stall until the new cycle opens.
