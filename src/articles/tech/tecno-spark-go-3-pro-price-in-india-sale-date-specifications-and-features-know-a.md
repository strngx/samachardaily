Tecno has officially introduced its latest budget offering, the Spark Go 3 Pro, to the Indian market. Priced at INR 15,999, the handset will be available for purchase from September 4, and the company is promoting it as a value‑packed alternative in the crowded sub‑₹20,000 segment. The launch comes as Tecno seeks to deepen its foothold in India after earlier models like the Spark 10 series gained modest traction.

The Spark Go 3 Pro sports a 6.78‑inch LCD panel with a 120 Hz refresh rate, delivering smoother scrolling and gaming experiences compared with many rivals that still use 60 Hz screens. Under the hood it runs on MediaTek’s Helio G81 chipset, paired with up to 6 GB of RAM and 128 GB of internal storage, a configuration that should handle everyday apps and casual games without lag. A standout feature for a phone in this price bracket is its IP68/IP69 water and dust resistance rating, meaning it can survive accidental splashes and dust exposure. Power is supplied by a sizable 5,200 mAh battery, which Tecno claims will last through a full day of heavy use, and the device supports fast charging. Photography is handled by a 13 MP primary sensor on the rear and an 8 MP shooter for selfies, complemented by an IR blaster for remote‑control functions and dual stereo speakers for richer audio.

With the Spark Go 3 Pro, Tecno aims to compete directly with offerings from Xiaomi’s Redmi, Realme and Samsung’s Galaxy M series, all of which dominate the Indian budget segment. The combination of a high‑refresh‑rate display, robust battery capacity and an IP rating is relatively rare at the ₹16,000 price point, and could attract cost‑conscious consumers who also value durability. The phone will be sold through Tecno’s online channels and selected retail partners, and the company has hinted at promotional bundles and exchange offers to boost early adoption.
