Google has launched Android 17 QPR1 for Pixel 11 phones as part of the October 2026 update. The release focuses on three critical fixes tailored to the Pixel hardware, addressing long-standing performance and stability concerns that have been affecting recent device iterations. Users can expect improvements across audio handling and system responsiveness as part of these targeted enhancements.

This update sits alongside the Android 17 QPR 3 Beta 1 build, representing continued refinement before the full rollout. Distribution remains tied to paid plans, indicating that access to the updated software depends on specific subscription tiers. The phased approach ensures quality control before wider deployment across the global market.
