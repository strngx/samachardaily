CXMT, a rising Chinese semiconductor firm, confirmed that its next‑generation low‑power DDR6 (LPDDR6) memory will be the first of its kind to reach a commercial device, with the Xiaomi 18 Fold earmarked as the launch platform.

The LPDDR6 module promises up to 30% higher data rates and markedly lower power consumption compared with the current LPDDR5 standard, positioning CXMT to challenge incumbents such as Samsung and SK Hynix in the high‑end mobile market.

Industry analysts note that the move could accelerate the adoption of advanced memory across flagship smartphones, potentially reshaping supply‑chain dynamics and prompting rival chipmakers to accelerate their own LPDDR6 roadmaps.
