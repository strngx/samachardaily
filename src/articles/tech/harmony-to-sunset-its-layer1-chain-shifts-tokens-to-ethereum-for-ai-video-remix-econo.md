Harmony disclosed that it will fully sunset its Layer‑1 network, directing all token holders to migrate to Ethereum. The move is tied to a fresh initiative that aims to build a "remix economy" where artificial‑intelligence tools enable video creators to generate and monetize derivative content. By relocating its token to Ethereum, Harmony intends to leverage the larger ecosystem’s security and developer base for this AI‑focused venture.

The Harmony blockchain, launched as a high‑throughput, proof‑of‑stake platform, has positioned itself as a low‑cost alternative for decentralized applications. Over time, the network faced competition from other Layer‑1 solutions and cross‑chain bridges, prompting the team to reassess its strategic direction. Sunsetting the chain means halting block production, deprecating smart contracts, and ceasing validator rewards on the original network.

The proposed remix economy will center on AI video creators who can remix existing footage, add generative effects, and sell the resulting works. Harmony’s token, once migrated to Ethereum, will serve as the medium of exchange and incentive within this marketplace, allowing creators to earn royalties and participants to stake for governance. Ethereum’s robust smart‑contract infrastructure is expected to provide the necessary scalability and composability for these AI‑driven applications.

To ease the transition, Harmony pledged to help validators shift their staking positions to the new Ethereum‑based framework. The company will release detailed migration guides, open a token bridge, and coordinate with validator groups to ensure that staked assets are safely transferred without loss of earnings. Validators are advised to monitor official channels for instructions on withdrawing from the legacy network and redeploying their stakes on Ethereum’s proof‑of‑stake chain.

Industry observers note that the migration could set a precedent for other niche Layer‑1 projects seeking to consolidate around larger ecosystems. By aligning with Ethereum, Harmony hopes to attract a broader pool of developers and investors interested in AI video technology, while offering its existing community a clear path forward. The shift also underscores the growing convergence of blockchain finance with generative AI content creation.
