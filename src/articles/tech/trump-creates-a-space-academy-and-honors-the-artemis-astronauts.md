President Donald Trump signed an executive order on Friday to establish a dedicated U.S. space academy, marking a formal policy push to bolster national infrastructure for specialized space training and education.

During the signing ceremony, Trump characterized the renewed focus on space flight as essential to national momentum, explicitly declaring that space exploration has become 'the hot thing.' The ceremony doubled as an official recognition event for astronauts serving under the Artemis program.

The creation of the space academy via presidential decree signals an attempt to institutionalize specialized educational and instructional tracks tailored to modern spaceflight and exploration needs. By officially honoring the Artemis astronaut corps alongside the order, the administration directly tied the institutional move to ongoing deep-space objectives.

The executive directive formally sets the framework for the academy, aiming to structure future training programs aligned with federal space goals. Further operational specifics and departmental oversight mechanisms remain framed around this core mandate to expand space exploration capability.
