Apple held its highly anticipated 2026 live event on Tuesday, unveiling a suite of new hardware that includes the company’s first foldable iPhone, the iPhone 18 Pro and the Apple Watch 12. The announcements were streamed globally and marked the first time Apple has ventured into a folding smartphone form factor, a move that aligns the brand with competitors that have already released foldable devices.

The foldable iPhone, presented as a premium device, features a flexible display that can transition between a compact phone size and a larger tablet-like screen. Apple executives highlighted the seamless integration of iOS across the two modes, promising a unified user experience without the need for separate devices. While detailed specifications such as screen size, processor and pricing were not disclosed during the presentation, the company emphasized durability and a new hinge mechanism designed to withstand daily use.

Alongside the foldable model, Apple launched the iPhone 18 Pro, its next‑generation flagship. The iPhone 18 Pro is said to be powered by Apple’s latest silicon generation, delivering faster performance and improved energy efficiency. The device also introduces a variable‑aperture camera system, allowing photographers to adjust the lens opening for better low‑light shots and greater depth‑of‑field control. The iPhone 18 Pro retains the familiar flat‑screen design but incorporates upgraded materials and a refined chassis.

The Apple Watch 12 was the third major product revealed. Building on the health‑focused features of its predecessors, the new watch adds a brighter always‑on display, enhanced biometric sensors and a faster S‑chip processor. Apple highlighted new software capabilities, including advanced sleep tracking and expanded integration with the iPhone 18 Pro’s camera system for remote photography.

Analysts note that the simultaneous launch of a foldable phone and a new flagship suggests Apple is hedging its bets between maintaining its traditional design language and exploring emerging form factors. The event also underscored Apple’s continued emphasis on premium pricing and ecosystem lock‑in, as each new device is positioned to work best when paired with the others.
