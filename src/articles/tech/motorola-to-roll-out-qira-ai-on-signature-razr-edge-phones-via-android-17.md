Motorola and parent company Lenovo have announced that the proprietary Qira personal artificial intelligence assistant will be deployed to Motorola's Signature, Razr, and Edge series smartphones as part of the upcoming Android 17 operating system update.

The Qira AI assistant, which was first demonstrated at CES, is engineered to provide cross-device contextual intelligence spanning both Lenovo personal computers and Motorola mobile hardware. While initial smartphone deployment began with the Edge 70 Max, the Android 17 firmware update will expand compatibility to the foldable Razr lineup and flagship Signature models.

The software integration introduces natural language task automation, smart notification summarization, and on-device generative capabilities. Motorola confirmed that Qira is structured as a unified ecosystem assistant, allowing users to synchronize tasks and workflows seamlessly between mobile devices and Lenovo workstations.

According to company disclosures, advanced capabilities within the Qira AI suite will be offered through tiered subscription plans, providing enhanced cloud processing alongside foundational on-device features.
