Motorola, together with parent company Lenovo, announced that the Qira personal AI assistant will be deployed to its Signature, Razr and Edge series smartphones as part of the upcoming Android 17 update.

The Qira assistant was first unveiled at CES and has been available on select Lenovo devices for some time; its initial rollout on Motorola hardware began with the Edge 70 Max model.

Motorola markets Qira as a single AI experience that spans both Motorola and Lenovo products, and the service will be offered exclusively through paid subscription plans.
