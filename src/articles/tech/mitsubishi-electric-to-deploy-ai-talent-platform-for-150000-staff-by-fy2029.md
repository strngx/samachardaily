Mitsubishi Electric Corporation has announced a comprehensive plan to deploy an enterprise artificial intelligence talent and human resources platform serving approximately 150,000 employees globally by fiscal year 2029.

The global conglomerate, which generates approximately $38.3 billion in annual revenue, intends to consolidate human resources and employee skill data across more than 200 group companies and international subsidiaries into a unified system. The platform is designed to standardize career progression pathways, streamline internal recruitment, and optimize technical talent allocation across disparate business units.

Under the multi-year implementation roadmap, Mitsubishi Electric will initiate a pilot program in 2026. The pilot will evaluate AI-driven job-matching algorithms, skills inventory mapping, and succession planning tools to identify next-generation leadership candidates and assess technical capabilities across specialized engineering divisions.

The company stated that the talent management system will help modernize its corporate workforce operations, enhance internal mobility, and improve collaboration across its global industrial, automotive, and electronic equipment businesses.
