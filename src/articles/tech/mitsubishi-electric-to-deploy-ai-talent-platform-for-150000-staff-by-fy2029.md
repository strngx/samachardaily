Mitsubishi Electric, a conglomerate with $38.3 billion in annual revenue, announced plans to build an AI-powered talent platform that will serve 150,000 employees by fiscal 2029.

The platform is designed to consolidate human‑resources data from over 200 group companies, providing a single source of truth for recruitment, development and succession planning across the entire corporate network.

A pilot program slated for 2026 will test AI‑based job matching aimed at identifying next‑generation leaders, allowing the firm to refine algorithms before the full rollout.
