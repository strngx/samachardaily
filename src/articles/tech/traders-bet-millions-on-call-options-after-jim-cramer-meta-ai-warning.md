Market participants executed a series of high-volume options trades after television host Jim Cramer warned viewers about companies vulnerable to Meta’s new artificial intelligence platform. While Cramer identified multiple businesses at risk, a glaring omission in his analysis caught traders' attention.

The oversight prompted an immediate surge into short-dated call options, with one investor placing a multi-million-dollar bet counter to broader concerns about Meta's technological disruption. The swift trading activity highlights how quick market reactions can follow televised stock commentary.
