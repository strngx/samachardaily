An Adobe software dupe maker has released free versions of Word, PowerPoint and Excel, directly challenging Microsoft Office's dominance. The move positions the company as a lower-cost alternative in the productivity software market.

Broader industry shifts surround the announcement as AI-vibecoded apps begin challenging established legacy software, including Photoshop and Microsoft Word. Earlier this month, Reddit user u/ai_art_is_art—identified by Tom's Hardware as a software engineer—fueled discussion on the trend.

Whether these free alternatives and AI-generated tools will displace paid legacy products remains an open question. The article frames the competition as a test of whether convenience and cost can outweigh the polish and ecosystem of incumbents.
