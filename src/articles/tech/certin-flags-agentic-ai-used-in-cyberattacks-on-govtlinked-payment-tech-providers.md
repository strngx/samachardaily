On Nov 20, India's Computer Emergency Response Team (CERT‑In) issued an advisory highlighting the deployment of agentic artificial intelligence in financial cyber‑attacks.

The notice says the AI‑driven threats target technology service providers that process government‑linked payments and platforms that manage vendor and partner payouts, marking a shift beyond the traditional banking and financial services sector.

CERT‑In’s alert underscores the emerging risk of autonomous AI tools being weaponised against critical payment infrastructure, prompting agencies to review security measures.
