Delaware is advancing a proposal to allow companies to be managed by AI agents rather than human CEOs, a structure designed to shield human owners from liability for AI-driven decisions. The initiative has triggered formal scrutiny from a government committee seeking details on the risks and motivations behind the AI governance model.

Governor Matt Meyer received a complaint letter questioning the proposal, prompting the committee's information request. Concerns center on accountability gaps and the broader implications of delegating corporate authority to autonomous systems.
