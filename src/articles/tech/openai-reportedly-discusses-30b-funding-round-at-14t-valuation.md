OpenAI is reportedly discussing a funding round of roughly $30 billion. The round could value the company at about $1.4 trillion, according to the report.

The company’s annualised revenue run rate is said to be approaching $70 billion. That revenue figure, alongside the proposed valuation, shows how quickly the business built around ChatGPT has expanded.

The report frames the two numbers as evidence of rapid growth tied to OpenAI’s ChatGPT business. It does not confirm that the funding round has been completed, and the reported discussion is described as ongoing.
