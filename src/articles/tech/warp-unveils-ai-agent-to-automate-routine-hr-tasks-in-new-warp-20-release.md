Warp today unveiled an artificial intelligence agent as part of its Warp 2.0 release, extending its AI-native employee management platform with customizable workflows that operate without continuous human prompting. The addition positions the agent as a core component of the updated HR solution designed for modern workplace needs.

The new agent is specifically engineered to automate routine human resources functions, including employee onboarding, tax compliance, and benefits administration. By functioning autonomously, the system reduces the need for repeated manual input while maintaining consistent execution of essential employment-related processes.
