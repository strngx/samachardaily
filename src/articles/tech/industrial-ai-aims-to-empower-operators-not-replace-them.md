Forbes published an article titled “Industrial AI Won’t Replace Operators—It Will Create Better Decision‑Makers,” asserting that the technology is intended to augment, not displace, human workers.

The piece contrasts the rapid, consumer‑focused rollout of generative AI with the more deliberate adoption pace in industrial settings, where safety, reliability and integration with existing processes demand caution.

According to the article, the goal is to transform operators into enhanced decision‑makers, leveraging AI insights to improve outcomes while retaining human oversight.
