AEO-Rank. Ai on November 27 announced a dedicated ChatGPT Ads practice for large businesses in India, with the launch confirmed from New Delhi. The agency specialises in answer engine and generative engine optimisation.

Brands within some of India's largest business ecosystems are already using the agency's ChatGPT advertising, the company said in a statement.

By combining organic AI visibility, a proprietary SaaS platform and paid conversational advertising under one roof, AEO-Rank. Ai describes itself as one of the few partners in the country able to offer all three services to a single enterprise client.
