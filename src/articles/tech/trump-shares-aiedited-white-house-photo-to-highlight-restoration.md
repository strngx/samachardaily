President Donald Trump posted a series of photos on Truth Social highlighting recent White House renovations, aiming to showcase the restoration work. The post was shared on Truth Social, a platform Trump often uses for direct public communication.

One image in the series appears to have been altered with AI, juxtaposing an old column with a ‘restored’ version that contains noticeable inconsistencies. Observers quickly noted the visual discrepancies, including mismatched lighting and texture between the two columns.

Google’s AI detection tool flagged the photo as ‘made or manipulated,’ raising questions about the authenticity of the visual evidence presented in the post. The detection result highlights concerns over the use of AI‑generated imagery in official political communications.
