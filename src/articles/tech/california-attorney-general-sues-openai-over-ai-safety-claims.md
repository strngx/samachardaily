OpenAI now faces a legal challenge in California over its artificial intelligence models. The state attorney general brought the action against the company.

Rob Bonta alleged OpenAI placed public safety at risk and claimed the firm lacked guardrails to prevent a breach at artificial intelligence startup Hugging Face. The attorney general is a Democrat.

The lawsuit focused on the company’s advanced AI technology and the allegations surrounding it. It raised questions about the startup’s AI systems and the claims made by Bonta. The state action involved OpenAI and Hugging Face. The suit was filed on Monday.
