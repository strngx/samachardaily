Aynak, Inc., a San Diego technology startup, wants to give glasses to adults who have hearing difficulty, a condition reported by about 15% of U.S. adults, according to recent surveys.

The company is developing a novel eyeglass frame that combines small microphones, speakers and a camera with artificial intelligence and machine learning software to deliver the same information as a traditional hearing aid.

The product is currently available only through paid plans, indicating a subscription model for the technology, and is aimed at adults with hearing difficulty, making it a novel approach to auditory assistance.
