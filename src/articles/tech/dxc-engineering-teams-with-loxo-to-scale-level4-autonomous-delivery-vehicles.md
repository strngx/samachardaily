Ashburn, Va., Sept. 17, 2026 – DXC Technology announced a strategic partnership with Swiss autonomous‑driving developer LOXO to accelerate the rollout of Level 4 autonomous commercial vehicles for middle‑ and last‑mile logistics.

LOXO brings a proven driverless platform that has earned Europe’s first approval for Level 4 logistics operations on public roads, while DXC contributes engineering, AI, data and systems‑integration expertise drawn from its work with more than 50 million vehicles worldwide.

The collaboration targets the logistics sector’s looming shortfall of roughly 750,000 truck drivers by 2028, aiming to shift autonomous fleets from isolated pilots to repeatable, enterprise‑scale deployments that integrate back‑end data services, fleet orchestration and operational workflows.

“Autonomous driving technology is on the road today; the next challenge is scaling it across enterprise operations,” said Karim Jeribi, Europe General Manager at DXC. LOXO CEO Amin Amini added that the partnership will turn proven autonomy into scalable commercial operations across European markets and beyond.
