Nothing announced on Oct 30 that the stable Nothing OS 5.0, based on Android 17, is now being rolled out worldwide to the Phone 4a, Phone 4a Pro, Phone 3a and Phone 3a Pro. The deployment is staggered, so devices will receive the update in batches; users without a notification should check Settings → System → System update.

OS 5.0 adds a redesigned home screen, smoother animations, expanded customization and new AI tools, plus updated Glyph and widget designs. The 3a and 3a Pro download is 3.09 GB (firmware C5.0‑261023‑2146); camera features differ between 3a and 4a models. Phone 1, Phone 2 and CMF Phone 1 will not receive the update.
