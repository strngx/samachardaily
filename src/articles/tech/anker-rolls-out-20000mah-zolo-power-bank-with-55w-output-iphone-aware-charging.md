Anker has launched a new 20K Zolo Power Bank in China, positioning the 20,000mAh portable charger as a flagship accessory designed to comply with the country's updated 2026 safety standard for power banks. The device features two integrated USB-C cables, eliminating the need for users to carry separate charging cables for most modern smartphones and laptops. Anker is marketing the Zolo as a high-capacity, travel-friendly solution for users who rely on fast charging across multiple devices.

The power bank supports up to 55W output, allowing it to charge not only smartphones but also larger devices such as tablets and compatible laptops at meaningful speeds. According to the product details shared by Anker, the bank is engineered to detect the connected device and adjust power delivery accordingly. This intelligent power management is designed to optimize charging efficiency while protecting battery health across different device categories.

A standout feature for Apple users is iPhone-specific compatibility: the Zolo can identify the connected iPhone model and tailor its charging profile accordingly. Beyond basic charging optimization, the power bank integrates with Apple's Dynamic Island interface, allowing it to display real-time charging status updates directly on the iPhone's notification area. This level of native-style integration is unusual for third-party accessories and signals Anker's continued focus on the iOS ecosystem.

The launch comes at a time when Chinese regulators have tightened safety requirements for portable lithium-ion products following past incidents involving substandard power banks. By aligning the Zolo with the 2026 safety standard, Anker is making a compliance argument alongside its performance claims, appealing to consumers who have grown more cautious about high-capacity chargers. With 20,000mAh capacity, the bank is sized for multi-day travel or for users who need to charge multiple devices away from outlets. Pricing and availability beyond the Chinese market have not yet been detailed.
