Edifier, a well‑known audio equipment manufacturer, unveiled its latest product, the K200 portable speaker, aimed at turning any living room into a karaoke stage. The compact unit is equipped with a high‑output driver system that promises clear music playback while also supporting vocal input through a dedicated karaoke mode. Users can switch seamlessly between listening to their favorite tracks and singing along, with built‑in echo and pitch‑correction features that are typical of dedicated karaoke machines. To complete the experience, the K200 comes bundled with two battery‑powered wireless microphones that connect via Bluetooth, offering freedom of movement without the clutter of cables.

The speaker is set to launch in mainland China on August 31 at 20:00 local time, with a retail price of 799 yuan (approximately $110). Edifier has positioned the K200 as a mid‑range offering, targeting families and young adults who want a portable, all‑in‑one entertainment hub. The device supports multiple connectivity options, including Bluetooth 5.0 for streaming from smartphones, as well as auxiliary input for wired sources. Its battery life is advertised at up to eight hours of continuous use, making it suitable for both indoor gatherings and outdoor picnics. Early promotional material highlights the speaker’s sleek matte finish and compact dimensions, designed to fit easily on a coffee table or be carried to a backyard party.
