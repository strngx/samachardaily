Gigabyte has launched the WRX90 AI TOP motherboard, targeting high-performance computing and AI workloads with support for up to 2 TB of ECC DDR5 memory.

The board uses a dual-fan active cooling system to manage heat, with Gigabyte claiming it can reduce memory temperatures by up to 43 °C under load.

It includes seven PCIe 5.0 slots, and Gigabyte demonstrated a configuration supporting up to nine GPUs through additional external connections.
