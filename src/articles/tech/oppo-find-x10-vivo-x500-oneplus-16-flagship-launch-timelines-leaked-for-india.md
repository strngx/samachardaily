Sources have tipped that the Indian rollout of three upcoming Android flagships—OPPO Find X10, Vivo X500 and OnePlus 16—is slated for the near future, though exact dates remain undisclosed.

The OPPO Find X10 Pro Max, highlighted in early camera samples, is reported to be the world’s first smartphone equipped with three 200‑megapixel sensors, delivering unprecedented detail according to the preview images.

In parallel, OPPO has also teased its Watch S2 ahead of a scheduled September 22 launch, signalling a broader product wave that could coincide with the flagship phone releases.
