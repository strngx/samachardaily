Industry supply chain sources have leaked preliminary launch windows for three anticipated Android flagship smartphones in the Indian market: the OPPO Find X10 series, Vivo X500 series, and OnePlus 16. While official release dates have not been confirmed by the respective manufacturers, leaks indicate that the devices are progressing through regional regulatory and testing phases.

Early camera preview leaks for the top-tier OPPO Find X10 Pro Max suggest that the handset is testing a sophisticated triple 200-megapixel rear camera sensor configuration, aimed at setting new benchmarks for mobile telephoto and primary sensor resolution. In parallel, Vivo and OnePlus are reportedly preparing updated silicon platforms and specialized image signal processing hardware for their upcoming flagship releases.

Alongside the smartphone leaks, OPPO has also teased the upcoming release of its Watch S2 wearable ahead of a scheduled September event, indicating a coordinated product ecosystem rollout.

Industry analysts emphasize that these early hardware details represent supply chain leaks and pre-production engineering samples, with final retail specifications, pricing, and official availability dates subject to formal confirmation at launch.
