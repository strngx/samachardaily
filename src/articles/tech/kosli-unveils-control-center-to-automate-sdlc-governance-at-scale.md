Kosli launched Control Center on November 26, 2026, in London, through a company press release. The new capability is aimed at enterprise engineering teams.

According to Kosli, artificial intelligence is massively accelerating the software delivery lifecycle, and governance work performed by humans no longer scales. Control Center is designed to automate those SDLC controls at scale rather than rely on manual oversight.

The company says the capability lets those teams apply SDLC governance at the pace that AI-driven software delivery now demands, extending oversight across large-scale engineering operations.
