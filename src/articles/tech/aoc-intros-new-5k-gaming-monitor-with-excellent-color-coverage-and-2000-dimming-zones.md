The Optimus Ultra is AOC's first dual‑mode 5K display, featuring a 27‑inch mini‑LED panel that supports a peak refresh rate of 180 Hz at native 5K resolution (5120×2880). The panel incorporates more than 2,000 dimming zones, enabling precise local dimming and high contrast ratios for both gaming and content creation.

Color accuracy is a key selling point, with the monitor covering 98% of the DCI‑P3 color gamut and offering a wide color depth that meets professional standards. AOC also equipped the unit with adaptive sync technologies, low input lag, and a suite of gaming‑centric features such as customizable cross‑hair overlays and HDR support.

Pricing and availability were announced for select markets, with pre‑orders opening next week. Early benchmarks from Notebookcheck suggest the monitor delivers smooth frame pacing and minimal ghosting, positioning it as a premium option against rivals like ASUS ROG Swift and Dell Alienware.
