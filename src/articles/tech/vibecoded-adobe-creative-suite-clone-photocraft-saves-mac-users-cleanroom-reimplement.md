ArtCraft has launched PhotoCraft, a vibe‑coded clone of Adobe’s Creative Suite that replicates Photoshop and Premiere for Mac users and promises a more affordable workflow.

The open‑source, clean‑room reimplementation sits on GitHub, is designed to be legally distinct from Adobe to avoid litigation while staying recognizable, and is available only in paid plans, which may help Mac users cut expenses and navigate nuanced feature differences.

AI’s surge in software development has turned PhotoCraft into what many view as its biggest scalp, as the vibe‑coded alternative directly challenges Adobe’s market position and illustrates a shift toward cost‑effective, user‑friendly creative tools.
