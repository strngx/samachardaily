Samsung has officially expanded its Galaxy Book6 personal computer lineup, introducing a new mainstream 14-inch variant designed to make the flagship laptop family accessible to a broader consumer market. Announced via the Samsung Global Newsroom, the addition aims to offer a balance between modern mobile computing performance and entry-level pricing.

The new 14-inch Galaxy Book6 model is priced starting at $800 and comes equipped with Intel Core 3 and Intel Core 5 processor configurations. By introducing this lower barrier to entry, Samsung is positioning the Galaxy Book ecosystem to capture budget-conscious buyers, students, and everyday productivity users who require dependable processing power in a portable chassis.

Alongside the entry-level announcement, Samsung retail channels and partner outlets are offering significant promotional adjustments on higher-tier models in the family. Consumers looking for elevated specifications can find discounts of up to $500 on the premium Samsung Galaxy Book6 Pro model without requiring a hardware trade-in.

The expansion comes amid an escalating hardware battle in the ultra-portable computing market. Rival manufacturer LG recently unveiled its Gram Book AI 2026 laptop, which promises a massive 30-hour battery life alongside dedicated artificial intelligence hardware. Samsung's latest counter-move focuses heavily on competitive price accessibility and tight integration with its broader Galaxy ecosystem.

With the addition of the $800 baseline 14-inch display model, Samsung completes its current personal computing hierarchy. The strategic move ensures the technology giant has tailored device options across both mid-range and premium laptop tiers as PC manufacturers push for market share.
