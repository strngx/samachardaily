Coherent shares jumped 13% as investors re‑evaluated a $20 billion AI partnership after a recent sell‑off. The move came despite the company having beaten earnings estimates earlier but then seeing its stock fall.

The rally contrasts with modest gains at peers Lumentum (+4%) and Applied Optoelectronics (+3%). A recent stock‑swap merger involving a rival left Coherent as the sole independent laser manufacturer, prompting traders to question whether its cash‑burn rate justifies staying separate.

Analysts note the market’s focus on whether Coherent can sustain growth without a merger partner.
