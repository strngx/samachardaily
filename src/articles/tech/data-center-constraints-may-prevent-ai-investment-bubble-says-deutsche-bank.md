Regulatory limits in US states that hold 45% of planned data-center capacity may slow the current AI buildout. According to a Deutsche Bank economist, these constraints could serve as a safeguard against the formation of an AI investment bubble.

Texas is a primary focal point of this expansion, accounting for 25% of all planned capacity. Construction spending in the state has already reached a pace that annualizes to nearly $100 billion per month.
