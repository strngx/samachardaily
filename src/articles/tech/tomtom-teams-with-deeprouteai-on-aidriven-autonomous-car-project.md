TomTom (ticker TOM2) and DeepRoute.ai have signed a memorandum of understanding to collaborate on AI‑based automated driving technology. The partnership merges TomTom’s mapping expertise with DeepRoute.ai’s AI systems, aiming to accelerate autonomous vehicle development.

An integrated demonstration vehicle will be unveiled at the Paris Motor Show, showcasing the joint solution. The MoU marks a step toward commercializing AI‑driven navigation and control for automakers and mobility providers.

The collaboration will focus on integrating TomTom’s precise maps with DeepRoute.ai’s real‑time AI processing, enabling safer lane‑level navigation. Both firms say the project will leverage existing automotive partnerships and regulatory frameworks to speed deployment.
