On 14 November, Italy’s data protection authority (Garante per la Protezione dei Dati Personali) imposed an €805 million penalty on Meta Platforms Ireland for breaching privacy rules.

The regulator found that a Facebook feature automatically scanned the media albums of roughly 20 million Italian Android users to suggest posts, using a single app‑wide permission and a brief ‘enable other functions’ warning that did not clearly disclose the gallery‑access purpose.

The investigation, launched in July 2025 after a complaint by the Bolzano Consumer Protection Center, concluded the practice violated Article 122 of Italy’s Privacy Code.
