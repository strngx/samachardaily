Sigma today announced Sigma Eval, an independent verification service designed to provide continuous, human-supervised evaluation of enterprise conversational AI agents. The platform combines automated assessment with expert oversight to create an assurance layer that measures AI system performance across 12 distinct dimensions covering safety, user experience, and operational reliability.

The service targets business risks associated with deploying generative AI in customer-facing and internal workflows. By establishing a standardized evaluation framework, Sigma Eval aims to give organizations measurable confidence in their AI agents before and during production use, addressing a gap where internal testing often lacks independence or consistent benchmarks.
