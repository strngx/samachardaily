The Digital India BHASHINI Division and the Office of the Comptroller and Auditor General of India (CAG) launched “CAG‑BHASHINI Anuvaad”, an AI‑enabled document translation solution, in New Delhi on September 30.

The new tool is designed to support translation of official documents, using artificial intelligence to speed up multilingual government communications and improve accessibility for diverse linguistic groups across the country. It aims to reduce processing time and minimize errors in cross‑language correspondence for CAG and other departments.

The initiative reflects the government’s push to digitize public services and enhance outreach through language technology, positioning the agency at the forefront of AI‑driven translation efforts in India. Officials say the solution will be rolled out gradually to cover additional languages in the coming months.
