Meta announced an indefinite delay to its planned commercial‑terms update that would have required Business account holders to permit Meta’s AI systems to train on content from external websites linked on Facebook.

The pause follows several weeks of user and industry backlash and a prior attempt by the company to address concerns, which did not succeed. In a statement, Meta said it is “pausing the changes as we continue to engage with…” stakeholders.

Meta indicated it will keep consulting affected parties before deciding whether to reinstate the clause.
