BERLIN, Sept. 5, 2026 – TECNO, the AI‑focused technology brand, announced that it has been honoured with two IFA Global Product Technology Innovation Awards at this year’s IFA exhibition. The accolades recognize the company’s modular phone concept and the Tonino Lamborghini‑styled TECNO TAURUS device, underscoring TECNO’s growing influence in the global consumer‑electronics arena.

IFA, the long‑standing Berlin trade fair that showcases the latest in consumer technology, draws manufacturers, developers and media from around the world. Winning an IFA Global Product Technology Innovation Award signals that a product stands out for its technical ingenuity, design excellence and market potential. TECNO’s dual win places the brand alongside established industry leaders who have previously been recognised at the event.

The modular phone, unveiled at the IFA 2026 showcase, is built around a interchangeable component system that allows users to upgrade or replace key hardware modules—such as camera, battery or processor—without discarding the entire device. TECNO describes the approach as a sustainable alternative to the traditional upgrade cycle, leveraging AI to optimise performance across different configurations.

The second award‑winning product, the Tonino Lamborghini TECNO TAURUS, merges the luxury automotive designer’s aesthetic with TECNO’s smartphone technology. The collaboration brings premium materials and distinctive styling cues to the device, positioning it as a high‑end offering for consumers seeking both performance and brand cachet.

TECNO’s CEO, who addressed the IFA press conference, highlighted the company’s commitment to AI‑driven innovation and its ambition to expand its footprint beyond its core markets. The dual recognition, he said, validates TECNO’s strategy of blending cutting‑edge technology with design partnerships that resonate with a global audience.
