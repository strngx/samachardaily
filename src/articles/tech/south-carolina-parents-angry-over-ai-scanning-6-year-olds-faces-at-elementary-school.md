South Carolina mother Nikki Anderson learned that her 6-year-old daughter Harper's elementary school had placed a photograph of the child into an AI system that studies students' faces, according to a Washington Post article by Caitlin Gibson.

The discovery has angered parents who expected their young children to focus on finger painting and phonics rather than encounter artificial intelligence in the classroom. Anderson is among the parents objecting to facial imaging technology being used with elementary school students, saying the situation caught families off guard.
