Generative Bionics has announced a strategic plan to build what it describes as Europe's largest Physical AI Data Factory. The project is aimed at advancing European humanoid sovereignty through AMD AI infrastructure.

The plan starts with a new 5,000-square-metre hub in Italy, the first node of a three-node Physical AI Data Factory network. The hub is intended to connect real-world robot learning and digital-twin simulation through AMD Helios infrastructure.

The network is designed to accelerate industrial humanoid deployments across the continent, the company said. The hub would form part of that broader effort.
