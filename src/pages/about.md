Welcome to **SamacharDaily**, an independent digital news publication dedicated to delivering fast, verified, and deeply contextualized reporting across India and the world. Founded and owned by **Arjun Khatri**, SamacharDaily operates under an independent solo-publisher and solo-developer model, with reporting and explanatory syntheses published under the institutional identity of [Samachar Daily]({{ '/authors/samachardaily-editorial-team/' | url }}).

In an era of endless scrolling and sensationalist headlines, SamacharDaily is built on a clear editorial premise: **readers need clarity, context, and speed**, not clickbait or noise. We cover five core categories: **India, World, Business, Tech, and Sports**.

## Editorial Mission

Our reporting is designed to answer three essential questions for every major story:
1. **What happened?** Clear, factual reporting of verified developments.
2. **Why does it matter?** Contextual analysis explaining the strategic, economic, and civic significance.
3. **What happens next?** Forward-looking timelines and regulatory or commercial milestones.

## AI-Assisted Journalism & Workflow Disclosure

At SamacharDaily, we believe in complete transparency regarding the modern technology that powers our publishing workflow:

- **Sourcing & Ingestion:** Our systems monitor real-time verified news wires and institutional press releases across our five categories.
- **AI-Assisted Synthesis:** We utilize advanced artificial intelligence models to assist in processing complex wire dispatches, generating executive summaries, and structuring deep explanatory context.
- **Editorial Quality Standards:** Automated systems are constrained by strict journalistic guidelines. They are strictly prohibited from fabricating quotes, statistics, or events. If source dispatches lack sufficient factual context, the story is not published.
- **Visuals & Media:** Editorial photography is sourced through licensed photography platforms (including Pexels and curated stock collections) with proper photographer attribution, or hosted directly on our domain. For select archival articles, historical dispatches retain publisher-attributed news wire imagery. Embedded video reports are linked directly to authorized broadcast networks.
- **Transparent Attribution:** Every article includes a clear citation of the primary wire services and releases that informed the coverage.

## Source Sourcing & Correction Policy

- **Source-Grounded Reporting:** Articles are synthesized directly from attributed source dispatches, retaining primary publisher citations in accordance with our [Editorial Policy & Standards]({{ '/editorial/' | url }}). While our automated pipeline does not perform independent multi-source investigative fact-checking, our systems enforce strict source fidelity to prevent unsupported additions.
- **Prompt Corrections:** If any factual error or ambiguity is discovered in our reporting, we promptly update the article and document the correction.
- **Reader Feedback & Grievances:** We encourage readers, organizations, and stakeholders to submit feedback, factual clarifications, or news inquiries. Step-by-step submission guidelines are available on our [Contact & Grievances Page]({{ '/contact/' | url }}).
