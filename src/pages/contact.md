## How to Submit a Factual Correction

To enable our editors to investigate and verify factual correction requests efficiently, please format your email to include:

1. **Article URL:** The complete link to the article on `thesamachardaily.in`.
2. **Headline & Date:** The published title and date of the dispatch.
3. **Specific Contested Detail:** The exact sentence, numerical figure, quotation, or statement in question.
4. **Primary Source Evidence:** A link or reference to official primary source documentation (e.g., government gazettes, regulatory filings, corporate press releases, or official agency statements).
5. **Proposed Clarification:** The precise factual correction requested.

## Review Protocol & Response Times

- **Operating Hours:** Editorial inquiries and correction submissions are monitored daily between **08:00 AM and 10:00 PM IST (UTC+5:30)**.
- **Verification Workflow:** All correction claims are cross-checked against authoritative wire dispatches, official source documents, or verified agency records.
- **Correction Implementation:** When a substantive factual error is verified, the article is promptly amended in accordance with our [Editorial Policy & Standards]({{ '/editorial/' | url }}).
