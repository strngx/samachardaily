Welcome to **SamacharDaily**. As an independent digital news publication covering **India, World, Business, Tech, and Sports**, we are committed to providing clear, verified, and contextualized reporting.

This Editorial Policy document outlines our newsroom principles, verification workflows, attribution standards, and corrections process.

---

## 1. Editorial Mission & Principles

Our reporting is structured around three fundamental questions for every major story:
1. **What happened?** Accurate, timely, and factual reporting of verified developments.
2. **Why does it matter?** Contextual analysis explaining the strategic, economic, regulatory, and civic significance.
3. **What happens next?** Forward-looking timelines, announced proceedings, and concrete subsequent steps.

We prioritize factual accuracy and clarity above all else. We do not publish clickbait, sensationalized speculation, or fabricated claims.

---

## 2. Verification Standards & Source Attribution

- **Primary Sourcing:** Our editorial workflows monitor real-time dispatches from verified news wires, institutional releases, government notifications, and authoritative industry publications.
- **Fact-Based Restraint:** We strictly report names, figures, dates, quotations, and official statements only when they are explicitly documented in primary source dispatches. If a specific detail is unconfirmed, our editors either omit it or describe the broader context in non-speculative terms.
- **Transparent Attribution:** Every published article features an explicit source citation noting the primary news wire or publisher whose dispatches informed the coverage, accompanied by a direct link to the primary release where available.

---

## 3. AI-Assisted Journalism & Workflow Disclosure

SamacharDaily utilizes modern artificial intelligence technology to assist our newsroom operations while maintaining strict editorial standards:

- **Ingestion & Synthesis:** AI models assist our editorial pipeline in ingesting complex wire dispatches, generating concise structured summaries, and organizing contextual analysis.
- **Accuracy Constraints & Guardrails:** Automated systems operate under strict algorithmic guardrails. They are explicitly prohibited from inventing facts, quotes, statistics, historical dates, or events. If available source dispatches lack sufficient substance or verification, the candidate story is discarded.
- **Editorial Tone & English Standards:** All published output undergoes automated and editorial language verification to ensure professional, fluent journalistic English.
- **Licensed Media & Attribution:** Editorial photography is sourced exclusively through licensed platforms (including Pexels and verified stock libraries) with proper photographer credit. Embedded broadcast reports are sourced directly from authorized broadcast channels via official YouTube embeds.

---

## 4. Treatment of Uncertain & Developing News

When reporting on fast-moving or developing events where complete details have not yet been independently verified:
- We clearly state what is officially confirmed versus what remains unconfirmed.
- If no confirmed subsequent steps have been announced by relevant authorities or participants, we explicitly state that no confirmed next steps are reported yet, rather than speculating on future actions.

---

## 5. Corrections & Clarifications Policy

- **Prompt Corrections:** If a factual error, inaccuracy, or misleading statement is identified in any of our published articles, our editorial desk updates the story promptly.
- **Reader Feedback & Corrections:** Readers, organizations, and stakeholders are encouraged to submit factual corrections, grievance notices, or news tips directly to our editorial team at [samachardaily.editorial@gmail.com](mailto:samachardaily.editorial@gmail.com).
- **Review Protocol:** All correction requests submitted via email are reviewed by our team between 08:00 AM and 10:00 PM IST.
