Welcome to **SamacharDaily**. As an independent digital news publication covering **India, World, Business, Tech, and Sports**, we are committed to providing clear, verified, and contextualized reporting.

This Editorial Policy document outlines our publishing principles, verification workflows, attribution standards, and corrections process.

---

## 1. Editorial Mission & Principles

Our reporting is structured around three fundamental questions for every major story:
1. **What happened?** Accurate, timely, and factual reporting of verified developments.
2. **Why does it matter?** Contextual analysis explaining the strategic, economic, regulatory, and civic significance.
3. **What happens next?** Forward-looking timelines, announced proceedings, and concrete subsequent steps.

We prioritize factual accuracy and clarity above all else. We do not publish clickbait, sensationalized speculation, or fabricated claims.

---

## 2. Source Sourcing & Attribution Standards

- **Primary Sourcing:** Our editorial workflows monitor real-time dispatches from verified news wires, institutional releases, government notifications, and authoritative industry publications.
- **Fact-Based Restraint:** Generated articles are constrained to report names, figures, dates, quotations, and official statements only when they are explicitly documented in the cited source dispatch. Automated generation instructions require unconfirmed details to be omitted or presented strictly as reported without speculative expansion.
- **Single-Source Ingestion & Scope:** Articles are synthesized from single incoming wire dispatches. The current automated pipeline does not conduct independent multi-source investigative reporting, proprietary fact-graph cross-verification, or universal pre-publication human review. Instead, factual fidelity is maintained through strict generation boundaries, automated quality filters, and an active reader corrections workflow.
- **Transparent Attribution:** Every published article features an explicit source citation noting the primary news wire or publisher whose dispatches informed the coverage, accompanied by a direct link to the primary release where available.

<h2 id="ai-workflow-disclosure">3. AI-Assisted Journalism &amp; Workflow Disclosure</h2>

SamacharDaily utilizes modern artificial intelligence technology to assist our editorial workflow while maintaining strict factual standards under a solo-publisher operational model owned and operated by Founder & Owner **Arjun Khatri**. We clearly distinguish between five distinct production phases:

1. **AI-Assisted Synthesis:** Large language models assist in parsing incoming wire dispatches, structuring key information, and drafting concise, readable news briefings.
2. **AI-Generated Contextual Annotations:** Explanatory modules such as "Why It Matters" are generated within strict boundaries to articulate economic, regulatory, and civic implications without introducing speculative claims.
3. **Automated Quality & Factuality Checks:** Output is systematically evaluated against algorithmic filters that enforce substance thresholds, eliminate prompt artifacts, detect duplicates, and prohibit fabricated names, dates, quotes, or figures.
4. **Human Review:** Applied selectively by the publisher to dispatches flagged in sensitive categories (health/clinical claims, fatal accidents, criminal allegations) and to all reader-submitted factual correction requests. We do not claim universal pre-publication line editing across every routine wire dispatch.
5. **Publication:** Approved dispatches are compiled into static HTML with primary source citations and accessible metadata.

- **Licensed Media & Attribution:** Current editorial photography is sourced through licensed platforms (including Pexels and verified stock libraries) with photographer attribution, or hosted directly on our domain. For historical/archival dispatches, select articles retain publisher-attributed news wire imagery from original reporting feeds. All embedded broadcast reports are sourced directly from authorized channels via official YouTube embeds.

## 4. Treatment of Uncertain & Developing News

When reporting on fast-moving or developing events where complete details have not yet been independently verified:
- We clearly state what is officially confirmed versus what remains unconfirmed.
- If no confirmed subsequent steps have been announced by relevant authorities or participants, we explicitly state that no confirmed next steps are reported yet, rather than speculating on future actions.

---

<h2 id="health-disclaimer">5. Health, Medical &amp; Dietary Information Standards</h2>

- **Informational Scope Only:** Reporting on medical treatments, public health emergencies, nutrition, diet, or wellness is strictly informational and journalistic. SamacharDaily does not provide medical, diagnostic, or personalized dietary advice.
- **Mandatory Review Gate:** Ingested wire dispatches containing health, medical, or dietary claims are automatically routed to our editorial draft staging area for human review prior to publication.
- **Informational Disclaimers:** Articles discussing clinical findings, wellness products, or dietary modifications include a clear informational disclaimer reminding readers to consult qualified healthcare professionals.

<h2 id="corrections-policy">6. Corrections &amp; Clarifications Policy</h2>

- **Prompt Corrections:** If a factual error, inaccuracy, or misleading statement is identified in any of our published articles, our editorial desk updates the story promptly.
- **Reader Feedback & Corrections:** Readers, organizations, and stakeholders are encouraged to submit factual corrections, grievance notices, or news tips directly to our editorial desk at [samachardaily.editorial@gmail.com](mailto:samachardaily.editorial@gmail.com). Detailed step-by-step submission instructions are available on our [Contact & Grievances Page]({{ '/contact/' | url }}).
- **Review Protocol:** All correction requests submitted via email are reviewed by the publisher between 08:00 AM and 10:00 PM IST (UTC+5:30).
